# PROJECT: SlateCamp

A high-performance, discipline-agnostic study engine for professional licensure exams in the Philippines.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To eliminate the psychological and financial burden of licensure exams by providing students with a single, data-driven "Educational OS" that guarantees readiness.

## 🏔️ The Problem

- **Fragmentation:** Students juggle blurry PDFs, outdated textbooks, and expensive physical review centers.
- **Feedback Gap:** No way to measure actual competence until the exam day.
- **Spatial Blindness:** Difficulty visualizing abstract building codes in 3D.
- **AI Hallucinations:** Generic AI tools often hallucinate critical board exam data.

## 🚀 The Big Six Features

1.  **SlateCamp Wiki:** Taxonomy-mapped, Markdown-based source of truth.
2.  **Adaptive Quiz Engine:** Transition from passive reading to active recall (Camp vs. Storm Mode).
3.  **TipTap Workspace:** Personal notes with source-badge capture from all features.
4.  **Interactive 3D Lab:** Parametric sandbox translating building codes into 3D geometry (R3F).
5.  **AI Contextual Tutor:** Zero-hallucination, temperature 0.0, citation-mandatory RAG engine.
6.  **Predictive Analytics:** Readiness Score, Taxonomy Heatmap, and Pressure Gap analysis.

## 🛠️ Technical Boundaries

- **Architecture:** Next.js 16 (App Router), Supabase (Auth/DB/RLS/Storage/pgvector).
- **Styling:** Apple HIG using Tailwind CSS v4.
- **Performance:** Target 3G/4G budget mobile connectivity.
- **Verification:** Mandatory auto-save (10s), IRT difficulty weighting, and Lucky Guess detection.
