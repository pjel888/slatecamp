# PROJECT: SlateCamp

A high-performance, discipline-agnostic study engine for professional licensure exams in the Philippines.

---

## 🎯 Mission
To eliminate the psychological and financial burden of licensure exams by providing high-fidelity, adaptive simulation and spatial learning tools.

## 🏛️ Architectural Identity
- **Pedagogy First:** Feedback loops are designed around elaborative interrogation and retrieval practice.
- **Data Integrity:** Living schema and automated seeds ensure environment parity across the team.
- **Institutional Quality:** High-density analytics and B2B admin operations for content QC.

## 📅 Success Criteria
- [x] Phase 1: Foundations (Core Loop) established.
- [x] Phase 2: Diagnostics & Synthesis (Readiness) established.
- [ ] **Living Schema:** All Supabase tables and RLS documented in a master `schema.sql`.
- [ ] **Seed Pipeline:** CLI-driven re-seeds enable one-command workspace setup.
- [ ] **3D Lab:** Parametric structural visualization functional.
- [ ] **The Control Room:** Admin dashboard for content management operational.
- [ ] **Commercial Readiness:** Stripe and PayRex webhooks verified.
