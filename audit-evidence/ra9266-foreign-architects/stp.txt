DOCUMENTARY REQUIREMENTS FOR SPECIAL TEMPORARY PERMIT

General requirements:
1. Duly accomplished Application Form for Special Temporary Permit (STP) (The form is available at the
https://www.prc.gov.ph/special-temporary-permits)
2. Photocopy of the data page of the valid passport as proof of citizenship (The validity of the passport must
be within the duration of the practice of profession.)
3. Authenticated or Apostilled Professional license issued by the competent regulatory authority in his/her
country with official English Translation thereof when necessary. For foreign Physicians, he/she must
submit an authenticated or apostilled Certificate  of Specialization. The validity/expiration date must be
reflected on the professional license or certificate and it remains valid during the practice of
profession in the Philippines. For professional licenses/registration with lifetime validity, the applicant
is required to submit an updated Certificate of Good Standing (COGS) issued by the Professional
Regulatory Authority (PRA) or Accredited Institution for the Profession from the applicant's country of
origin. The same must be apostilled/authenticated.
Please see asterisk at the bottom of this page on the legalization of the document . The laws are
available at https://www.prc.gov.ph/professional-regulatory-boards.

4. For Categories STP1, STP2, and STP3 , at least one (1) Filipino Professional counterpart for every
foreign professional whose services will be engaged in the Philippines, who shall be employed for the
same duration as the foreign national’s tenure with the company or firm; indicate the name of counterpart
in the letter request supported by a contract of employment with a photocopy of current PRC Professional
I.D. Card (The validity of the Professional ID Card must be within the duration of the practice of profession
of the foreign national. However, with regard to the following:
• Accountancy: If the foreign national is an ASEAN CPA, the counterpart should be a Filipino
ASEAN CPA.
• Electronics Engineering : There must be at least two (2) Filipino electronics engineer
counterparts for every foreign professional.
• Geodetic Engineering: The Filipino counterpart must be a member in good standing with the
A.P.O.
• Interior Design: The Filipino counterpart must be in continuous practice of interior design for at
least ten (10) years).

5. For Category STP4:
• At least one (1) Filipino Professional counterpart for every foreign professional whose services
will be engaged in the Philippines to be indicated in the letter request to PRC/PRB with a
photocopy of current PRC Professional I.D. However, with regard to Midwifery, Nutrition and
Dietetics, Physical Therapy, Occupational Therapy, Respiratory Therapy  and Veterinary
Medicine, instead of providing a Filipino Professional counterpart, applicant must submit names
of officers or representatives of local sponsoring organization/institution. This is not applicable
to Agriculture, Chemistry and Social Workers.
• At least one (1) Filipino Professional counterpart of the same specialization for every foreign
dentist or physician -specialists, whose services are under co -management arrangement
characterized by shared responsibility, authority, and accountability in patient care.

6. One (1) piece passport size I.D. picture in formal attire with complete name tag in white background
7. For profession specific documentary requirements, the same is available at https://prc.gov.ph/special -
temporary-permits.

Category-specific requirements

Category STP1
1. Letter request to the PRB Chairperson requesting for the issuance of STP to the foreign professional
indicating the following in bullet points the date and venue of the undertaking and name of Filipino
Professional counterpart with current roles and responsibilities in the company to qualify as counterpart
and provide with narratives of the detailed expertise, skills and competencies of foreign applicant that
will be transferred to the counterpart for the duration of the undertaking of foreign applicant;
2. Authenticated/apostilled official copy of the foreign law or international agreement as proof of reciprocity
for the practice of profession, officially translated in the English Language, if applicable. (Please see
asterisk at the next page on the legalization of document executed/issued abroad)
3. Notarized copy of Contract of Employment or Services, or Memorandum of Agreement between the
employer and the foreign professional indicating the terms of reference, nature of engagement, scope,
duration and other project or contract details

Category STP2
1. Letter request addressed to the PRB Chairperson requesting for the issuance of STP to foreign
professional to be engaged as a consultant in foreign -funded, joint venture or foreign-assisted projects
of the government indicating the following in bullet points the date and venue of the undertaking and
name of Filipino Professional counterpart with current roles and responsibilities in the company to qualify

as counterpart and provide with narratives of the detailed expertise, skills and competencies of foreign
applicant that will be transferred to the counterpart for the duration of the undertaking of foreign applicant;
2. Notarized copy of the Contract of Employment or Services, or Memorandum of Agreement between the
government agency and the foreign professional, indicating the terms of reference, nature of
engagement, scope, duration and other project or contract details

Category STP3
1. Letter request addressed to the PRB Chairperson requesting for the issuance of the STP to the foreign
professional to be employed by the local or foreign private firms or institutions indicating the following in
bullet points the date and venue of the undertaking and name of Filipino Professional counterpart with
current roles and responsibilities in the company to qualify as counterpart and provide with narratives of
the detailed expertise, skills and competencies of foreign a pplicant that will be transfe rred to the
counterpart for the duration of the undertaking of foreign applicant; if a foreigner is a teacher, please also
include the following information on the letter: year level and subjects to be taught.
2. Notarized copy of the Contract of Employment or Services, or Memorandum of Agreement between the
private firm or institution and the foreign professional, indicating the terms of reference, nature of
engagement, scope, duration and other project or contract details


Category STP4
1. Letter request to the PRB Chairperson requesting for the issuance of STP to the foreign professional for
the conduct of humanitarian or medical, dental or surgical mission indicating the following in bullet
points the date and venue of the mission in the Philippines, name of Filipino professional counterpart
(Please see No. 4 above for the details) and names of foreign professionals indicating their respective
undertaking with supporting Certificate/s of Training/Co mpetency in the discipline of area of
specialization for which the foreign professional is to be engaged in the Philippines

*** Legalization of document executed/issued abroad: Starting 14 May 2019, foreign documents from Apostille-contracting countries
(available at https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=41) need not be authenticated abroad by Philippine
Embassies and Consulates General in order to be recognized and accepted in the Philippines.  Apostille Certification or e-Apostille
(electronic copy)  issued by the foreign competent authority responsible for the implementation of the Apostille Convention will be
sufficient. While foreign documents issued in non-member countries including Germany, shall have notarial (i.e., acknowledgement and
jurat) with authentication from the Philippine Embassies and Consulates . The document must be accompanied by an official English
translation, if applicable.


REQUEST FOR AN EXEMPTION OR EXCLUSION FROM THE STP REQUIREMENT
1. Letter request addressed to PRC/PRB requesting for an exemption from authority to practice by citing
clear factual and/or legal basis for an exemption
2. Photocopy of valid passport as proof of citizenship
3. Notarized copy of the Contract of Employment or Services, or Memorandum of Agreement between
the employer and the foreign professional, indicating the terms of reference, nature of engagement,
scope, duration and other project or contract details

IMPORTANT NOTE:
1. For group applications, documentary requirements should be segregated and submitted per profession.
2. Applicant must also submit the application documents in one pdf file.  Kindly scan the documents in an
orderly manner based on the General Requirements list, followed by the Category-Specific Requirements
list. The same applies to the request for Exemption  or Exclusion from the STP Requirement list. Email
the same at the official email address of the Regional Office where the hard copies of the documents will
be submitted.
3. The names of the PRB Chairperson are available at the https://prc.gov.ph/professional-regulatory-boards
4. For Categories STP1, STP2 and STP3, the Accomplishment report must include the following information:
1) Performance of the Filipino counterpart within the duration of the STP including his/her specific learnings
on the advance trends and technologies; and, 2) Information in dealing with d elays, emergencies and
mitigating risks at the project site, if applicable.
5. For STP4, Accomplishment report/Post Mission Report of foreign professional for the period covered by the
STP, previously issued to the foreign professional/s must be immediately submitted after the expiration of
the STP at qrd2@prc.gov.ph with the subject: Post Report – (Name of the Requesting Group).
