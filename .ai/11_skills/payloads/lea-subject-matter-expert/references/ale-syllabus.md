## Area A: History & Theory; Principles of Planning; Architectural Practice (30%)

### Part I: History of Architecture

**Rationale:** Analysis of architectural manifestations from the beginning of civilization to contemporary periods. Analysis of environmental, historical, and sociocultural influences on buildings, structures, and human settlements.

#### Scope:

**1. Introduction**
- History and history of architecture
- Historic style of architecture
- Origin of architecture
- Factors affecting the style of architecture (environmental, historical, socio-cultural)

**2. Pre-Historic Architecture**
- Centers of development
- Dolmen, Menhir, Cromlech

**3. Historic Styles — Ancient Architecture and the Western Succession**

For EACH historic style, candidates must know:
- Period/extent and centers of development
- Factors affecting development (historical, environmental, socio-cultural)
- General characteristics: architectural, structural, decorative
- General contributions
- Notable examples

Styles in sequence:
1. **Egyptian** — pyramids at Giza, temples at Karnak/Luxor, hypostyle halls, post-and-lintel, lotus/papyrus columns
2. **West Asiatic (Mesopotamian)** — Sumerian ziggurats, Babylonian gates (Ishtar Gate), mud brick, arch/vault
3. **Greek** — Orders (Doric, Ionic, Corinthian), Parthenon, Acropolis, entasis, cella, peristyle
4. **Roman** — arch, vault, dome (Pantheon), concrete (opus caementicium), Colosseum, basilica plan
5. **Early Christian** — basilica church plan, atrium, nave/aisle, apse
6. **Byzantine** — pendentive, squinch, mosaic, Hagia Sophia, centralized plan
7. **Romanesque in Europe** — round arches, thick walls, small windows, barrel vault, nave/transept/choir
8. **Gothic in Europe** — pointed arch, ribbed vault, flying buttress, large stained glass windows, verticality; Notre Dame de Paris, Chartres, Cologne
9. **Renaissance in Europe** — classical revival, Brunelleschi (dome of Florence Cathedral), Alberti, Palladio (Palladian style), perspective
10. **19th–20th Century Great Britain** — Neo-Gothic, Arts & Crafts, Crystal Palace (Paxton), Art Nouveau
11. **Architecture of Africa, Australia, New Zealand** — vernacular traditions, colonial overlays
12. **19th–20th Century Continental Europe** — Art Nouveau (Gaudi), Bauhaus (Gropius), Le Corbusier's 5 Points, Mies van der Rohe, De Stijl
13. **Architecture of the Americas** — Pre-Columbian (Maya, Aztec, Inca), Colonial Spanish, American modern (Frank Lloyd Wright — organic, prairie style)

**4. Architecture in Asia and the Pacific Region**
- Chinese architecture: timber frame, bracketing (dougong), pagodas, courtyard houses
- Japanese architecture: modularity, shoji screens, Ise Shrine, tatami module
- Indian architecture: Hindu temples (shikhara), Buddhist stupas, Mughal (Taj Mahal)
- Southeast Asian: Angkor Wat, Borobudur
- Pacific/Oceanian vernacular

**5. Architecture in the Philippines**
- **Pre-Colonial architectural legacies:** bahay kubo (indigenous), torogan (Maranao), ifugao rice terraces
- **Spanish Colonial:** stone churches (Baroque), bahay na bato, walled fortifications (Intramuros), town plaza system
- **American Colonial:** neoclassical public buildings (Burnham Plan of Manila), wood-frame bungalow
- **Contemporary Philippine Architecture:** modern independence-era buildings, Leandro Locsin, Carlos Arguelles, major NHCP heritage buildings
- **Architectural preservation, conservation, restoration:** NHCP, conservation principles, adaptive reuse
- **Pillars of Philippine Architecture:** Juan Nakpil (National Artist), Pablo Antonio, Carlos Arguelles, Leandro Locsin (National Artist), Francisco Mañosa, Ildefonso Santos Jr.

### Part II: Theory of Architecture

**Rationale:** Understanding theories and principles of design and architectural design process; anthropometric, proxemic, kinesthetic requirements; sociocultural and technological trends.

#### Scope:

**1. Introduction**
- The nature of architecture
- Architecture as art and science
- Processes in architectural design (programming → schematic → design development → construction documents → CA)

**2. Elements of Architecture and Basic Principles of Design**
- Need-specific elements
- Structural, circulatory, protective, and decorative elements
- Influences in architectural design
- Basic principles of design:
  - Unity and Variety
  - Balance (symmetrical, asymmetrical, radial)
  - Proportion and Scale (Golden Section, Fibonacci, human scale)
  - Rhythm (repetition, alternation, progression)
  - Emphasis / Focal Point
  - Contrast and Harmony

**3. Design Perception**
- Anthropometric basis: human body dimensions (Neufert / Architectural Graphic Standards equivalents for PH context)
- Space articulation and territoriality (Edward Hall's proxemics)
- Visual and perceptual language (Gestalt principles: figure-ground, continuity, closure, similarity)
- Psychology of space (Kevin Lynch: image of the city — paths, edges, districts, nodes, landmarks)
- Proxemics and culture: intimate (0-45cm), personal (45-120cm), social (1.2-3.6m), public (3.6m+) [verify with Hall's original]

**4. Tropical Architecture**
- Influences and elements: sun path, prevailing winds, rainfall, humidity
- Passive cooling strategies: cross ventilation, overhangs, shading, elevated floors
- Specific examples: Ken Yeang's bioclimatic design, Southeast Asian vernacular

**5. Masters of Architecture**
- **Walter Gropius:** Bauhaus school, prefabrication, universal design
- **Le Corbusier:** 5 Points of Architecture (pilotis, free plan, free façade, horizontal windows, roof garden); Unite d'Habitation; Chandigarh
- **Mies van der Rohe:** "Less is more," glass and steel minimalism, Barcelona Pavilion, Farnsworth House
- **Frank Lloyd Wright:** Organic architecture, prairie style, Fallingwater, Guggenheim Museum NY
- **Louis Kahn:** served and servant spaces, monumentality; Salk Institute; National Assembly Bangladesh
- **Tadao Ando:** Brutalist concrete, light as material; Church of Light
- **Zaha Hadid:** Parametric design, flowing forms
- **I.M. Pei:** Modernism, Louvre Pyramid, EDSA Shangri-La (Manila)

### Part III: Architectural Practice

**Rationale:** Role, legal rights, obligations, and responsibilities of the architect; statutes, codes, regulations; professional practice tools and project management.

#### Scope:

**1. Certification of Architects and Standards of Professional Practice**
- **Statutes regulating architecture in the Philippines:**
  - RA 9266 (Architecture Act of 2004) — primary law
  - IRR of RA 9266 (effective December 1, 2004)
  - SPP (Standards of Professional Practice, 2010 update)
- **Professional organization:** UAP = Integrated and Accredited Professional Organization of Architects (IAPOA)
- **Code of Ethical Conduct** (SPP Doc. 200)
- Rights and responsibilities of the profession
- Spectrum of architectural services (SPP Docs. 201–209)
- Architectural fees and charges (SPP Doc. 210 / UAP Doc. 208)
- Architectural competitions

**SPP Documents — Know These:**
| Document | Service |
|---|---|
| SPP Doc. 201 | Pre-Design Services |
| SPP Doc. 202 | Design Services (primary service) |
| SPP Doc. 203 | Specialized Allied Services |
| SPP Doc. 204 | Construction Services |
| SPP Doc. 205 | Post-Construction Services |
| SPP Doc. 206 | Comprehensive Architectural Services |
| SPP Doc. 207 | Design-Build Services |
| SPP Doc. 208 | Architectural Interiors |
| SPP Doc. 209 | Landscape Architecture |
| SPP Doc. 210 | Methods of Compensation & Schedule of Fees |

**Stages of Design Service (SPP Doc. 202):**
1. Pre-Design Phase
2. Schematic Design Phase (SD)
3. Design Development Phase (DD)
4. Contract Documents Phase (CD) — working drawings + specifications
5. Bidding / Tender Phase
6. Construction Phase (Architect in supervision role)
7. Post-Construction Phase

**Methods of Compensation:**
- Percentage of Project Construction Cost (PCC) — most common
- Multiple of Direct Personnel Expense (MDPE) — pre-design services
- Lump Sum / Fixed Fee
- Per Diem / Hourly
- Professional Fee + Expenses

**2. Building Standards, Laws, and Regulations**
- **Architectural design standards, building laws:**
  - PD 1096 (NBCP, 1977) + 2004 Revised IRR
  - RA 9514 (Fire Code of the Philippines, 2008) + IRR
  - BP 344 (Accessibility Law, 1983) + 2024 Revised IRR
  - NSCP 2015 (National Structural Code)
  - PEC (Philippine Electrical Code)
  - National Plumbing Code (NPC)
- **Laws on real estate and subdivisions:**
  - PD 957 (Subdivision and Condominium Buyers Protective Decree)
  - BP 220 (Economic and Socialized Housing)
- **Laws on housing and human settlements:**
  - RA 7279 (Urban Development and Housing Act — UDHA)
  - RA 7160 (Local Government Code — zoning authority)
  - PD 1067 (Water Code of the Philippines)

**3. Aspects of Architectural Practice**
- Operating divisions: Development, Production, Administration, Management
- Emphasis on **contract documents preparation:**
  - Drawings (architectural, structural, MEP)
  - Technical specifications (descriptive, performance, proprietary)
  - Bill of Quantities / Cost Estimates
  - General Conditions of Contract (GCC)
- Specifications writing: CSI MasterFormat concepts
- Quantity surveying methods
- Legal and business aspects: corporation law, contractor licensing (PCAB), insurance

### Part IV: Theory and Principles of Planning

**Rationale:** Concepts and techniques in general planning, urban/regional planning, land use planning, human settlements planning; art and science of site planning.

#### Scope:

**1. General Principles of Planning**
- Definitions and classification (comprehensive, master, zoning)
- History and scope of planning (Burnham Plan of Manila, Comprehensive Land Use Plan)
- General planning process: survey → analysis → plan → implementation → monitoring

**2. Urban and Regional Planning and Urban Design**
- History of the city: Hippodamus (grid), Haussmann (Paris boulevards), Cerdà (Barcelona)
- **Theories of urban and regional planning:**
  - Garden City (Ebenezer Howard)
  - Radiant City (Le Corbusier's CIAM)
  - New Urbanism (Duany, Plater-Zyberk)
  - Transit-Oriented Development (TOD)
  - Smart Growth
- Comprehensive planning
- **Land use planning:** residential, commercial, industrial, institutional, open space, agriculture
- **Urban design elements:** Kevin Lynch's 5 elements
- Urbanization and urban social relationships
- Philippine context: HLURB/DHSUD, Local Government Code zoning

**3. Housing and Human Settlements Planning**
- Definition and classification (socialized, economic, middle-class)
- Housing policies: BP 220, PD 957, RA 7279 (UDHA), SHFC, NHA, HLURB/DHSUD
- Housing finance: PAG-IBIG, SSS, HDMF, private banks
- Housing production: developer-built, government-built, community-led (CMP — Community Mortgage Program)

**4. Art and Science of Site Planning and Landscape Architecture**
- Site analysis: topography, drainage, soil bearing capacity, vegetation, solar access, prevailing winds
- Site development: grading, earthwork, site drainage, landscaping
- Landscape design: hardscape vs. softscape, xeriscaping
- Primary site planning considerations:
  - Physical and aesthetic
  - Ecological (environmental impact assessment)
  - Socio-psychological (territoriality, CPTED)
  - Management and maintenance

## Area B: Structural Design; Building Materials; Utilities (30%)

### Part I: Structural Design

**Rationale:** Fundamentals of mechanics, strength of materials, and theory of structures; general design, principles, and analysis of structural elements.

#### Scope — General Application including Seismic Analysis:

**a. Timber**
- Properties: species, grades, moisture content, allowable stresses
- Design: beams, columns, joists, rafters (ASD method)
- Connections: nails, bolts, split rings
- Philippine species: apitong, yakal, molave, tanguile

**b. Reinforced Concrete (RC)**
- Design methods: ASD (Working Stress) and LRFD (Strength Design — NSCP 2015 preferred)
- Load combinations (NSCP 2015 Section 203)
- Beams: T-beams, rectangular beams, shear design
- Columns: tied vs. spiral, eccentricity
- Slabs: one-way, two-way, waffle, flat plate
- Footings: isolated, combined, mat/raft
- Seismic analysis: Philippine seismic zones (Zone 2 — entire PH), NSCP seismic provisions, base shear calculation

**c. Structural Steel**
- Wide flange, channel, angle, hollow sections
- LRFD method (AISC, adopted in NSCP)
- Beams: bending, shear, deflection
- Columns: effective length, slenderness ratio
- Connections: welded, bolted (friction vs. bearing)

**d. Composite Structures**
- Steel-concrete composite beams
- Shear studs

**e. Advanced Construction Methods**
- Post-tensioned/pre-stressed concrete
- Pneumatic structures
- Space frames, tensile structures
- Foundation types: deep (pile, caisson), shallow (footing)

**Key seismic facts for Philippines:**
- Philippines is in **Seismic Zone 2** (high seismicity) per NSCP 2015
- Earthquake-resistant design required for all structures
- Soft story irregularity is a common failure mode

### Part II: Building Materials and Methods of Construction

**Rationale:** Properties of building materials; systems and methods of construction; principles of design and construction methods.

#### Scope — Design and Specifications for:

**a. Civil Works** — earthwork, grading, sub-base, paving, drainage

**b. Carpentry and Joinery**
- Lumber grades and sizes (nominal vs. actual)
- Joinery: mortise-and-tenon, dovetail, rabbet
- Ceiling systems: metal framing, gypsum board

**c. Concrete and Masonry**
- CHB (Concrete Hollow Blocks): standard sizes (4", 5", 6"), typical course heights
- Mortar mix proportions
- Portland cement types (Type I = OPC, Type III = high early strength)
- Grout and reinforcement spacing for CHB walls
- Concrete mixes: Class A (1:2:4), Class B (1:2.5:5), Class C (1:3:6) [approximately]
- Slump, water-cement ratio, strength relationship

**d. Sheet Metal and Tinsmithry**
- GI sheet gauges, flashing, gutters, downspouts

**e. Structural Steel**
- Fabrication, erection, fireproofing

**f. Waterproofing, Damproofing, and Insulation**
- Torch-applied membrane, crystalline, cementitious
- Vapor barriers
- Thermal insulation: R-values

**g. Glass and Glazing**
- Float glass, tempered, laminated, insulated glass unit (IGU)
- U-value, SHGC, visible light transmittance
- Glazing systems: silicone, structural sealant

**h. Painting and Varnishing**
- Primers, undercoats, topcoats
- Paint types: latex (water-based), oil-based, epoxy, polyurethane

**i. Fenestration**
- Doors: hollow core, solid core, fire-rated doors
- Windows: casement, sliding, awning, jalousie, fixed

**j. Hardware**
- Door hardware: cylindrical, mortise locks; butt hinges, continuous hinges
- Accessibility hardware (lever handles, push plates)

**k. Specialized Works**
- Bank vaults, signage, waterfeatures

### Part III: Utilities

**Rationale:** Basic practices, principles, general design and installation of utilities required for a building; aesthetic, functional, and structural relationships.

#### Scope:

**1. Sanitary and Plumbing Systems**
- Water source, storage, supply:
  - Upfeed vs. downfeed distribution
  - Pressure requirements (minimum 7 psi at fixtures)
  - Roof tank sizing
- Plumbing roughing-in and fixtures:
  - Fixture unit values (DFU — Drainage Fixture Units; WFU — Water Supply Fixture Units)
  - Trap requirements (P-trap, S-trap)
- Drainage and sewerage:
  - Pipe slopes (1:50 standard for soil pipes)
  - Horizontal drain sizing
  - Septic tank design (Philippine National Plumbing Code)
- Waste disposal, treatment, recycling:
  - Septic tank + dosing tank + leach field
  - Sewage treatment plants (STP) for larger developments

**2. Mechanical Systems**
- **HVAC:**
  - Cooling load calculation (TONSOFREFRIGERATION)
  - 1 ton of refrigeration = 3.517 kW = 12,000 BTU/hr
  - Split type, package unit, centralized chilled water systems
  - Ventilation rates per occupancy (ACH — Air Changes per Hour)
- **Conveyors and mechanical equipment:**
  - Elevators: hydraulic vs. traction (cable)
  - Escalators: 30° typical angle
  - Dumbwaiters
  - Minimum elevator dimensions (BP 344 wheelchair-accessible: 1.10m x 1.40m cab minimum)

**3. Electrical and Other Power Systems**
- Electrical power supply: metering, main circuit breaker, distribution boards
- Wire sizes (AWG), circuit ampacity
- Power density (watts/m²) by occupancy
- Lighting: footcandle requirements by task
- Alternative power: solar PV, generators (emergency power requirements)
- Grounding and lightning protection

**4. Acoustics and Illumination**
- **Acoustics:**
  - Decibel scale (dB), noise criteria (NC) curves
  - Reverberation time (RT60) — Sabine formula
  - Sound transmission class (STC) for walls and floors
  - HVAC noise control
- **Illumination:**
  - Illuminance levels (lux) by task (e.g., 500 lux for office work)
  - Footcandles vs. lux (1 fc = 10.76 lux)
  - Coefficient of Utilization (CU), Light Loss Factor (LLF)
  - Zonal Cavity Method for lighting calculations
  - Natural daylighting: window-to-wall ratio, daylight factor

**5. Disaster Prevention and Protection Systems; Security Systems**
- **Fire protection (RA 9514):**
  - Sprinkler systems: wet pipe, dry pipe, pre-action, deluge
  - Activation temperature: standard (74°C), intermediate (100°C), high (141°C)
  - Fire suppression: standpipe classes (Class I, II, III)
  - Fire extinguisher types (ABC dry chemical, CO₂, halon equiv.)
  - Smoke detection: ionization vs. photoelectric
  - Fire alarm: automatic, manual pull stations, notification appliances
- **Disaster prevention:**
  - Seismic dampers, base isolation systems
  - Storm surge and flood mitigation

**6. Communication Systems**
- Structured cabling (Cat5e/Cat6)
- Telephone, intercom, audio-visual systems
- PA (public address) system
- CCTV, access control, security systems
- BMS (Building Management System)

**7. High-Tech Systems**
- Building Automation System (BAS)
- Intelligent buildings / Smart building systems
- Robotics in construction
- BIM (Building Information Modeling) — mention in professional practice context

## Area C: Architectural Design and Site Planning (40%)

**Rationale:** Application of logical approach to design and site planning; design methodology; qualitative and quantitative aspects of space; circulation; structural/form envelope; building utilities; visualization and graphical presentation.

**This area is the most heavily weighted (40%) and includes design problems.**

### Building Types Covered:

**1. Residential**
- Single-family residential, duplex, rowhouse
- Multi-family: apartment, condominium
- Housing for special groups: elderly (accessible design), low-cost (BP 220 standards)
- Lodging houses: hotel room sizing, typical programs

**2. Commercial and Business**
- Business: office, bank, hotel
- Commercial: department store, market (wet and dry), retail
- Mixed-use development

**3. Industrial and Agricultural**
- Large-scale: manufacturing plant, shipyard
- Small-scale: factory, studio
- Agro-industrial establishments

**4. Public and Government**
- Educational: school, university, research laboratory, library, museum
- Health: hospital, clinic, health fitness center, specialty medical center
- Government: national/public buildings, police/fire stations, embassies, penitentiary
- Parks and recreational: botanical garden, theater, cinema, casino, resort
- Sports: sports complex, stadium, gymnasium, courts

**5. Facilities**
- Transportation: airport, seaport, train station, bus terminal
- Service: power station, water treatment plant, TV/radio/telephone facilities
- Military: camp, depot

**6. Complex Projects**
- Multi-building campus or mixed-use development on a given site

### Design Competencies for Area C:
- **Space programming:** calculating net vs. gross floor area, efficiency ratios
- **Space relationships:** bubble diagrams, adjacency matrix, circulation hierarchy
- **Building setbacks, lot coverage:** applying PD 1096 IRR development controls
- **Parking computation:** applying NBCP parking standards per occupancy
- **Accessibility compliance:** BP 344 requirements integrated into design
- **Fire egress:** RA 9514 travel distance, exit width, exit discharge
- **Structural module selection:** appropriate for building type
- **Environmental design:** orientation, sun path, ventilation strategy
- **Graphic presentation:** floor plans, elevations, sections, perspective, site plan
