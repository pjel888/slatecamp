## PD 1096 — National Building Code of the Philippines (NBCP)
**Signed:** February 19, 1977 by President Ferdinand Marcos
**Administered by:** DPWH Secretary
**Technical staff:** National Building Code Development Office (NBCDO)
**Revised IRR:** Published April 1, 8, 15, 2005 (Manila Standard Today)
**Note for ALE:** The 2004 Revised IRR is specifically allowed in the exam room (Rules VII and VIII of the 2004 Revised IRR only)

### Key Rule Structure (2004 Revised IRR):
- **Rule I:** General Provisions (Sec. 101–104) — policy, scope, general building requirements
- **Rule II:** Administration and Enforcement (Sec. 201–208) — Building Official, permit process
- **Rule III:** Permits and Inspection (Sec. 301–309) — building permits, certificates
- **Rule IV:** Types of Construction (Sec. 401–412) — Type I (wood) through Type V (fireresistive)
- **Rule V:** Architectural Requirements (Sec. 501–) — the core architectural section
- **Rule VI:** Structural Requirements
- **Rule VII:** Mechanical/Electrical/Plumbing/Sanitary
- **Rule VIII:** Referral Code Sub-system (occupancy-specific requirements)

### Section 302.3 — The Most Important Architectural Provision
Only Registered Licensed Architects (RLAs) shall prepare, sign and seal architectural documents. This is the enforcement mechanism of RA 9266 within the building permit process.

### Development Controls (DCs) — Heavily Tested
These are the mathematical heart of many ALE design questions:

**Key Terms:**
- **TLA** — Total Lot Area (the entire lot, including setbacks)
- **PSO** — Percentage of Site Occupancy = AMBF/TLA × 100
- **AMBF** — Allowable Maximum Building Footprint (buildable area within required setbacks)
- **OLF** — Open Lot/Space within the Lot (TLA minus AMBF)
- **TGFA** — Total Gross Floor Area (sum of all floor areas of the building)
- **FLAR** — Floor-to-Lot Area Ratio = TGFA / TLA
- **BHL** — Building Height Limit

**What TGFA includes/excludes:**
TGFA includes all covered floor areas.
TGFA **excludes:**
- Covered parking areas and driveways
- Vertical penetrations in parking floors (without residential/office use)
- Uncovered areas: helipads, AC cooling towers, roof decks, laundry areas, wading pools, swimming pools, whirlpools/jacuzzis, terraces, gardens, courts, plazas
- Balconies exceeding 10.0 sq.m.
- Fire escape structures

**PSO by Lot Type (approximate — verify against current IRR tables):**
| Lot Classification | PSO |
|---|---|
| Corner lot | Higher allowed PSO |
| Interior lot | Lower allowed PSO |
| Through lot | Special conditions |
*Exact PSO percentages vary by zone and local ordinance. Defer to specific NBCP IRR table.*

### Stairway Requirements (NBCP — Frequently Tested)
- **Minimum clear width:** 750 mm (residential); 1,100 mm (public buildings)
- **Maximum riser height:** 200 mm
- **Minimum tread depth:** 250 mm (verify exact value against Rule V)
- **Minimum headroom clearance:** 2,000 mm (2.0m)
- **Maximum flight length between landings:** verify against code
- **Handrail height:** 750–900 mm from nosing line

*Important: BP 344 accessible stair requirements supersede NBCP minimums for accessible routes.*

---

### Parking Requirements (NBCP Annex to Rule VIII)
Parking is a major exam topic. General principles:
- Requirements vary by occupancy type and floor area
- One parking space minimum for every X sq.m. GFA depending on use
- Minimum parking stall dimensions: 2.5m × 5.0m (standard); 3.0m × 6.0m (large)
- Accessible parking: minimum 3.7m wide stall
- Percentage of accessible stalls required per total count
*Verify exact ratios per occupancy from the current Annex to Rule VIII.*

## RA 9514 — Fire Code of the Philippines (2008)
**Full title:** An Act Establishing the Comprehensive Fire Code of the Philippines
**Administered by:** Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) under DILG
**Companion document:** IRR of RA 9514

### Key Provisions:

**Occupancy Classifications:**
- Group A: Residential (single/multi-family)
- Group B: Residential Hotels
- Group C: Education and Recreation
- Group D: Institutional (hospitals, penal)
- Group E: Business and Mercantile
- Group F: Industrial
- Group G: Storage and Hazardous
- Group H: Assembly (theaters, stadiums, churches)
- Group I: Mixed occupancy
- Group J: Miscellaneous (structures not otherwise classified)

**Fire Resistance Ratings (hours):**
- Depend on construction type and occupancy
- Common: exterior bearing walls, interior bearing walls, columns, beams, floor/ceiling assemblies

**Means of Egress:**
- **Travel distance:** Maximum distance from any point in a floor to the nearest exit
  - Generally 45m–60m depending on occupancy (verify with current IRR)
  - Sprinklered buildings get increased travel distance allowances
- **Exit width:** Minimum 900mm for a single exit (varies)
- **Occupant load factor:** Number of persons per floor area unit by occupancy
  - Office: 1 person per 9.3 sq.m. net
  - Assembly (chairs fixed): 1 per 0.65 sq.m.
  - Mercantile: 1 per 2.8 sq.m. ground floor / 1 per 5.6 sq.m. upper floors
  - *Verify all values against current IRR*
- **Exit discharge:** Path from exit to public way

**Sprinkler Systems:**
- Required for buildings of certain height/occupancy thresholds
- Standard head activation temperature: 74°C (165°F)
- Intermediate: 100°C
- High: 141°C
- Types: wet pipe (most common), dry pipe, pre-action, deluge

**Fire Exits (RA 9514 and IRR):**
- Must swing in direction of egress for occupant loads >50
- Fire doors: must be self-closing
- Emergency lighting: required at exits, corridors

## BP 344 — Accessibility Law (1983)
**Full title:** An Act to Enhance the Mobility of Disabled Persons
**Revised IRR:** 2024 Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations (most current)

### Critical Accessible Design Standards:

**Ramps:**
- Maximum slope: 1:12 (8.33%) for new construction
- Minimum clear width: 1,200mm (1.2m)
- Maximum rise per run: 750mm
- Landing required at top, bottom, and every 9m of run
- Handrails: both sides, 700–900mm height, 300mm extension at top and bottom

**Accessible Parking:**
- Minimum stall width: 3,700mm (3.7m)
- International symbol of accessibility required
- Location: nearest to building entrance

**Doorways:**
- Minimum clear opening: 900mm
- Lever-type hardware required (no knobs)
- Vision panel for doors in accessible routes

**Elevators (Wheelchair Accessible):**
- Minimum cab dimensions: 1,100mm (width) × 1,400mm (depth) — verify against current 2024 IRR
- Door clear opening: minimum 800mm
- Control panel reach height: max 1,200mm
- Floor indicators with audible signal

**Accessible Toilet/Comfort Room:**
- Minimum clear floor area: 1,700mm × 1,700mm (or equivalent turning radius)
- Grab bars: 33–36 inches (84–91cm) from floor
- Lavatory: knee clearance below, max 34" (86cm) height
- Mirror bottom: max 900mm from floor
- Accessible signage with raised/braille characters

**Public Areas:**
- Curb cuts at pedestrian crossings
- Tactile warning strips at hazardous areas
- Signage: international symbol of accessibility (ISA)

## NSCP 2015 — National Structural Code of the Philippines
**7th Edition, 2015**
**Published by:** Association of Structural Engineers of the Philippines (ASEP)
**Application:** All structures in the Philippines

### Key Facts for ALE:

**Seismic Design:**
- Philippines is classified as **Seismic Zone 2** (high seismicity) per NSCP 2015
- Design earthquake based on 10% probability of exceedance in 50 years
- Response spectrum analysis required for irregular or tall structures
- Minimum base shear V = CsW (where Cs = seismic response coefficient, W = seismic weight)
- Structural irregularities: horizontal (torsional, re-entrant corner) and vertical (soft story, mass, geometric)

**Load Combinations (LRFD):**
Primary load combinations from NSCP Section 203:
- 1.4D
- 1.2D + 1.6L + 0.5(Lr or S)
- 1.2D + 1.6(Lr or S) + (1.0L or 0.5W)
- 1.2D + 1.0W + 1.0L + 0.5(Lr or S)
- 0.9D + 1.0W
- 1.2D + 1.0E + 1.0L
- 0.9D + 1.0E
*D=dead, L=live, W=wind, E=earthquake, Lr=roof live, S=snow*

**Basic Live Loads (NSCP Table 204.3):**
| Occupancy/Use | Uniform Load (kPa) |
|---|---|
| Residential/Hotel rooms | 1.9 kPa |
| Office floors | 2.4 kPa |
| Classrooms | 1.9 kPa |
| Assembly areas (fixed seats) | 2.9 kPa |
| Assembly areas (movable seats) | 4.8 kPa |
| Retail stores | 4.8 kPa |
| Corridors (above 1st floor) | 3.8 kPa |
| Stairs and exits | 4.8 kPa |
| Parking (passenger cars) | 2.4 kPa |
*Always verify against current NSCP tables*

**Concrete Design (Section 406 — RC):**
- Minimum concrete strength f'c for structural members typically 20.7 MPa (3,000 psi)
- Steel yield strength fy: 275 MPa (Grade 40) or 415 MPa (Grade 60) typical
- Maximum water-cement ratio for durability
- Minimum concrete cover: 40mm for beams/columns; 20mm for slabs (non-exposed)

## PD 957 — Subdivision and Condominium Buyers Protective Decree (1976)
**Also known as:** The Subdivision and Condominium Law
**Administered by:** DHSUD (formerly HLURB)

### Key Provisions:
- Requires developers to obtain License to Sell before marketing lots/units
- Sets minimum road widths (6m for local roads in subdivisions)
- Requires facilities: parks and playgrounds, community facilities
- Open space: minimum percentage of subdivision area as parks
- Condominium corporations required for common areas
- Master Deed required for all condominium projects

---

## BP 220 — Economic and Socialized Housing (1982)
**Full title:** An Act Authorizing the Ministry of Human Settlements to Establish and Promulgate Different Levels of Standards and Technical Requirements for Economic and Socialized Housing Projects

### Minimum Standards (Socialized Housing):
- **Minimum lot area:** 36 sq.m. (for a 2-storey structure)
- **Minimum floor area:** 22 sq.m. (starting unit)
- **Minimum road right-of-way:** varies
- Simplified building standards compared to PD 957
- Intended for Pag-IBIG funded and government-subsidized housing

## PD 1067 — Water Code of the Philippines (1976)
**Key provision for architects:**
- 3-meter easement/setback from the banks of rivers and streams (urban areas)
- 20-meter easement from banks of major rivers
- Coastal setbacks (varies)
*These setback requirements affect site planning directly.*

## UAP Standards of Professional Practice — Summary

### SPP Doc. 201: Pre-Design Services
Includes: economic feasibility studies, project financing, architectural programming, site selection and analysis, site utilization/land-use studies, space/management studies, promotional services.
Compensation: MDPE (Multiple of Direct Personnel Expense) — appropriate because no design is involved.

### SPP Doc. 202: Design Services (The Primary Service)
**Phase 1: Schematic Design (SD)**
- Translates owner's requirements to floor plans, sections, elevations
- Produces: area computations, outline specs, preliminary cost estimate

**Phase 2: Design Development (DD)**
- Further development of approved SD
- Coordination with structural, MEP engineers begins
- Produces: more detailed drawings, refined cost estimate

**Phase 3: Contract Documents (CD)**
- Working drawings (signed and sealed by RLA)
- Technical specifications
- Bill of quantities / cost estimate
- This is the most critical phase for code compliance

**Phase 4: Bidding / Tender**
- Pre-qualification of bidders
- Bid evaluation
- Preparation of contract documents

**Phase 5: Construction**
- Periodic inspection by architect (not daily full-time supervision — that is the Architect-in-Charge of Construction)
- Approval of shop drawings
- Issuance of Certificate of Substantial Completion

**Compensation:** Percentage of Project Construction Cost (PCC)
- Typical residential: ~6–10% of PCC
- Typical commercial/institutional: ~4–8% of PCC
*Verify current UAP fee schedule — published in Official Gazette.*

### SPP Doc. 208: Architectural Interiors
- Covers interior space planning, furniture design/specification, FF&E
- Separate profession but architects may practice this

### SPP Doc. 209: Landscape Architecture
- Covered under RA 8549 (Landscape Architecture Act)
- Separate license required; architect may coordinate but not sign landscape documents
