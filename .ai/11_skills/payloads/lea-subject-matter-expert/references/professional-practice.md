# Professional Practice Reference — RA 9266, UAP, SPP

---

## RA 9266: The Architecture Act of 2004

**Signed:** March 17, 2004 by President Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo
**Full title:** An Act Providing for a More Responsive and Comprehensive Regulation for the Registration, Licensing and Practice of Architecture
**Repeals:** RA 545 (original architecture law, 1950)
**IRR effective:** December 1, 2004

---

### Article I: Key Definitions

**Architecture:** The art, science, or profession of planning, designing and constructing buildings in their totality taking into account their environment, in accordance with the principles of utility, strength, and beauty.

**Architect:** A person professionally and academically qualified, registered and licensed under RA 9266, who has been entrusted by the public with the planning, design, and construction supervision of buildings.

**General Practice of Architecture:** The act of planning and architectural designing, structural conceptualization, specifying, supervising and giving general administration and responsible direction to the erection, enlargement or alterations of buildings and building environments and architectural design in engineering structures.

**Scope of Practice:** Includes site, physical, and planning services; design, construction, enlargement, conservation, renovation, remodeling, restoration, or alteration of buildings.

**Structural Conceptualization:** The process of envisioning, selecting, and designing the type, disposition, arrangement, and proportioning of structural elements while considering safety, cost efficiency, functionality, and aesthetic considerations.

**Architect-of-Record:** The architect responsible for the total design and civil liability.

**Architect-in-Charge of Construction:** The architect responsible for construction supervision and liability.

**Consulting Architect:** An architect with recognized expertise or specialization in a branch of architecture.

**Architectural Firm:** A sole proprietorship, partnership, or corporation registered with DTI and/or SEC, and with the Board of Architecture and PRC.

**Authorship:** Author or authors of a set of architectural plans or specifications who are in charge of their preparation.

**IAPOA:** Integrated and Accredited Professional Organization of Architects = UAP (United Architects of the Philippines).

**CPD:** Continuing Professional Development — sustaining and progressive learning process that maintains, enhances, or increases the knowledge and ability of architects.

### Article II: Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture (PRBoA)

**Composition:** Chairman + 2 Members (3 total)
**Appointed by:** The President of the Philippines upon recommendation from PRC from nominees submitted by UAP

**Qualifications of Board Members:**
- Filipino citizen
- Registered architect for at least 10 years
- Not faculty of any school or review institution for 5 years prior to nomination
- Never convicted of crime involving moral turpitude
- Not an elective officer of UAP or other architect professional organizations

**Terms:**
- Chairman: 3 years
- Member 1: 2 years
- Member 2: 1 year
- Each may be reappointed for one full term only

**Powers and Functions (Section 7):**
a. Prescribe and adopt the IRR
b. Supervise registration, licensure, and practice
c. Administer oaths
d. Issue, suspend, revoke, or reinstate Certificates of Registration
e. Adopt official seal
f. Monitor conditions affecting practice
g. Prescribe and adopt Code of Ethical Conduct and SPP
h. Hear and decide administrative cases
i. Prescribe CPD guidelines
j. Prepare, adopt, issue, or amend exam syllabi
k. Approve, issue, limit, or cancel temporary/special permits for foreign nationals
l. Coordinate with CHED on architecture education compliance
m. Adopt full computerization program for exam

### Article III: Examination, Registration, and Licensure

**Qualifications for Examination (Section 13):**
a. Filipino citizen (or foreign national meeting reciprocity requirements)
b. Good moral character
c. BS Architecture degree from CHED-recognized institution
d. Minimum **2 years of Diversified Architectural Experience (DAE)** certified by a Registered Licensed Architect
   - Exception: Master's Degree in Architecture credits **1 year** of DAE
e. No conviction of crime involving moral turpitude

**Exam Subjects (Section 14):**
1. History and Theory of Architecture; Principles of Planning and Architectural Practice
2. Structural Design, Building Materials, Architectural Specifications, and Methods of Construction; Utilities
3. Urban Design and Architectural Interiors
4. Architectural Design and Site Planning

**Note on current syllabi:** The PRBoA issued updated syllabi that reorganized these into 3 areas (A, B, C) as detailed in the LEA syllabus reference. The Board may revise subjects for technological advancements.

**Passing Standard (Section 15):**
- Weighted average of **70%** or above
- **No subject below 50%**

**Process after passing:**
1. PRC announces results within 30 days
2. Candidates receive rating by mail
3. Successful candidates take **oath** before practicing
4. Certificate of Registration issued upon payment of fees
5. Professional ID Card (PIC) issued — valid until withdrawn/suspended/revoked

**Retaking the Exam:**
- Candidates who passed some subjects but failed others can apply for re-examination
- Specific rules about carry-over of passed subjects — verify current PRC rules

---

### Article IV: Reciprocity (Foreign Architects)

- Foreigners may take the LEA only if their country admits Filipino architects to the same profession without restriction or on terms of equality
- Upon issuance of temporary/special permit, the foreign national may become a UAP member

---

### Article V: Prohibitions and Penalties

**Grounds for suspension/revocation of Certificate of Registration:**
- Use of fraud or deceit in obtaining certificate
- Gross negligence or incompetence
- Unprofessional or dishonorable conduct
- Signing/affixing name/seal on plans not prepared by or under architect's immediate supervision
- **The "signing-sealing" prohibition is one of the most heavily tested provisions**

**Penalties for illegal practice:**
- Fine and/or imprisonment (specific amounts/terms in the law)
- Cannot practice without PRC registration

**Civil Liability (Art. 1723, Civil Code):**
- The architect who prepared the plans is liable for damages if a building collapses within **15 years** due to defect in plans/specifications or ground defects
- The architect who supervised construction is **solidarily liable** with the contractor for collapse due to defective construction or inferior materials

## UAP: United Architects of the Philippines

**Founded:** May 19, 1975 (through merger of LPA, APGA, and PIA)
**Status:** Integrated and Accredited Professional Organization of Architects (IAPOA)
**Headquarters:** Quezon City
**Membership:** Mandatory for all registered Filipino architects (RA 9266)
**Student arm:** UAPSA (established 1989) — 94 member schools, 16,000+ members
**Graduate arm:** UAPGA — 48 chapters, ~4,000 members

**UAP Responsibilities under RA 9266:**
a. Nominate candidates to vacant Board of Architecture positions
b. Prepare and administer CPD programs for architects
c. Endorse temporary/special permits for foreign nationals
d. Recommend compliance with liability insurance
e. Monitor and file complaints for RA 9266 violations

**UAP Vision:** The UAP as the Integrated and Accredited Professional Organization of Architects, vanguard of the Architecture profession, catalyst of dynamism and unity among Filipino Architects.

## Code of Ethical Conduct (SPP Doc. 200)

Key ethical obligations of the architect:

**To the Public:**
- Maintain highest standards of health, safety, and welfare
- Do not misrepresent qualifications
- Report illegal practice

**To the Client:**
- Faithfully represent client's interests
- Maintain confidentiality
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Do not accept compensation from contractors/suppliers without client knowledge

**To the Profession:**
- Uphold dignity of the profession
- Do not compete unfairly
- Do not sign/seal work not prepared under your supervision
- Do not offer services below minimum fees in a way that is detrimental to the profession

**To Fellow Architects:**
- Do not assume a commission being negotiated by another architect without proper notification
- Do not malign or make false statements about another architect

## Mandatory Contract Documents (Contract Package)

The full set of contract documents an architect must prepare and seal:

1. **Architectural Plans:**
   - Site Development Plan
   - Floor Plans (all floors)
   - Roof Plan
   - All Elevations (minimum 4 sides)
   - Building Sections (minimum 2)
   - Reflected Ceiling Plans
   - Finish and Hardware Schedule
   - Door and Window Schedule
   - Detailed Architectural Plans (stairs, toilet, kitchen, facade details)

2. **Technical Specifications:**
   - Division 1: General Requirements
   - Divisions 2–16: Technical trades specifications
   - Master format sections following CSI structure

3. **Cost Estimates / Bill of Quantities**

4. **Structural Plans** — prepared and sealed by the Registered Licensed Civil/Structural Engineer (RLCE), coordinated with architect

5. **MEP Plans** — prepared and sealed by respective professionals (mechanical, electrical, sanitary/plumbing)

**Key signing and sealing rule:** The architect signs and seals only the architectural documents. The structural engineer signs the structural documents. Each professional is responsible for their own discipline.

## Architect's Liability Framework

| Scenario | Liable Party | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Defective plans causing collapse | Architect who drew plans | 15 years from completion |
| Defective construction causing collapse | Architect who supervised + contractor (solidarily) | 15 years from completion |
| Signing plans not prepared under supervision | Architect (disciplinary action by PRBoA) | Upon complaint |
| Practicing without license | Unlicensed person | Criminal penalty |

## PRBoA vs. PRC Distinctions

| | PRBoA | PRC |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Collegial body | Government agency |
| Function | Regulates architecture profession | Administers all licensure exams |
| Decisions on | Exam syllabi, SPP, ethics, discipline | Budget, HR, overall exam administration |
| Appeals | From PRBoA → PRC | From PRC → Regular courts |
| UAP relationship | Receives nominations from UAP | Approves CPD providers |
