# SlateCamp Interview Guide Templates

## Master Interview Script — First-Taker (45 min)

### Setup (5 min)
"Thanks for joining me today. My name is [name]. I'm working on making SlateCamp better for people preparing for the LEA. I want to be clear: there are no right or wrong answers here — I'm trying to understand your experience, not test you. If anything feels confusing or strange, that's actually really useful information for me. Do you mind if I record this? The recording is just for my notes and won't be shared."

**[Start recording. Set timer for 45 min.]**

### Warm-Up (5–8 min)
1. "Tell me a little about yourself — where you went to school, what you're up to now."
2. "When did you decide to take the LEA? What triggered that decision?"
3. "How long have you been preparing?"

### Core Discovery (20–25 min)

**Study behavior baseline:**
4. "Walk me through a typical day of studying. Start from the moment you decide to study."
5. "What materials are you using?" [Let them list. Don't suggest anything.]
6. "How do you know when you've studied enough for the day?"
7. "Have you ever had a session where you finished and thought 'that was really useful'? Tell me about that."
8. "Have you had sessions where it felt like a waste of time? What was different?"

**Mental model of readiness:**
9. "If I asked you right now, 'Are you ready for the LEA?' — what would go through your mind?"
10. "How would you know you're ready? What would that feel like or look like?"
11. "Are there parts of the exam content you're confident about? Which parts worry you the most?"

**SlateCamp-specific (only if they've used it):**
12. "How did you first hear about SlateCamp?"
13. "Tell me about the first time you used it. What did you do?"
14. "What happened when you saw your Readiness Score for the first time?"
15. "What did you do with that information?"

### Emotional Depth Probe (5 min)
16. "What would it mean to you to pass the LEA? Not practically — emotionally."
17. "What would happen if you didn't pass? Who would know?"
18. "Has anyone around you — family, batchmates — influenced how you feel about the exam?"

### Close (5 min)
19. "If you could change one thing about how you're preparing right now, what would it be?"
20. "Is there anything about your study experience that you wish I had asked about?"

"Thank you so much. This was genuinely helpful. Is it okay if I follow up by message if I have one or two more questions later?"

## Master Interview Script — Retaker (45 min)

⚠️ **Retaker sensitivity protocol:** Never open with "So you failed last time." Open with curiosity about their journey. Allow them to bring up the previous attempt themselves. If they don't, gently probe around exam number, not failure.

### Setup (5 min)
Same as First-Taker. Add: "I especially want to understand the experience of people who've been through the exam preparation process — the full arc, not just where you are today. Your perspective is really valuable."

### Warm-Up (5–8 min)
1. "Tell me about your journey with the LEA — take me from the beginning."
2. "How many times have you gone through the review process?"
3. [If they mention previous attempt:] "Tell me about that experience."

### Core Discovery (20–25 min)

**What changed:**
4. "This time around, what are you doing differently in how you prepare?"
5. "What did you learn from last time about how you study?"
6. "Is there anything you've discovered about yourself as a learner through this process?"

**Trust and credibility:**
7. "When you got your Readiness Score on SlateCamp, did it feel accurate? Why or why not?"
8. "Is there any study tool or signal you've tried that you actually trusted? What made you trust it?"
9. "Have you ever felt like a study system was lying to you — making you feel more ready than you were?"

**The emotional arc:**
10. "How did you feel in the period right after [previous attempt]? How do you feel now?"
11. "Is there a moment when you decided to try again? What was that like?"
12. "What does passing mean to you now, after going through this?"

### Close (5 min)
13. "What does SlateCamp give you that nothing else has?"
14. "What would make you absolutely certain you'll pass this time?"

## Master Interview Script — Working Professional Returner (30 min)

**Note:** This persona is time-scarce. Keep it tight. No tangents.

### Setup (3 min)
Shorter: "I know your time is valuable. This will be exactly 30 minutes. I want to understand how someone with a full work schedule prepares for the LEA."

### Core (20 min)
1. "How long has it been since you graduated?"
2. "What finally made you decide to go for the license now?"
3. "Walk me through one actual study session from this week."
4. "What's the hardest part about preparing while working?"
5. "When do you study? Morning, night, lunch break?"
6. "How much time can you realistically give per day?"
7. "How do you know if a study tool is worth your time?"
8. [SlateCamp] "When you saw your Readiness Score, how did it feel given how long it's been?"
9. "Does the Camp Trail pacing work for your schedule? What would you change?"
10. "What would make you feel like SlateCamp was worth paying for?"

### Close (5 min)
11. "If you could only do one thing to prepare in 30 minutes a day, what would it be?"
12. "What's your biggest fear between now and exam day?"

## Feature-Specific Mini Probes (15–20 min)

Use these when you need focused insight on a specific feature, not a full interview.

### Probe: Score Reveal UX
1. "Tell me exactly what you did the moment you saw your score."
2. "What did you think the score meant?"
3. "What did you want to do next?"
4. "Did you tell anyone your score? Who? What did they say?"
5. "If the score was lower than you hoped, how did you feel about the platform?"

### Probe: Taxonomy Heatmap
1. "Can you show me how you use the heatmap?"
2. "If I asked you to find your weakest area, what would you do?"
3. "What does a 'red' node mean to you in terms of what you need to do?"
4. "Have you ever been surprised by what the heatmap showed?"
5. "If you could add one thing to the heatmap, what would it be?"

### Probe: Camp Trail
1. "Show me what happens when you open the Camp Trail."
2. "What does a typical Trail day look like?"
3. "Have you ever skipped a Trail day? Tell me about that."
4. "Does the Trail feel like it's designed for your situation specifically?"
5. "What's the difference between following the Trail and just studying on your own?"

### Probe: Paywall (Free → Pro decision)
1. "Tell me about the moment you hit the paywall."
2. "What went through your mind?"
3. "What would have made you upgrade immediately, without hesitation?"
4. "What made you hesitate?" [if they hesitated]
5. "Who else was involved in the decision to pay?" [family, batchmates]

### Probe: Campfire / Community
1. "Do you talk to other people who are preparing for the LEA?"
2. "Have you ever used the SlateCamp community features?"
3. "What kind of community support would actually help you prepare?"
4. "Have you talked to someone who passed? What did you learn?"
