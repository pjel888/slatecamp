# PD 1096 — 2004 Revised IRR Structure

Source: 2004 Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations of PD 1096, published by DPWH on April 1, 8, and 15, 2005 in The Manila Standard Today. With annotations by PRBoA in 2008.

**This is the document that governs the LEA.** The original 1977 PD 1096 chapter numbers are different from the IRR rule numbers. Always cite the IRR, not the original decree.

## Key Warnings

### Rule XVIII is Glass and Glazing, NOT Elevators
- Section 1801: General Requirements (glass)
- Section 1802: Area Limitation (glass)
- Section 1803: Glazing
- Section 1804: Louvered Windows
- Section 1805: Impact

### Rule XIX is Use of Computers, NOT Parking
- Section 1901: General Rule
- Section 1902: Program Documentation
- Section 1903: Submission of Computer-Generated Computations

### Rule VIII ends at Section 811, NOT 815
- Rule VIII covers Light and Ventilation only
- Sections 801 through 811
- There is no Section 815 in Rule VIII

## Sections Frequently Referenced in LEA Content

### Rule VII — The Primary Rule for Development Controls

Rule VII is the most heavily tested rule. It covers:
- Section 701: Occupancy classifications
- Section 702-706: Requirements by occupancy group
- Section 707: ⚠️ Commonly cited for setbacks — VERIFY exact content
- Section 708: ⚠️ VERIFY
- Section 709: Guidelines on Building Bulk, Development Control
  - Section 709.1: Guidelines on Building Bulk, Development Control, Buildings & Other Accessory Structures within Cemeteries and Memorial Park

### Rule VIII — Light and Ventilation

- Section 801: General Requirements
- Section 806: ⚠️ Commonly cited for natural light and ventilation minimums — VERIFY
- Section 808: ⚠️ Commonly cited for sanitation — but sanitation may be Rule IX
- Section 811: End of Rule VIII
  - Section 811.1: Guidelines on Easements, View Corridors/Sight Lines, Streets/Road Right-of-Way (RROW), Sidewalks, Arcades, Basements, Lots, and Public Buildings/Structures

### Rule IX — Sanitation
- Sections 901-904
- This is where fixture counts may actually live (not Rule VIII Section 808)

## Original PD 1096 Chapter vs. 2004 IRR Rule Mapping

The original PD 1096 decree has chapters numbered 1-20+. The 2004 IRR has Rules I-XX. They are NOT identical in content alignment:

| Original PD 1096 Chapter | IRR Rule | Match? |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter 1: General Provisions | Rule I | ✅ Matches |
| Chapter 2: Administration | Rule II | ✅ Matches |
| Chapter 3: Permits | Rule III | ✅ Matches |
| Chapter 7: Occupancy | Rule VII | ✅ Matches |
| Chapter 8: Light and Ventilation | Rule VIII | ✅ Matches |
| **Chapter 18: Elevators and Mechanical** | **Rule XVIII: Glass and Glazing** | ❌ DOES NOT MATCH |
| — | Rule XIX: Use of Computers | No original chapter equivalent |

This mismatch is why "Rule XVIII Section 1802" gets wrongly cited for elevators — people assume the IRR Rule numbers match the original PD chapter numbers. They do for Rules I-VIII but diverge after that.
