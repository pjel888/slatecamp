# High-Risk Citations — Verified vs. Unverified

This file tracks provisions that are frequently cited in LEA prep content with WRONG section numbers. Every entry documents the common wrong citation and its correction status.

**Legend:**
- ✅ VERIFIED — confirmed against primary source document
- ⚠️ UNVERIFIED — provision exists but exact section number not confirmed against primary source
- ❌ CONFIRMED WRONG — the commonly cited section is demonstrably incorrect

---

## PD 1096 / 2004 Revised IRR

### Elevator Requirement (5+ Storeys)

| Status | ❌ CONFIRMED WRONG (common citations) / ⚠️ UNVERIFIED (correct citation) |
|---|---|
| **Common wrong citation 1** | "Rule VIII, Section 815" — WRONG. Rule VIII (Light and Ventilation) ends at Section 811. Section 815 does not exist in Rule VIII. |
| **Common wrong citation 2** | "Rule XVIII, Section 1802" — WRONG. Rule XVIII in the 2004 IRR is "Glass and Glazing." Section 1802 is "Area Limitation" for glass. |
| **What is known** | PD 1096 Chapter 18 (original decree, not IRR) addresses elevators. The original Section 1803 states the requirement. In the 2004 Revised IRR, the mechanical regulations were reorganized. |
| **Safe citation** | "Under PD 1096, buildings of five storeys or more require at least one passenger elevator." Do NOT cite a specific section until verified against the 2004 IRR primary document. |
| **Action** | Obtain the full 2004 Revised IRR PDF and locate the exact elevator provision section number. |

### Setbacks (Rule VII)

| Status | ⚠️ UNVERIFIED (commonly cited as Section 707) |
|---|---|
| **Common citation** | "Rule VII, Section 707" |
| **What is known** | Rule VII covers "Classification and General Requirements of All Buildings by Use or Occupancy" (Sections 701-709). Section 707 is commonly cited for setbacks. The provision exists in Rule VII but the exact section needs primary source verification. |
| **Safe citation** | "Setback requirements under Rule VII of the 2004 Revised IRR" |

| Status | ⚠️ UNVERIFIED (commonly cited as Section 806) |

### Sanitation Fixtures

| Status | ⚠️ UNVERIFIED (commonly cited as Rule VIII Section 808, but may be Rule IX) |

### Parking Requirements

| Status | ❌ CONFIRMED WRONG (commonly cited as "Rule XIX") |

## BP 344 / 2024 Revised IRR

### All Dimensional Values

| Status | ✅ VERIFIED against Official Gazette Vol. 120 No. 28, July 8, 2024 |
|---|---|
| **Source** | Official Gazette of the Republic of the Philippines, Vol. 120, No. 28, July 8, 2024 (effective August 8, 2024) |
| **Key values verified** | Cubicle 1,800 x 1,800 mm, corridor 1,500 mm, approach space 1,800 x 1,800 mm, threshold 12 mm, grab bar diameter 30-40 mm |
| **Citation format** | "Section C.6.3.a of the 2024 Revised IRR of BP 344" — the section references (C.1.4, C.2.1, etc.) are from the IRR structure, not from BP 344 itself. |

### Penalty Provisions

| Status | ✅ VERIFIED |
|---|---|
| **Common wrong citation** | "BP 344 prescribes a fine of ₱50,000" |
| **Correct citation** | Penalties come from **RA 9442** (2007), not BP 344. BP 344 is the technical standard. RA 9442 amends RA 7277 and adds criminal liability. |
| **Penalty values** | First violation: ₱50,000-₱100,000 fine or 6 months-2 years imprisonment. Subsequent: ₱100,000-₱200,000 or 2-6 years. |
