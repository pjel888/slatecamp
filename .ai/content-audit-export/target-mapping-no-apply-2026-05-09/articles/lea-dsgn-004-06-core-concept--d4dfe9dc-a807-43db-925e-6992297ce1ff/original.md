## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal

Classify libraries and archives as Group C occupancies, calculate floor area using DepEd and PRC 2007 standards, determine occupant load per RA 9514, and apply archive environmental and accessibility requirements.

## Core Concept

Libraries and archives are classified as _Group C (Education and Recreation)_ per **PD 1096**, along with museums, schools, and churches. This classification applies to all library types regardless of public or private ownership: university research libraries, school libraries, municipal public libraries, corporate archives, and national heritage collections are all Group C.

The space program for libraries differs significantly from museums:
- **School Library**: 1.5 sqm per student served (minimum 50% of enrollment capacity) per **DepEd Standards**
- **Public Library**: Area varies by community size; Philippines follows **PRC Standards for Libraries 2007** which recommends proportional to population served
- **Research/Archive Library**: Collection-driven; stack density 150–250 linear shelf meters per 100 sqm (very high density)

**Occupant Load Calculation** uses RA 9514 Table 3.1-2:
- Reading/study areas: 1 person per 4.6 sqm (education rate, accounting for seated work stations and low-movement behavior)
- Stacks (non-public storage): **not counted in occupancy load** (staff-access only, or open-shelf low-traffic areas count as 1 per 30 sqm minimum)
- Computer labs: 1 person per 4.6 sqm (education rate, seated activity)

This is a critical distinction from museums: library occupant load is **LOWER** (4.6 sqm/person) than galleries (2.8 sqm/person) because library patrons are stationary—seated reading, research, computer use—whereas museum visitors are mobile and clustering at displays.

**Archive/Special Collections:** Climate-controlled restricted-access areas (18–21°C, 45–55% RH per national heritage standards, similar to museums) where the collection is preserved. These are mechanical support spaces and do not contribute to occupancy load.

## Key Provisions

### Occupancy Classification (Group C per PD 1096 Section 701)

Libraries are Group C regardless of type. Secondary spaces classified separately:
- Administrative offices: Group E (business)
- IT server room: Mechanical support (not counted)
- Archive storage: Mechanical support (not counted)

Fire-rated separation required between Group C and Group E per PD 1096 Rule VIII (fire-resistance ratings for walls).

### Space Program Standards (DepEd + PRC 2007)

#### School Library (Per DepEd)

| School Size | Min Floor Area (sqm) | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| 500 students | 750 sqm | 1.5 sqm × 500 students (min 50% enrollment) |
| 1,000 students | 1,500 sqm | 1.5 sqm × 1,000 students |
| 2,000 students | 3,000 sqm | 1.5 sqm × 2,000 students |

_Note: Minimum 50% of total enrollment; adjust if school has multiple campuses._

#### Public Library (Per PRC 2007)

| Community Population | Recommended Floor Area (sqm) | Service Model |
|---|---|---|
| 5,000–10,000 | 300–500 | Barangay/municipal branch |
| 10,000–50,000 | 500–1,500 | Municipal main library |
| 50,000–500,000 | 1,500–5,000 | City library |
| 500,000+ | 5,000–15,000 | Metropolitan library system |

#### Research/Academic Library

Collection-driven; typically 120–200 sqm per 1,000 volumes housed.
- Compact shelving: 150 linear meters per 100 sqm floor
- Standard shelving: 100 linear meters per 100 sqm floor
- Rare books vault: 80 linear meters per 100 sqm (maximum density, controlled access)

### Occupant Load Calculation (RA 9514 Table 3.2.1)

| Space Type | Rate | Example (1,500 sqm library) |
|---|---|---|
| Reading/study areas | 1 per 4.6 sqm | 1,500 ÷ 4.6 = **326 occupants** |
| Computer lab | 1 per 4.6 sqm | 200 sqm lab ÷ 4.6 = **43 occupants** |
| Open stacks (low-traffic) | 1 per 30 sqm min | 500 sqm stacks ÷ 30 = **17 occupants** |
| Closed archive storage | Excluded (non-public) | Not counted |

**Total occupancy for egress design** = sum of public-access areas.

### Accessibility & Circulation (BP 344, RA 9514)

- Accessible door: **900 mm minimum** width
- Study carrel depth: 600 mm min (accessibility standard)
- Aisle between shelves: **1.5 m minimum** (allowing wheelchair passage and two-way traffic)
- Reading room: allow 1 person per 4.6 sqm; seating % = 60% of occupancy load (seated), 40% standing/browsing
- Ramp gradient: **1:20 maximum (2024 Revised IRR)** per BP 344 Section 3.1
- Standard parking stall: **2.5 m × 5.0 m**; Accessible: **3.7 m × 5.0 m (2024 Revised IRR)**

### Environmental Standards (Archive/Special Collections)

Staff-only restricted-access collections:
- Temperature: 18–21°C ± 2°C per day (per national heritage standards)
- Humidity: 45–55% ± 5% RH
- Lighting: 30–50 lux, 12-hour max daily exposure, 100% UV filtration (0 μW/lumen)
- Air quality: Filtration to remove particulates, acid gases (ISO Class 6 or better per archival standards)

## Philippine Practice Context

A typical **public library serving a municipality of 25,000 residents** (PRC 2007):
- Recommended floor area: **750–1,000 sqm**
- Layout: 600 sqm public services (reading, stacks, computer lab) + 200 sqm administration + 100 sqm mechanical/storage
- Occupant load: 600 ÷ 4.6 = **130 occupants maximum**
- Seating: 60% × 130 = **78 reading seats** (carrels, tables)
- Stacks: 300 sqm, 1.5 m aisles = ~200 linear shelf meters (capacity 40,000–50,000 volumes at 200 vols/linear meter)
- Emergency exits: 2 exits required (130 > 50 persons); exit width = 130 × 7.6 mm = 988 mm = minimum 1 door at 1.0 m, not adequate → need 2 doors at 0.7 m each or 1 door at 1.2 m (stair egress different)
- Parking: 130 occupants ÷ 3–4 = 30–40 spaces typical for municipal library (depends on transit availability)

A **university library serving 10,000 students** (DepEd + academic standards):
- Recommended floor area: 1.5 sqm × 10,000 = **15,000 sqm** (or reduced by multi-campus adjustment)
- Multi-story building: 4–5 floors × 3,000–3,750 sqm per floor
- Occupant load: 15,000 ÷ 4.6 = **3,261 occupants**
- Seating allocation: 60% × 3,261 = **1,956 seats** (study carrels, reading tables, lounges)
- Collection: 15,000 sqm ÷ 120 sqm per 1,000 volumes = ~125,000 volumes capacity
- Stacks with compact shelving = 200+ linear meters per floor
- Emergency exits: High-rise egress design required; 2 stairwells minimum, travel distance ≤ 60 m (sprinklered)

## How It's Tested

**Occupancy Classification Pattern (Difficulty 0.7):**
"A private research library houses 50,000 historical documents in a university archive. Under PD 1096, what is the occupancy group?"
- Answer: **Group C** (education/research), regardless of restricted access or private ownership

**Space Program Pattern (Difficulty 0.7):**
"A 500-student school requires a library per DepEd standards. What is the minimum floor area?"
- Answer: 1.5 sqm × 500 students = **750 sqm minimum** (assuming 50% enrollment served)

**Occupant Load Analysis Pattern (Difficulty 0.9):**
"A 1,200 sqm public library has 700 sqm reading/study areas, 300 sqm open stacks, and 200 sqm archive storage. Calculate occupancy load and verify exit requirements."
- Reading areas: 700 ÷ 4.6 = **152 occupants**
- Open stacks: 300 ÷ 30 = **10 occupants** (minimum rate for low-traffic)
- Archive: **0** (excluded, non-public)
- **Total: 162 occupants**
- Exit width required: 162 × 7.6 mm = 1,231 mm → need 2 exits at 0.7 m each or 1 door at 1.25 m minimum

**Archive Climate Pattern (Difficulty 0.9):**
"A research library maintains a rare manuscripts vault (18–21°C, 45–55% RH). A HVAC malfunction caused temperature to rise to 24°C for 3 hours. Assess damage risk."
- Temperature exceeded target by 3°C (tolerance ±2°C)
- **Result: DAMAGE RISK** — paper, ink, binding adhesives at risk; collection condition assessment required; potential molecular degradation
