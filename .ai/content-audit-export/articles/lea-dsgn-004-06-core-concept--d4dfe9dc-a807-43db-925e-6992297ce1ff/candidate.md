# Libraries and Archives: Classification, Occupant Load, and Egress

## Source Scope

This repair is limited to the local PD 1096 RIRR occupancy tables, RA 9514 2019 Revised IRR, and the local Fire Code source family. It does not assert unsourced archive humidity ranges, shelving loads, reading-room ratios, or preservation standards.

## LEA Anchor

For LEA use, first identify the library or archive space in the question. A public reading room, seminar room, auditorium, stack area, archive workroom, and school library can be controlled by different provisions. Answer from the specific occupancy and use condition given.

## Occupancy Classification

PD 1096 lists libraries, museums, exhibition halls, and art galleries in the checked public-use classification matrix. RA 9514 includes libraries in assembly occupancies when the capacity condition applies. RA 9514 also states that occupancies associated with educational institutions must be handled under the appropriate part of the Fire Code.

## Egress and Public Safety

For means of egress, RA 9514 requires capacity to be sufficient for the occupant load. The general width of a means of egress must be measured by clear width and must not be less than 915 mm unless a specific occupancy division provides another rule. The general rule requires at least two means of egress from a balcony, mezzanine, storey, or portion unless a specific division permits otherwise. Exit access must be recognizable and must not require travel toward a high-hazard area unless the path is suitably protected.

## Exam Use

Do not answer a library question from a generic memory rule. Use PD 1096 for the classification context, then use RA 9514 for occupant load, egress count, clear width, exit access, alarms, and any assembly or educational use named in the item.
