# Architectural Interiors under SPP 203

## Source Scope

This local repair is limited to SPP Document 203 source material checked from the raw UAP PDF bundle and its local OCR/refined Markdown conversion. It does not carry forward fee percentages, statute subsection labels, or service-section numbers that were not verified in the checked source lines.

## LEA Anchor

SPP 203 treats Architectural Interiors as one of the specialized architectural services. The same checked list also includes services such as Acoustic Design, Architectural Lighting Layout and Design, Site Development Planning, Site and Physical Planning Services, Building Systems Design, Facilities Maintenance Support, Building Testing and Commissioning, Forensic Architecture, Contract Documentation and Review, Post-Design Services, and Management of Architectural Practices.

## Manner of Providing Architectural Interiors Services

For Architectural Interiors, the checked SPP 203 source identifies two service positions:

1. The architect may work in a dual capacity as Architect-of-Record and as Consulting Architect for Architectural Interiors services.
2. The architect may work as Consulting Architect for Architectural Interiors services only.

## Exam Use

When a question asks about Architectural Interiors in SPP 203, first classify it as a specialized architectural service. Then distinguish whether the prompt describes dual capacity or consulting-architect-only service. Avoid importing compensation figures or separate statute citations unless the question provides its own verified source.

## Real World Scenario

A condominium client asks one RLA to serve as Architect-of-Record for the shell project and also advise on lobby interiors, while a second tenant asks the same RLA only to review interior layouts as a consulting specialist. Both arrangements can be source-safe under the checked SPP 203 lines: the first is dual capacity as Architect-of-Record and Consulting Architect for Architectural Interiors, while the second is consulting-architect-only service. The exam trap is to treat Architectural Interiors as an SPP 202 phase or as site development work instead of as a specialized architectural service.
