# Warehouses and Cold Storage Facilities: Source-Safe Code Framing

## Source Scope

This local-first revision keeps only claims supported by the checked PD 1096 RIRR, RA 9514 Revised IRR 2019, and Fire Code source-family excerpts. Prior operational standards for clear heights, dock dimensions, insulation thickness, temperature zones, or logistics planning are not retained as article claims because they were outside the selected raw-source gate.

## LEA Anchor

A warehouse or cold storage answer starts with what is stored and how hazardous it is. The source-safe route is classification before sizing shortcuts.

## Occupancy Classification

PD 1096 lists cold storage and storage or sales rooms for incombustible and non-explosive materials under Group F industrial occupancies. It separately places storage and handling of hazardous or highly flammable materials, flammable materials, and warehouses where highly combustible materials are stored under Group G storage and hazardous occupancies.

RA 9514 treats storage occupancy as buildings or structures used primarily for storage or sheltering of goods, merchandise, products, vehicles, or animals. Its list includes warehouses and cold storages. RA 9514 also defines high-piled storage by pile height and floor-area thresholds.

## Fire and Life Safety Checks

For storage occupancies, the checked RA 9514 provisions restrict means of egress to listed exit types, require at least two separate means of egress for each storage building or separately considered section, and allow a narrow exception for small rooms or spaces below the stated area threshold where fewer than ten persons may normally be present. High-hazard storage areas must have an exit within twenty-three meters, or thirty meters where automatic fire suppression protection is provided.

The same checked source requires egress signs, illumination, emergency lighting, and detection/alarm provisions for storage occupancies, with automatic fire suppression for high-hazard occupancy.

## Exam Use

Do not answer a warehouse item from generic logistics practice when the prompt asks for code classification. Identify whether the source facts point to ordinary incombustible storage, cold storage, hazardous storage, high-piled storage, or incidental storage within another occupancy.
