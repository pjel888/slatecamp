## Board Exam Quick Check

For Section 7 questions, identify whether the Board action is **rulemaking**, **licensing**, **discipline/adjudication**, **examination/curriculum oversight**, or **professional standards oversight**. The list has fourteen lettered powers, (a) through (n).

## PRBoA Powers and Functions: Section 7 in Full

Section 7 of the RA 9266 IRR lists the specific powers, functions, and responsibilities of the Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture. This article uses the verified RA 9266 IRR text, not a paraphrased reviewer table.

### The Fourteen Lettered Powers

The Board's powers are lettered (a) through (n):

- **(a)** prescribe and adopt the IRR for carrying out RA 9266.
- **(b)** supervise the registration, licensure, and practice of architects.
- **(c)** administer oaths in connection with RA 9266.
- **(d)** issue, suspend, revoke, or reinstate the Certificate of Registration and Professional Identification Card.
- **(e)** adopt an official Board seal.
- **(f)** monitor conditions affecting architectural practice and adopt measures for high professional, ethical, and technical standards.
- **(g)** prescribe or adopt the Code of Ethical Conduct and Standards of Professional Practice.
- **(h)** hear and decide administrative cases involving violations of RA 9266, the IRR, the Code of Ethical Conduct, and the Standards of Professional Practice, with subpoena powers.
- **(i)** prescribe CPD guidelines in consultation with the integrated and accredited professional organization of architects; the IRR text states that attendance to CPD shall not be a mandatory requirement for PIC renewal.
- **(j)** prepare, adopt, issue, or amend examination syllabi, determine and prepare questions, administer, correct, and release licensure examination results.
- **(k)** approve, issue, limit, or cancel temporary or special permits to practice architecture.
- **(l)** coordinate with CHED to ensure higher educational instruction and architecture offerings comply with prescribed policies, standards, and requirements.
- **(m)** adopt a program for full computerization of the licensure examination.
- **(n)** discharge other duties and functions necessary for the enhancement, upgrading, development, and growth of architecture and architecture education.

### Commission Review

Immediately after the Section 7 list, the IRR states that Board policies, resolutions, rules, and regulations are subject to review and approval by the Commission. Board decisions, resolutions, or orders in administrative cases are subject to review only if appealed.

### Why the Powers Matter

The Board is not only an exam body. It touches the full regulatory cycle: rules, registration, professional standards, discipline, temporary permits, examinations, and education alignment. For LEA questions, the safest move is to match the fact pattern to the exact lettered power instead of relying on a generic statement like "the Board regulates architects."

### Exam Takeaway

Count fourteen powers from (a) to (n). Remember the main anchors: (b) registration/licensure/practice supervision, (d) COR/PIC action, (h) administrative cases and subpoena powers, (j) exams, (k) temporary/special permits, and (l) CHED coordination.
