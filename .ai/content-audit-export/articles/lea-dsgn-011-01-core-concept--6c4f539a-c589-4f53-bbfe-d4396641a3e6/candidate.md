# Floor Plans: Source-Safe Code Framing

## Source Scope

This local-first revision removes unsupported drafting conventions and room-dimension assumptions. It keeps floor-plan content grounded in checked PD 1096 plan-document requirements, BP 344 accessibility requirements, and fire-safety document requirements.

## LEA Anchor

A floor plan is not just a drawing convention. In the checked PD 1096 source, floor plans are part of architectural documents and must be drawn to scale of not less than 1:100 showing gridlines and complete identification of rooms or functional spaces. PD 1096 also requires plans and specific locations of accessibility facilities and fire-safety documents showing evacuation routes, signs, emergency lights, fire exits, and fire-resistive construction details.

## Accessibility on Plans

BP 344 2024 makes accessibility plan review source-checkable: accessible routes must connect accessible elements without impediments; accessible parking, ramps, signs, doors, elevators, and refuge areas have checked requirements. A floor-plan answer is source-safe when it identifies which accessible element must be shown and what checked requirement controls it.

## Exam Use

Do not answer floor-plan questions from generic graphic habits when the item asks for code compliance. Anchor the answer to the required plan content, accessible route, accessible facility, or egress document named by the source.
