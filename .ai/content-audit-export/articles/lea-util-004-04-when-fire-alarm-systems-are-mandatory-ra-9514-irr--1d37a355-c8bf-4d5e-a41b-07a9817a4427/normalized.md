# Fire Detection and Alarm Systems

## Board Exam Quick Check
For RA 9514 fire alarm questions, identify the occupancy first, then match it to the trigger condition. Section 10.2.6.6 explains how required systems initiate alarms, notify occupants and emergency forces, and handle maintenance outages. Divisions 8 to 20 decide when a system is required for a specific occupancy.

## Learning Goal
Apply the RA 9514 2019 Revised IRR rules for fire alarm initiation, notification, manual boxes, automatic fire-department notification, fire watch, and selected occupancy triggers.

## Source-Grounded Core Rules

### System standard and maintenance
When fire detection, alarm, and communication systems are required by Divisions 8 to 20, the system must follow NFPA 72, the latest Philippine Electronics Code, or the Philippine Electrical Code. If a required fire alarm system is out of service for more than four hours in a 24-hour period, notify the City/Municipal Fire Marshal within 24 hours and assign a fire watch until the system is restored. Maintenance and testing records must be kept.

### Initiation and manual fire alarm boxes
A complete fire alarm system may be initiated by manual initiation, automatic detection, or operation of an extinguishing system. Manual fire alarm boxes are for fire protective signaling only. Locate boxes in the natural path of escape near exits, visible, unobstructed, and readily accessible. Additional boxes are needed so that horizontal travel on the same floor to a box does not exceed 30 m.

Automatic detection or water-flow detection does not automatically remove the need for manual initiation. The audited source still requires at least one manual fire alarm box when the Fire Marshal requires it, and sprinkler water-flow must initiate the alarm system.

### Occupant and emergency notification
Occupant notification uses audible and visible signals under NFPA 72 or another Fire Marshal-approved method. The general evacuation alarm must operate throughout the entire building. Emergency forces notification is required under the audited source, and automatic fire-department notification is mandatory for:

- high-rise buildings;
- hospitals;
- educational institutions;
- hotels and apartment buildings at least four storeys high;
- highly hazardous occupancies; and
- malls.

### Selected occupancy triggers
| Occupancy | Source-grounded trigger |
|---|---|
| Assembly | Manual fire alarm is required for all assembly occupancies. If occupant load exceeds 300, initiation must be through automatic detection. |
| Hotels and dormitories | Automatic fire detection and alarm is required for 15 or more guests. Fewer than 15 guests require a manual fire alarm. Guest rooms or suites need single-station smoke detectors when no automatic detection and alarm system is installed. |
| Business | Fire alarm is required if the building is two or more storeys above exit discharge, if there are 50 or more occupants above or below exit discharge, or if there are 300 or more total occupants. For existing business occupancies, the checked source uses 100 occupants above or below exit discharge and 1000 total occupants. If the listed thresholds are not met, provide a manual fire alarm. |

## Exam Values To Memorize
- Out of service threshold: more than 4 hours in a 24-hour period.
- Fire Marshal notice: within 24 hours.
- Manual box travel distance: not more than 30 m on the same floor.
- Assembly automatic initiation threshold: more than 300 occupants.
- Hotel/dormitory automatic detection threshold: 15 or more guests.
- Business occupancy triggers: two or more storeys above exit discharge, 50 or more occupants above or below exit discharge, or 300 or more total occupants.

## Common Trap
Do not answer a mandatory-system question by naming a detector technology first. The RA 9514 audit support used here verifies required alarm behavior and occupancy thresholds. Detector technology, device addressing, and detailed equipment selection need separate NFPA 72, PEC, or engineering design support.

## Real World Scenario
A four-storey dormitory operator reports that the automatic fire alarm panel will be disabled for a maintenance repair. Under the RA 9514 IRR source, the decision is not just whether smoke detectors exist. Confirm that the required alarm system follows the referenced alarm standards, that any outage exceeding four hours in a 24-hour period is reported to the City or Municipal Fire Marshal within 24 hours, and that a fire watch is assigned until service is restored. For layout review, check that manual boxes remain visible, unobstructed, near escape paths and exits, and within the 30 m same-floor travel limit where boxes are required.
