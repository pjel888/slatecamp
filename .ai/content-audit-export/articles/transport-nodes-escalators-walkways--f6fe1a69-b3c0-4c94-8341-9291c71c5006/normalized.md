# Transport Nodes, Escalators, and Walkways

## Route hierarchy

In a transport node, an escalator is not an accessible route substitute. BP 344 defines an accessible route as free of steps, drops, stairways, turnstiles, revolving doors, escalators, and impediments. Elevators, ramps, lifts, clear doorways, and clear floor spaces carry the accessible-route obligation.

## Tactile guidance

The 2024 IRR requires tactile paths to key user decisions. Warning tactile blocks at stairs have a depth of 600 mm, are placed 300 mm from the start and end, and extend across the full stair width. Elevator tactile routes prioritize elevators and terminate in front of the Braille-equipped control panel, with warning tactile blocks 300 mm before the control panel. Escalators require warning tactile blocks before the metal landing plate, with a depth of 600 mm across the escalator width.

## Escalator and passenger-conveyance rules

Escalators and passenger conveyances in public-use settings must satisfy the source details. Provide upward and downward escalators together unless opposite-direction simultaneous use is not needed. Minimum tread width is at least S800-type, or 804 mm. Inclination is not more than 30 degrees. Provide at least three horizontal steps at both ends. Install tactile warning strips 600 mm deep and 300 mm before the floor plates at both ends. For fixed-direction escalators, use tactile directional blocks with a voice guide. Provide voice guidance near the entry point. Maximum speed is 30 m per minute. Required visual contrast is 30 percent for relevant parts such as step nosing, comb plate, approach, belt, and handrails.

## Transport-node specifics

Transport infrastructure has stricter operating expectations. Platform screen doors have a clear width of 900 mm, and platform-level elevators must lead to platform screen doors where applicable. Accessible elevators in transport facilities have a minimum car depth of 1400 mm, minimum width of 1500 mm, and door opening of at least 900 mm. When the 30 m maximum elevator-distance target cannot be achieved, provide respite areas. At least two elevators close together on each floor are required for redundancy. Boarding platforms should be leveled with the transit vehicle finished floor as much as possible; if a horizontal or vertical gap remains, provide a portable ramp or automatic gap filler.

Primary source basis: BP 344 2024 IRR refined markdown lines 450-463, 992-1003, 1062-1088, 1580-1613, and 1649.
