# Pre-Design Services under SPP 201

## Source Scope

This local repair is limited to UAP SPP Document 201 checked from the local raw PDF and refined Markdown conversion. It removes unsupported compensation methods and keeps only the checked compensation formula.

## LEA Anchor

SPP 201 explains why the architect may be involved before regular design services begin. The checked source says early architect involvement helps the client through objective project analysis, project parameters, building needs, available resources, and constraints.

## Scope of Pre-Design Services

The checked SPP 201 source lists consultation, pre-feasibility studies, feasibility studies, site selection and analysis, site utilization and land-use studies, architectural research, architectural programming, space planning, space management studies, value management, design brief preparation, and promotional services.

Consultation covers oral or written advice, conferences, evaluations, and appraisals regarding a contemplated project. Pre-feasibility studies use secondary information to aid early decision-making. Feasibility studies provide more detailed analysis, including primary data gathering, to determine viability. Site selection and site utilization studies connect site criteria, context, potentials, and development controls.

Architectural programming is a problem-seeking process that identifies horizontal and vertical requirements and incorporates a space program. Space planning determines adequate size and configuration for use, allocation, and interface of spaces. Value management seeks an unimpaired program at minimum cost. Design brief preparation states the project terms of reference, concept, objectives, and other requirements to bid out architectural services.

## Manner and Compensation

SPP 201 identifies three ways of providing these services: as an individual, through the architect's own staff, or through association, consultation, or networking. The checked compensation method is computed by adding all costs of technical services, described as man-hours multiplied by rate, and multiplying that result by a multiplier for overhead and profit. The checked multiplier range is 1.5 to 2.5.

## Exam Use

When a question asks whether an activity is pre-design, compare the activity to the checked SPP 201 service list. When it asks about compensation, use the checked technical-services-cost formula and 1.5 to 2.5 multiplier. Do not import lump-sum, per-diem, or fee-plus-expense methods unless the item gives a separate verified source.

## Real World Scenario

A client has not yet decided whether a proposed site can support the intended building program. Before regular design services begin, the architect may be asked to compare site potentials, study development controls, clarify the space program, and prepare a design brief for later architectural services. In that situation, treat the work as SPP 201 pre-design work and compute compensation from technical-service costs with the checked 1.5 to 2.5 multiplier, not from the SPP 202 regular-design payment milestones.
