# American Colonial Era

## Planning And Civic Order

The local history sources identify the early American period with modern twentieth-century planning and civic architecture. Daniel Burnham is associated with city plans for Manila and Baguio. This is the exam anchor: American colonial influence is not only a style question; it also concerns government planning, civic institutions, and professionalized architectural practice.

## Architects And Institutions

The Filipino Architecture and Architectural History sources list William Parsons, Juan Arellano, Tomas Mapua, Alejandro Legardo, Antonio Toledo, and Carlos Barredo among early American-period architects. Tomas Mapua is identified as the first registered architect in the country. William Parsons is linked to the Philippine General Hospital. Juan Arellano is linked to the Post Office Building in the checked source family.

## Building Examples

The source families name the Masonic Temple on Escolta as the first concrete building in Escolta, the Philippine Normal School, the University of the Philippines at Padre Faura, the National Museum as the former Legislative Building, the Army and Navy Club, De La Salle College by Tomas Mapua, the Manila Hotel, Philippine General Hospital, and the Post Office Building. Use these as recognition anchors rather than unproven lists of stylistic claims.

## LEA Takeaway

Answer American colonial questions by separating planning figures, registered professionals, and civic buildings. Burnham anchors city planning; Mapua anchors professional registration; Parsons and Arellano anchor institutional public architecture in the checked local source families.
