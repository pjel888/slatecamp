# Adaptive Reuse

## Source-Safe Scope

Batch 136 grounds adaptive reuse in local history-source evidence: Hull House reuse of a former mansion, modern preservation activism after Pennsylvania Station, sensitive modern alterations, neighborhood preservation, renovation and infill, reused materials, warehouse loft conversions, and Lina Bo Bardi reusing a former brick factory for SESC.

The rewrite avoids unsupported claims about exact carbon savings, tax credits, Philippine heritage statutes, or project-specific engineering approval. It frames adaptive reuse as a design and preservation strategy that still requires feasibility checks.

## How to Use the Concept

Use adaptive reuse as a reuse-first test. Study structure, heritage value, neighborhood fit, material recovery, program compatibility, and safety upgrades before deciding whether to retain, alter, or replace a building.

## Real World Scenario

An Iloilo warehouse district needs a public market hall and learning center. A source-safe adaptive-reuse approach starts by testing whether the existing structure, volume, neighborhood memory, and material stock can serve the new program before defaulting to demolition.
