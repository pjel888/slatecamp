# Day Care Centers and Kindergarten Facilities: Occupancy and Egress

## Source Scope

This local repair is limited to PD 1096 occupancy classification, the RA 9514 2019 Revised IRR, and the local Fire Code source family. Earlier DSWD room-area, child-count, furniture-height, toilet-height, and BP 344 statements are not carried forward because those source families were outside this Batch 064 raw-PDF audit.

## LEA Anchor

Separate educational, day-care, and institutional nursery triggers. A kindergarten that is primarily educational does not always answer the same way as a child day care occupancy or a full-time nursery for children below kindergarten age.

## PD 1096 Classification

PD 1096 classifies Group C as buildings used for school or day-care purposes involving assemblage for instruction, education, or recreation, unless they fall under another listed group. It also lists pre-schools, elementary schools, and high schools in the checked A-2 row when they do not exceed the stated classroom threshold, and it separately treats nurseries for full-time care of children below kindergarten age under institutional use when the stated conditions are met.

## RA 9514 Day Care Rules

RA 9514 classifies day care occupancies as buildings or portions where four or more clients receive care, maintenance, and supervision by persons other than relatives or legal guardians for less than 24 hours per day. The day care section applies to more than 12 clients, keeps day care centers at or above the level of exit discharge, sets occupant load at one person per 3.3 m2 or maximum probable population, and includes exit, rescue-window, detection, corridor, and ramp provisions.

## Exam Use

Use the source wording: Group C for school or day-care purposes under PD 1096, educational or day-care occupancy under RA 9514 depending on the facts, and stricter day-care requirements where the age group or client count triggers them. Do not answer from unverified child-space ratios in this batch.
