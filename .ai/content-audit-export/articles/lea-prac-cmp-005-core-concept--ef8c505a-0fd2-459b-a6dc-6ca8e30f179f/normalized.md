## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to:

- Describe the fee structures for specialized architectural services.
- Explain how specialized services (interior design, urban planning) are compensated.
- Identify the SPP documents that govern specialized service fees.
- Distinguish specialized service fees from basic service fees.

Fee computation converts a project's estimated construction cost into architect compensation using classification-specific percentages established in the Architect's Guidelines and SPP documents. This calculation is foundational to professional practice and frequently tested.

## Core Concept

The percentage-of-PCC method multiplies Project Construction Cost (PCC) by a group-specific fee rate. Classification determines which rate applies; PCC accuracy affects the resulting fee. The Architect's Guidelines distinguish between DAEDS (Detailed Architectural and Engineering Design Services — architect plus structural/MEP engineers) and DADS (Detailed Architectural Design Services — architecture only, approximately 60% of DAEDS). The 10 Project Classification Groups represent progressively complex building types.

### Worked Example 1: Group 1 Simple Warehouse

A warehouse project (Group 1: Simplest Utilization) has an estimated PCC of ₱30 million. Group 1 DAEDS fee for projects ≤₱50M is 5% of PCC.

Calculation: ₱30M × 5% = ₱1,500,000 total fee.

SPP 202 Phase distribution (SD=15%, DD=20%, CD=50%, Bidding=5%, CA=10%): Schematic Design ₱225,000 | Design Development ₱300,000 | Contract Documents ₱750,000 | Bidding Assistance ₱75,000 | Construction Administration ₱150,000.

### Worked Example 2: Group 2 Office with Sliding Scale

An office building (Group 2: Moderate Complexity) has PCC of ₱120M. Group 2 DAEDS applies a sliding scale: 6% for first ₱50M, 5% for next ₱50M, 4% for remaining amount.

Calculation: (₱50M × 6%) + (₱50M × 5%) + (₱20M × 4%) = ₱3M + ₱2.5M + ₱0.8M = ₱6.3M total fee.

Phase distribution: Schematic Design ₱945,000 | Design Development ₱1,260,000 | Contract Documents ₱3,150,000 | Bidding Assistance ₱315,000 | Construction Administration ₱630,000.

### Worked Example 3: Group 4 Single-Family Residence

A residential house (Group 4: Low-Rise Residential) has PCC of ₱8M. Group 4 DAEDS fee is flat 10% of PCC regardless of cost brackets.

Calculation: ₱8M × 10% = ₱800,000 total fee.

Phase distribution: Schematic Design ₱120,000 | Design Development ₱160,000 | Contract Documents ₱400,000 | Bidding Assistance ₱40,000 | Construction Administration ₱80,000.

### Worked Example 4: Group 9 Renovation at Enhanced Rate

A Group 2 office renovation (Group 9: Renovations) has PCC of ₱50M. Group 9 applies 150% multiplier to the base group's basic fee rate. Base Group 2 fee: ₱50M × 6% = ₱3M. Renovation enhancement: ₱3M × 150% = ₱4.5M total fee.

Phase distribution: Schematic Design ₱675,000 | Design Development ₱900,000 | Contract Documents ₱2,250,000 | Bidding Assistance ₱225,000 | Construction Administration ₱450,000.

### Worked Example 5: DAEDS vs DADS Comparison

Same ₱40M warehouse (Group 1), showing the difference between full DAEDS and architecture-only DADS. DAEDS (all disciplines): ₱40M × 5% = ₱2,000,000. DADS (architecture only, approximately 60% of DAEDS): ₱40M × 3% = ₱1,200,000. Difference: ₱800,000 (the engineering component).

When a client engages only an architect without structural or MEP consultants under separate contracts, DADS applies. When architects coordinate all disciplines, DAEDS applies.

## Key Provisions

| Group | Building Type | DAEDS Rate (≤₱50M) | Above ₱50M Scale |
|-------|---------------|--------------------|------------------|
| 1 | Warehouses, factories | 5% | 4%, 3%, 2%, 1%, 0.5% |
| 2 | Offices, schools | 6% | 5%, 4%, 3%, 2%, 1% |
| 3 | Churches, museums | 7% | 6%, 5%, 4%, 3%, 2% |
| 4 | Low-rise residential | 10% | Flat rate |
| 5 | Monumental buildings | 12% | Flat rate |
| 6 | Repetitive buildings | Varies by unit count | Declining percentages |
| 7 | Repetitive housing | 10% (1st) +60% (2-10) +30% (11+) | Modular reduction |
| 8 | Interior + site planning | 15% | Flat rate |
| 9 | Renovations | 150% of base group rate | Applied to basic fee |
| 10 | Technical consultation | MDPE method | No percentage |

DADS = approximately 60% of DAEDS rate (used when architect contracts separately from engineers). Phase percentages per SPP 202: Schematic Design 15%, Design Development 20%, Contract Documents 50%, Bidding Assistance 5%, Construction Administration 10%.

## Philippine Practice Context

In Philippine architectural practice, the Architect's Guidelines fee computation reflects the complexity and risk associated with different building types. Warehouses and factories (Group 1) require straightforward design; residential towers (Group 4-5) demand sophisticated systems coordination. Renovations (Group 9) incur premium fees due to hidden conditions and phasing constraints. Practitioners must verify PCC estimates before committing to fees; underestimated construction costs lead to unprofitable projects.

## How It's Tested

Exams test four competencies: (1) recognizing project group classification, (2) selecting correct fee percentage from the Architect's Guidelines, (3) computing total and phased fees accurately, and (4) distinguishing DAEDS from DADS and applying sliding scales correctly. Calculation accuracy is non-negotiable.
