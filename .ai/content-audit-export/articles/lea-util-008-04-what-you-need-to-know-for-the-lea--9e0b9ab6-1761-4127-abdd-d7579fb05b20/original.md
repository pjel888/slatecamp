## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal

Identify Philippine utility-related laws and codes — PD 1096 (NBCP), RA 6541 (Revised Plumbing Code), RA 9514 (Fire Code), PEC (Philippine Electrical Code), RA 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste), RA 9275 (Clean Water Act), RA 9513 (Renewable Energy Act) — and explain their scope and interrelationships.

<ExamTip>
LEA code-scope questions often ask which law governs which aspect. Use this grouping: PD 1096 = building structure and occupancy; RA 6541 = plumbing and sanitary; RA 9514 = fire safety; PEC = electrical; RA 9003 = solid waste; RA 9275 = water quality; RA 9513 = renewable energy.
</ExamTip>

<Tip>
A memory aid for the acronyms: "NBCP-Plumb-Fire-Elec-Waste-Water-Renew" — think of the building lifecycle from structure (NBCP) to utilities (Plumb, Fire, Elec) to environment (Waste, Water) to energy (Renew).
</Tip>

<Warn>
Do not confuse RA 6541 with the National Building Code (PD 1096). RA 6541 is the Revised Plumbing Code; PD 1096 is the NBCP. The LEA may test whether you know which code applies to drainage pipe sizing versus building structural requirements.
</Warn>
