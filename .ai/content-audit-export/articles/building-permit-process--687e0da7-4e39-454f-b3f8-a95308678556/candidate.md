# Building Permit Process

## Source-Safe Scope

This local-first repair is grounded in the checked 2004 Revised IRR of PD 1096, with Fire Code sources used only where the permit workflow refers to fire safety review and occupancy coordination. It removes media blocks, unsupported workflow explanations, and claims not checked against local raw PDFs.

## Permit Required Before Work

PD 1096 Rule III states that no person, firm, corporation, agency, or government instrumentality may construct, alter, repair, convert, use, occupy, move, demolish, or add to a building or structure without first obtaining the required building permit from the Building Official assigned to the locality.

## Ancillary And Accessory Permits

Ancillary permits accompany the building permit. The IRR lists architectural, civil/structural, electrical, mechanical, sanitary, plumbing, and electronics permits as ancillary permits. The building permit is null and void if not accompanied by the applicable ancillary permits. Accessory permits cover special project parts or activities such as swimming pools, firewalls, towers, chimneys, sidewalk work, scaffolding, signs, demolition, and other listed items.

## Application Documents

The application package includes the prescribed forms and multiple sets of survey plans, design plans, specifications, and other documents prepared, signed, and sealed by the proper registered professionals. Architectural documents include site development plans, floor plans, elevations, sections, reflected ceiling plans, accessible-facility details, schedules, accessibility plans, and fire safety documents.

## Review And Issuance

The Building Official gives due course when documents are in order, checks zoning conformity and clearances, and routes discipline reviews. The architectural review covers construction type, fire zones, projections, access, occupancy classification, maximum building height, parking and loading, occupant load, accessibility, light and ventilation, and related items. For fire safety requirements, one set of plans is referred to the City or Municipal Fire Marshal for review and recommendation.

If the application, plans, and specifications conform to the Code and IRR, the Building Official must issue the building permit within fifteen days from payment of the required fees.

## Permit Validity

Issuance of a building permit is not permission to violate the Code. A permit expires and becomes null and void if work does not start within one year after issuance, or if work is suspended or abandoned after commencement for a continuous period of 120 days.
