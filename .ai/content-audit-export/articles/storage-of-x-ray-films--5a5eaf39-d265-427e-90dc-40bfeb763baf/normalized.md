# Storage of X-Ray Films

## Source Boundary

This article is limited to Rule XIV, Sections 1401 through 1403 of the 2004 Revised IRR of PD 1096. It does not add archive-conservation or chemical-fire behavior claims beyond the code text.

## Automatic Fire Extinguishing Systems

Storage rooms for unexposed photographic and x-ray films must have automatic fire extinguishing systems in the listed cases: when unexposed films in accepted safety shipping containers exceed 14.00 cu.m in aggregate; when shelving for individual packages not in those containers exceeds 1.40 cu.m in capacity; and when storage is not in accepted safety shipping containers in any section not exceeding 14.00 cu.m.

## Negatives, Cabinets, and Vaults

Film negatives in storage or handling must be kept in heavy Manila envelopes with not more than twelve films per envelope. Expanding envelopes must not be used.

Film negatives must be kept in properly insulated vented cabinets, vented storage vaults, or outside storage houses. A single cabinet may store not more than 110 kg. If stored film exceeds 450 kg, it must be placed in vented storage vaults, a detached structure, or a roof vault. Vault door openings must be of four-hour fire-resistive construction and kept closed except when in use.

## Ignition and Heat Controls

Only incandescent electric light is allowed, protected with substantial wire guards or vapor-proof globes or both. Portable lights on extension cords are prohibited. Conspicuous NO SMOKING signs must be posted. Films must not be stored within 600 mm of steam pipes, chimneys, or other heat sources.

First-aid provisions must use water or water solutions. Discarded films must be stored and handled in the same manner as other films until removed from the premises.

## Exempt Film Classes

Section 1402 exempts listed film classes, including amateur roll or film-pack films in original packages below 1.40 cu.m, safety film, dental x-ray film, manufacturing storage incidental to film manufacturing, and films stored or used in standard motion-picture booths. Safety photographic x-ray film may be identified by edge marking.

## Exam Use

For LEA review, focus on the exact thresholds and prohibitions: 14.00 cu.m, 1.40 cu.m, twelve films per heavy Manila envelope, no expanding envelopes, 110 kg per cabinet, 450 kg vault threshold, four-hour vault doors, 600 mm heat-source clearance, protected incandescent lighting only, and water or water-solution first-aid provisions.
