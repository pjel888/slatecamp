## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal
Classify multi-purpose halls as Group H or Group C based on stage permanence and seating configuration, calculate occupant load per assembly standards, apply exit width at 7.6 mm/person, and verify dual exits at 50+ occupancy.

Multi-purpose halls and community centers are classified as **Group H (Assembly)** or **Group C (Education and Recreation)** depending on configuration, seating, and stage permanence. The correct classification determines exit capacity, occupant load calculations, and egress requirements.

---

## Core Concept

[SLATECAMP_UI_BLOCK:ui_0004 component="Tip" status="preserved"]

[SLATECAMP_UI_BLOCK:ui_0001 component="Tip" status="preserved"]

Multi-purpose halls are **Group H (Assembly)** occupancies when designed for large-scale public gatherings with standing or chair-based configurations. If a permanent stage is integral to the design (as in a theater or auditorium), the venue may be classified as **Group C**.

### Occupant Load Factors

- **Standing capacity (no seating):** 0.65 square meters per person.
- **Seated arrangement (folding chairs):** 1.1 square meters per person.
- These space allocations include the person's body and immediate movement clearance.

### Stage and Storage Standards

- **Permanent Stage Height:** Typically 600–750 mm above the floor for visibility and safety.
- **Demountable (Temporary) Stages:** Require a depth of 4.0–6.0 meters.
- **Chair Storage:** Budgeted at **0.1 square meters per folding chair** (e.g., 200 chairs = 20 sqm storage).

### Egress and Safety

- **Exit Width:** Minimum **5.0 mm per occupant** for level components (assembly) per **PD 1096 Rule VIII Table VIII-3**.
- **Number of Exits:** A minimum of **two exits** is required when the occupant load exceeds **200** or the travel distance to an exit exceeds **20 meters** per **Rule VIII Section 1007**.
- **Travel Distance:** Shall not exceed **60 meters** in sprinklered buildings or **45 meters** in non-sprinklered buildings.

## Key Provisions

[SLATECAMP_UI_BLOCK:ui_0005 component="Example" status="preserved"]

[SLATECAMP_UI_BLOCK:ui_0002 component="Example" status="preserved"]

**Occupant Load Determination (Group H):**

| Configuration | Area per Person | Calculation |
|:---------------------- |:--------------- |:------------------------------------------------- |
| Standing | 0.65 sqm/person | Hall area ÷ 0.65 |
| Seated with chairs | 1.1 sqm/person | Hall area ÷ 1.1 |
| Permanent stage theater | Varies by design | Group C specific standards |

**Exit Requirements (Group H Assembly):**

- **Exit Width Factor:** 5.0 mm per occupant per **PD 1096 Table VIII-3**.
- **Minimum Exits:** 2 separate exits if occupant load > 200 or travel distance > 20 m (**Rule VIII Sec 1007**).
- **Travel Distance:** 60 m maximum (45 m without sprinklers).

**Accessible Design per BP 344 (2024 Revised IRR):**

- **Wheelchair Seating Space:** Minimum **1.50 m × 1.50 m** per wheelchair space (Section 5.4.2).
- **Accessible Route to Stage:** 1.20 m minimum width, 1:12 maximum slope.
- **Accessible Toilet:** 1.7 m × 1.7 m minimum.

## Philippine Practice Context

Multi-purpose halls are ubiquitous community infrastructure serving celebrations, rallies, and seminars. The 1.1-sqm-per-person seated standard reflects occupied seat spacing, while chair storage standards prevent cluttered egress. In many provincial centers, these halls are integrated with office space, necessitating mixed-occupancy egress design.

## How It's Tested

**Pattern 1: Occupancy Classification (Group C vs. H)**

[SLATECAMP_UI_BLOCK:ui_0006 component="ExamTip" status="preserved"]
[SLATECAMP_UI_BLOCK:ui_0003 component="ExamTip" status="preserved"]
"A community center with a temporary wooden stage serving barangay meetings is classified as **Group H**, but if it had a permanent concrete stage with theater seating, it would be **Group C** (ERR-DSGN-005)." 

**Pattern 2: Occupant Load and Exit Capacity**
"A 275-sqm multi-purpose hall with folding chairs (1.1 sqm/person) has an occupant load of **250 persons**. The minimum required aggregate exit width is **1,250 mm** (250 × 5.0 mm)."

**Pattern 3: Chair Storage and Spatial Planning**
"A hall plans to store 200 folding chairs. The minimum storage area required is **20 sqm** (200 × 0.1)." 

> **Memory Rule:** Group H = standing 0.65 sqm/person, seated 1.1 sqm/person. Stage height 600–750 mm. Exit width factor = 5.0 mm/person per Table VIII-3. Chair storage = 0.1 sqm/chair.

**Exam Tip — ERR-DSGN-026:** Multi-purpose halls without fixed stages = Group H (assembly). With permanent stage = Group C. Flexible furniture does not change classification — only the presence of a fixed stage matters.

## Common Mistakes

- **ERR-DSGN-005:** Classifying a hall with a permanent stage as Group H instead of **Group C**.
- Using 0.5 sqm/person for standing capacity instead of **0.65 sqm/person**.
- Calculating seated capacity at 1.0 sqm/person instead of **1.1 sqm/person**.
- Applying the 7.6 mm/person exit factor for Level 2 assembly components instead of the correct **5.0 mm per occupant** per **Rule VIII Table VIII-3**.
- Assuming a single exit is sufficient for a 150-person hall; while it meets the &lt;200 occupant rule, a second exit is often required if the travel distance exceeds **20 meters**.
- Sizing wheelchair spaces at 4.0 m x 5.0 m instead of the **1.50 m x 1.50 m** minimum per **BP 344 2024 Revised IRR Section 5.4.2**.
