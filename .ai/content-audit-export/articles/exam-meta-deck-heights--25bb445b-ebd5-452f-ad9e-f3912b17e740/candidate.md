# Exam Meta Deck Heights
## Learning Goal
After reading this article, you should be able to:

Apply all code provisions governing deck, balcony, porch, and terrace heights under PD 1096 Rule VII-VIII: determine when a deck counts toward FAR vs when it is excluded; identify minimum guardrail height (1.10 m) for elevated decks; apply minimum ceiling height requirements for covered decks; and distinguish decks from porches and balconies by their code classification.

## SECTION A: THE "1800 CLUB" — Every Instance Where 1800 mm Appears

<LawCallout code="bp344">

All dimensions in this deck are governed by BP 344 (2024 IRR), specifically Appendix A (General Provisions) and Appendix D (Specific Space Requirements). The 1800 mm turning space appears across App. A, Sec. 5.3 (elevators), Sec. 11.4 (telephones), Sec. 12.1 (ATMs), Sec. 13.4 (dressing rooms), and App. A Ramps (landings). Memorize the contexts — the LEA pairs 1800 × 1800 with specific fixtures.

</LawCallout>


> **Memory trigger:** "1800 = room to maneuver." Anywhere a wheelchair must turn, pass another chair, or approach a facility — expect 1800 mm.

### Quick-Fire A-List Recall

<Warn>

⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING: The WC cubicle is 1800 × 2250 mm — NOT 1800 × 1800 mm! The 1800 × 1800 applies to turning spaces, elevator lobbies, ATMs, phones, and roll-in showers. But the accessible WC cubicle requires 1800 mm WIDTH × 2250 mm DEPTH. This is the single most frequently missed dimensional distinction on the LEA.

</Warn>


- **All 1800 × 1800** → turning space, elevator lobby, ATM, phone, drinking fountain, roll-in shower, vestibule
- **1800 mm linear** → ramp landings (all), sidewalk passing, rest spaces on walkways
- **1800 mm as a width** → double-leaf door, vestibule width
- **1800 mm depth but different pairing** → WC cubicle is 1800 × **2250** (not square!)

## SECTION B: THE HEIGHT LADDER — Vertical Stack from 0 mm to Ceiling

<Tip>

**Memory Strategy: "1-8-2-5" Ladder Trick.** The height ladder from floor to ceiling follows a predictable sequence: 200 (floor-level reach), 300 (handrail extension), 400 (kick plate), 500 (urinal), 600–700–750 (alarm, grab bar, WC handrail), 900 (handle, stair rail), 1100 (guard), 1200 (max side reach). Memorize the 5 critical heights between 750–1200 mm — this is the most tested band.

</Tip>


> **Memory strategy:** Read this ladder top-to-bottom once, then quiz yourself by covering the left column. On exam day, if you know whether a height is "below 750," "750–900," or "above 1200," you can eliminate three wrong answers instantly.

## SECTION C: THE "900 CLUB" — Every Place Where 900 mm Appears

> **Memory trigger:** "900 = the human gate." It is the minimum a wheelchair can pass through — so every gateway, every handle, every control that a wheelchair user must operate is at 900 mm.

| # | Element | Dimension | Section Reference | Critical Distinction |
|---|---------|-----------|-------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Accessible door clear width** | 900 mm | BP 344 IRR App. A | Measured face of open door to jamb; **NOT** the nominal door size (use 1000–1100 mm nominal for 900 mm clear) |
| 2 | **Upper handrail height — ramps** | 900 mm AFF | BP 344 IRR Ramps | From ramp surface to top of handrail gripping surface |
| 3 | **Upper handrail height — stairs** | 900 mm above nosing | BP 344 IRR Stairs | Measured vertically from stair nosing; lower rail at 700 mm |
| 4 | **Door handle / pull / latch height** | 900 mm centered | BP 344 IRR Doors | Vertical door pulls preferred; centered at 900 mm AFF |
| 5 | **Car operating panel (COP) height** | 900 mm | BP 344 IRR Elevators | Fixed height — **not** a range; controls and Braille at 900 mm |
| 6 | **Elevator handrail height** | 900 mm | BP 344 IRR Sec. 5 | On 3 walls of accessible elevator car |
| 7 | **Elevator lobby approach width** | 900 mm minimum door width | BP 344 IRR Sec. 5 | Elevator door clear opening width |
| 8 | **Curb ramp width** | 900 mm minimum | BP 344 IRR Site | Exclusive of flared sides; measured at surface |
| 9 | **Access route / corridor minimum clear width** | 900 mm | BP 344 IRR Routes | Absolute minimum; standard accessible corridor = 1500 mm; 900 mm for limited segments |
| 10 | **Wheelchair space width** | 900 mm | BP 344 IRR Sec. 3 / 4 | Space width dimension; length = 1200 mm |

### 900 Club Sub-Rules to Memorize

- **900 mm = clear width** (doors, curb ramps, access routes, elevator doors)
- **900 mm = upper height** (handles, COP, elevator handrails, upper handrail on ramps/stairs)
- **900 mm = wheelchair footprint width** (spaces, refuge, assembly, exercise)
- **900 mm = counter width minimum** (service counters, workstations)

## CONFLICT RESOLUTION CHEAT SHEET

| Topic | BP 344 2024 IRR | PD 1096 NBC | Rule |
|-------|----------------|-------------|------|
| Stair handrail height | **700 mm** (lower) + **900 mm** (upper) | 800–900 mm above nosing | BP 344 governs for accessible stairs |
| Guard/railing height | ≥ 1100 mm (drop > 750 mm) | 1100 mm (Rule XII) | Consistent — both 1100 mm |
| Headroom | 2000 mm | 2000 mm (Rule VII) | Consistent |
| Stair landing interval | Every **3 m** vertical rise (accessible) | Every **3600 mm** vertical rise (general) | BP 344 more stringent for accessible |
| Corridor minimum width | 1500 mm (accessible route) | 1100 mm (most occupancies) | BP 344 governs accessible paths |
| Ramp landing | 1800 mm (BP 344) | Width of ramp (PD 1096 exit ramps) | BP 344 1800 mm standard for accessible ramps |
