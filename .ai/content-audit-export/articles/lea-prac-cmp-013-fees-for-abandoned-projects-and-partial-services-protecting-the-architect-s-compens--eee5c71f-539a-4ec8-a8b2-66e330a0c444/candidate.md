# Fees for Abandoned Projects and Partial Services

SPP 202 protects compensation when architectural services have already been performed but the project is suspended, abandoned, changed, or not implemented. The safe exam rule is stage-based: identify the service stage reached, then pay for services rendered at the amount due for that stage.

## Suspended or Abandoned Work

When the Architect's work is abandoned or suspended in whole or in part, the Owner pays the Architect for services rendered corresponding to the amount due at the stage of suspension or abandonment. This is compensation for professional work already completed, not an automatic penalty and not a waiver of all fees.

SPP 202 also treats the preparation of architectural plans/designs, specifications, and other building construction documents as completion of the Detailed Design and Contract Documents phase. The checked local UAP Standards source states that this phase is equivalent to ninety percent of the Architect's work. If the Owner fails to implement the plans and documents prepared by the Architect, the Architect is entitled to compensation corresponding to ninety percent of the Architect's fee.

## Changes After Approval

If the Owner orders changes after approving the Architect's outputs, the Owner pays for the Architect's extra time, resources, drafting, or other office expenses. That boundary matters in LEA questions because post-approval changes are not silently absorbed into the basic fee merely because they relate to the same project.

## Remaining Work After Long Suspension

When portions of a building are erected at different periods and the construction phase increases the Architect's burden of services, construction-phase charges are adjusted proportionately. If suspension of construction exceeds six months, the fee for remaining works is doubled under the checked SPP 202 wording.

## Real World Scenario

A residential client approves the contract-document package, then shelves the project before bidding because financing falls through. The source-safe answer is not "no fee because no construction happened." The Architect has completed the documents that SPP 202 treats as the detailed design and contract-documents phase, so compensation follows the stage reached and the non-implementation rule.

## Exam Boundary

A source-safe answer should identify the reached service stage, whether prepared plans and documents were not implemented, whether the Owner ordered post-approval changes, and whether a construction suspension exceeded six months. Avoid automatic full-fee claims unless the source condition supports them.
