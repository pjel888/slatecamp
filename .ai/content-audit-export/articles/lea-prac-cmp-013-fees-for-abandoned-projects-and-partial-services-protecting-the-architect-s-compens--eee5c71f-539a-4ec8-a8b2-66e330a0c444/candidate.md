# Fees for Abandoned Projects and Partial Services

SPP 202 protects compensation when architectural services have already been performed but the project is suspended, abandoned, changed, or not implemented. The source-safe rule is stage-based: pay for the service already rendered at the amount due for that stage.

## Suspended or abandoned work

When work is suspended or abandoned in whole or in part, the owner pays the architect for services rendered corresponding to the amount due at the stage of suspension or abandonment. This is not a new penalty; it is payment for completed professional service.

SPP 202 also states that detailed design and contract documents account for 90 percent of the architect's work. If the owner fails to implement the plans and documents prepared by the architect, the architect is entitled to 90 percent of the fee.

## Changes after approval

If the owner orders changes after approving the drawings, specifications, and other documents, the owner pays the architect for extra time, resources, drafting, and office expenses. This keeps post-approval changes from being treated as unpaid basic work.

## Exam boundary

A source-safe answer should identify the reached service stage, whether prepared plans and documents were not implemented, and whether the owner ordered post-approval changes. Avoid automatic full-fee claims unless the source condition supports them.
