# Architecture Beyond Shelter

## Source-Audited Scope

Use this topic as a theory-of-architecture topic about why architecture is more than physical cover. The checked local sources support shelter, human activities, preservation, recognition, association, comfort, convenience, beauty, and environmental meaning.

## Board Exam Quick Check

When a question asks why architecture is beyond shelter, choose the answer that includes human activity, social and psychological needs, function, construction, and aesthetic composition. Avoid answers that say shelter alone is enough.

## Core Concepts

- Shelter is a basic need, but architecture protects and organizes the activities of human life.
- Salvan links architecture to work, recreation, sleep, preservation, recognition, and social association.
- A building becomes architecture when enclosed form and surface relate to human activities and human needs.
- Theory sources also connect shelter to security, affiliation, esteem, self-actualization, and environmental meaning.
- Good architecture requires more than engineering sufficiency; it must also carry logical beauty and human fit.

## LEA Use

Architecture-beyond-shelter questions test scope. The best answer includes shelter as a base requirement while adding social, cultural, psychological, functional, and aesthetic dimensions.

## Active Recall

1. Why is shelter not the whole definition? Architecture also organizes human activities and meanings.
2. What human activities are source-backed? Work, recreation, sleep, preservation, recognition, and association.
3. What is the exam-safe distinction? Shelter is necessary, but architecture adds human fit, use, and beauty.
