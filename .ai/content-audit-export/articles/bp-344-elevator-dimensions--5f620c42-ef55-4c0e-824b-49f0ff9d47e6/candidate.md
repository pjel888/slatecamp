# BP 344 Elevator Dimensions
## Learning Goal
After reading this article, you should be able to:

Apply BP 344 elevator cab dimension requirements: minimum cab size based on building occupancy (1.20 m × 1.30 m minimum, 1.60 m × 1.50 m for larger buildings), door clear opening (900 mm minimum), call button height (900-1,100 mm), Braille and tactile markings, and visual/audible floor indicators; calculate minimum elevator quantity by floor area and occupancy.

## The Real-World Hook (Sensory Activation)

You are standing at the main entrance of a newly designed public hospital.[file:4] A visually impaired patient using a wheelchair enters.[file:4] They cannot see the floor indicator.[file:4] They cannot hear the floor announcement.[file:4] The elevator car is too narrow for their chair, and the lobby outside the elevator has no room to turn around.[file:4] **Every one of these failures violates BP 344 — and every one of them has a specific dimension or requirement that the 2024 IRR mandates for accessible elevators.**[file:4] The LEA tests your ability to protect human dignity using exact numbers; you must integrate these with the general circulation and occupancy framework of PD 1096 and NBC Rules VII & VIII for real-world compliance.[file:1][file:3][file:4]

## The E-D-C-H-J-V Framework (Mnemonic Chunking)

_Read this aloud twice and visualize the physical space to build spatial memory:_

- **E (Elevator Car):** **1200 mm** Width x **1400 mm** Depth (minimum clear internal dimensions) for the accessible car.[file:4] \[Section 8, Elevators (car dimensions), BP 344 2024 IRR\][file:4][web:6][web:16][web:34]
- **D (Door and Distance):** **900 mm** clear door opening; accessible elevator located within a maximum **30 meters** horizontal travel from the main entrance or principal accessible route in public buildings.[file:4] \[Section 8, Elevators (max travel distance), 8.5\][file:4]
- **C (Controls / COP):** Car Operating Panel centerline at **900 mm** above floor; buttons minimum **20 mm** diameter, maximum **1 mm** depression depth, with tactile and Braille characters.[file:4] \[Section 8, Elevators (COP height), 8.7.d\][file:4]
- **H (Handrails):** **900 mm** height, mounted on **both sides and the rear** of the car, with maximum **150 mm** gap from wall to rail.[file:4] \[Section 8, Elevators (handrails), 8.6.c\][file:4]
- **J (Jambs):** Braille floor indicators at **1200 mm AFF** on **both** side door jambs of each landing.[file:4] \[Section 8, Elevators (Braille on jambs)\][file:4]
- **V (Voice and Visual):** Voice synthesizer required for floor announcements, plus emergency two-way **visual** communication for deaf and hard-of-hearing users.[file:4] \[Section 8, Elevators (voice synthesizer), 8.9\][file:4]

_Chant this: **"E is the box. D is the door and the distance. C is the controls. H is the handrail. J is the jamb. V is the voice and the vision."**[file:4]_

<LawCallout code="bp344" section="Section 10 - Vertical Access">


Why the letter V matters (and how it fits with PD 1096):
PD 1096 frames elevators as part of vertical circulation and fire safety but does not prescribe detailed accessibility tech like voice synthesizers or visual emergency links.[file:1] The 2024 BP 344 IRR closes this gap by explicitly requiring auditory and visual communication so that elevators remain usable by all occupants in everyday use and during emergencies.[file:4]


</LawCallout>

<LawCallout code="bp344" section="Section 10.1 - Elevator Car Dimensions">


Why 1200mm width (not the old 1100mm)?
Modern power wheelchairs and scooters are wider than older manual chairs, and PD 1096’s general exit width logic anticipates larger occupant flow paths in public occupancies.[file:1] The 2024 IRR aligns with this reality by requiring 1200mm clear car width, future‑proofing the accessible car for current and near‑future mobility devices.[file:4]


</LawCallout>

<LawCallout code="bp344" section="Section 10.3 - Controls and Signals">


Why 1800 x 1800mm lobby turning space, given NBC Rules VII & VIII?
NBC Rule VIII establishes general minimum room and circulation sizes but does not fix a turning circle dimension for wheelchairs.[file:1][file:3] BP 344’s 1800 x 1800mm turning space overlays on that framework to guarantee a full turn without violating light, ventilation, and exit layout logic set by PD 1096.[file:4]


</LawCallout>

## Expanded Section: Communication and Safety Systems (Sec 8.8–8.10)

These provisions overlay the NBC’s general requirements for exits and emergency egress, focusing specifically on PWDs trapped if elevators fail.[file:1][file:4]

| System                  | Requirement                                                      | Section           |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Voice synthesizer       | Required — announces each floor arrival                          | Section 8, Elevators (voice synthesizer)[file:4] |
| Emergency communication | Two-way **visual** system required, in addition to audio         | Section 8, Elevators (emergency visual)[file:4]   |
| Power outage operation  | Elevator must remain operable for evacuation under power failure | Section 8, Elevators (power outage)[file:4]  |

<Callout variant="authority" title="Power Outage (Sec 8.10) vs NBC Exit Stairs">

Power Outage (Sec 8.10) vs NBC Exit Stairs:
PD 1096 exit and stair provisions assume ambulatory occupants can use stairs.[file:1] PWDs who rely on elevators cannot; thus BP 344 requires that at least the accessible elevator remain operable via backup or alternate power, so the egress strategy remains inclusive.[file:4]

</Callout>

## The Board Exam Traps (Error Management Training)

Examiners love to test whether you can separate older NBC-era habits from updated 2024 BP 344 numbers.[file:4]

**TRAP LEART 1 — The "Old 1100mm Car" Trap**

- Options: (A) 1100 x 1400 mm, (B) 1200 x 1400 mm.
- **Wrong:** 1100mm width reflects older practice or foreign codes.
- **BP 344 2024 Truth:** **1200 mm Wide x 1400 mm Deep** for the accessible car (Section 8, Elevators (car dimensions)).[file:4]

**TRAP LEART 2 — The "Overall vs Clear" Door Trap**

- **Wrong wording:** “Elevator door is 900mm wide overall.”
- **BP 344 Truth:** The requirement is **900mm clear opening** (Section 8, Elevators (car dimensions)). The physical leaf and frame are wider; the clear passage is what matters.[file:4]

**TRAP LEART 3 — The "Turning Inside the Car" Trap**

- **Wrong assumption:** “Elevator car provides 1800mm turning space.”
- **Truth:** The **1800 x 1800mm** turning space is in the **lobby/corridor outside** the 1200 x 1400mm accessible car (Section 8, Elevators (lobby space)); the car is too small for a full 1.8m circle.[file:4]

**TRAP LEART 4 — The "Jamb vs Panel" Trap**

- **Wrong answer:** “Braille is required only on the control panel buttons.”
- **Truth:** Floor level indicators in Braille **must also be on both side door jambs** at **1200mm AFF** (Section 8, Elevators (Braille on jambs)), in addition to Braille on COP buttons.[file:4]

**TRAP LEART 5 — The "Audio-Only Emergency" Trap**

- **Wrong answer:** “An audio intercom is sufficient for emergency communication.”
- **Truth:** Section 8, Elevators (emergency visual) requires a **two-way visual communication system**; audio alone excludes deaf or hard-of-hearing users.[file:4]

**TRAP LEART 6 — The "COP Range" Trap**

- **Distractor:** “COP height: 900–1200mm AFF.”
- **Truth:** COP is **900mm on center** — a single fixed dimension, not a range (Section 8, Elevators (COP height)).[file:4]

## Active Recall Battery (The Testing Effect)

_Cover the answers and retrieve from memory; do not just reread._

**Q1:** What is the maximum allowed distance from the accessible building entrance to the accessible elevator?

> **Ans:** **30.00 meters** (Section 8, Elevators (max travel distance)).[file:4]

**Q2:** Under the 2024 IRR, what is the minimum clear internal dimension of the accessible elevator car?

> **Ans:** **1200 mm Wide x 1400 mm Deep** (Section 8, Elevators (car dimensions)).[file:4]

**Q3:** Where must Braille floor indicators be placed, and at what height?

> **Ans:** On **both side door jambs** of each landing at **1200mm AFF** (Section 8, Elevators (Braille on jambs)).[file:4]

**Q4:** What is the required button diameter and maximum depression depth?

> **Ans:** Minimum **20mm** diameter, maximum **1mm** depression (Section 8, Elevators (button size)).[file:4]

**Q5:** Are handrails on both side walls only compliant?

> **Ans:** No. Section 8, Elevators (handrails) requires handrails on **both side walls and the rear wall**.[file:4]

**Q6:** Is an audio-only emergency intercom sufficient?

> **Ans:** No. Section 8, Elevators (emergency visual) requires a **two-way visual communication system** in addition to audio.[file:4]

**Q7:** What must happen to the elevator during a building power outage?

> **Ans:** It must remain **operable for evacuation** via backup or alternative power (Section 8, Elevators (power outage)).[file:4]

**Q8:** What is the clear turning space required in the elevator lobby?

> **Ans:** **1800 mm x 1800 mm** clear (Section 8, Elevators (lobby space)).[file:4]
