# BP 344 Elevator Dimensions

## Learning Goal

Apply BP 344 2024 Revised IRR requirements for accessible elevators, including location, car size, door opening, controls, handrails, guides, warnings, and lobby clear space.

## Elevator Access

BP 344 states that elevators or other means of conveyance must be provided in buildings for public use to move a user from one storey to another. All elevators provided in a building must include suitable provisions for persons with sensory disabilities and ambulant persons with disabilities.

All elevators must be made accessible to persons with disabilities. Accessible elevators must be located not more than 30 meters from the entrance and must be easy to locate with directional signs.

## Car and Door Dimensions

Accessible elevator cars must have a minimum clear dimension of 1400 mm in depth and 1200 mm in width, with a clear door opening of not less than 900 mm.

Elevator lobbies must have a minimum clear space of at least 1800 mm by 1800 mm.

## Controls, Handrails, and Communication

Handrails must be placed at 900 mm from the finished floor, fixed on both sides and at the rear of the elevator. The car operating panel must be installed at 900 mm on center from the floor. Control buttons must have Braille at the left of the buttons and tactile buttons. Floor level indicators in Braille must be installed at 1200 mm from the finished floor on both side jambs of the elevator door.

Accessible elevators must include audio, visual, and tactile guide and warning provisions, voice synthesizers, audio-visual emergency indicators, and an emergency two-way communication system with audio, visual, and tactile control options.

## Practice Cue

Do not answer with generic elevator dimensions. For BP 344 accessibility, use the 1400 mm by 1200 mm clear car, 900 mm clear door opening, 1800 mm by 1800 mm lobby, 900 mm handrail/control-panel heights, and audio-visual-tactile features.
