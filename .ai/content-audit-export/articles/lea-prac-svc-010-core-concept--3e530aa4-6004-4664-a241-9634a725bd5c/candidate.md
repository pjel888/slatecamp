# SPP Doc 208: Architectural Design Competitions

## Source Scope

This local repair uses SPP Document 208 from the raw UAP PDF bundle and its local OCR/refined Markdown conversion. It focuses on the source-backed roles named in the checked Architectural Design Competition material.

## LEA Anchor

SPP Document 208 is the standard for Architectural Design Competitions. The checked source supports these role anchors:

1. The Owner is the person or organization that undertakes or promotes an ADC, issues the invitation to architects, and finances the ADC.
2. The Jury is appointed by the owner to assess competition entries. Its members are called jurors. The checked source describes a majority of architects assisted by a lay juror who represents and voices the owner's intention.
3. The Professional Advisor is an architect nominated by the owner and approved by IAPOA to organize the ADC on behalf of the owner.
4. Technical Advisors are specialist personnel consulted by jurors so the jury can obtain necessary relevant information.
5. The Competition Secretariat is formed by the owner and approved by the Professional Advisor to assist the Professional Advisor and Jury in the administrative conduct of the ADC.
6. The IAPOA ADC Committee is appointed by the IAPOA National President to oversee ADCs referred to IAPOA.

## Exam Use

For source-safe questions, match each SPP 208 role to its function. Do not import SPP 202 fee distributions, SPP 203 specialized services, or SPP 204-A supervision scope into this ADC cluster.
