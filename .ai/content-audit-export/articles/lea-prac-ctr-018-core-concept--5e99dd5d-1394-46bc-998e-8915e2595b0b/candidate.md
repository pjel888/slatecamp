# Punch List

A punch list is the owner's list, or lists, of defects found or discovered on completed work after substantial completion. CIAP Document 102 uses this term directly: the owner issues one or more lists of defects, otherwise known as a punch list or lists, and the contractor must receive them not later than thirty days from the date of substantial completion.

CIAP Document 102 limits later additions. The owner may add to the punch list only as to corrective work on items in the original punch list or lists, and only not later than sixty days from the date of substantial completion.

The punch list connects to the Period of Making Good of Known Defects or Faults. That period is not more than thirty calendar days, counted from the contractor's receipt of the last item in the punch list submitted within the allowed period. During it, the contractor completes the corrective works required by the punch list.
