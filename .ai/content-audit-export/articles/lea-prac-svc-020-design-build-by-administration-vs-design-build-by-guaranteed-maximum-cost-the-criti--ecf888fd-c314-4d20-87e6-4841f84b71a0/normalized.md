# Design-Build by Administration vs Design-Build by Guaranteed Maximum Cost

## Source-Audited Core

SPP 207 frames Design-Build Services as a way to balance time, quality, and cost. The checked UAP source says the arrangement can simplify and expedite the process and can provide creative, cost-effective solutions.

The risk point is professional responsibility. In Design-Build Services, the Architect assumes professional responsibility and civil liability for both design and construction.

SPP 207 identifies two types: Design-Build Services by Administration and Design-Build Services with Guaranteed Maximum Project Construction Cost. By Administration has a scope similar to SPP 202. The Guaranteed Maximum Project Construction Cost mode is essentially the same except that the Architect provides a guaranteed maximum project construction cost.

The checked source also limits the guarantee by identifying owner-borne additional expenditures: construction variation orders, extra work orders, substantial escalation evidenced by NEDA, or causes not attributable to the Architect.

## LEA Practice Guardrails

For exam purposes, do not reduce SPP 207 to ordinary design services. The defining issue is that the Architect carries both design and construction responsibility. The clean distinction between the two modes is the guaranteed maximum project construction cost.
