# UAP Standards of Professional Practice: General Standards

## Learning Goal

Use the general UAP Standards of Professional Practice source to orient LEA questions on the SPP framework: effective date, RA 9266 basis, acronyms, selection methods, foreign architects, Architectural Firms, SPPCC, and the SPP document map.

## Source-Audited Scope

The audited source is the UAP Standards of Professional Practice reference copy for Philippine Registered and Licensed Architects. The source states that the SPP took effect on 09 March 2011 and connects the SPP to the RA 9266 Implementing Rules and Regulations.

## RA 9266 and Annex A

Annex A explains that the SPP is required under Section 41 of the RA 9266 IRR. It defines basic terms used across the SPP, including Architect, Registered and Licensed Architect, Client, Owner, Contractor, and Architectural Firm.

## Core Acronyms

The source uses acronyms that recur in LEA professional-practice questions:

- IAPOA means Integrated and Accredited Professional Organization of Architects.
- RLA means Registered and Licensed Architect.
- SPP means Standards of Professional Practice.
- TSP means Temporary or Special Permit.
- ADC means Architectural Design Competition.
- SPPCC means Statement of Probable Project Construction Cost.

## Methods of Selecting the Architect

The general SPP material identifies three main selection methods:

1. Direct selection.
2. Comparative selection.
3. Architectural Design Competition.

The SPP describes direct selection as appropriate when the Owner already knows the Architect's reputation, performance, or professional ability. Comparative selection compares qualified architects or firms. ADC selection follows the competition rules later detailed in SPP Document 208.

## Foreign Architects and Architectural Firms

Foreign architects working on Philippine projects must comply with Philippine requirements, including the Temporary or Special Permit framework and collaboration with a local counterpart when required. Architectural Firms must also comply with the definitions and professional controls under RA 9266 and its derivative regulations.

## SPPCC in Regular Design Services

SPP Document 202 uses SPPCC for the Statement of Probable Project Construction Cost. The Architect prepares or updates the SPPCC during design phases, but the SPP warns that such estimates have only a certain degree of accuracy because the Architect does not control labor, materials, and competitive bidding factors.

## Document Map

The SPP framework includes general and service-specific documents. The audited source includes Annex A and service documents such as SPP 201, SPP 202, SPP 203, SPP 204-A, SPP 204-B, SPP 205, SPP 206, SPP 207, SPP 208, and SPP 209. SPP 208 is the Architectural Design Competition document.

## LEA Traps

- Do not confuse IAPOA with a government agency; it is the integrated and accredited professional organization.
- Do not add a fourth Architect-selection method unless the SPP source supports it.
- SPPCC is an estimate, not a guaranteed construction cost.
- Foreign participation must follow the RA 9266 permit and local-practice controls.
- Use the SPP document number before choosing an answer; each document governs a different service type.

## Real World Scenario

An owner planning a civic building wants to compare architects by lowest fee and also invites a foreign architect to join the competition team. Under the UAP SPP source, split the issue into selection method and legal capacity. The recognized selection routes are direct selection, comparative selection, and Architectural Design Competition; lowest-bid selection is not one of the three. If a foreign architect participates on a Philippine project, check the Temporary or Special Permit, work-permit, and local RLA counterpart requirements before treating the entry as compliant. When the owner asks for a construction-cost guarantee, remember that the SPPCC is an Architect's probable cost estimate, not a guaranteed bid price.
