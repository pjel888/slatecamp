# Specialized Health Facilities: Source-Safe Code Framing

## Source Scope

This local repair is limited to PD 1096 occupancy classification, the RA 9514 2019 Revised IRR, and the local Fire Code source family. Earlier DOH room-area, Sentrong Sigla, toilet-ratio, and BP 344 statements are not carried forward because those source families were outside this Batch 064 raw-PDF audit.

## LEA Anchor

Treat a specialized health facility as a classification problem before treating it as a room-planning problem. A clinic, laboratory, lying-in clinic, hospital, nursing home, birth center, or multi-purpose clinic can point to different PD 1096 and RA 9514 paths. Answer from the source named by the question.

## Occupancy Classification

PD 1096 lists outpatient clinics, family planning clinics, lying-in clinics, diagnostic clinics, and medical or clinical laboratories in the checked A-2 residential row for limited non-leasing occupants. The same checked PD 1096 matrix lists hospitals, sanitaria, homes for the aged, and certain nurseries under institutional health-care use, and it lists general hospitals, medical centers, and multi-purpose clinics in a C-2 matrix context. The controlling prompt must therefore identify the facility use and the table being applied.

## Fire and Life Safety

RA 9514 classifies health care facilities as places used for medical or other treatment or care where occupants are mostly incapable of self-preservation because of age, disability, or conditions not under their control. The checked examples are hospitals, nursing homes, and birth centers. For health care occupancies, RA 9514 requires remote exits, specific exit-access paths, corridor widths, smoke compartmentation, alarm or detection systems, and sprinkler protection as applicable.

## Exam Use

Do not answer a health-facility item by importing unverified room-area schedules. First classify the facility, then apply the fire and life-safety rule tied to that classification. If the question asks about FSIC, egress, smoke compartments, alarms, or sprinklers, use RA 9514 and the Fire Code source family rather than a clinic-program shortcut.
