## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal
Classify specialized health facilities as Group D institutional, apply consultation room minimums (7.43-9 sqm), calculate waiting areas at 1.5 sqm/person, verify DOH Sentrong Sigla compliance, and apply egress at 45m/60m travel distance.

Specialized health facilities (primary care, BHC, RHU, outpatient clinics) are classified as **Group D** (institutional). These facilities require consultation rooms of minimum 9 sqm, waiting areas at 1.5 sqm per seat, and strict DOH regulatory compliance. They differ from acute-care hospitals, which have larger bed area requirements (typically 10.5 sqm per bed).

Specialized health facilities—including primary care clinics, barangay health centers (BHC), rural health units (RHU), and outpatient specialist facilities—fall under **Group D** (institutional) occupancy per **PD 1096**. Unlike acute-care hospitals, these facilities provide ambulatory care without bed count requirements.

### Space Standards

- **Consultation Room**: The minimum area is **7.43 sqm per examination table** or **9 sqm** total per DOH standards to accommodate the physician, patient, and confidentiality.
- **Minor Surgical/Treatment Room**: Requires a minimum of **12 sqm**.
- **Waiting Areas**: Must provide **1.4 to 1.5 sqm per person** based on the occupancy load.
- **Diagnostic Rooms**: X-ray rooms typically require 14 sqm, while laboratories require 10 sqm.
- **Pharmacy**: Dispensing areas require a minimum of **4 sqm**, though total pharmacy space varies (8–15 sqm) based on facility capability.

### Sanitation and Egress

- **Toilets**: Facilities must segregate staff from patient use. Institutional ratios often follow 1 WC per 30 persons per DOH Sentrong Sigla standards.
- **Accessible Features**: Per the **BP 344 2024 Revised IRR**, doors must be **900 mm** wide, and ramps must have a maximum slope of **1:20**.
- **Fire Egress**: Egress must comply with **RA 9514 (Fire Code 2019 Revised IRR)** and **PD 1096 Rule XIV**. Travel distance is limited to **45 m** (unsprinklered) or **60 m** (sprinklered).
- **Dual Exits**: Required for any space or facility with an occupancy of **50 or more persons** per RA 9514 and PD 1096 Rule XIV.

## Philippine Practice Context

DOH-accredited facilities under the **Sentrong Sigla** program require formal space standards verification. Common design failures include undersized consultation rooms (failing to meet the 9 sqm minimum) and insufficient waiting areas during peak hours.

- **Rural Health Units (RHU)**: Multi-functional rooms (consultation/treatment combo) are permitted if they achieve at least **12 sqm** and maintain clear functional separation.
- **Infection Control**: Post-pandemic standards have increased the demand for isolation rooms and dedicated medical waste storage.
- **Waste Management**: Storage must be isolated from general circulation with dedicated ventilation and external access.

## How It's Tested

1. **Occupancy Distinction**: Do not confuse outpatient primary care (9 sqm consultation room) with acute hospitals (10.5 sqm bed area).
2. **Room Sizing**: Calculations often involve determining the total area for a suite (e.g., 3 consultation rooms at 9 sqm each + waiting area at 1.5 sqm/seat).
3. **Fire Safety Integration**: A BHC with a peak occupancy of 80 persons (staff + patients + visitors) **must** have dual exits.

> **Health Facility Anchors**: Group D occupancy. Consultation = 9 sqm. Treatment = 12 sqm. Waiting = 1.5 sqm/seat. Pharmacy = 4 sqm. Exit width = 7.6 mm/person.

## Common Mistakes

- **ERR-DSGN-002**: Classifying specialized health as Group C (Education) instead of Group D (Institutional).
- **ERR-DSGN-029**: Applying hospital bed standards (10.5 sqm/bed) to outpatient consultation rooms (9 sqm).
- **ERR-DSGN-019**: Using a 5 mm/person exit width instead of the correct **7.6 mm/person**.
- **ERR-DSGN-021**: Providing only a single exit for clinics with a total occupancy of 50 or more persons.
- **ERR-DSGN-014**: Designing ramps at 1:12 instead of the **1:20 maximum** required by the BP 344 2024 Revised IRR for health facilities.
