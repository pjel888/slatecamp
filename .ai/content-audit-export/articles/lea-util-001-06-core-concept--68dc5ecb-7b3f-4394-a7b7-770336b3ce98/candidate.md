# Building Waste Disposal: Core Concept

Building waste disposal is a public-health system, not a back-of-house afterthought. For LEA purposes, separate the problem into solid waste, wastewater, and building plumbing discharge.

## Solid Waste

RA 9003 is the source frame for ecological solid waste management. Its policy emphasizes source reduction, waste minimization, resource recovery, recycling, composting, reuse, and proper segregation, collection, transport, storage, treatment, and disposal.

A materials recovery facility is part of that system. It can serve transfer, sorting, drop-off, composting, and recycling functions. The point for the exam is that solid waste management begins before final disposal; segregation and recovery are system functions, not optional housekeeping details.

## Wastewater and Sewage

RA 9275 governs water pollution from land-based sources and defines the vocabulary used in wastewater control. Wastewater is waste in liquid state containing pollutants. Sewage and septage are separate regulated concepts. Effluent, discharge, treatment, and sewerage are also defined because water-quality control depends on what leaves the building and where it goes.

The Clean Water Act also directs sewerage and septage planning. Highly urbanized cities are expected to connect existing sewage lines to available sewerage systems, while other localities use septage or combined sewerage-septage approaches as applicable.

## Building-Level Disposal Path

PD 1096 Section 903 gives the building-level routing rule: discharge wastewater to the nearest sanitary sewer main where one exists. If no sanitary sewer main exists, the acceptable path is an Imhoff tank, septic tank with subsurface absorption, or another suitable treatment plant, subject to the Code on Sanitation and RNPC requirements.

PD 856 then supplies the sanitation frame for sewage collection and disposal, excreta disposal, drainage, and refuse disposal. It identifies septic tanks, septic tank absorption fields, approved excreta disposal facilities, and local-government responsibilities for refuse collection and disposal.

## LEA Takeaway

Classify the waste before answering: solid waste points to RA 9003, wastewater and discharge to RA 9275, building sewage routing to PD 1096 and PD 856, and fixture-to-pipe plumbing design to the Revised National Plumbing Code.
