# Chinese architecture

## LEA Point

Chinese architecture questions usually test order before ornament. Identify the structural system, planning hierarchy, and ritual setting: dougong for bracketed timber load transfer, siheyuan for four-sided courtyard domestic order, the Forbidden City for imperial axial hierarchy, and the Temple of Heaven for state ritual cosmology.

## Source Anchors

- UNESCO anchors the Imperial Palaces listing to the Ming and Qing palace complexes, with the Forbidden City serving as the Beijing imperial palace reference.
- UNESCO anchors the Temple of Heaven as an imperial ritual complex tied to sacrifices to Heaven and harvest prayers.
- Local Chinese Architecture, HOA Module 3, and Fletcher locators carry the exam vocabulary for dougong, raised platforms, siheyuan/courtyard hierarchy, Yingzao Fashi/manualized construction, Temple of Heaven recognition, and Forbidden City recognition.

## Exam Pattern

If the stem names interlocking wooden blocks and bracket arms supporting deep eaves, choose dougong rather than generic post-and-beam framing. If the stem describes four pavilions arranged around a central court, choose siheyuan. If it describes imperial Beijing, nested courts, and a north-south ceremonial sequence, choose the Forbidden City. If it describes ritual harvest prayer, circular heaven symbolism, or a state altar complex, choose the Temple of Heaven.

## Guardrails

Do not treat feng shui as decorative color symbolism only; use it as source-safe planning vocabulary for orientation, site selection, cardinal order, and courtyard/axis logic. Do not preserve unsupported exact water/mountain placement unless the local packet carries it. Keep Chinese timber and courtyard logic distinct from Japanese, Korean, and Southeast Asian examples that may also use wood, courtyards, or ritual axes.

## Real World Scenario

When documenting a heritage courtyard adaptive-reuse proposal, use the Chinese source map to separate structure from symbolism: dougong belongs to timber bracket construction, the courtyard sequence belongs to social hierarchy and privacy, and Temple of Heaven imagery should not be borrowed for a domestic siheyuan unless the project is explicitly interpreting ritual cosmology.
