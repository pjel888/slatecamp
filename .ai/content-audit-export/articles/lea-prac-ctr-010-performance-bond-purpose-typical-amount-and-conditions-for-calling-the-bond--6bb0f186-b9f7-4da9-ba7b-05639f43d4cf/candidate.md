## Performance Bond: Purpose, Amount, and Source-Safe Conditions

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to:

- Define the performance bond from UAP Document 301.
- State the CIAP Document 102 private-construction bond amount used in this source-audited batch.
- Distinguish performance bond coverage from payment bond and guarantee bond coverage.

## Definition

UAP Document 301 defines the performance bond as the approved form of security furnished by the contractor and surety as a guarantee that the contractor will execute the work according to the contract. The surety is the person, firm, or corporation providing the guarantee for the contractor's bonds.

## Amount and Timing in CIAP Document 102

CIAP Document 102 states that before signing the contract, the contractor furnishes a performance bond equal to 15% of the contract amount for the faithful performance of the work. The same provision separately requires a 15% bond covering obligations to workers, subcontractors, and suppliers. These bonds remain in effect until replaced by the contractor's guarantee bond.

## Mandatory Bond Conditions

CIAP Document 102 treats mandatory bond conditions as part of the bond. The checked source states that the bond continues beyond its stated effectivity period if the work or obligation has not been completely performed, and the owner retains recourse until cancellation, replacement, reservation of rights, or unconditional acceptance of the work.

## Exam Boundary

For this article, use the 15% CIAP Document 102 figure. Do not substitute an unstated 100% performance-bond amount unless the question names a different source that provides that amount.
