# Reimbursable Expenses in Architectural Services

SPP 202 separates the architect's professional fee from reimbursable expenses. In the checked source, the total cost of service is the professional fee plus reimbursable expenses, not a single undefined amount. For exam purposes, keep reimbursables separate from the fee and require source or contract facts before adding markups, caps, or absorption rules.

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to identify reimbursable expense categories and keep them separate from professional fees.

## What the UAP Source Treats as Reimbursable

The checked UAP lines list transportation, housing and living allowances, per diem, extra sets of drawings or specifications, long-distance communication, technical and laboratory tests, licenses, fees, taxes, and similar project-needed costs. The owner-responsibility section also requires the owner to pay reimbursable expenses and taxes imposed because of the architect's services, excluding income tax.

Extra sets of contract documents are a specific example: the owner pays for additional sets. When the architect or authorized representative must perform services beyond 50.0 kilometers from the established office, the UAP Standards locator makes per diem plus traveling and living expenses chargeable to the owner. Batch 106 uses that 50.0-kilometer UAP Standards locator; older proforma agreement language should not be imported unless the question specifically gives that agreement as the source.

## Documentation and Billing Control

For professional consulting services, the source says reimbursable direct costs are billed to the client and supported by receipts or other documents if required. That makes documentation part of fee control. A source-safe invoice should separate the professional fee, reimbursable categories, supporting documents, and taxes.

## What Is Not Automatically Reimbursable

General office overhead is not automatically a reimbursable line item merely because the firm has operating costs. The MDPE multiplier covers overhead and reasonable profit. Specialist consultants, separate services, extra document sets, travel beyond the source threshold, and owner-ordered changes can be chargeable when the source or contract supports them.

## Real World Scenario

An architect travels from Quezon City to a provincial site for an owner-requested inspection, buys additional plan sets for the owner's bidders, and pays courier charges for contract documents. A source-safe invoice separates the agreed professional fee from reimbursables: travel and living expenses, extra document sets, and delivery costs. The architect attaches receipts where required and avoids adding an invented percentage markup unless the service agreement states one.

## Exam Traps

- Do not say reimbursables are part of the architect's profit by default.
- Do not invent a standard markup or reimbursement cap.
- Do not absorb extra document sets or project-required tests when the source makes them owner-paid.
- Do not mix the 50.0-kilometer UAP Standards locator with older agreement distances unless the item identifies the governing document.
