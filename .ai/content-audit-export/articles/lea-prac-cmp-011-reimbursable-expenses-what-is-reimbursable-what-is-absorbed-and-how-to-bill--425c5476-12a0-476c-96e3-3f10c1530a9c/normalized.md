# Reimbursable Expenses in Architectural Services

SPP 202 separates the architect's professional fee from reimbursable expenses. The source expresses the total cost of services as the professional fee plus reimbursable expenses, not as a single undefined amount.

## What the UAP source treats as reimbursable

The checked UAP lines list transportation, housing and living allowances, technical and laboratory tests, licenses, fees, taxes, extra sets of drawings or specifications, long-distance communication, and other project-needed costs as reimbursable expense categories. The owner responsibilities section also requires payment for reimbursable expenses and taxes due the architect.

Extra document sets are a specific example: copies beyond the required sets are paid by the owner. When services are required at a place more than 50 kilometers from the architect's established office, the source makes per diem, traveling, and living expenses chargeable to the owner.

For professional consulting architecture, reimbursable direct costs should be supported by receipts and other documents if required. That makes documentation part of the billing control, not an optional afterthought.

## Exam boundary

A source-safe answer should name the reimbursable category and keep it separate from the professional fee. Do not invent percentage markups, automatic absorption rules, or reimbursement caps unless they are actually in the governing agreement or source.

Source basis: UAP Standards refined markdown lines 421-435, 461-490, and 1621-1631; SPP 202 local raw PDF checked.
