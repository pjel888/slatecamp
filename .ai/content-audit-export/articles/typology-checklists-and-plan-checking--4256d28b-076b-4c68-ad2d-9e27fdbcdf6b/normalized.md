## SECTION D: SEQUENTIAL PLAN-CHECKING WORKFLOW

> **HOW TO USE:** On the LEA or in professional practice, run a plan through this sequence top-to-bottom. Each step builds on the last. A "NO" at any step = a code violation.

---

### STEP 1: SITE

```
IS ACCESSIBLE PARKING PROVIDED?
├── Count: Is it min 1 per 25 regular spaces?
├── Stall: Is each stall 4000 mm wide × 5000 mm long?
├── Aisle: Is there a 1800 mm shared access aisle?
├── Clearance: Is vertical clearance ≥ 2300 mm?
├── Location: Is accessible parking the nearest to the accessible entrance?
├── Signage: ISA on pole (600 × 600 mm) at each accessible stall?
└── FAIL if any item is NO → non-compliant parking
```

**Key numbers:** **3700 × 5000 mm** stall | **1200 mm** aisle | 2300 mm clearance | 1 per 25

---

### STEP 2: ROUTE FROM PARKING / STREET TO ENTRANCE

```
IS THERE A CONTINUOUS ACCESSIBLE ROUTE?
├── Sidewalk: Is clear width ≥ 1500 mm?
├── Cross-slope: Is cross-slope ≤ 1:50?
├── Level changes: Are all level changes addressed (ramp/curb ramp)?
├── Curb ramp: Is width ≥ 900 mm, slope ≤ 1:12?
├── Tactile: Are warning domes at all hazards/stair tops?
├── Tactile: Are directional bars leading from parking to entrance?
└── FAIL if route is interrupted or any element is non-compliant
```

**Key numbers:** 1500 mm sidewalk | 900 mm curb ramp | 1:12 max curb ramp slope | 50% tactile contrast

---

### STEP 3: ENTRANCE

```
IS THE ENTRANCE ACCESSIBLE?
├── Door: Is clear width ≥ 900 mm?
├── Threshold: Is threshold ≤ 12 mm (beveled if 6–12 mm)?
├── Vestibule: Is there a level area 1800 mm deep × 1800 mm wide?
├── Hardware: Is door handle at 900 mm, lever type?
├── Kick plate: Is kick plate 300–400 mm height on major doors?
├── Vision panel: Is vision panel ≥ 100 mm wide?
├── ISA: Is ISA sign posted at accessible entrance?
└── FAIL if door is too narrow, threshold too high, or no level vestibule
```

**Key numbers:** 900 mm clear | 12 mm max threshold | 1800 × 1800 mm vestibule | 900 mm handle

---

### STEP 4: HORIZONTAL CIRCULATION (CORRIDORS & COMMON AREAS)

```
ARE CORRIDORS AND COMMON AREAS ACCESSIBLE?
├── Width: Are corridors on accessible route ≥ 1500 mm clear?
├── Turning: Are 1800 × 1800 mm turning spaces provided?
│   ├── At every corridor intersection on accessible route
│   ├── At every 12 m on straight accessible runs
│   └── At dead ends: is a turning/backing space within 3500 mm of dead end?
├── Floor surface: Is it firm, stable, slip-resistant?
├── Protrusions: Are all wall protrusions ≥ 2200 mm AFF (or cane-detectable)?
├── Service counters: Are they two-level (750 mm / 950–1100 mm)?
└── FAIL if corridors too narrow or turning spaces absent
```

**Key numbers:** 1500 mm corridor | 1800 × 1800 mm turning | 2200 mm protrusion clearance | every 12 m

---

### STEP 5: VERTICAL CIRCULATION (RAMPS, STAIRS, ELEVATORS)

```
IS VERTICAL CIRCULATION ACCESSIBLE?

[RAMPS]
├── Width: Is ramp clear width ≥ 1200 mm?
├── Slope: Is slope ≤ 1:20 (preferred) or ≤ 1:15 (acceptable)?
│   └── Absolute maximum: 1:12 (only where space constraints)
├── Landings: Are landings provided at top, bottom, and every 10.5 m?
│   └── Landing length ≥ 1800 mm?
├── Edge protection: Is raised edge ≥ 100 mm on open sides?
├── Handrails: Are dual handrails provided at 700 mm AND 900 mm?
└── Surface: Is surface slip-resistant?

[ELEVATORS]
├── Car: Is car min 1200 mm wide × 1400 mm deep? (1500 × 1400 in transport)
├── Door: Is door clear width ≥ 900 mm?
├── Lobby: Is lobby 1800 × 1800 mm clear?
├── COP: Are controls at 900 mm AFF?
├── Braille: Is Braille on BOTH jambs at 1200 mm AFF?
├── Handrails: Are handrails on 3 walls at 900 mm?
└── Visual alarm: Is visual emergency comms provided?

[STAIRS]
├── Clear width: Is accessible stair ≥ 1500 mm clear?
├── Risers: Are risers ≤ 150 mm?
├── Treads: Are treads ≥ 300 mm?
├── Nosing: Is nosing 30% luminance contrast, slip-resistant?
├── Handrails: Dual rails at 700 mm AND 900 mm above nosing?
├── Landing interval: Landing at every 3 m vertical rise (BP 344)?
├── Clear space: Is 1200 mm clear space provided before/after stair?
└── Tactile: Warning domes at top of stair, directional bars on approach?

FAIL if any element is absent or undersized
```

**Key numbers:** Ramp 1200 mm, 1:20/1:15/1:12 max, 1800 mm landings | Elevator 1200 × 1400 mm, 1800 mm lobby | Stair 1500 mm, 150 mm riser, 300 mm tread, 700/900 handrails

---

### STEP 6: TOILETS & WASHROOMS

```
ARE ACCESSIBLE TOILETS PROVIDED?
├── Count: At least **1 male + 1 female accessible cubicle per floor for every 20 or fewer gendered cubicles?**
│   └── Add 1 male + 1 female accessible cubicle for every additional 20 cubicles or fraction thereof
│   └── Where only one toilet room is provided, at least 1 unisex accessible toilet per floor
├── Cubicle: Is it at least 1800 mm wide × 1800 mm deep clear? (larger like 1800 × 2250 mm is acceptable)
├── Toilet: Seat height 450 mm?
├── Grab bars: Provided both sides, 300 mm above seat?
├── Lavatory: Rim ≤ 800 mm; knee clearance 650 mm high?
├── Mirror: Bottom edge ≤ 1000 mm AFF?
├── Shower: Threshold ≤ 12 mm? Roll-in shower 1800 × 1800 mm?
├── Emergency button: At 400–600 mm AFF?
├── Strobe alarm: ≤ 3 flashes/sec, ≥ 2 candela, white, word "FIRE"?
├── Urinal: Front lip 350–400 mm AFF?
└── FAIL if any toilet element missing or dimensioned incorrectly
```

**Key numbers:** min 1800 × 1800 mm clear cubicle (1800 × 2250 ok) | 450 mm seat | 800 mm lavatory | 400–600 mm emergency button | 1000 mm mirror

---

### STEP 7: COMMON AREAS & AMENITIES

```
ARE COMMON AREAS FULLY ACCESSIBLE?
├── Service counters: Two-level (750 mm lower / 950–1100 mm upper)?
├── Drinking fountains: Dual-height or paired? 750–900 mm spout? 1800 × 1800 mm clear?
├── ATMs: 1800 × 1800 mm clear? Controls 900–1200 mm? Braille + audio?
├── Assembly/cinema: Wheelchair spaces provided per capacity table (4–50 = 2; 51–300 = 4; 301–500 = 6; >500 = +1 per additional 100 seats)? Each ≥ 800 × 1100 mm? Route clear?
├── Priority seating: 5% of waiting area designated for PWDs?
├── Dining: 10% of tables accessible? Table height 750 mm?
├── Benches: Every ≤ 30 m on accessible routes? Seat height 450 mm?
├── Swimming pool (if applicable): Transfer wall 450 mm? Transfer space 1500 × 1500 mm?
└── Exercise equipment: One of each type with 900 × 1200 mm clear space?
```

**Key numbers:** 3% cinema WC spaces | 5% priority seating | 10% dining tables | 900 × 1200 mm WC space

---

### STEP 8: SIGNAGE & TACTILE PATHS

```
IS THE SIGNAGE AND TACTILE SYSTEM COMPLETE?
├── ISA at: entries, parking, toilets, elevators, assembly WC seating
├── Sign mounting: max 1500 mm center AFF (except dressing room = 1800 mm)
├── Projecting signs: ≥ 2200 mm clearance below
├── Braille: On both elevator door jambs, at 1200 mm AFF
├── ATM signage: max 2400 mm AFF
├── Tactile paths:
│   ├── Warning domes at all hazards (stair tops, platform edges, ramp tops)
│   ├── Directional bars along accessible routes
│   ├── 50% luminance contrast (tactile vs. floor)
│   ├── 600 mm deep strip, 300 mm setback from hazard
│   └── Connected from entrance/parking to all key areas
├── Evacuation plans: Embossed, with Braille, different textures per zone
│   └── Located near floor entries; show areas of refuge
└── FAIL if any element incomplete or contrast insufficient
```

**Key numbers:** 1500 mm sign height | 2200 mm projecting clearance | 1200 mm Braille on elevator | 50% tactile contrast | 600 mm × 300 mm setback

---

### STEP 9: LEGAL COMPLIANCE (RA 7277/9442/10754)

```
IS THE BUILDING LEGALLY COMPLIANT?
├── RA 7277 compliance:
│   └── Priority lanes at ALL service points (counters, ticket offices, banks)
│   └── Reserved seating in public transport / terminals
├── RA 9442 additions:
│   └── Anti-discriminatory policies posted
│   └── 20% discount compliance
├── RA 10754:
│   └── 20% discount on goods and services
│   └── 12% VAT exemption for PWD purchases
│   └── Discount signage visible
├── Building permits:
│   └── Building Official (local government) issued permit per PD 1096
│   └── DPWH implements IRR; LGU enforces
├── Penalties reminder:
│   └── Non-compliance: up to ₱300,000 fine + up to 6 years imprisonment
└── FAIL if priority lanes absent or mandatory service privileges not provided
```
