# PD 1096 Rule VIII Setbacks and Easements

## Source-Safe Scope

This repair limits the article to checked local raw sources: PD 1096 for yards, courts, site occupancy, line-and-grade review, and parking/open-space coordination, plus PD 1067 for water-edge public-use easements. It removes derivative web citations, media blocks, and unverified computed examples.

## What The Building Official Checks

During building permit processing, the line-and-grade unit checks lot relation, easements, setbacks, grades, road lots, property lines, streets or highways, road widening, utilities, and waterways. This makes setbacks a permit-review gate, not a decorative planning preference.

## Yard And Setback

PD 1096 defines a yard as the required open space between the outermost face of the building or structure and the property lines. The width of that yard is the setback. Courts, yards, and light wells are measured clear of projections, except for limited items such as roof leaders, wall copings, sills, or steel fire escapes not exceeding 1.20 meters in width.

## Site Occupancy And Open Space

Percentage of Site Occupancy is measured at ground level and excludes courts, yards, and light wells. For a lot with an existing structure, the existing building footprint is included in the PSO computation for the total lot area. If the maximum PSO conflicts with other light and ventilation provisions, the smaller compliant footprint or ground-floor gross floor area prevails.

## Courts And Incremental Setbacks

The minimum horizontal dimension of courts and yards is not less than 2.00 meters. For buildings more than two storeys high, the minimum rear or side court width increases by 300 millimeters for each additional storey up to the fourteenth storey. For buildings above fourteen storeys, the court width is computed using fourteen storeys.

## Water Code Easement

PD 1067 Article 51 places the banks of rivers and streams and the shores of seas and lakes under public-use easements along their margins: three meters in urban areas, twenty meters in agricultural areas, and forty meters in forest areas. Structures of any kind are not allowed in that public-use zone.
