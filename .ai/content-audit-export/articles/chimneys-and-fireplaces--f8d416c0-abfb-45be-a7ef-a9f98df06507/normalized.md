# Chimneys and Fireplaces

Chimneys and fireplaces in the LEA should be studied from PD 1096 2004 Revised IRR, Rule XII, Section 1211. The exam risk is not the idea of a chimney; it is mixing the dimensions for one chimney class with another.

## What the Code Separates

Section 1211 treats these as separate checks:

- Residential chimneys: minimum walls of 100 mm solid masonry or 300 mm rubble stone.
- Low-heat appliances: minimum chimney walls of 200 mm firebrick, 200 mm solid masonry, or 300 mm rubble stone.
- Medium-heat appliances: minimum walls of 200 mm solid masonry lined with not less than 100 mm firebrick.
- High-heat appliances: two 200 mm solid masonry walls separated by a 50 mm air space, with the inside wall lined with 100 mm firebrick.
- Apartment-house, commercial, and industrial incinerator chimneys: separate thresholds for grate area, wall thickness, and firebrick lining.

## Line, Height, and Clearance Checks

A chimney in a wood-framed building must be anchored at the ceiling line and at each floor line that is more than 1.80 m above grade. Chimneys must extend at least 600 mm above the roof and 600 mm above the highest part of the building within 3.00 m.

Corbelling is tightly limited: not more than 150 mm from the wall, not more than 25 mm for each course, and not from a wall less than 300 mm thick unless projected equally on each side. The code also bars a change in size or shape within 150 mm above or below roof joists or rafters.

Clearance from combustible material is a separate life-safety check. The minimum is 50 mm when the chimney is within a building or structure, and 25 mm when it is outside a building or structure.

## Fireplace Construction

Fireplace and barbecue walls, smoke chambers, and fireplace chimneys are required to be solid masonry or reinforced concrete. Firebox walls are 250 mm minimum, or 200 mm where lined with firebrick. Firebox depth must be at least 500 mm. Firebrick joints in the firebox are limited to 10 mm.

A fireplace hearth slab must be reinforced concrete at least 100 mm thick. It must extend at least 300 mm beyond each side of the fireplace opening and at least 450 mm in front of the fireplace opening. Dampers must be not less than No. 12 gauge metal and must open to at least 90 percent of the required flue area.

## LEA Takeaway

Do not answer chimney items from a generic memory of masonry. First identify the appliance or use case, then apply the corresponding wall, lining, clearance, height, and fireplace-specific checks from Section 1211.
