## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal

Select appropriate lighting systems by lamp type (LED, fluorescent, halogen, induction, HID) based on luminous efficacy, CRI, color temperature, life expectancy, dimming capability, and application in different interior and exterior building spaces.

<ExamTip>
LEA lighting questions typically ask you to match lamp type to application. Remember: LED = high efficacy + long life + dimmable; fluorescent = moderate efficacy + limited dimming; halogen = warm light + short life; HID = high output + poor CRI + slow restrike for exterior/industrial.
</ExamTip>

<Tip>
For exam recall, group lamp types by CRI and color temperature: LEDs and halogen offer CRI > 80 and warm 2700–3000 K for residential; fluorescent and induction are cooler (4000–5000 K) for offices; HID is lowest CRI and used where color fidelity is less critical.
</Tip>

<Warn>
Not all LEDs are dimmable. The LEA may test whether you verify dimming compatibility at specification stage. Always check driver/driverless design and confirm dimming protocol (0–10 V, DALI, or phase-cut) matches the control system.
</Warn>
