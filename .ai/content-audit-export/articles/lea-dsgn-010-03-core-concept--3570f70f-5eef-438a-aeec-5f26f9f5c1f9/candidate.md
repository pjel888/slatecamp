# Building Parking Design

## Source Boundary

This article is limited to PD 1096 Rule VII parking design controls and BP 344 2024 accessible parking controls. It uses only local raw PDF source families for code claims.

## Ordinary Parking Geometry

PD 1096 gives the design baseline for ordinary parking slots. Perpendicular or diagonal car slots use 2.50 m by 5.00 m. Parallel car slots use 2.15 m by 6.00 m. Standard truck or bus slots use at least 3.60 m by 12.00 m. Jeepney or shuttle parking/loading/unloading slots use at least 3.00 m by 9.00 m.

The number of slots must be drawn to scale and shown on the plans, with attendant-managed status stated where applicable. Parking and loading requirements are minimum off-street, on-site, and outside the road right-of-way. Direct access of parking, loading, utility slots, and terminals to the road right-of-way is generally disallowed to prevent using the RROW as a maneuvering area.

## Accessible Parking Geometry

BP 344 2024 adds the accessible parking overlay. Accessible parking slots are for vehicles driven by persons with disabilities or with passengers with disabilities. The required number is based on the total parking count in Table A.6.1. Each accessible slot must allow transfer from vehicle to wheelchair and must be nearest to accessible main entrances.

An accessible parking slot must be at least 4000 mm wide and 5000 mm long. A walkway at least 1200 mm clear is provided between the front ends of parked cars. Accessible parking slots must have a firm, level surface and must never be located in ramped or sloping areas. Pole-mounted signage is 600 mm by 600 mm and mounted at a minimum clear height of 2200 mm from the parking floor.

## Exam Use

For LEA review, do not mix ordinary parking geometry with accessible parking geometry. Ordinary slots answer PD 1096 layout questions; accessible slots answer BP 344 access questions. A complete parking answer checks both when the building is public-use or otherwise requires accessible parking.
