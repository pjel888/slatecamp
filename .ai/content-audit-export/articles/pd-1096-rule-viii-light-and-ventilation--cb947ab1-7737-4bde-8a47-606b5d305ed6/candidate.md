# PD 1096 Rule VIII: Light and Ventilation

## LEA Point

Rule VIII is a minimum-health rule. It controls open spaces, courts, yards, ceiling heights, room sizes, air space, windows, vent shafts, skylights, and artificial ventilation.

For board questions, separate the issue:

- Ceiling height: Section 805.
- Room area and least dimension: Section 806.
- Air space per person: Section 807.
- Window openings: Section 808.
- Vent shafts: Section 809.
- Ventilation skylights: Section 810.
- Artificial ventilation: Section 811.

## Ceiling Heights

Section 805 gives these minimums:

- Habitable rooms with artificial ventilation: at least 2.40 m.
- Rooms with natural ventilation: at least 2.70 m.
- Buildings with more than one storey: first storey at least 2.70 m, second storey at least 2.40 m, succeeding storeys with unobstructed typical headroom of at least 2.10 m.
- Mezzanine floors: clear ceiling height of at least 1.80 m above and below the mezzanine.

The mezzanine trap is checking only one side. The rule requires both clearances.

## Room Sizes

Section 806 gives the minimum room area and least horizontal dimension:

- Room for human habitation: 6.00 sq.m and 2.00 m least dimension.
- Kitchen: 3.00 sq.m and 1.50 m least dimension.
- Bath and toilet: 1.20 sq.m and 900 mm least dimension.

An area can pass while the least dimension fails.

## Air Space

Section 807 sets the minimum air-space requirements:

- School rooms: 3.00 cu.m plus 1.00 sq.m of floor area per person.
- Workshops, factories, and offices: 12.00 cu.m per person.
- Habitable rooms: 14.00 cu.m per person.

For occupancy calculations, compute volume first when the question gives floor area and ceiling height.

## Window Openings

Section 808 sets the natural-window rule for rooms not provided with artificial ventilation:

- Total free area of window openings must be at least 10% of the floor area of the room.
- The opening must not be less than 1.00 sq.m.
- Toilet and bath rooms, laundry rooms, and similar rooms have a separate rule: window area must be at least 1/20 of floor area and not less than 240 sq.mm.
- Required windows must open directly to a court, yard, public street or alley, or open watercourse.

Required windows may open into a roofed porch only when the porch abuts a court, yard, public street or alley, open watercourse, or other public open space; has a ceiling height of at least 2.70 m; and has one longer side at least 65% open and unobstructed.

Eaves, canopies, awnings, or media agua over required windows must be at least 750 mm from side and rear property lines.

No openings are allowed on, at, within, or through abutments such as firewalls along property lines, except permitted vent wells.

## Vent Shafts and Skylights

Section 809 requires a vent shaft horizontal cross-sectional area of at least 1.00 sq.m for every meter of shaft height, but never less than 1.00 sq.m. The least dimension must not be less than 600 mm.

If a vent shaft is not open to the outer air at the top for its full area, it must be covered by a skylight with net free area or fixed louver openings equal to the maximum required shaft area.

Section 810 allows ventilation skylights to replace windows only when their glass area is at least the area required for the replaced windows. Movable sashes or louvers must provide aggregate net free area at least equal to the openable parts of the replaced window, unless artificial ventilation of equivalent effectiveness is provided.

## Artificial Ventilation

Section 811 requires artificial ventilation for rooms or spaces housing industrial or heating equipment to prevent excessive hot or polluted air. When artificial ventilation is required, use Table VIII.4 for the applicable air-change requirements.

## Exam Workflow

1. Identify whether the question asks for height, room size, air space, window area, vent shaft, skylight, or artificial ventilation.
2. Use the matching section. Do not combine unrelated minimums.
3. For natural windows, compute the percentage and then apply the absolute minimum.
4. For toilet, bath, laundry, and similar rooms, use the separate 1/20 rule.
5. Check where the window opens; free area alone is not enough.

## Board-Style Memory

Ceilings are Section 805. Room dimensions are Section 806. Air volume is Section 807. Windows are Section 808. Shafts are Section 809. Skylights are Section 810. Artificial ventilation is Section 811.
