## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal
Classify barangay halls with dual-occupancy (Group D administrative + Group E/C assembly), apply space programming per population thresholds, calculate occupant load for mixed-use egress, and verify accessibility compliance.

Barangay halls and small government buildings serve multiple administrative and civic functions. They require understanding of dual-occupancy classification (Group D for administrative/institutional functions, Group E/C if assembly function dominates) and space programming tied to population thresholds.

Barangay halls in the Philippines operate as mixed-use facilities typically classified as **Group D (Institutional)** for administrative functions, though large assembly components may trigger **Group H (Assembly)** classification. The occupancy classification depends on the primary use and the proportion of assembly space.

### Space Programming Standards

- **Barangay Captain's Office:** Minimum ~9 square meters per LGU guidelines.
- **SK Office:** Minimum ~10 square meters.
- **General Records:** ~8–12 square meters following general office standards.
- **Multi-Purpose Hall:** ~100 square meters; may be classified separately as Group H if occupant load exceeds office density.
- **Public Counter:** Sized at **2.0 meters linear length per 1,000 barangay population** to ensure adequate queuing.

### Zoning and Parking

The **GI (Government Institutional)** zone requires parking per NBC Table VII.5.1.

- **GI Zone PSO:** Typically 50%, recognizing that small government buildings operate at lower traffic intensity than commercial centers.
- **Standard Parking Stall:** 2.5 m wide × 5.0 m long.
- **Accessible Parking Stall:** 3.6 m × 5.0 m with an additional 1.2 m aisle per the **BP 344 2024 Revised IRR**, placed nearest the main entrance.

### Fire & Accessibility Details

- **Exit Width:** Calculated at **5.5 mm per occupant** for non-sprinklered level components per **RA 9514 2019 IRR Table 10.2.5.1.1**.
- **Travel Distance:** 22 m unsprinklered or 44 m sprinklered for Group D institutional occupancies.
- **Dual Exits:** Two separate exits are required once the occupancy of a group or room reaches **50+ persons**.
- **Ramp Landing:** Ramps must not exceed a 3 m continuous rise without providing a landing.

## Philippine Practice Context

Barangay halls are the civic infrastructure linking national and local government to the grassroots level. They house the Barangay Captain, SK officials, and administrative personnel while providing essential community assembly space. The **2.0-meter public counter per 1,000 population** reflects Philippine transaction culture and queuing patterns. While **RA 7160 (Local Government Code)** defines the governance structure, the **NBC** and **BP 344** provide the architectural and accessibility standards.

## How It's Tested

**Pattern 1: Occupancy Classification**

\
"A barangay hall with a 120-sqm multi-purpose hall plus administrative offices is classified as Group E or H depending on the dominant occupant load." 

**Pattern 2: Population-Scaled Sizing**
"A barangay serving 8,000 persons requires a public counter with a minimum length of **16 meters** (2.0m × 8,000 / 1,000)." 

**Pattern 3: GI Zone Parking**
"A barangay hall site has a total land area of 1,200 sqm. If zoned as GI with 50% PSO, the building footprint/parking area must align with the 50% threshold." 

> **Memory Rule:** Captain = 9 sqm; SK = 10 sqm. Counter = 2.0m per 1k people. Exit width = 5.5 mm/person. Ramp = 1:20 (short) or 1:12 (long).

## Common Mistakes

- **Misclassifying Occupancy:** Purely classifying as Group C when administrative offices (Group E) dominate the occupant load.
- **Under-sizing Counters:** Calculating the public counter at 1.0 m instead of **2.0 m per 1,000 population**.
- **Parking Ratios:** Applying a 100% parking ratio to a GI zone instead of the **50% PSO**.
- **Ramp Slopes:** Assuming a single 1:12 standard instead of distinguishing **1:20 max (&lt;3m rise)** for short ramps per the **2024 Revised IRR**.
- **Exit Width Confusion:** Using 7.6 mm/person (assembly) for level office components instead of **5.5 mm/person**.
