# Space Programming Process Deliverables and Use in Design

## Source-Audited Core

SPP 201 treats architectural programming as a pre-design service. In the checked source, programming is an analytical, problem-seeking process that identifies horizontal and vertical requirements and produces a space program with the ambiance and cost range needed by the client.

Space planning is the related step that determines the size and configuration of spaces for a stated purpose. The source-supported methods are practical information-gathering tools: interviews, consultations, focus group discussions, surveys, space audits, and analysis.

Space management studies go beyond room counting. They analyze space requirements, organizational structure, linkages, and interactions. The resulting space program becomes the basis for the architectural plan and design.

## LEA Practice Guardrails

For the LEA, keep the sequence clear: programming defines and organizes requirements before detailed design decisions harden. Space planning translates those requirements into size and configuration. Space management checks the organization, relationships, and interactions that make the program usable.

Avoid treating programming as contractor selection, bidding, or construction administration. The checked SPP 201 source supports programming, space planning, and space management as pre-design services used to prepare the design brief and design basis.
