# Specialized Architectural Services Scope and Compensation

## Learning Goal

Use SPP Document 203 to identify Specialized Architectural Services and keep them distinct from Regular Design Services under SPP 202.

## Source Anchor

SPP Document 203 is the source anchor for Specialized Architectural Services. These services deal with specific expertise that further enhances the architectural interior and exterior components of a project.

The source list includes Architectural Interiors, Acoustic Design, Architectural Lighting Layout and Design, Site Development Planning, Site and Physical Planning Services, Comprehensive Development Planning, Historic and Cultural Heritage Conservation and Planning, Security Evaluation and Planning, Building Systems Design, Facilities Maintenance Support, Building Testing and Commissioning, Building Environmental Certification, Forensic Architecture, Building Appraisal, Structural Conceptualization, Preliminary Services, Contract Documentation and Review, Post-Design Services, Dispute Avoidance and Resolution, Architectural Research Methods, Special Building or Facility Planning and Design, Building Components, and Management of Architectural Practices.

## Architectural Interiors

Architectural Interiors involves detailed planning and design of indoor or enclosed areas of a proposed building or structure, including retrofit, renovation, rehabilitation, or expansion work. The checked source says it covers architectural and utility aspects, including architectural layouting of building engineering systems.

## Acoustic and Lighting Services

Acoustic Design Services involve detailed planning and design to control sound transmission for compatibility with the architectural design concept. Architectural Lighting Layout and Design involves detailed planning and design of light transmission, timing, and control for compatibility with the architectural design concept.

## Compensation Cue

For Architectural Interiors, the source states that the architect's fee may be a percentage of the cost of the AI work for projects involving extensive detailing of AI components, and that engineering or specialist consultant fees are excluded. For acoustic and lighting services, the source ties the architect's fee to the complexity of the work and states that separately hired specialist consultants are for the owner or client's account.

## Exam-Safe Rule

Name the specialized service, define its boundary, and keep compensation separate from Regular Design Services unless the agreement clearly includes it. Specialized work is not assumed free just because the same architect also performs regular design services.

## Real World Scenario

A theater client asks the architect of record to handle interior fit-out, acoustic treatment, and lighting layout. A source-safe answer treats these as Specialized Architectural Services, not automatically as ordinary SPP 202 regular design work.

Architectural Interiors covers detailed indoor planning and design. Acoustic Design focuses on sound transmission and compatibility with the architectural concept. Architectural Lighting Layout and Design focuses on light transmission, timing, and control. If separate specialist consultants are hired, their fees are not folded into the architect's compensation by default.
