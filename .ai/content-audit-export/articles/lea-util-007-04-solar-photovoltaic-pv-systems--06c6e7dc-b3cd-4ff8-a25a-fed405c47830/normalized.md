## What You Need to Know for the LEA

Solar photovoltaic systems in building work sit at the intersection of renewable-energy policy, energy-efficiency planning, and electrical-code coordination. The source-safe exam approach is to identify the PV system type, its circuits, its inverter relationship, and whether it operates with another power source or as a stand-alone supply.

## Policy Context

RA 11285 promotes the efficient use of energy resources and the development and utilization of renewable energy technologies and systems. It defines an energy audit as the evaluation of energy consumption and current energy cost to determine appropriate intervention measures and efficiency projects.

For buildings, RA 11285 requires energy-efficiency and conservation measures in new and existing commercial and industrial buildings through building permit issuance. It also allows programs that promote energy efficiency, conservation, and sufficiency to include installation of renewable energy technologies.

## PEC Article 6.90 Scope

The Philippine Electrical Code Article 6.90 applies to solar photovoltaic electrical energy systems, including array circuits, inverters, controllers, and related system components. Covered systems may be interactive with another electrical power production source or stand alone. They may operate with or without batteries, and they may provide ac or dc output for utilization.

Important Article 6.90 definitions:

- Solar cell: the basic photovoltaic device that generates electricity when exposed to light.
- Module: a protected unit of solar cells and other components that generates dc power.
- Panel: modules mechanically fastened together as a field-installable unit.
- Array: an assembly of modules or panels with support structure and related components.
- Inverter: equipment that changes voltage level or waveform, commonly changing dc input to ac output.
- Interactive system: a PV system operating in parallel with an external electrical production and distribution network.
- Stand-alone system: a PV system that supplies power independently of that network.
- Hybrid system: a system made of multiple power sources, such as PV with wind, micro-hydro, or engine-driven generators.

## Installation Coordination

Article 6.90 permits a PV system to supply a building or structure in addition to another electricity supply system. It also requires care in circuit separation: PV source circuits and PV output circuits must not occupy the same raceway, cable tray, cable, outlet box, junction box, or similar fitting as feeders or branch circuits of other systems unless separated by a partition or connected together.

Inverters or motor generators must be identified for use in solar photovoltaic systems. Roof-mounted dc PV arrays on dwellings require dc ground-fault protection to reduce fire hazards.

## Circuit Sizing and Protection

PV system currents are treated as continuous currents. Article 6.90 calculates PV source circuit current from the sum of parallel module rated short-circuit currents multiplied by 125 percent. Photovoltaic output circuit current is based on the sum of source circuit maximum currents, while inverter output circuit current is based on the inverter continuous output current rating.

Circuit conductors and overcurrent devices are generally sized to carry not less than 125 percent of the maximum calculated currents. Overcurrent protection must consider backfeed from more than one source, including batteries or inverter sources.

## LEA Focus

When a question asks about solar PV in buildings, first classify the system:

- Interactive: operates in parallel with the utility or other production network.
- Stand-alone: supplies the building independently.
- Hybrid: combines PV with another power source.

Then identify whether the question is about array/module terminology, inverter function, raceway separation, ground-fault protection, current calculation, or energy-efficiency policy. Avoid choosing answers based on financing, payback, or net-metering numbers unless those values are provided by the item or by the checked source.
