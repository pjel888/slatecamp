# Translating PWD Rights

## Source Boundary

This article translates accessibility rights into source-backed design checks from the 2024 Revised IRR of BP 344. It uses the local raw PDF as source truth and the refined markdown only as a locator aid.

## From Right to Route

Accessibility is not a slogan in building review. It becomes a continuous route from arrival points, parking, entrances, doors, ramps, stairs, and interior circulation. BP 344 requires a ramp for changes in level unless a dropped sidewalk, curb ramp, elevator, or another mechanical device serves that change.

## Ramps and Handrails

Accessible ramps require a minimum clear width of 1200 mm and a gradient not steeper than 1:20 as the general rule. Ramps longer than 10.50 m require intermediate landings at least 1800 mm long. Level areas at the upper and lower ends of a ramp must be at least 1800 mm. Handrails are required on both sides at 700 mm and 900 mm, with 300 mm horizontal extensions at the upper and lower ends.

## Entrances, Doors, and Parking

All public entrances must be accessible, including those serving leased spaces, parking facilities, pedestrian tunnels, and elevated walkways. Entrances on a different level from site arrival grade must use accessible ramps, wheelchair lifts, or similar access mechanisms. Doors require a minimum clear width of 900 mm, and a clear level space of 1800 mm by 1800 mm before and extending beyond the door.

Accessible parking is part of the same route. BP 344 requires accessible parking slots nearest to accessible main entrances, with enough transfer space for wheelchair users. The accessible parking slot size is 4000 mm by 5000 mm, with a 1200 mm clear walkway between the front ends of parked cars. Accessible parking slots must not be placed in ramped or sloping areas.

## Exam Use

When a question says PWD access, identify the physical control: route continuity, ramp width and landings, handrail heights, door clear width, door maneuvering space, parking location, accessible parking slot size, and signage.
