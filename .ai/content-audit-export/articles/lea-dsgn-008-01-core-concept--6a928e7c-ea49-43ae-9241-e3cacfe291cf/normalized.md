# Fire Egress

## Source-Safe Scope

This repair keeps the lesson inside local raw Fire Code and PD 1096 evidence. It removes unsupported numeric examples, media blocks, and mixed foreign-code framing. The article now teaches the source-safe sequence for reading egress questions.

## Means Of Egress

The Fire Code source family defines means of egress as a continuous and unobstructed route from a point in a building, structure, or facility to a public way. The route has three parts: exit access, exit, and exit discharge. An egress answer is incomplete if it checks only the door width but ignores whether the route is continuous, unobstructed, illuminated, and arranged to remain usable during an emergency.

## Number And Separation Of Exits

Where the size, occupancy, or arrangement of a building could endanger occupants if a single means of egress were blocked by fire or smoke, at least two remote means of egress are required. Under RA 9514 RIRR, when more than one exit is required, the exits must be arranged to reduce the chance that one fire or emergency blocks more than one exit.

## Width, Capacity, And Dead Ends

The general egress rule measures clear width at the narrowest point. The RA 9514 RIRR states that means of egress width is not less than 915 millimeters except where individual occupancy divisions provide otherwise. Dead-end pockets, hallways, corridors, passageways, or courts must not exceed the limits set by the occupancy provisions; the general RA 9514 RIRR text records six meters unless a specific occupancy provision modifies the limit.

## Travel Distance

Maximum travel distance is measured along the natural path of travel from the most remote point subject to occupancy to the center of the doorway or point where the exit begins. This is a path measurement, not a straight-line shortcut through walls, counters, or furniture.

## PD 1096 Coordination

PD 1096 architectural documents include fire safety documents such as floor evacuation routes, standpipes, fire extinguishers, fire alarm, emergency lights, signs, fire exits, and fire-resistive construction details. The design review therefore starts in the building permit drawings and is then coordinated with Fire Code egress requirements.

## Source Checkpoints

- RA 9514 RIRR lines 2476-2490 and 2635-2716: egress scope, exit protection, capacity, width, number, separation, dead ends, and travel distance.
- PD 9514 lines 1599-1629 and 1766-1854: life-safety and egress principles.
- PD 1096 RIRR lines 625-631: fire safety document requirements in architectural submissions.
