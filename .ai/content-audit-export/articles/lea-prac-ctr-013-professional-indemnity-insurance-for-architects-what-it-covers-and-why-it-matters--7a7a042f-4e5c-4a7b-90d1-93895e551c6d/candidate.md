## Professional Indemnity Insurance for Architects

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to:

- Identify the local source basis for professional liability insurance, professional indemnity insurance, or equivalent bond form.
- Distinguish source-backed insurance requirements from policy mechanics not stated in the checked sources.
- Connect professional liability insurance to professional responsibility and civil liability.

## Source-Safe Requirement

The UAP Standards of Professional Practice for Professional Architectural Consulting Services state that, where applicable, it is imperative for a Professional Consulting Architect to secure a Professional Liability Insurance Policy, professional indemnity insurance, or equivalent in bond form. The coverage or bond must be commensurate with the magnitude and scope of project involvement and the corresponding compensation, and the cost forms part of the total project cost chargeable to the client.

RA 9266 IRR Section 39 separately requires foreign nationals, including former Filipinos allowed to practice under a temporary or special permit, to secure local professional liability insurance, malpractice insurance, or an acceptable equivalent in bond form commensurate with the nature and magnitude of project involvement and compensation.

## Civil Liability Context

Civil Code Article 1723 remains a separate liability rule. It addresses damages when a building collapses within fifteen years from completion due to defects in plans, specifications, ground, construction, inferior materials, or contract violations. Insurance or bond requirements should not be treated as erasing that legal responsibility.

## Exam Boundary

This source-audited article does not teach commercial policy mechanics such as claims-made coverage, deductibles, per-occurrence limits, or insurer defense control because those details are not established by the checked local sources for this batch. If a question asks those mechanics, it needs its own named source.
