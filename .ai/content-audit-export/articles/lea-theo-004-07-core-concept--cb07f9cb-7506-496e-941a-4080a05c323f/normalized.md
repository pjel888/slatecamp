# Vegetation, Microclimate, and Biophilic Design

For this local source audit, biophilic design is kept to the source-supported climate role of vegetation. TOA 4 shows vegetation as a way to improve tropical microclimate: reduce hard paving, absorb radiation, provide shade, cool air through moisture loss, and support shaded air paths into buildings.

## Source-Safe Principles

- Replace excessive paving and hard surfaces with vegetation where possible.
- Use planting to create a cooler environment around the building.
- Draw incoming air from shaded planted areas rather than heated paved surfaces.
- Arrange vegetation so it does not impede air circulation.
- Use trees and shaded outdoor areas as part of passive cooling, not as isolated decoration.

## Exam Use

If the item asks why planting matters in tropical design, choose the answer that links vegetation to shade, evaporation, cooler microclimate, and airflow. Reject answers that claim planting is only symbolic or that place vegetation where it blocks the needed breeze.

## Common Trap

Do not add wellness or ecological claims unless the item gives a source for them. In this Batch 060 scope, the checked source supports vegetation as a climate-moderating design tool.
