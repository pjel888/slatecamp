> "The Palazzo Farnese with colossal order columns, which exemplifies axial symmetry rather than radial"

and the rationale reinforces the error:

> "Palazzo Farnese demonstrates colossal order and bilateral symmetry of the facade."

**External finding:** Palazzo Farnese does **not** demonstrate colossal/giant order. Its facade uses **superimposed orders** — distinct orders on each floor (Tuscan/Doric, Ionic, Corinthian), inspired by the Theatre of Marcellus. The colossal/giant order (single order spanning multiple stories) is associated with other Renaissance works (e.g., Michelangelo's Laurentian Library, Palladio's Palazzo Valmarana), not Palazzo Farnese.
