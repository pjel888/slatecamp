# Axis is the most elementary means of organizing

## Source-Audited Scope

Batch 055 treats axis as a design-ordering concept only within the checked Theory of Architecture source families. The checked reviewer identifies axis as the most elementary means of organizing forms and spaces in architecture.

## Board Exam Quick Check

For LEA theory questions, choose axis when the stem asks for the elementary means of organizing forms and spaces. Do not answer scale, balance, or character unless the question asks about size relationship, equilibrium, or expressive quality.

## Core Concepts

- Axis is the source-backed answer for the most elementary means of organizing forms and spaces.
- Form and mass can carry a vertical or horizontal direction.
- Cylinders and cones are discussed in relation to their central or vertical axes.
- Forms can be transformed by elongating, tipping, or shifting along an axis.

## LEA Use

Axis questions usually test ordering language, not building-code compliance. If the choices include balance, scale, character, and axis, the checked reviewer source supports axis for the elementary organizing means.

## Active Recall

3. What happens when a form is changed along an axis? Its proportion, stability, or visual direction can change.
