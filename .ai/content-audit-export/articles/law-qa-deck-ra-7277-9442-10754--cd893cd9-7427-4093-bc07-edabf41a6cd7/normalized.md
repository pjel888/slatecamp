## B. Ramp and Route Checks

**Q3.** What minimum clear ramp width is supported by the 2024 BP 344 IRR?

> **A:** 1200 mm minimum clear width for accessible ramps, with slope and landing rules checked together.

**Q4.** How does the 2024 BP 344 IRR treat ramp slope?

> **A:** The default gradient is not steeper than 1:20, with steeper gradients allowed only in special cases depending on length.

**Q5.** What is the key landing rule for long ramps in the 2024 BP 344 IRR?

> **A:** Accessible ramps longer than 10.50 m require intermediate landings, and level areas are required at the upper and lower ends.

## C. Walkways and Parking

**Q6.** What minimum sidewalk/walkway width is supported by the 2024 BP 344 IRR?

> **A:** 1500 mm minimum, or 1800 mm where space is needed for two wheelchairs to pass.

**Q7.** What should a designer check before using curb ramps?

> **A:** The curb ramp must not obstruct the sidewalk/walkway or reduce the required level or turning area.

**Q8.** What accessible parking principle is reinforced by PD 1096 Appendix A?

> **A:** Accessible parking should be close to accessible entrances and wide enough for wheelchair transfer.

## D. Entrances, Signs, and Handrails

**Q9.** What entrance principle appears in PD 1096 Appendix A?

> **A:** Entrances should connect arrival and departure points to the interior lobby, and ramps should be provided where the entrance is not on the same level as site arrival grade.

**Q10.** What handrail heights are supported by the PD 1096 accessibility appendix and BP 344 source family?

> **A:** Handrails are placed at 0.70 m and 0.90 m above steps or ramps in the older PD 1096 appendix language, and the 2024 BP 344 IRR likewise uses 700 mm and 900 mm handrail heights.

## Real World Scenario

A mall renovation team asks whether its new ramp and accessible toilet are enough for compliance. The architect can answer the design side locally: verify ramp width, slope, landings, handrails, entrance level areas, walkway clearance, and parking access against BP 344 and PD 1096. Any claim about discounts, VAT, tax deductions, employment quotas, or penalties is a separate legal-benefits review and must wait for the missing RA/BIR source packet.
