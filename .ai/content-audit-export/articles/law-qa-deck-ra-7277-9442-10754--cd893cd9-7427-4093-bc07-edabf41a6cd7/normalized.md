## A. Which Law?

**Q1.** Which law is known as the **Magna Carta for Disabled Persons**?

> **A:** **RA 7277** (“Magna Carta for Persons with Disability”).[web:16][web:30]

**Q2.** Which law amends RA 7277 to add **discounts, privileges, and anti‑ridicule** provisions for PWDs?

> **A:** **RA 9442**, which amends several provisions of RA 7277 including privileges and penalties related to public ridicule and vilification.[web:24][web:27]

**Q3.** Which law further amends RA 9442 / RA 7277 to add the **12% VAT exemption** and expand benefits?

> **A:** **RA 10754**, which further amends Section 32 and related provisions of RA 7277 on discounts and VAT exemption.[web:29][web:30]

**Q4.** Which law deals with **employment quotas (1% positions for PWDs)** in government and encourages the same in large private entities?

> **A:** **RA 10524** (amending RA 7277), which provides for at least 1% of positions in government agencies for PWDs and encourages similar allocation in private corporations with more than 100 employees.[web:18][web:23]

---

## B. Discounts and VAT

**Q5.** Under RA 7277 as amended by RA 9442 and RA 10754, what two main financial benefits does a PWD get on qualifying goods and services?

> **A:** A **20% discount** and **exemption from the 12% VAT** on specified sleas of goods and services for the **exclusive use and enjoyment** of the PWD.[web:29][web:30]

**Q6.** Name at least four categories where this 20% + 12% applies under RA 10754 and its IRR.

> **A:**  
> • Medicines and medical supplies;  
> • Medical and dental services, diagnostics and laboratory fees;  
> • Domestic air, sea, and land transport fares;  
> • Hotel and similar lodging services;  
> • Restaurants and recreation centers (cinemas, amusement, etc.);  
> • Funeral and burial services;  
> • Certain educational expenses when directly for the PWD, as specified by revenue regulations.[web:18][web:19][web:29][web:30]

## C. Tax Incentives

**Q8.** What additional tax deduction can an employer claim for **wages paid to qualified PWD employees**?

> **A:** An additional **25%** of the total salaries/wages paid to qualified PWD employees, deductible from gross income **on top of** the regular salary expense deduction, subject to RA 7277 and revenue regulations.[web:17][web:19][web:23]

**Q9.** What additional deduction is available for **accessibility improvements** (ramps, elevators, accessible toilets) made beyond mandatory BP 344 compliance?

**Q10.** Which law is the primary basis for these tax incentives?

> **A:** **RA 7277**, particularly its provisions on incentives, as implemented by BIR revenue regulations (e.g., RR 8‑93), with later commentaries referencing the same framework.[web:16][web:18][web:19]

## D. Employment and Non‑Discrimination

**Q11.** What is the minimum percentage of positions in **government agencies** that must be reserved for PWDs?

> **A:** At least **1%** of all positions in national government agencies, offices, or corporations, under RA 10524 amending RA 7277.[web:18][web:23]

**Q12.** Are **private companies** with more than 100 employees mandated or merely encouraged to hire PWDs?

> **A:** They are **encouraged and incentivized**, with RA 10524 and RA 7277 promoting at least **1%** of their workforce for PWDs through policy direction and tax incentives, but the law frames it more as a policy objective than a strictly penalized quota for the private sector.[web:18][web:20][web:23]

---

## E. Penalties and Anti‑Ridicule

**Q13.** Which law explicitly criminalizes **public ridicule and vilification** of PWDs?

> **A:** **RA 9442**, which inserts specific penal provisions on public ridicule and vilification into RA 7277.[web:24][web:27]

**Q14.** After RA 9442, RA 10754, and their IRR, what is the typical penalty range for serious violations of PWD privileges (fines and imprisonment)?

> **A:** For various violations (such as refusal to honor discounts), fines can range roughly from **₱50,000 up to around ₱300,000**, with imprisonment periods from about **6 months up to 6 years**, plus possible suspension or revocation of business permits for serious or repeated offenses, depending on the specific act and number of convictions.[web:21][web:24][web:27][web:29]

**Q15.** List three acts that can trigger these penalties.

> **A:** Denying the 20% discount and/or VAT exemption without valid basis; refusing admission or service to a PWD; committing public ridicule or vilification; failing or refusing to provide express lanes or priority service when required; using fake PWD IDs to claim benefits.[web:21][web:24][web:27][web:29]

## 2. Heights by Element

### 2.1 Handrails & Guards

- **Accessible ramps and stairs (BP 344):** Handrails at **700 mm** and **900 mm** from finished surface to top of rail, continuous with closed returns, with approx **300‑mm** horizontal extensions at top and bottom.[web:25][web:28]
- **PD 1096 stairs:** General stair handrails **800–900 mm** above tread nosings, which can be coordinated with BP 344’s upper band.[web:14][web:15]
- **Guard/railings for protection:** Around **1100 mm** minimum height above floor where stairs, ramps, balconies, or landings are more than about **750 mm** above adjacent grade, in addition to the accessible handrail system.[web:14][web:25]
- **Handrail extensions (ramps/stairs):** About **300 mm** horizontal extension beyond the start and end of ramps and stairs, returning to wall/post without projecting into the path of travel.[web:25][web:28]

### 2.2 Elevators & Controls

- **Car Operating Panel (COP):** Centerline typically around **900 mm** above car floor for reach range compliance.[web:22][web:25]
- **Braille floor indicators on door jambs:** Around **1200 mm** above finished floor on both jambs, within typical tactile reach range.[web:22][web:25]
- **Elevator handrails:** Approximately **900 mm** above car floor, aligned with the upper handrail band for consistency.[web:22]

### 2.3 Washrooms & Accessories

- **Toilet seat height:** Approximately **450 mm** above finished floor (AFF), following common BP 344 modules.[web:22][web:28]
- **Grab bars:** Generally located about **300 mm above the seat top** (so around **750 mm AFF** for a 450‑mm seat), with side and rear bars sized per BP 344 diagrams.[web:22]
- **Lavatory rim:** Maximum around **800 mm AFF**, with clear knee space roughly **650 mm high** and **500 mm deep** underneath.[web:22]
- **Mirror bottom edge:** Maximum around **1000 mm AFF**, or use tilting mirrors to achieve visibility from seated positions.[web:22]
- **Accessories (soap, towels, shelves, hooks):** Often kept at or below **1000 mm AFF** to be reachable from a seated position.[web:22]
- **Emergency call button in toilet:** Located between about **400 mm and 600 mm AFF**, easily reachable from the floor and from the WC, with contrasting color and clear pictogram.[web:22][web:28]

### 2.4 Tactile & Signage

- **Wall signage (tactile/Braille):** Maximum centerline around **1500 mm** above finished floor for seated reach, with adequate luminance contrast (often ≥30%).[web:22][web:28]
- **Tactile warning setback from hazards (stairs, ramps, escalators, control panels, information maps):** Tactile blocks are commonly placed starting about **300 mm** before the hazard edge, with a tactile field depth of around **600 mm**, based on updated BP 344 guidance and reviewers.[web:22][web:28]

### 2.5 Parking Headroom

- **Accessible parking vertical clearance:** Recent BP 344 IRR reviewer materials and exam notes indicate about **2300 mm** clear height as the new standard target above accessible parking slots, suitable for most wheelchair vans and high‑roof vehicles.[web:22][web:25]

## Exam Tip

LEA Trap: Three laws with different privileges: RA 7277 = original Magna Carta (accessibility mandates), RA 9442 = added 20% discount/VAT exemption, RA 10754 = expanded to all PWD (removed the 70% disability threshold). The VA T exemption is ON TOP of the 20% discount — not a replacement. Total savings = 20% discount + VAT on the discounted amount.
