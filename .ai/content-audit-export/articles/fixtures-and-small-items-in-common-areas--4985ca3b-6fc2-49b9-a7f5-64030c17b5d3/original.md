# Fixtures and Small Items in Common Areas
### BP 344 (2024 IRR) — Philippine Licensure Examination for Architects Review

---

## REAL-WORLD HOOK

You are thirsty after a long trip through a busy transport terminal. The drinking fountain is there — but the spout is at 1,100 mm, and there is no knee clearance underneath. A man on crutches tries to reach the telephone bank, but the coin slot is at 1,350 mm. A woman in a wheelchair wants to check her bank balance at the ATM, but the keypad is at 1,400 mm and there is no audio output. Every "small" fixture is a potential wall. This module is about the measurements that keep these everyday items *actually* usable.

## SECTION 1: DRINKING FOUNTAINS

### Legal Basis: BP 344 (2024 IRR) Section 18.3

### Complete Specification Table

| Element | Dimension | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Spout height** | **750–900 mm** AFF | Both wheelchair and standing users |
| **Spout extension** | Min **50 mm** from front edge | So wheelchair user can reach without bumping into unit |
| **Dual access requirement** | One wheelchair-accessible + one standing-height unit **OR** high-low combo unit | Cannot have only one height |
| **Clear floor space** | **1,800 × 1,800 mm** | In front of the fixture |
| **Knee clearance — width** | **750 mm** wide | For wheelchair approach |
| **Knee clearance — depth** | **200 mm** deep | Measured from front edge |
| **Knee clearance — height** | **650 mm** high | Minimum clearance under unit |
| **Toe clearance — width** | **750 mm** wide | Consistent with knee clearance |
| **Toe clearance — depth** | **200 mm** deep | Below the knee clearance zone |
| **Toe clearance — height** | **200 mm** high | From floor to underside |
| **Controls** | One-hand / closed-fist operable | No tight grasping, no pinching |
| **Bottle filler spout** | **100 mm** above fill platform | When combined bottle filler provided |

### Spatial Sketch — Knee & Toe Clearance

```
Front Edge
    ↓
 ___________
|           |
|  UNIT     |  ← 750–900 mm AFF spout
|___________|
|←200mm dep→|← Knee zone: 650 mm high, 750 mm wide, 200 mm deep
|           |
|___________| ← 200 mm high toe zone: 750 mm wide, 200 mm deep
    Floor
```

> **Exam distinction:** Knee clearance and toe clearance share the same width (750 mm) and depth (200 mm) — but their **heights are different**: knee = 650 mm, toe = 200 mm.

---

## > TRAP LEART #1 — Dual Access is Mandatory

**Question stem:** *"A building installs one drinking fountain with its spout at 900 mm AFF. Is this compliant?"*

**Correct answer:** **No.** A single unit at 900 mm serves wheelchair users but not standing users effectively for ergonomic drinking. BP 344 requires **dual access** — either two separate units (one wheelchair-height, one standing) **or** a combination high-low unit. A single-height fountain alone does not satisfy dual access.

---

## > TRAP LEART #2 — Spout Extension vs. Height

Exam questions sometimes isolate one dimension. Remember: even if the height is correct (750–900 mm), the spout must **extend at least 50 mm** from the front edge. A recessed spout that sits flush with the housing is non-compliant even at the right height.

## SECTION 2: PUBLIC TELEPHONES

### Legal Basis: BP 344 (2024 IRR) Section 11

### Complete Specification Table

| Element | Dimension/Requirement | IRR Section |
|---------|----------------------|-------------|
| **Controls max height** | **1,200 mm** AFF | Sec. 11.3 |
| **Coin slots / receivers max height** | **1,200 mm** AFF | Sec. 11.3 |
| **Illumination** | **200 lux** minimum | Sec. 11.2 |
| **Clear floor space** | **1,800 × 1,800 mm** | Sec. 11.4 |
| **Volume control** | Required | Sec. 11.1 |
| **Accessible phones per floor** | **1 per floor** | Sec. 11.5 |
| **Accessible phones per group** | **1 per group of 2 or more** phones | Sec. 11.5 |
| **Hearing aid compatibility** | Required | Sec. 11.1 |

### "1 Per Group" Rule Explained

```
Bank of 5 phones
[ Phone 1 ] [ Phone 2 ] [ Phone 3 ] [ Phone 4 ] [ Phone 5 ]
     ↑
Accessible phone required:
- 1 per floor (this bank counts as the floor requirement)
- Plus 1 per group of 2+
  → This bank of 5 = 1 group of 5
  → Requires 1 accessible phone within this group
```

If a floor has two separate banks of phones (e.g., near entrance and near elevator), **each bank of 2+ requires at least 1 accessible phone**.

---

## > TRAP LEART #3 — 200 Lux for Phones, Not General

**Question stem:** *"What illumination level does BP 344 require for accessible public telephone areas?"*

**Correct answer:** **200 lux** (Sec. 11.2). Do not confuse with general corridor lighting (which has different standards under PD 1096 or OSHS). The 200 lux standard for phones is specifically because low-vision users need to read instructions, dial, and check displays.

---

## > TRAP LEART #4 — Controls at 1,200 mm is a MAXIMUM

The 1,200 mm for telephone controls is a **maximum**, not a target. A phone with controls at 1,050 mm is compliant. A phone with controls at 1,250 mm is non-compliant. On exam questions, "maximum" and "minimum" language is the trap.

|---------|-----------|---------------|
| **Maximum spacing interval** | **30 m** along circulation routes | Sec. 18.1.c |
| **Seat depth** | **500–600 mm** | Sec. 3.2.b |
| **Seat length (min)** | **1,200 mm** | Sec. 3.2.b |
| **Seat height** | **450 mm** AFF | Sec. 3.2.b |
| **Back support height** | **1,200 mm** minimum | Sec. 3.2.b |
| **Back support start** | **50 mm above seat surface** | Sec. 3.2.b |
| **Back support top** | **450 mm above seat surface** | Sec. 3.2.b |
| **Load capacity** | Must withstand **113 kg** | Sec. 3.2.c |
| **Adjacent clear space** | Space for wheelchair alongside | Sec. 18.1 |

### Back Support Geometry — Visualized

```
Top of back support: 450 mm above seat
            ↑
            |  ← Back support material (1,200 mm long)
            |
Start of back support: 50 mm above seat
            ↑
Seat surface ─────────────────────── 450 mm AFF
```

> The back support is **not** flush with the seat. There is a 50 mm gap at the bottom — this is intentional for persons with mobility impairments to transfer more easily.

---

## > TRAP LEART #5 — Back Support Length vs. Seat Length

Both the seat **and** the back support have a **1,200 mm** minimum length. The back support runs the same length as the seat. Exam questions sometimes present a bench with a 1,200 mm seat and a 900 mm back support and ask if this is compliant. **Answer: No.** Both must be 1,200 mm minimum.

---

## > TRAP LEART #6 — 30 m Interval is a MAXIMUM

Benches must appear **at least** every 30 m along accessible circulation routes. Fewer benches spaced farther than 30 m apart is non-compliant. More frequent benches (every 15 m, for example) is compliant.

## > TRAP LEART #7 — ATM Controls: Range, Not a Single Number

ATM controls must fall **within** 900–1,200 mm AFF — this is a **range** with both a floor and a ceiling. A keypad at 850 mm is too low; a keypad at 1,250 mm is too high. Both are non-compliant. The entire usable control surface must be within 900–1,200 mm.

## SECTION 6: COAT HOOKS

### Legal Basis: BP 344 (2024 IRR) — General Provisions

| Element | Dimension |
|---------|-----------|
| **Maximum height** | **1,200 mm** AFF |
| **Maximum projection from wall** | **40 mm** |

> **Why 40 mm max projection?** Objects projecting more than ~100 mm from a wall at heights between 680 mm and 2,000 mm are detected by a white cane user only if the cane hits the wall below the projection. A 40 mm projection is shallow enough to reduce injury risk while still being functional. Contrast with projecting signs, which require **2,200 mm** clearance below them.

## > TRAP LEART #8 — Three Fixtures All Have 1,800 × 1,800 mm Clear Space

Drinking fountains, public phones, and ATMs all share the **1,800 × 1,800 mm** clear floor requirement. Examiners love to swap the numbers — e.g., showing 1,500 × 1,800 mm and asking if it complies. **Answer: No** — all three require the full square 1,800 × 1,800 mm.

---

## > TRAP LEART #9 — Bottle Filler Spout Height

When a drinking fountain includes a bottle-filling station, the bottle filler spout is set at **100 mm above the fill platform** — not at the 750–900 mm drinking spout height. These are two separate elements.

## REASON FOR THE LAW

| Rule | Why It Exists |
|------|--------------|
| **Fountain dual access** | PWD users have vastly different standing heights and reach ranges. A single height excludes one group |
| **Spout extends 50 mm** | A wheelchair user's footrests prevent them from getting close to a flush-mounted spout |
| **1,800 × 1,800 mm clear for fountains/phones/ATMs** | The "turning radius" of a standard manual wheelchair is approximately 1,500–1,800 mm. The 1,800 mm square allows both approach and turning |
| **Bench every 30 m** | Fatigue is a real disability trigger. Persons with cardiac conditions, COPD, arthritis, or limb disabilities cannot walk indefinitely. 30 m is the threshold distance from studies on accessible pedestrian networks |
| **Bench seat 450 mm** | This matches a wheelchair seat height, making lateral transfers easier. Too low (e.g., 300 mm) = hard to stand up. Too high = feet dangle |
| **113 kg bench load** | Accounts for a large-framed user plus assistive device. Standard park benches are often rated lower |
| **ATM audio + Braille** | Blind users cannot operate visual-only ATMs. Audio output + tactile keypad = independence without assistance |
| **Coat hooks max 40 mm projection** | Projecting objects at waist/chest height are major injury hazards for persons with visual impairments |

## ACTIVE RECALL DRILL

**Q1.** What is the minimum spout height for a wheelchair-accessible drinking fountain?
> **A1.** 750 mm AFF (range: 750–900 mm).

**Q2.** How far must a drinking fountain spout extend from the front edge of the unit?
> **A2.** Minimum 50 mm.

**Q3.** What are the three dimensions of knee clearance under a drinking fountain?
> **A3.** 750 mm wide × 200 mm deep × 650 mm high.

**Q4.** What is the maximum height for telephone controls under BP 344?
> **A4.** 1,200 mm AFF.

**Q5.** How many accessible phones are required per floor and per group?
> **A5.** 1 per floor; 1 per group of 2 or more phones.

**Q6.** What is the seat height of an accessible bench?
> **A6.** 450 mm AFF.

**Q7.** What is the maximum interval between benches along accessible circulation routes?
> **A7.** 30 m.

**Q8.** What is the minimum bench seat length?
> **A8.** 1,200 mm.

**Q9.** What is the ATM control height range under BP 344?
> **A9.** 900–1,200 mm AFF.

**Q10.** What three output modes must ATMs provide for accessibility?
> **A10.** Braille, tactile keypad, and audio output.

**Q11.** What is the clear floor space required in front of an ATM?
> **A11.** 1,800 × 1,800 mm.

**Q12.** What is the maximum projection for a coat hook?
> **A12.** 40 mm from the wall.
