# Fixtures and Small Items in Common Areas

## Exam scope

This article focuses on common-area fixtures with explicit dimensions or service rules in the BP 344 2024 Revised IRR: public telephones, ATMs/kiosks/ticket machines, coat hooks, drinking water facilities, accessible benches, and exercise equipment clear floor spaces.

## Public telephones

Accessible public telephones must not be treated as ordinary wall fixtures. BP 344 requires volume control, a minimum illumination of 200 lux beside the telephone, and dialing controls, coin slots, receivers, and instructional signs no higher than 1200 mm from the finished floor.

Public telephones also need a clear unobstructed space of 1800 mm by 1800 mm in front of wall-mounted and free-standing units. Where public telephones are provided, accessible public phones are required one for each floor and one for every group of two or more phones.

## ATMs, kiosks, and ticket machines

ATMs require a minimum clear unobstructed space of 1800 mm by 1800 mm in front of the machine. BP 344 also requires speech output and information that can be independently used by persons with visual disabilities. The listed access modes are Braille, tactile information, and audio through speakers or earphone jacks.

Controls, buttons, bill dispensers, and card slots must be from 900 mm to 1200 mm. Where counters are provided for kiosks, ATMs, or ticket machines, BP 344 gives a 750 mm counter height and about 480 mm minimum depth under the counter to let wheelchair users approach.

## Coat hooks and dressing-area fixtures

In dressing areas, the coat hook must be installed on a side wall not more than 1200 mm from the floor and must project not more than 40 mm from the wall. For LEA purposes, do not convert this into a generic "protruding object" rule unless the question expressly asks for protruding objects. The source text here is the coat-hook provision.

## Drinking water facilities

Where drinking water facilities are provided, BP 344 requires access for wheelchair users and standing users. It calls for at least two units or a high-low combination in each secured area with drinking water facilities.

The drinking fountain height must be 750 mm to 900 mm above the floor, and the spout must extend at least 50 mm from the front edge. A bottle-filling station spout, if included, must extend at least 100 mm. The required clear floor space is at least 1800 mm by 1800 mm in front of and alongside the unit. The knee clearance is 750 mm wide, 200 mm deep, and 650 mm high; the toe clearance is at least 750 mm wide, 200 mm deep, and 200 mm high.

## Benches and exercise equipment

For accessible benches in dressing, fitting, locker, sauna, and steam-room contexts, BP 344 states a bench depth of 500 mm to 600 mm, a minimum length of 1200 mm, and a 450 mm seat height. Where the bench is not next to a wall, back support must be at least 1200 mm long and must extend from a point 50 mm maximum above the seat to a point at least 450 mm above the bench.

For exercise equipment and machines, at least one of each type must have a clear floor space of at least 900 mm by 1200 mm and must be served by an accessible route. The clear floor space must be positioned for transfer from a wheelchair or for use while seated in a wheelchair.

## Exam handling

When the item mentions a common-area fixture, identify the exact fixture first. Public telephones use the 200 lux, 1200 mm control-height, and 1800 mm by 1800 mm clear-space rules. ATMs use the 1800 mm by 1800 mm clear-space rule, 900 mm to 1200 mm control range, and accessible output modes. Drinking water facilities use the dual-access, spout-height, clear-space, knee-clearance, and toe-clearance rules.
