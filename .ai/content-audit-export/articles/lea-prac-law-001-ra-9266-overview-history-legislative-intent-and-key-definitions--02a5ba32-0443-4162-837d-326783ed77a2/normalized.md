# RA 9266 Overview: Core Legal Frame and Key Definitions

RA 9266 is the Architecture Act of 2004. Its IRR states that the Act repealed Republic Act No. 545, as amended, and provides the regulatory frame for the practice of architecture in the Philippines.

## Why This Matters for the LEA

The LEA often tests whether you can separate what the law actually says from familiar review-center shortcuts. For RA 9266, the safest anchors are the title and policy provisions, the Section 3 definitions, the Section 4 Board structure, and the Section 40 IAPOA integration rule.

## Core Statutory Frame

Section 1 gives the short title: Architecture Act of 2004. Section 2 states the policy: the State recognizes the importance of architects in nation building, promotes architectural services, and develops competent and globally competitive architects through regulatory measures, programs, and activities.

This matters because RA 9266 is not only an examination law. It governs registration, licensure, professional practice, professional organizations, and the regulatory bodies that supervise architecture.

## Key Section 3 Definitions

Section 3 defines architecture as the art, science, or profession of planning, designing, and constructing buildings in their totality, taking into account their environment and the principles of utility, strength, and beauty.

The same section distinguishes several professional roles:

- Architect: a natural person registered and licensed under RA 9266 who holds a valid Certificate of Registration and Professional Identification Card.
- Architect-of-Record: the architect directly and professionally responsible for the total design of a project and civilly liable for signed and sealed plans, specifications, and contract documents.
- Architect-in-Charge of Construction: the architect directly and professionally responsible and liable for construction supervision.
- Consulting Architect: a registered and licensed architect, or a foreign architect permitted to practice under RA 9266, with exceptional expertise or specialization.

Section 3 also defines Structural Conceptualization. It covers conceiving, choosing, and developing the type, disposition, arrangement, and proportioning of structural elements while considering safety, cost-effectiveness, functionality, and aesthetics.

## Board and IAPOA Anchors

Section 4 creates the Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture under the supervision and administrative control of the Professional Regulation Commission. It also describes the appointment process: the President appoints the chair and members from recommendees selected from nominees submitted by the Integrated and Accredited Professional Organization of Architects.

Do not use Section 41 as the nominee rule. Section 40 is the integration provision identifying the United Architects of the Philippines as the IAPOA under Board Resolution No. 3, series of 2004. Section 41 concerns the implementing rules and regulations.

## Term Rule

Section 6 gives each Board member a three-year term and allows reappointment for one full term. The first Board used staggered terms so all seats would not expire at the same time.

## Exam Guardrails

- Use Section 1 for the short title and repeal of RA 545.
- Use Section 3 for definitions and scope concepts.
- Use Section 4 for Board creation, composition, and IAPOA nominee mechanics.
- Use Section 6 for term and reappointment rules.
- Use Section 40, not Section 41, for the UAP/IAPOA integration rule.
