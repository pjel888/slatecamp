# Theater and Performance Venue Classification

## Source Scope

This local repair uses the checked PD 1096 RIRR occupancy tables, RA 9514 2019 Revised IRR assembly provisions, and the local Fire Code source family. It removes unsourced theater-planning claims that were not verified from those raw PDFs.

## LEA Anchor

Theater questions are usually assembly questions, but the exact answer depends on the condition: occupant load, stage or no stage, fixed seating, waiting spaces, exit arrangement, stage protection, alarm, sprinkler, emergency announcement, or overcrowding.

## PD 1096 Classification

PD 1096 places assembly buildings with stages and occupant load below 1000 in Group H-1. It places assembly buildings with stages and occupant load of 1000 or more in Group I-1. The checked table examples include theaters, auditoriums, concert halls, opera houses, convention halls, and similar performance venues.

## RA 9514 Assembly Rules

RA 9514 includes theaters, assembly halls, auditoriums, and similar public-gathering spaces in assembly occupancies. For theater-type seating, occupant load with fixed seats is determined by the number of fixed seats. RA 9514 requires a main exit sized for one-half of the total occupant load, additional exits sized for two-thirds of the load served by that level, exit lighting and signs, emergency lighting, and panic hardware on exit doors from assembly occupancies.

For travel distance, RA 9514 states that places of assembly are limited to 46 m to an exit where not protected by an approved supervised sprinkler system, and 61 m where protected. Stage and enclosed-platform provisions include automatic fire suppression for qualifying stages or platforms, stage ventilation, a fire resistant proscenium curtain, and a stage fire control station.

## Exam Use

Classify the venue from PD 1096 first, then apply RA 9514 to the exact feature named by the question. Do not mix stage protection, fixed-seat occupant load, aisle widths, sprinkler triggers, and general exit-count rules unless the item asks for that feature.
