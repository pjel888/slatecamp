## Subject Coverage

Eight major MEPF system areas:

- **Electrical and Power Systems** — service entrance, metering, distribution, branch circuits, load calculations, emergency power, PEC
- **Mechanical Systems (HVAC)** — psychrometrics, cooling load, air distribution, chiller plants, ASHRAE 62.1/90.1
- **Sanitary and Plumbing Systems** — water supply, sanitary drainage, DWV, fixture units, NPCP
- **Acoustics and Illumination** — sound absorption/transmission, RT60, STC, lighting levels, daylighting
- **Communication Systems** — structured cabling, PA systems, nurse call, CCTV, BAS
- **Disaster Prevention, Fire Safety and Protection** — RA 9514, sprinkler types, detectors, alarm panels, egress lighting
- **High-Tech and Intelligent Building Systems** — BAS/BMS, IoT, smart metering
- **Building Utilities Integration and Design Standards** — coordination, clash detection, equipment rooms, riser diagrams

## Study Strategy

### Phase 1 — System by System (Weeks 1–5)

Dedicate approximately one week per major system. For each, know: purpose, major components, governing Philippine code, architect's design decisions, space/clearance requirements.

## Common Exam Traps

- **Mixing up codes across systems.** PEC = electrical; NPCP = plumbing; RA 9514 = fire. Apply only the code governing the system in question.
- **Forgetting Philippine-specific requirements.** 230/400V voltage, NPCP fixture unit values, RA 9514 occupancy thresholds.
- **Underestimating acoustics and illumination.** Know illuminance levels for specific occupancies and RT60 requirements for specific room types.
- **Ignoring communication and intelligent building subtopics.** Growing in prominence; often straightforward conceptual questions.
- **Neglecting coordination principles.** Route the most constrained system first (gravity drains, large ducts) before smaller conduits.
