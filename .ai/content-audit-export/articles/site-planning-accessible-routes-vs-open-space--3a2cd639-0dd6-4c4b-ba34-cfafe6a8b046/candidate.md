# Site Planning Accessible Routes vs Open Space

## Learning Goal

Use BP 344 2024 Revised IRR requirements for sidewalks, walkways, pathways, rest stops, headroom, obstruction clearance, open spaces, tactile surfaces, and edge protection.

## Sidewalks And Walkways

Pathways, sidewalks, and walkways must be kept as level as possible and slip resistant in wet and dry conditions. Whenever possible, sidewalks and walkways must have a gradient not steeper than 1:20 or 5 percent, and a maximum cross gradient of 1:100 or 1 percent.

Sidewalks, walkways, and pathways must have a minimum width of 1500 mm. Where two wheelchair users need to pass, the minimum width is 1800 mm.

## Turning, Rest, And Obstruction Rules

In lengthy or busy pathways, sidewalks, and walkways, spaces must be provided so a wheelchair may pass another or turn around. These spaces must have a minimum clear dimension of 1800 mm and must be spaced at a maximum distance of 12000 mm between rest stops. Turnabout spaces must also be provided at or within 3500 mm of every dead-end corridor.

Sidewalk and walkway headroom must not be less than 2200 mm. Sidewalks and walkways must not be obstructed by street furniture, bollards, signposts, utility poles, or similar hazards along the defined route. Where support pillars, columns, or other barriers are present, at least 900 mm clear passage must remain.

## Open Spaces

Where open spaces are provided, persons with visual disabilities can become disoriented, so sidewalks, walkways, or paths should have defined edges such as planters with edge protection, grass verge, or similar texture changes.

Tactile floor surfaces and markings must be provided in open spaces according to the Signage and Wayfinding section of the IRR. Where a direct drop of 200 mm or more occurs along the access route, edge protection at least 100 mm high and 100 mm wide must be provided.
