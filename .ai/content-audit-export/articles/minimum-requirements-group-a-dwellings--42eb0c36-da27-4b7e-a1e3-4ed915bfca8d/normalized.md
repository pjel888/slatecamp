# Minimum Requirements Group A Dwellings

## Board Exam Quick Check

For Group A dwelling questions, first identify whether the item asks about lot occupancy, light and ventilation, sanitation, foundation, wooden posts, floor and roof loads, stairs, entrances/exits, or electrical/mechanical requirements. The controlling local source for this article is the 2004 Revised IRR of PD 1096, Rule VII Section 708, with Rule VIII Sections 805 through 808 supplying the detailed light and ventilation values.

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to apply the PD 1096 minimum requirements for Group A dwellings and connect Section 708 to the Rule VIII dimensional standards it references.

## Source-Grounded Core Rules

### Dwelling location and lot occupancy

A Group A dwelling must occupy not more than 90% of a corner lot and not more than 80% of an inside lot. It is also subject to Civil Code easements of light and view and must be at least 2.00 m from the property line.

### Light and ventilation

Every dwelling must be constructed and arranged to provide adequate light and ventilation as provided under Sections 805 through 811 of the Code. In the checked DPWH raw markdown, the detailed source lines for ceiling heights, room sizes, air space, and window openings appear in Sections 805 through 808.

Habitable rooms with artificial ventilation must have ceiling heights of at least 2.40 m. In buildings of more than one storey, the first storey must be at least 2.70 m, the second storey at least 2.40 m, and succeeding storeys must have unobstructed typical headroom of at least 2.10 m above the finished floor. Habitable rooms with natural ventilation must have ceiling heights of at least 2.70 m. Mezzanines require at least 1.80 m clear height above and below.

Rooms for human habitation must have at least 6.00 sq.m. floor area and a least dimension of 2.00 m. Kitchens must have at least 3.00 sq.m. and a least dimension of 1.50 m. Bath and toilet rooms must have at least 1.20 sq.m. and a least dimension of 900 mm.

For rooms without artificial ventilation, window free opening area must be at least 10% of the room floor area and not less than 1.00 sq.m. Toilet, bath, laundry, and similar rooms must have window area at least 1/20 of floor area and not less than 240 sq.mm.

### Sanitation

Every dwelling must have at least one sanitary toilet and adequate washing and drainage facilities.

### Foundation

Footings must be of sufficient size and strength to support the dwelling load. They must be at least 250 mm thick and 600 mm below the surface of the ground.

### Wooden posts

Wooden posts must follow Table VII.5 dimensions and spacing, and each post must be anchored to the footing by straps and bolts of adequate size. Logs or tree trunk suportales may be used in indigenous traditional construction if their size and spacing sustain vertical loading at least equivalent to the table capacities.

### Floor and roof loads

The live load of the first floor must be at least 200 kg/sq.m. The live load of the second floor must be at least 150 kg/sq.m. The wind load for roofs must be at least 120 kg/sq.m. for vertical projection.

### Stairs, entrances, exits, electrical, and mechanical

Stairs must be at least 750 mm clear width, with 200 mm rise and at least 200 mm run. A dwelling must have at least one entrance and another one for exit.

Electrical installations must conform to the Philippine Electrical Code. Mechanical systems or equipment installations must follow the Philippine Mechanical Engineering Code.

Section 709 states that requirements for other group occupancies are promulgated separately by the Secretary and cover allowable construction, height and area, location on property, exit facilities, light and ventilation, sanitation, vertical openings, fire extinguishing systems, and special hazards.

## Real World Scenario

For a proposed two-storey Group A dwelling on an inside lot, check Section 708 in layers: lot occupancy must not exceed 80%, the dwelling must be at least 2.00 m from the property line, footings must be at least 250 mm thick and 600 mm below ground surface, and stairs must be at least 750 mm clear width with a 200 mm rise and at least 200 mm run. Then use the Rule VIII provisions referenced by Section 708 for room height, room size, and window-opening checks.

## Common Exam Traps

- Do not use 80% for all Group A lots. The source distinguishes 90% for corner lots and 80% for inside lots.
- Do not replace the footing rule with a vague firm-ground statement. Section 708 gives 250 mm minimum thickness and 600 mm depth below ground surface.
- Do not forget that Section 708 points light and ventilation to Sections 805 through 811, while the checked dimensional lines for room sizes and windows are in Sections 805 through 808.
- Do not confuse the Group A dwelling stair minimum with broader exit stair standards for other occupancies.
