# Islamic architecture in the Philippines

## LEA Point
Philippine Islamic architecture is best read as adaptation, not transplantation. For the LEA, combine mosque requirements such as qibla orientation, mihrab, prayer hall, minaret, and ablution with Mindanao-Sulu climate, timber construction, raised floors, steep roofs, ventilation, and Maranao/Tausug decorative traditions.

## Source Anchors
NCCA cultural references and the local Filipino Architecture source establish the regional frame: Maranao, Tausug/Sulu, and other southern Philippine traditions matter for identity and material expression.

HOA Module 3 supplies the general mosque vocabulary: masjid, minaret, qibla, mihrab, minbar, fountain/ablution, and Islamic geometric, vegetal, and script-based decoration. These terms should be used cautiously with Philippine examples because local form, material, and climate change the expression.

Official NHCP/Bangsamoro anchors are useful for named sites such as Sheikh Karimul Makhdum Mosque and the Grand Mosque of Cotabato, but this batch avoids unsupported exact superlatives unless a local source map records them.

## Exam Pattern
If the item asks what makes Philippine Islamic architecture distinct, choose synthesis: Islamic prayer requirements plus indigenous elevated timber building, steep roof forms, ventilation, carved wood, and local ornament.

If the item mentions okir, panolong, torogan, or Lanao/Maranao vocabulary, answer Maranao cultural architecture rather than Middle Eastern arabesque or Ottoman tilework.

If the item mentions modern concrete mosque complexes, answer continuity through changed materials: the mosque program remains Islamic while construction, scale, and urban setting become contemporary.

## Guardrails
Do not say Philippine Islamic mosques must look like Ottoman, Persian, or Mughal mosques. Domes and minarets may appear in modern examples, but the source-safe point is local adaptation.

Avoid precise arrival dates or oldest-mosque claims unless the question stem supplies them. When source certainty is mixed, answer from region, function, material, and decoration.

## Real World Scenario
On an LEA item, a raised timber building with steep roof, strong ventilation, carved Maranao ornament, and mosque orientation points to Philippine Islamic adaptation. A large contemporary concrete mosque with minarets points to continuity of Islamic program through modern material and urban scale.
