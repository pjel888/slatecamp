## Learning Goal

Master the materials and construction methods tested in the ALE, including: masonry and concrete, wood and timber, metals/glass/glazing, roofing/waterproofing/insulation, interior finishes, hardware/joints/fasteners, construction systems, estimating quantities, architectural specifications (CSI MasterFormat), and sustainable/green materials (BERDE). By the end of this study map, you should understand the material-to-delivery chain and be fluent in estimating computations.

## Subject Coverage

Ten subtopics span the full lifecycle of building materials:

- **Masonry and Concrete** — Portland cement types, mix design, CHB, reinforced concrete principles
- **Wood and Timber Products** — species, grading, engineered wood (plywood, LVL, glulam), DENR regulations
- **Metals, Glass, and Glazing** — structural steel sections, aluminum, glass types (tempered, laminated, IGUs), curtain walls, U-value, SHGC
- **Roofing, Waterproofing, and Insulation** — roofing types, waterproofing membranes, R-values
- **Interior Finishes** — flooring, wall finishes, ceiling finishes, selection criteria
- **Hardware, Joints, and Fasteners** — hinges, locks, anchors, expansion joints, connections
- **Construction Systems and Methods** — cast-in-place vs. precast, steel frame, light wood framing, modular
- **Estimating Quantities and Material Computation** — concrete volume, rebar weight, board feet, CHB count, paint coverage
- **Architectural Specifications and Documentation** — CSI MasterFormat, three-part section format, prescriptive vs. performance specs
- **Sustainable and Green Materials** — BERDE, embodied carbon, recycled content, VOC limits

## Common Exam Traps

- **Forgetting formulas under pressure.** Write key formulas on a reference sheet and drill under timed conditions.
- **Confusing material properties.** Compressive vs. tensile vs. flexural strength; R-value vs. U-value — know the difference.
- **Assuming Philippine practice = US/European.** Always apply Philippine-specific standards (NSCP, DPWH specs).
- **Neglecting specifications.** Specification questions are reliable exam content and easier points with focused study.
- **Underestimating sustainable materials.** BERDE and green building criteria have entered recent LEA cycles.
