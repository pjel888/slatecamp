## Phases 4 and 5: Bidding Assistance and Construction Administration Under SPP 202

SPP 202 identifies six phases of regular design services: Project Definition Phase, Schematic Design Phase, Design Development Phase, Contract Documents Phase, Bidding or Negotiation Phase, and Construction Phase. Bidding Assistance and Construction Administration are the fifth and sixth phases.—complete the architect's engagement with the project. According to SPP 202, the Contract Documents Phase represents 90% of the architect's total design fees. The specific percentage allocations for Bidding Assistance and Construction Administration phases are not explicitly detailed in SPP 202 as separate percentages.

### Bidding Assistance Phase Activities

In the Bidding or Negotiation Phase, the architect prepares bid documents including forms for contract letting, construction documents, invitation and instruction to bidders, forms for bidders' proposals, and general or specific conditions of contract. The architect assists the owner from the early stage of establishing a list of prospective contractors. For competitive bids, the architect furnishes complete sets of Bid Documents loaned to bidders covering direct and indirect costs. The architect helps organize and conduct pre-bid conferences, responds to bidder questions, and assists in obtaining proposals and analyzing bid results.

### Construction Administration Phase

Construction Administration begins after the contractor commences work and continues until final project completion and turnover to the owner. The architect makes decisions on all owner and contractor claims relating to execution and progress of work or interpretation of Contract Documents. The architect issues certificates for payment based on observed contractor work quality and contractual compliance. The architect makes periodic site visits to familiarize himself with construction progress and ensure work proceeds according to Contract Documents, though full-time supervision is available as a separately compensated service.

### Fee Distribution and Timeline

Bidding Assistance earns 5% of total fees, representing the shortest phase. Construction Administration earns 10%, the second-smallest percentage despite its extended timeline. According to SPP 202, the Contract Documents Phase represents 90% of total design services fees. The remaining phases (Project Definition, Schematic Design, Design Development, Bidding Assistance, and Construction Administration) collectively represent the remaining 10%. This modest allocation reflects that by construction, the major design work is complete, even though significant coordination and oversight activities continue. The architect's role shifts from creator to administrator and inspector of the design.
