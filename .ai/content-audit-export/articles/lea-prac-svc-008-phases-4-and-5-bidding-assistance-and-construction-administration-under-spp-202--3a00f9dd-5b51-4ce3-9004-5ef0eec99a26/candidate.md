# Bidding or Negotiation and Construction Phase Under SPP 202

## Source Scope

Use SPP Document 202 for the Bidding or Negotiation Phase and Construction Phase duties. Use UAP Document 401-402 only where a question asks about the payment schedule or abandoned-work breakdown.

## Learning Goal

For LEA questions, separate procurement support from construction-phase administration. Bidding or Negotiation helps the owner get and evaluate contractor proposals. Construction Phase duties begin after contract award and focus on claims, changes, site visits, payment certification, and completion.

## Bidding or Negotiation Phase

| Duty | Source-safe exam anchor |
|---|---|
| Prepare Bid Documents | Includes forms for contract letting, construction documents, bidder instructions, proposal forms, and contract conditions. |
| Assist with contractor list and award | The architect assists from establishing prospective contractors through award of the construction contract. |
| Competitive bids | The architect furnishes complete bid-document sets in as many sets as needed for successful bidding. |
| Bid-document ownership | Bid documents are the architect's intellectual property and must be returned by entities acquiring them. |
| Pre-bid and questions | The architect helps conduct pre-bid conferences and responds to bidder questions. |
| Bid analysis | The architect helps obtain proposals, analyze bid results, and prepare abstracts, notices of award, notices to proceed, and construction contracts. |
| Negotiated contracts | The architect performs similar functions but negotiates with one contractor instead of many bidders. |

## Construction Phase

| Duty | Source-safe exam anchor |
|---|---|
| Claims and interpretations | The architect makes decisions on owner and contractor claims related to execution, progress, or interpretation of Contract Documents. |
| Change orders and guarantees | The architect prepares change orders and turns over required written guarantees. |
| Periodic site visits | The architect visits periodically to become familiar with progress and quality and to check general conformance with Contract Documents. |
| No default continuous supervision | The architect is not required to provide continuous 8-hour on-site supervision unless separately agreed. |
| Payment certification | The architect determines amounts due and issues Certificates for Payment based on observations and contractor applications. |
| Completion | The architect conducts necessary inspection for substantial and final completion and issues the final Certificate of Payment. |

## Fee Questions Need Care

The checked payment source says total payments remain at the 85% milestone after award adjustment and that the remaining 15% is handled through progress billing and final balance. In abandoned-work wording, it breaks the remaining 15% into 10% for Civil Code liability and 5% for construction phase service. Do not rewrite this as a separate SPP 202 bidding-fee allocation.

## Exam Trap

If the task is about bid documents, bidder questions, pre-bid conferences, bid abstracts, or notice of award, choose Bidding or Negotiation. If the task is about claims, change orders, site visits, Certificates for Payment, or substantial and final completion, choose Construction Phase.
