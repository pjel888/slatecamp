# Construction Claims

Construction claims must be handled through the notice and dispute mechanisms in the governing contract. CIAP Document 102 states that if the contractor claims instructions, drawings, or specifications issued after bid submission involve a change, the contractor gives the owner written notice within fifteen days after receipt of the instruction, drawings, or specifications, as much as possible before executing the work, except in emergency work.

UAP Document 301 uses a parallel notice idea for claims for extra cost, with written notice to the architect within fifteen days after receipt of an instruction and before proceeding, except in emergency. It also states that the architect makes decisions within a reasonable time on claims of the owner or contractor and on matters relating to execution, progress, or interpretation of the contract documents.

Claims may also affect payment closeout. CIAP Document 102 provides that acceptance of final payment waives contractor claims against the owner except reserved claims, pending unresolved claims, disputes referred to arbitration, and claims the owner acknowledges are not covered by the payment. For disputes not resolved under the contract, CIAP Document 102 refers them to arbitration under the CIAC rules.
