# Alternative Energy And Sustainability Systems

## Exam Anchor

For LEA utility questions, read renewable-energy integration as a code-coordinated building system, not as a slogan. RA 11285 sets energy efficiency and conservation as national policy and includes efficient renewable technologies in that framework. For buildings, it points new construction and retrofits to DOE Guidelines on Energy Conserving Design of Buildings. Those Guidelines cover new buildings, expansions, modifications, energy-efficient lighting, controls, daylighting coordination, and envelope OTTV.

## Photovoltaic Integration Under PEC

The Philippine Electrical Code treats solar photovoltaic systems as complete electrical energy systems that include array circuits, inverters, controllers, and related utilization output. PV systems may be utility-interactive or stand-alone and may include storage. Article 6.90 controls when its requirements differ from other articles; this matters for disconnects, raceways, grounding, plaques, inverter behavior, and point of connection.

A building-integrated PV decision therefore affects architecture in three ways: location and orientation of collector surfaces, accessible disconnect and service-equipment coordination, and clear identification of all power sources. The same article requires plaque or directory information for stand-alone systems and for buildings that combine utility service with PV.

## Solar Thermal And Passive Design

Salvan's utilities reference separates passive solar design from active systems. Passive design uses siting, orientation, envelope choices, and openings to reduce energy demand. Active systems use collectors, transfer media, storage, and equipment, usually with a conventional backup. Efficient solar performance also depends on thermal efficiency, insulation, collector orientation, and avoiding shade from nearby structures.

## LEA Takeaway

Do not treat renewables as an isolated add-on. A strong answer ties the system to RA 11285 and DOE energy performance, PEC Article 6.90 electrical safety, and architectural decisions on envelope, roof or wall integration, access, labels, and maintenance.
