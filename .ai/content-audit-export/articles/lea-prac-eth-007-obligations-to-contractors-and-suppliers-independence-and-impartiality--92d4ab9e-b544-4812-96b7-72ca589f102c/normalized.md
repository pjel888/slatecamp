# Obligations to Contractors and Suppliers

The UAP Document Code of Ethics gives the architect a fair-dealing role between the client, contractor, and suppliers. The source does not require the architect to favor the contractor; it requires clear contract information, prompt rejection of nonconforming work after personal knowledge and inspection, and independence from benefits that create moral obligation.

## Contractor-Facing Duties

The contractor depends on the architect to safeguard fairly the contractor's interests as well as the client's interests. The architect should give reasonable aid so the contractor can understand the Contract Documents through clear, definite, and consistent information. The architect should not knowingly require the contractor to correct or remedy architect-side document oversights or errors to the contractor's financial disadvantage.

When the architect has personal knowledge and inspection of nonconforming materials, equipment, or workmanship, the architect should reject or condemn the nonconforming work promptly so unnecessary delay and additional expense are avoided. Upon request, each completed phase should be inspected promptly; if it conforms to the Contract Documents, the architect issues the corresponding payment or completion certificate.

## Supplier and Benefit Rules

The Code bars free engineering services, substantial aid, gifts, commissions, or favors from contractors or subcontractors when they place the architect under moral obligation. It also bars technical services from manufacturers or suppliers when the accompanying obligation is detrimental to the client or adversely affects professional opinion.

The narrow discount exception is client-protective: a market discount from firms handling building materials or equipment may be accepted only when credited to the client. The architect may not treat the discount as personal income.

## LEA Study Anchor

For exam scenarios, separate three issues: contractor fairness, supplier technical services, and personal benefits. A contractor payment certificate scenario points to conformity with the Contract Documents. A free service, commission, gift, or supplier favor points to independence and moral obligation. A market discount is safe only if the benefit goes to the client.
