# Solar Shading Fundamentals

Solar shading protects windows and glazed areas from direct sunlight. In the checked TOA 4 source, the purpose is to reduce glare and excessive solar heat gain in warm weather.

## Device Selection

- Horizontal overhangs are most effective on southern orientations.
- Horizontal louvers allow air circulation near the wall and reduce conductive heat gain.
- Slanted louvers provide more protection than louvers parallel to the wall, and their angle should respond to the range of solar angles.
- Louvers hung from a solid overhang can protect against low sun angles.
- Vertical louvers are most effective for eastern or western exposures.
- Eggcrate shading combines horizontal and vertical louver behavior and is efficient in hot climates.
- Solar blinds and screens can reduce solar radiation depending on their reflectivity.

## Exam Use

Choose the shading device from the sun angle and facade exposure. A wrong option usually treats every orientation the same, forgets low sun angles, blocks needed air movement, or exposes large glass areas without shade.

## Common Trap

Do not select a shading device because it sounds familiar. Tie the device to orientation: horizontal for high-angle sun, vertical for east or west exposure, and combined systems where both horizontal and vertical control are needed.
