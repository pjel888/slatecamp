## Subject Coverage

Seven subtopic areas organized by scale:

- **General Principles and History of Planning** — Haussmann's Paris, Howard's Garden City, City Beautiful, Jane Jacobs
- **Housing and Human Settlements Planning** — BP 220, PD 957, density calculations, housing typologies
- **Innovation Layer — 21st Century Planning** — smart cities, TOD, resilience planning, climate-adaptive urbanism
- **Landscape Architecture and Ecology** — green infrastructure, ecological corridors, stormwater management
- **Planning Agencies, Governance & Philippine Planning Law** — RA 7160 (LGC), DHSUD, NEDA, DILG, CLUP process
- **Site Planning and Environmental Analysis** — topography, drainage, solar orientation, winds, access, hazard zones
- **Urban and Regional Planning** — metropolitan planning, land use theory, transportation planning

## The Philippine Context Is the Core

Three legislative pillars:

- **RA 7160 — Local Government Code of 1991.** Devolved planning authority to LGUs. Mandates CLUP and Zoning Ordinance for every municipality.
- **PD 957 — Subdivision and Condominium Buyers' Protective Decree.** Minimum standards for roads, open space, community facilities.
- **BP 220 — Economic and Socialized Housing.** Minimum design and construction standards for affordable housing.

<Callout>The Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) is one of the most important planning instruments in the Philippine system. Every LGU is required to prepare one. It classifies land into zones (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, etc.) and is the legal basis for the Zoning Ordinance. Understanding the CLUP process — who prepares it, who approves it, how long it is valid — is regularly tested.</Callout>

## Study Strategy

1. **Start with the legislation.** RA 7160, BP 220, PD 957 — specific, testable figures.
2. **Build the conceptual framework.** History and principles give the "why" behind the standards.
3. **Treat 21st-century layer as vocabulary acquisition.** Smart cities, TOD, resilience — recognize terms and apply to scenarios.
4. **Use site planning as a bridge to design.** The most directly connected subtopic to Architectural Design.

## Common Exam Traps

- **Confusing HLURB and DHSUD.** HLURB was reorganized into DHSUD by RA 11201 in 2019. DHSUD absorbed HLURB functions.
- **Mixing up BP 220 categories.** Socialized, economic, low-cost, medium-cost — each has different minimum standards.
- **Treating planning as pure memorization.** Planning questions present scenarios requiring principle application.
- **Underweighting governance.** Agency jurisdiction questions are reliably correct-or-wrong — invest time in the governance map.
- **Neglecting the CLUP/Zoning Ordinance distinction.** CLUP = policy document; Zoning Ordinance = implementing legal instrument.
