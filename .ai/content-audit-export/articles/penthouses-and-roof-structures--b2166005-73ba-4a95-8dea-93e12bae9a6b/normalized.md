# Penthouses and Roof Structures

PD 1096 2004 Revised IRR Rule XII, Section 1210 controls roof projections. The LEA distinction is important: a penthouse is not automatically an extra rentable floor, and if it is used beyond the allowed purpose it must comply as an additional storey.

## Height and Area

For structures other than Type V construction, a penthouse or other roof projection must not exceed 8.40 m above the roof when used as an enclosure for tanks or elevators running to the roof. In other cases, it must not extend more than 3.60 m in height above the roof.

The aggregate area of all penthouses and other roof structures must not exceed one third of the area of the supporting roof.

## Allowed Use

A penthouse, bulkhead, or similar projection above the roof may be used only to shelter mechanical equipment or to shelter vertical shaft openings in the roof. If it is used for another purpose, it must conform to the code requirements for an additional storey.

## Construction and Towers

Roof structures must generally have walls, floors, and roofs as required for the main portion of the building, subject to the stated exceptions for Types III and IV construction and distance from property or exterior walls.

Towers and spires have separate controls. Enclosed towers and spires must have exterior walls as required for the building to which they are attached. Unenclosed towers extending more than 20.00 m above grade must have iron, steel, or reinforced concrete framework. A tower or spire must not occupy more than one fourth of the street frontage of the building and must not have a base area over 150.00 sq m unless it conforms fully to the construction requirements and height limits for the main building.

## Exam Use

Answer roof-structure questions in this sequence: identify the roof projection, check whether its use is allowed, apply the height and one-third roof-area limits, then apply construction or tower/spire rules.
