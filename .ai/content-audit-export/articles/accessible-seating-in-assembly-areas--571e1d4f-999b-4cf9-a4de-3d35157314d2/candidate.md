# Accessible Seating in Assembly Areas

## Learning Goal

Use the BP 344 2024 Revised IRR to size and locate wheelchair accommodations in assembly areas without importing non-local seating rules.

## Assembly Seating Rule

For auditoriums, arenas, theaters, and other assembly areas, BP 344 requires wheelchair parking accommodations equal to 3 percent of the total seating capacity.

Accessible routes must not overlap wheelchair seating or block waiting spaces. Wheelchair viewing areas must adjoin accessible circulation, so the wheelchair space is not isolated from the accessible route.

## Wheelchair Space Size

Each wheelchair viewing position must be clear, firm, and level. The minimum footprint for a single wheelchair seating space, whether front-entry or rear-entry, is 900 mm by 1200 mm.

Accessible seating must be provided with handrails 750 mm high at the front and sides.

## Team or Player Seating

Where fixed team or player seating areas are provided, BP 344 requires wheelchair spaces and companion seats based on the number of seats provided, but not less than one wheelchair space. The accessible route to team or player seating must connect to the area of sports activity.

## Practice Cue

In exam problems, first identify whether the space is an assembly area, then calculate 3 percent of total seating capacity, and then check location, accessible route, footprint, level surface, and handrail requirements. Do not substitute dining-table rules or parking-slot rules for assembly seating.
