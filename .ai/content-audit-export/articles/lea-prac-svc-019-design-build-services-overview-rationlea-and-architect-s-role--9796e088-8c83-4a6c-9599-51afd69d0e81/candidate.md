# Design-Build Services: Overview, Rationale, and Architect's Role

## Board Exam Quick Check

For SPP 207 questions, identify which design-build model is being tested. Design-Build by Administration and Design-Build with Guaranteed Maximum Project Construction Cost share a similar service scope, but the guaranteed-maximum model adds cost-guarantee rules and savings/overrun consequences.

## Learning Goal

After reading this article, you should be able to distinguish the two SPP 207 design-build models, explain the architect's responsibility in design-build, and avoid importing project-delivery theories not stated in the local SPP source.

## Why SPP 207 Allows Design-Build

SPP 207 starts from the need to balance time, quality, and cost in building projects. The source says design-build can simplify and expedite project delivery while providing creative cost-effective solutions. It also states that the architect's education, training, and expertise qualify the architect to take a direct role from conceptualization to implementation.

In Design-Build Services, the architect renders professional services in implementing the architect's design. The source-safe responsibility statement is strong but specific: in design-build, the architect assumes professional responsibility and civil liability for both design and construction of the project.

## Two Design-Build Models

Design-Build Services by Administration has a scope similar to regular design services: project definition, schematic design, design development, contract document phase, and construction phase. In the construction phase, the architect's role goes beyond periodic inspection and assessment. The source lists tasks such as preparing schedule, program, and estimates; organizing and hiring construction personnel; negotiating with piecework contractors; procuring materials, plants, equipment, licenses, and permits; authorizing and undertaking payments; keeping records and books of account; negotiating with agencies; and managing business transactions related to construction implementation.

Design-Build Services with Guaranteed Maximum Project Construction Cost is essentially the same as Design-Build by Administration, except the architect gives the owner/client a guaranteed maximum project construction cost. If completed actual cost is lower than the estimated project construction cost, the savings are divided equally between owner/client and architect. If actual cost exceeds the estimated project construction cost plus ten percent, the architect is liable for the excess only up to the amount of the administration fee. Additional costs from legitimate change or variation orders, extra work orders, substantial escalation certified by a recognized agency such as NEDA, or causes not attributable to the architect are borne by the owner/client.

## Manner and Ethics

SPP 207 recognizes that the architect may be part of the entity constructing the design, may authorize an entity to construct it, or may be a state-licensed contractor implementing the design. Whatever arrangement is used, the architect must retain a separate professional identity, prerogatives, and integrity as architect. The architect must also disclose business investments or ventures that may create a conflict of interest and ensure the conflict does not compromise the client's legitimate interests or the architect's duty of impartial judgment.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating design-build as no-liability design advice.
- Saying Design-Build by Administration has a guaranteed maximum cost.
- Saying all savings revert only to the owner under the guaranteed-maximum model.
- Ignoring the source's conflict-of-interest and separate-professional-identity requirements.
