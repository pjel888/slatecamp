## Design-Build Services: Overview, Rationale, and Architect's Role

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to:

- Define design-build project delivery and the architect's role within it.
- Explain the advantages and risks of design-build for the architect.
- Distinguish design-build from traditional design-bid-build.
- Describe the architect's relationship with the design-build entity.

### Architect's Qualifications and Involvement

The architect possesses unique qualifications to guide design-build projects from concept through execution. The architect's understanding of construction processes, cost implications of design decisions, and integration requirements enables effective collaboration between design and construction activities.

In Design-Build Services, the architect assumes the professional responsibility and civil liability for both the design and the construction of the project. This comprehensive responsibility distinguishes Design-Build from traditional Design-Bid-Build arrangements where the architect provides design only.

### Two Types of Design-Build Services

Design-Build services divide into two distinct approaches, each with specific characteristics and contractual structures.

_Design-Build Services by Administration_ includes the architect's regular design services comprising Project Definition Phase, Schematic Design Phase, Design Development Phase, Contract Documents Phase, and Construction Phase. During construction, the scope extends beyond periodic inspection and assessment to encompass significant responsibilities including organization and hiring of construction personnel, negotiation and contracting with piecework contractors, procurement of materials and equipment, keeping records and accounts, and management of all business transactions related to project construction and implementation.

_Design-Build Services with Guaranteed Maximum Project Construction Cost_ follows the same scope of design services but adds a critical distinction: the client receives a guaranteed maximum project construction cost. The guarantee provides cost certainty, though specific conditions govern additional expenditures. The project construction cost is guaranteed not to exceed ten percent above the estimated cost, with the architect bearing liability for cost overruns within this threshold.

### Cost Sharing and Risk Management

In Design-Build with Guaranteed Maximum Cost, when realized savings occur from estimated project construction cost, savings are _divided equally between the client and the architect_. This arrangement aligns the architect's financial interests with cost efficiency and value optimization.

Should the actual cost exceed the estimated project construction cost plus ten percent, the architect bears responsibility only up to the amount of the administration fee. Additional expenditures from legitimate change orders, escalation, or conditions beyond the architect's control are borne by the client.

### Integration with Project Delivery

Design-Build services integrate design and construction expertise to achieve project objectives more efficiently than purely sequential methods. The architect's technical knowledge combines with construction management expertise to optimize both design quality and project delivery.

This integrated approach requires clear contractual definition of responsibilities, change order procedures, and cost control mechanisms. The Design-Build method succeeds when the architect and construction team share commitment to project success and maintain transparent communication throughout the delivery process.

Design-Build represents an evolution in how architects and builders collaborate, positioning both parties as partners in achieving optimal project outcomes rather than adversaries in traditional bidding scenarios. The method reflects modern understanding that design and construction expertise must integrate to deliver projects efficiently and effectively.
