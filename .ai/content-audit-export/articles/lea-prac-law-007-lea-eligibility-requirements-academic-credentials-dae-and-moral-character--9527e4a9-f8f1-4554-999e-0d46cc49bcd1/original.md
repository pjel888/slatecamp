## LEA Eligibility Requirements — Academic Credentials, DAE, and Moral Character

<JSX.Frontless />

### Academic Foundation and Moral Character

To apply for the Licensure Examination for Architects (LEA) (LEA), candidates must meet four fundamental requirements established under RA 9266, Section 13. First, applicants must hold a Bachelor of Science degree in Architecture from a school, college, academy, or institute duly recognized and accredited by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED). This ensures all candidates have completed a rigorous five-year architectural curriculum. Second, all applicants must be of _good moral character_, established through documentary evidence and a valid NBI clearance. Third, candidates must possess Diversified Architectural Experience (DAE) of _two (2) years_ documented through an Architect-Mentor Affidavit. Fourth, applicants must not have been convicted of any criminal offense involving moral turpitude.

### Diversified Architectural Experience (DAE) Requirements

DAE represents post-baccalaureate, pre-licensure experience that develops a candidate's competence across multiple phases of architectural service. The 3,840-hour requirement translates to approximately 37 to 40 hours per week over two years (essentially full-time work), ensuring exposure to design, planning, construction administration, site analysis, and project management. Candidates compile their DAE through a _Diversified Architecture Experience Logbook_ certified by their architect-mentor, who must hold a valid Professional Identification Card. The experience must demonstrate variation across different phases and types of architectural service, not concentration in a single area. This requirement was codified in RA 9266 Section 13(c) and subsequent PRBoA resolutions.

### Master's Degree Credit and Documentation

A critical advantage exists for candidates holding a Master's degree in Architecture from a government-recognized institution: _the DAE requirement is reduced by one (1) year of mandatory experience_. This reduction acknowledges the advanced coursework and research embedded in postgraduate architectural studies. Candidates claiming this reduction must submit their official Master's diploma with clear graduation dates and the institution's CHED accreditation status. Additionally, all applicants must provide a Certificate of Live Birth on Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Security Paper, proof of citizenship, an Architect-Mentor Affidavit, and an NBI clearance with no convictions for crimes of moral turpitude.

### Supporting Documents and Verification

The Board requires submission of all supporting documents before examination registration is finalized. These include the Diversified Architecture Experience Logbook, the architect-mentor's Professional Tax Receipt (PTR) and Integrated and Accredited Professional Organization of Architects (IAPOA) number, copies of the mentor's valid Professional ID, and original college diplomas or baccalaureate transcripts with graduation dates and the remark "For Board Examination Purposes" clearly marked. Fraudulent applications containing false evidence or misrepresentations result in denial of registration or, if discovered later, suspension or revocation of the Certificate of Registration. The Board reserves the right to verify all credentials with CHED and the National Bureau of Investigation before proceeding with examination approval.
