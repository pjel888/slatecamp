# Case Study: Hospital, Mall, and School Code Classification

## Source Scope

This local-first revision keeps the case study within the checked PD 1096, RA 9514, and Fire Code source-family evidence for Batch 065. Prior BP 344, RA 9442, RA 10754, discount, VAT, curb-ramp, parking-stall, and accessibility-dimension questions are not repeated because those source families were not selected and raw-audited for this batch.

## LEA Anchor

Hospital, mall, and school cases are useful because they force classification by actual use. The source-safe answer is not a single universal public-building rule.

## Occupancy Classification

RA 9514 classifies health care facilities as medical or care uses where occupants are mostly incapable of self-preservation, and lists hospitals, nursing homes, and birth centers. It classifies educational occupancies as buildings or portions used by six or more persons for instruction, including schools, universities, colleges, academies, and kindergartens. It classifies mercantile occupancies as stores, markets, and rooms or buildings for display or sale of merchandise, and explicitly includes malls, supermarkets, department stores, and shopping centers.

PD 1096 provides matching occupancy anchors: hospitals and nursing homes appear under institutional Group D; schools and day-care purposes are Group C unless another checked exception applies; drinking and dining establishments below one hundred occupants appear under Group E Division E-2.

## Fire and Life Safety Checks

The Fire Code source family requires exits appropriate to occupancy, occupant count, fire protection, height, and construction type. RA 9514 mixed occupancy requires sufficient egress for each individual room or section and the most stringent safeguards of the occupancies involved when separation of safeguards is impracticable.

For schools, checked RA 9514 educational provisions require at least two doorways for rooms or spaces with capacity of fifty or more persons or over ninety-three square meters, at least two separate exits on every storey, and travel-distance limits of forty-six meters without approved supervised sprinklers or sixty-one meters when protected throughout by such a system.

## Exam Use

When the prompt combines hospital, mall, and school, classify each facility and then any component spaces. Do not import accessibility, discount, or service-priority rules unless those exact source families are raw-audited for the item.
