# Stairs, handrails, and guardrails

Stair questions often combine two source families. BP 344 controls accessible-route stair details, tactile warnings, accessible stair width, handrail heights, and protection railings. PD 1096 controls general exit stair, door, corridor, occupant-load, and egress requirements.

## BP 344 accessibility checks

Where staircases are present along an accessible route, complementary ramps, lifts, or escalators are provided. Warning tactile blocks with a depth of 600 mm are installed 300 mm before hazardous areas and at the top, bottom, and intermediate landings of stairs.

Accessible staircases have a clear width of 1.50 m. Landings are provided where rest is appropriate, at intervals of 3 m or less. Landing depth is equivalent to the clear width of the stair and not less than 1500 mm.

Handrails are installed on both sides of ramps and stairs at 900 mm and 700 mm above ramps and stairs. A 300 mm horizontal handrail extension is provided before the start and after the end of ramps and stairs. Protection railings are installed at a minimum height of 1100 mm where stairs, ramps, balconies, landings, or porches are more than 750 mm above adjacent grade.

## PD 1096 egress checks

For required exits, PD 1096 ties stairway and exit requirements to occupant load. Stairways serving an occupant load of more than 50 are not less than 1.10 m wide; stairways serving 50 or less may be 900 mm wide; private stairways serving less than 10 may be 750 mm wide. The rise of every stair step does not exceed 200 mm and the run is not less than 250 mm. Required stairway headroom is not less than 2.00 m.

Exit doors serving hazardous areas or occupant loads of 50 or more swing in the direction of exit travel. Required exit doors are openable from the inside without a key or special knowledge. Corridors serving required exits have their own width and obstruction rules.

## Exam-safe method

First decide whether the question is about an accessible route or a general egress stair. Then apply the correct source family. BP 344 answers focus on accessibility: tactile warnings, accessible width, handrail heights, and guardrail protection. PD 1096 answers focus on occupant load, exit width, doors, corridors, stair geometry, and exit enclosure rules.
