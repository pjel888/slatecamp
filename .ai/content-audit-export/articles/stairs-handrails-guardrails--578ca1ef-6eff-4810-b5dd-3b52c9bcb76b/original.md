## 1. Big Picture: How BP 344 and PD 1096 Interact

- **PD 1096 IRR** (National Building Code) governs general **stair geometry, exits, and fire safety** (rise/run, landings, enclosures, guardrails, exit capacity, travel distance)..[web:45][web:47]
- **BP 344 IRR (with 2013–2024 amendments)** overlays **accessibility refinements**: dual handrail heights, tactile warnings, and coordination with ramps and elevators, particularly for public and institutional buildings.[web:1][web:2][web:9][web:33][web:49]
- Exam mindset: **PD 1096 = life‑safety baseline; BP 344 = accessibility overlay.** Both must be satisfied whenever the building is required to be accessible.

---

## 2. PD 1096 Stair Basics (You Must Know These First)

From PD 1096 IRR (Rule XII – Means of Egress, as summarized in standard reviewers):[web:45][web:47]

- **Landings:** Dimension in the direction of travel at least equal to the stair width; for straight runs, landings **need not exceed about 1.20 m** in length, unless design/exits demand more.[web:45]
- **Vertical distance between landings:** Maximum **3.60 m** vertical rise between successive landings.[web:45]
- **Handrail height band (general stairs):** **800–900 mm** above the tread nosing for required stair handrails.[web:45]
- **Handrails:** Required on **each side** of stairways; if stair width exceeds about **3.00 m**, provide at least one intermediate handrail, approximately equally spaced.[web:45]
- **Headroom:** Minimum **2.00 m** clear vertical headroom measured from tread nosing line to ceiling or overhead obstruction.[web:45]
- **Exit enclosure rules:** Exit stairways typically enclosed with fire‑rated construction (often 2‑hour walls), protected openings, self‑closing fire doors, and **no usable enclosed space** under exit stairs except as allowed for storage when protected and permitted by the code.[web:45][web:47]

These form the **baseline** for all stairs, whether accessible or not.

## 3. BP 344 Accessibility Overlay on Stairs

The BP 344 IRR extends many **ramp** requirements to **stairs**, then adds tactile-wayfinding rules tied into accessible routes.[web:1][web:2][web:9][web:25][web:33][web:46][web:49]

### 3.1 Handrails (Accessibility Layer)

- Handrails on **both sides** of accessible **ramps and stairs**.[web:1][web:2][web:9]
- **Two heights** for accessible ramps and stairs:
  - **700 mm** and **900 mm** from the finished surface (tread or ramp) to the top of the handrail.[web:1][web:2][web:9][web:10][web:43][web:48]
- **Extensions:**
  - Provide at least a **300‑mm horizontal extension** of the handrail at **top and bottom** of both ramps and stairs, returning to the wall, post, or floor so there are **no open, snag‑prone ends**.[web:2][web:3][web:9][web:34][web:46]
- **Grip size:**
  - Handrails and grab bars intended for full grip should have an outside diameter of about **38–45 mm**, with clearances from the wall of **40–50 mm** depending on mounting, per BP 344 diagrams.[web:2][web:3][web:34][web:46]
- **Protective railings (guards):**
  - Where ramps, stairs, balconies, or landings are more than **750 mm** above adjacent grade, **railings for protection** should be installed at **1100 mm minimum height**, measured from the top of the protective rail to the finished floor, **in addition to** required accessible handrails.[web:2][web:3][web:9]
  - This cross‑references PD 1096 guard‑rail requirements; the **1100‑mm guard** is separate from the **700/900‑mm accessible grip bands**.[web:3][web:45]

**Exam note:**

- PD 1096 general stair: handrail band **800–900 mm** above nosing.[web:45]
- BP 344 accessible overlay: **two handrail heights, 700 and 900 mm**, plus **1100‑mm protective guard** on drops >750 mm.[web:1][web:2][web:3][web:9]

### 3.2 Tactile Warnings at Stairs

From BP 344 IRR on tactile warning surfaces:[web:1][web:2][web:25][web:33][web:46][web:49]

- Tactile floor surfaces are required on accessible circulation paths, including at **stairs** (except where specifically exempted, such as certain fire‑exit stairs used only in emergencies).[web:25][web:49]
- **Warning tactile blocks (domes)** at stairs:
  - **Depth:** **600 mm** tactile strip (warning tactile blocks).[web:25][web:33][web:49]
  - **Setback:** installed **300 mm before hazardous areas** or intended stops, such as sudden changes in floor level and at the **top, bottom, and intermediate landings** of stairs.[web:25][web:33][web:46][web:49]
  - **Width:** extends across the **entire clear width of the stair** so a visually impaired user cannot miss it.[web:25][web:33][web:49]
- On long or direction‑changing landings, ensure the tactile strip is placed so that users approaching from all directions encounter the warning before the stair edge; if full‑width placement is not possible due to constraints, designers must provide an **equivleant level of warning**.[web:33][web:49]

Mnemonic: **“600 deep, 300 back, full width at every stair landing.”**[web:25][web:33][web:49]

## 4. Stairs–Ramps–Elevators Integration Pattern

Think of one vertical core in a multi‑storey public building, combining PD 1096 life‑safety and BP 344 accessibility:[web:1][web:2][web:3][web:9][web:10][web:25][web:33][web:49]

```text
RAMP  →  STAIR  →  ELEVATOR
```

- **PD 1096** ensures:
  - Correct **rise/run**, landings, headroom, and enclosure ratings.[web:45]
  - Required **number and width** of exits, permissible **travel distance**, and **exit discharge** to public way.[web:45][web:47]

- **BP 344** ensures:
  - **Ramps:**
    - Minimum **1.00–1.20 m** width (1.00 m is code minimum, 1.20 m common in reviewers), **maximum 1:12 slope**, **1.50–1.80 m** landings at top/bottom and at intervals, dual 700/900‑mm handrails, **300‑mm** extensions, edge curbs, and **tactile warning strips** at head, foot, and landings (600‑mm depth, 300‑mm setback).[web:2][web:3][web:25][web:33][web:49]
  - **Stairs:**
    - 700/900‑mm handrails on both sides, **300‑mm** extensions, **continuous** rails around short landings, **1100‑mm protective guard** where drop >750 mm, and tactile warning strips (600‑mm depth, 300‑mm before top/bottom and intermediate landings).[web:1][web:2][web:3][web:25][web:33][web:46][web:49]
  - **Elevators:**
    - Accessible car and door dimensions (commonly around **1.10–1.40 m** width × **1.40–1.50 m** depth with **0.90‑m** clear door), **1.50–1.80 m** clear turning space in front of doors, **COP around 900 mm**, and **Braille on jambs at about 1200 mm**, per BP 344 IRR diagrams and reviewer summaries.[web:1][web:3][web:10][web:33]

Exam tip: a **fully compliant vertical core** in an exam sketch shows **all three** (ramp, stair, elevator) sharing landings and lobbies, each meeting both PD 1096 and BP 344.
