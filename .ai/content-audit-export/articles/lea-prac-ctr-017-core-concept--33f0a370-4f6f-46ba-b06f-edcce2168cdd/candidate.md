# Substantial Completion

CIAP Document 102 defines substantial completion by function and percentage. There is substantial completion when the contractor completes ninety-five percent of the work, provided the remaining work and the performance needed to complete the work do not prevent normal use of the completed portion.

The owner may issue a written acknowledgement of substantial completion, such as a Certificate of Substantial Completion or equivalent document. CIAP Document 102 cautions that the date of that document is not controlling if substantial completion is shown to have been made at an earlier date, unless the contractor accepts the certificate without written exceptions within fifteen days from receipt.

Substantial completion is not the same as final completion. It starts follow-on controls: punch list timing, the period for making good known defects, and limits on liquidated damages for delay. Under CIAP Document 102, liquidated damages for delay beyond completion time do not accrue after the date of substantial completion.
