# Universal Design in Practice: Source-Safe BP 344 Framing

## Source Scope

This local-first revision uses the checked BP 344 2024 IRR as the accessibility source and uses PD 1096 only for plan-document and accessibility-detail requirements. RA 7277 is named in the legacy title but not used as a factual source in this batch because no RA 7277 raw source was selected.

## LEA Anchor

Universal design answers must stay tied to concrete accessibility requirements. In the checked BP 344 source, accessible routes, ramps, handrails, parking, signs, doors, elevators, tactile warnings, and areas of refuge are the source-safe anchors.

## Accessibility Controls

BP 344 2024 defines an accessible route as a continuous unobstructed path connecting accessible elements and spaces. It requires accessible ramps for changes in level unless served by a dropped sidewalk, curb ramp, elevator, or other mechanical device. The checked ramp text includes minimum clear width, gradient, landings, handrails, raised edges, and tactile warning strips.

BP 344 2024 also gives accessible parking counts and dimensions, directional signage using the International Symbol of Access, accessible elevator car dimensions, accessible pathway/corridor width for passenger paths, and area-of-refuge requirements for persons unable to use stairs during emergency evacuation.

## Exam Use

For this batch, do not convert universal design into a vague value statement. Identify the exact accessible element on the plan and cite the checked BP 344 requirement or PD 1096 plan-document requirement that makes it verifiable.
