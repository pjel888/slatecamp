## Issue

Question Q4 asks "What is the recommended ramp slope ratio according to BP 344?" and currently marks **option b** as correct: "1:12 preferred, steeper allowed temporarily with conditions". The rationale states: "BP 344 specifies maximum ramp slope of 1:12 (1V:12H) for permanent ramps; maximum 1:20 allowed temporarily" and the takeaway says "Use 1:12 ramp slope".

Both the correct answer and rationale are **wrong** under the 2024 Revised IRR of BP 344.

## 2024 Revised IRR Correct Standard

Per the 2024 Revised IRR (accessible ramps section):
- **Gradient not steeper than 1:20** -- general maximum
- 1:15 -- max 10m length -- for special cases only
- 1:20 -- no maximum length specified -- max rise 700mm
- 1:12 is now ONLY for swimming pool transfer walls, NOT general accessible ramps
