# SPP Document 205: Post-Construction Services

## Learning Goal

Use SPP Document 205 to identify what the Architect may do after construction is completed: building administration, maintenance coordination, business development, post-construction evaluation, and compensation for these services.

## Source-Audited Scope

SPP Document 205 covers Post-Construction Services under the RA 9266 Standards of Professional Practice. It recognizes that the Architect may continue to serve the Owner after turnover through building administration or property-management work.

## Building and Facilities Administration

The Architect may act as Building Administrator or Property Manager. In that role, the Architect helps establish and maintain communication channels among the Owner, tenants, and building users so that the completed facility is properly operated.

## Building Maintenance

Building maintenance under SPP 205 includes keeping the building in good condition, enforcing building rules and regulations, monitoring security and janitorial services, and arranging maintenance and upkeep. The service is operational after turnover; it is not the same as construction supervision.

## Grounds, Landscaping, and Equipment Maintenance

Post-construction services can also include maintenance of grounds and landscaping, as well as equipment maintenance. The Architect's role is to help the Owner keep the completed facility, site improvements, and equipment functioning as intended.

## Business Development and Occupancy

SPP 205 includes business development services such as occupancy assistance, tenant-related work, and billing tenants. This makes SPP 205 important for questions involving rental property operations or property-management functions after completion.

## Post-Construction Evaluation

The Architect may perform post-construction evaluation to assess whether the building performs as intended. SPP 205 also connects forensic investigation to SPP Document 203, making forensic work a specialized service when technical investigation is required.

## How Services Are Provided

The Architect may serve in a dual capacity as Building Administrator or Property Manager, or may act only as a consultant. SPP 205 therefore supports both operational management and advisory arrangements.

## Compensation

Compensation may be based on a percentage of gross rentals, maintenance fees, security fees, or similar service charges. It may also be paid as a monthly salary or fee, depending on the engagement.

## LEA Traps

- Post-construction services begin after turnover; do not classify them as design or construction supervision.
- Building administration and property management are SPP 205 functions.
- Forensic investigation is not the same as ordinary post-construction evaluation; SPP 205 points forensic work back to specialized services.
- Compensation can be tied to rentals or operational fees, or paid as a monthly salary or fee.
