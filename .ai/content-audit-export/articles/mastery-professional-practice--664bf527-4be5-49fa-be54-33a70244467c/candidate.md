## The Internal Chain: RA 9266 → Everything Else

**RA 9266 establishes the profession.** It defines architecture, who may practice, qualifications, responsibilities, and consequences of violations.

**Registration establishes the practitioner.** The Board of Architecture administers the LEA, evaluates qualifications, and issues the Certificate of Registration.

**Ethics governs behavior.** UAP Code of Ethical Conduct + RA 9266 ethical provisions: honesty, public interest, conflict avoidance.

**Services define what the architect does.** SPP documents translate RA 9266 into specific service definitions and deliverables.

**Fees define compensation.** SPP 301 standard fee scales; methods: percentage of PCC, lump sum, time-based, per unit.

**Contracts define legal relationships.** Owner-architect and owner-contractor agreements formalize services into enforceable commitments.

<Callout>The chain runs in one direction for most purposes: law → registration → ethics → services → fees → contracts. But the LEA can test any link in the chain and any connection between links. A question about an architect's fee dispute involves services, fees, and contracts simultaneously. A question about professional misconduct involves ethics, registration, and RA 9266 simultaneously. Train yourself to see the whole chain.</Callout>

## The SPP Document Chain in Detail

- **SPP 200** — Master document defining architectural services generally
- **SPP 201** — Pre-Design Services (programming, feasibility, site selection)
- **SPP 202** — Regular Design Services (five phases: Pre-Design, SD, DD, CD, Construction)
- **SPP 203** — Specialized Architectural Services (acoustics, historic preservation, interior)
- **SPP 204** — Architect's Role in Construction Management
- **SPP 205** — Post-Construction Services
- **SPP 206** — Comprehensive Architectural Services
- **SPP 207** — Architectural Interior Design Services
- **SPP 208** — Design-Build Services
- **SPP 301** — Standard Fee Guidelines

<ExamTip>LEA questions about SPP documents often describe a service scenario and ask which SPP document governs it. The key is knowing what makes each document distinct: SPP 201 is pre-design, SPP 202 is regular design, SPP 208 is design-build, SPP 301 is fees. When in doubt, ask: what phase? what delivery method? what type of service?</ExamTip>

## Connections to Laws & Codes

**In Professional Practice**, RA 9266 questions focus on scope, registration, disciplinary grounds, Board/PRC roles. **In Laws & Codes**, RA 9266 focuses on exact statutory language, penalties, interaction with other regulations, and legal liability.

Other bridging laws: PD 1096 (architect's code compliance responsibility), RA 9184 (government procurement), EO 398/RA 4566 (contractor licensing).

## Connections to Architectural Design

Project delivery method shapes design decisions. In design-build, architect and contractor coordinate from earliest stages. In design-bid-build, design is complete before contractor input. Construction administration is the final phase of design — RFIs, shop drawings, change orders.

<Tip>When studying construction administration, think of it as the final phase of design — not a separate activity. The RFI, the shop drawing review, the change order: each one is a moment where the design intent meets construction reality, and the architect's job is to ensure the intent survives the collision.</Tip>

## Integration Patterns

**Pattern 1 — Multi-Relationship Scenario:** Owner + architect + contractor + subcontractor — who owes what to whom?

**Pattern 2 — Ethics-Meets-Business:** Referral fees, gifts from contractors, certifying uninspected work.

**Pattern 3 — Fee Computation with a Twist:** Change of scope, suspension, partial services, specialized service layer.

**Pattern 4 — Registration/CPD Question:** Who needs a license? What are CPD renewal requirements? What happens when a license lapses?

## Common Exam Traps

- **Confusing fee computation methods.** Know the difference and when each is appropriate.
- **Mixing up bond types.** Performance bond (completion) vs. payment bond (subs/suppliers).
- **Assuming uniform architect role across delivery methods.** Design-bid-build vs. design-build vs. CM — roles differ fundamentally.
- **Neglecting SPP document distinctions.** Know which document covers which service type.
