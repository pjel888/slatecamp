## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal
Distinguish DHSUD's regulatory and policy-formulating role (permit issuance, plan approval, complaint adjudication) from production agencies, identify RA 11201 as its enabling law, and describe the HLURB-to-DHSUD transition.

**DHSUD** (Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development, created by RA 11201 in 2019) is the regulatory and policy authority for housing and urban development. It absorbed the functions of the former HLURB (Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board, 1987–2019) and the coordination role of HUDCC (Housing and Urban Development Coordinating Council). HLURB is now dissolved; DHSUD is the current agency.

## Core Concept

DHSUD is a _regulatory and policy-formulating agency_, not a producer. It issues permits, approves plans, and adjudicates housing complaints—it does not build housing or finance loans.

DHSUD's three primary functions:

1. **Developer regulation under PD 957:** All developers of open-market and medium-cost residential subdivisions and condominiums must register with DHSUD and obtain a License to Sell (LTS) before marketing units. DHSUD reviews project feasibility, ensures compliance with development standards, and monitors construction.

2. **CLUP approval:** Every local government unit (LGU) must prepare a Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) under RA 7160. DHSUD reviews and approves CLUPs for conformity with national planning standards and policies. This is DHSUD's most significant interface with local planning.

3. **Policy formulation and dispute resolution:** DHSUD formulates national housing policy, issues implementing rules and regulations (IRR), and handles complaints and disputes involving developers, buyers, and housing programs.

**Historical context:** The Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board (HLURB), created in 1987, was the regulatory body for developers. The Housing and Urban Development Coordinating Council (HUDCC), created in 1992, coordinated inter-agency housing policy. These two bodies were merged into DHSUD under RA 11201 (2019) to consolidate housing governance. When reading older reference materials, "HLURB" now means "DHSUD."

## Philippine Context

DHSUD is headquartered in Quezon City and maintains regional offices in each region. Every subdivision or condominium project for slea in the Philippines passes through DHSUD's regulatory approval process. The CLUP review function makes DHSUD central to local planning—mayors and city/municipal planners work closely with DHSUD to get CLUPs approved. This is a frequent source of examination questions: students confuse DHSUD's review role with execution (DHSUD reviews; LGUs execute).

## How It's Tested

1. **Agency function:** "Which agency reviews and approves CLUPs?" → DHSUD. "Which agency issues the License to Sell?" → DHSUD.
2. **Regulatory distinction:** "DHSUD regulates developers" vs. "DHSUD builds housing" (the latter is false—NHA builds).
3. **CLUP process:** "Who prepares the CLUP?" → LGU. "Who approves the CLUP?" → DHSUD.
