# DHSUD as Planning, Policy, and Regulatory Authority

## Board Exam Quick Check

Use RA 11201 and its IRR as the controlling source for DHSUD. DHSUD is the national housing, human settlement, and urban development department created by consolidating HUDCC and HLURB functions; it is not the LGU body that enacts local zoning ordinances.

## What You Need to Know for the LEA

RA 11201 creates the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development through the consolidation of the Housing and Urban Development Coordinating Council and the Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board. The Act describes DHSUD as the primary national government entity for housing, human settlement, and urban development, and as the main planning and policy-making, regulatory, program coordination, and performance monitoring entity in that field.

For CLUPs, RA 11201 defines a Comprehensive Land Use Plan as a document formulated by the local government in consultation with stakeholders. The same Act gives DHSUD the function to formulate and prescribe land-use planning and zoning standards and regulations for cities and municipalities in the formulation of their CLUPs. It also requires a shelter and urban development management, standards, and monitoring information system that includes CLUPs.

The RA 11201 IRR makes the transferred land-use function more operational. DHSUD receives the former HLURB land-use planning and monitoring function, including ensuring that LGUs follow planning guidelines and implement CLUPs and zoning ordinances. DHSUD regional offices provide technical assistance, conduct capability-building, undertake review of PPFPs, CLUPs, and zoning ordinances as members of land-use committees, monitor land-use changes and zoning implementation, and maintain GIS-based monitoring records.

The key distinction for exams is this: DHSUD sets standards, assists, reviews, monitors, and regulates within its statutory scope. The LGU prepares the CLUP. The Sanggunian adopts the CLUP or zoning ordinance through legislative action. Local officials implement the zoning ordinance and local clearances. DHSUD should not be treated as the body that directly writes every local plan or enacts every local zoning rule.

Also avoid treating the former HLURB name as the current agency name without context. RA 11201 consolidated HUDCC and HLURB functions into DHSUD and reconstituted the adjudicatory function separately in HSAC. In older materials, references to HLRB/HLURB review functions must be mapped carefully to the current DHSUD framework.

## Exam Focus

- Enabling law: RA 11201.
- Created by: consolidation of HUDCC and HLURB functions.
- Core role: national planning, policy, regulation, program coordination, and performance monitoring for housing, human settlements, and urban development.
- CLUP role: standards, technical assistance, review participation, monitoring, and transferred land-use planning functions.
- Not DHSUD's role: local Sanggunian enactment of zoning ordinances.

## Practice Cue

When the item asks "who sets national land-use planning and zoning standards," answer DHSUD. When it asks "who prepares the CLUP," answer the LGU planning process. When it asks "who enacts the zoning ordinance," answer the Sanggunian.
