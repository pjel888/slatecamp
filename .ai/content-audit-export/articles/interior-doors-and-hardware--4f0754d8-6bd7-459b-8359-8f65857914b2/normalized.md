# Interior Doors and Hardware

## Learning Goal

Use BP 344 2024 Revised IRR door and hardware rules for accessible entrances, door clear widths, approach spaces, opening force, sweep timing, and reachable hardware.

## Accessible Entrances

All public entrances must be accessible, including entrances serving leased spaces, parking facilities, pedestrian tunnels, and elevated walkways. Entrances must be accessible from arrival and departure points to the interior lobby, and accessible route design must avoid longer travel distances or exclusion of persons with disabilities from the standard path.

If entrances are not on the same level as site arrival grade, accessible ramps, wheelchair lifts, or similar mechanisms must provide access to the entrance level.

## Door Widths And Approach Space

All doors must have a minimum clear width of 900 mm. Double-leaf doors must have a minimum clear width of 1800 mm. Clear openings are measured from the face of a fully open door at 90 degrees and the door jamb.

A minimum clear level space of 1800 mm by 1800 mm must be provided before and extending beyond a door. Where a door opens onto but not into a corridor, the required clear level space on the corridor side may be a minimum 1500 mm corridor width.

## Door Operation And Hardware

Interior door closer devices must not require more than 2.0 kg of opening force, except for fire doors, exterior hinged doors, and devices such as latch bolts.

Swing-out doors must be used for storage rooms, closets, toilets, and accessible restroom stalls. For swing doors, the sweep period must be adjusted so the door takes 4 seconds or more to move from 70 degrees open to approximately 12 degrees.

Latching or non-latching hardware must not require wrist action, tight grasping, pinching, or fine finger manipulation. Door handles, pulls, latches, and lever type locksets must be centered at 900 mm above floor finish. Door kick plates along major circulation routes must be durable and mounted 300 mm to 400 mm high.
