# Minimalism

## Source-Safe Scope

Batch 136 treats minimalism as disciplined reduction: fewer elements, clearer light, stronger proportion, and material restraint. The local source support comes from modern theory quotations around light, space, materials, wonder, Miesian discipline, Tadao Ando, John Pawson, and TOA 5 modernist references.

The rewrite avoids unsupported building-specific claims about every minimalist architect or national movement. It uses the captured local quotation and TOA 5 evidence only as a source-safe conceptual frame.

## How to Use the Concept

Use minimalism as an editing discipline. Remove what weakens the spatial idea, but keep the elements required for use, comfort, structure, light, and meaning.

## Real World Scenario

A small chapel and gallery in Makati is being value-engineered. A minimalist answer is not to leave the project empty; it is to protect the few decisions that carry the experience: proportion, daylight, quiet circulation, honest materials, and details that do not distract from space.
