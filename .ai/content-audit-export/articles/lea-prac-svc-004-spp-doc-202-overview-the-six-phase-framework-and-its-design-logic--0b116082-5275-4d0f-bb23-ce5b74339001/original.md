## SPP Doc 202 Overview: The Six-Phase Framework and Its Design Logic

SPP Document 202 defines regular design services under a six-phase framework that organizes the architect's work from project definition through construction administration. The document addresses both individual and group architectural practice while clarifying foreign architect requirements and business process outsourcing restrictions for Philippine projects.

### The Six Phases of Regular Design Services

SPP 202 establishes six distinct phases: Project Definition Phase, Schematic Design Phase, Design Development Phase, Contract Document Phase, Bidding or Negotiation Phase, and Construction Phase. However, the fee structure references five fee percentages totaling 100% of the contract sum. The architect must understand that Project Definition Phase is foundational work preceding the five fee-earning phases in regular practice fee calculations.

### Fee Distribution Across Phases

The five fee-earning phases under SPP 202 are distributed as follows: Schematic Design earns 15% of the Project Construction Cost fee, Design Development earns 20%, Contract Documents earns 50%, Bidding Assistance earns 5%, and Construction Administration earns 10%. The cumulative fee reaches 15% after schematic design, 35% after design development, 85% after contract documents, 90% after bidding, and 100% upon completion of construction administration. Contract Documents represents the largest single phase payment, earning fifty percent of total fees.

### Applicability and Requirements

SPP 202 applies to both individual architectural practice and group practice through partnerships or corporations, provided full compliance with architectural legislation is maintained. Foreign architects must secure a Temporary/Special Permit, a work permit from the Department of Labor and Employment, and must collaborate with a local registered and licensed architect. Business Process Outsourcing and Knowledge Process Outsourcing firms registered in the Philippines cannot provide architectural services for Philippine projects unless they are PRC-registered architectural firms.
