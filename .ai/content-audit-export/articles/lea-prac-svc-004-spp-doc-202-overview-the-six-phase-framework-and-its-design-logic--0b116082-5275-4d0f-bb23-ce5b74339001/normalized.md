# SPP Doc 202 Overview: Regular Design Services

## Source Scope

Use SPP Document 202 in the UAP Standards of Professional Practice for the scope and sequence of regular design services. Use UAP Document 401-402 only for the owner-architect payment milestones that support fee questions.

## Learning Goal

For LEA professional-practice questions, identify the service phase first, then match the phase to the architect's documented duty. Do not assume that every phase label carries a separate fee percentage unless the payment source states it.

## What SPP 202 Covers

SPP Document 202 is the standard for Regular Design Services. It applies to individual practice and may also apply to group practice through a juridical entity when the practice complies with architecture law and implementing regulations. It also flags limits on foreign architects and on BPO or KPO firms that are not PRC-registered architectural firms for Philippine projects.

SPP 202 describes the architect as the owner's adviser or representative in regular practice. The work starts when the owner outlines project requirements and continues through study, instruments of service, and supervision during implementation until turnover of the completed project.

## The Six Service Phases

| SPP 202 phase | Source-safe exam anchor |
|---|---|
| Project Definition | Define requirements, confirm the owner's needs, refine space requirements into an architectural program, and prepare an initial probable construction cost statement. |
| Schematic Design | Prepare schematic studies and conceptual plans from the Project Definition Phase. |
| Design Development | Turn approved schematics into plans, elevations, sections, outline specifications, system diagrams, and an updated SPPCC. |
| Contract Document | Prepare detailed construction drawings, technical specifications, permit sets, updated SPPCC, and assist with government filings. |
| Bidding or Negotiation | Prepare bid documents, assist with contractor lists, conduct bidding support, answer bidder questions, and help analyze results. |
| Construction Phase | Decide claims, prepare change orders, make periodic site visits, issue payment certificates, and handle completion-related certifications. |

## Fee Questions Need Care

UAP Document 401-402 supports the common payment milestones: schematic design reaches 15% of the Basic Fee, design development increases total payments to 35%, and contract documents increase total payments to 85%. The remaining 15% is handled through progress billing and final balance; when contract documents are complete but work is abandoned, UAP Document 401-402 breaks that remaining 15% into 10% for Civil Code liability and 5% for construction phase service.

For source-safe exam explanations, say that Contract Documents bring the UAP Document 401-402 cumulative payment milestone to 85% and represent the largest increment from the prior milestone. If a question specifically asks about SPP 202's work-suspended-or-abandoned wording, distinguish that source's 90% completed-work statement from the 401-402 payment schedule. Do not say that SPP 202 itself assigns a standalone 5% to bidding assistance or 10% to construction administration unless the specific source text being tested says so.

## Exam Trap

A question may use familiar labels such as schematic design, design development, contract documents, bidding, or construction. Anchor the answer to the phase duty stated in SPP 202, then use UAP Document 401-402 only when the question asks about payment timing.

## Real World Scenario

An owner asks why the architect cannot jump from conceptual sketches straight to permit drawings. The source-safe answer is to map the request to the SPP 202 sequence: define the project, prepare schematic studies, develop the approved scheme, then produce contract documents. If the same owner asks about billing, use the 401-402 milestones: 15% after schematic design, 35% cumulative after design development, and 85% cumulative after contract documents.
