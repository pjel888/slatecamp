# Sheet Metal and Spray Booths

PD 1096 2004 Revised IRR Rule XVII gives the LEA rules for sheet metal paint spray booths. Treat it as a special-hazard topic: the code controls construction, area, floor finish, fire protection, lighting, and mechanical exhaust.

## Construction and Area

A paint spray booth must be built of steel not less than No. 18 U.S. gauge. Its area must not exceed 150 sq m and must also not exceed 10 percent of the basic area permitted for the major use of the building according to its occupancy group.

If the spray booth floor or the operator working area is combustible, it must be covered with noncombustible, nonsparking material that allows safe cleaning and residue removal. The booth must also permit free exhaust-air passage from all parts of the interior, and interior surfaces must be smooth and continuous without outstanding edges.

## Fire, Light, and Separation

An open-front spray booth larger than 1.00 sq m and not equipped with doors requires a fire curtain or metal deflector at least 100 mm deep at the upper outer edge of the booth opening. The booth must be separated from other operations by at least 91 cm, a greater distance, or a partition or wall required by the Local Fire Service Marshall.

Lighting must be isolated from the spray hazard. Booth illumination is through hammered wire or heat-treated glass panels located to reduce ignition risk from paint-spray deposit. Panels must isolate the spraying area from the lighting unit area and must be noncombustible or protected against breakage.

## Mechanical Exhaust

Mechanical ventilation must discharge directly to the building exterior. The exhaust system must move air through any portion of the paint spray area at not less than 30.00 lineal meters per minute. Exhaust fan blades must be non-ferrous, the motor must not be mounted in the booth or duct system, and belts must be enclosed where they enter the booth or duct system.

The discharge point for booth ducts must be at least 2.00 m from adjoining combustible construction and at least 8.00 m from adjoining exterior wall openings, except for the stated waterwash spray booth condition.

## Exam Use

For LEA items, do not answer spray-booth questions as ordinary mechanical ventilation. First check steel gauge and booth area, then fire curtain/separation, lighting isolation, exhaust velocity, motor/fan restrictions, and duct discharge clearances.
