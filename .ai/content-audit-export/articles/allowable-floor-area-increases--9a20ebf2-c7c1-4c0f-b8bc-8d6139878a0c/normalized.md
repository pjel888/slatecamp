# Allowable Floor Area Increases

## Source Boundary

This article is limited to Rule VII, Sections 705 through 707 and Table VII.1 of the 2004 Revised IRR of PD 1096. It does not use IBC formulas or foreign-code frontage/sprinkler rules as primary truth.

## Allowable Maximum TGFA

Section 705 states that the allowable maximum total gross floor area (TGFA) of a proposed building or structure is only as allowed under Rule VII. Table VII.1 uses percentages of the Total Lot Area (TLA) to initially determine allowable maximum TGFA. The storey multipliers shown in the table, such as 3, 5, 12, 18, and 30, are not themselves percentage values.

The allowable maximum TGFA must be crosschecked with the Allowable Maximum Volume of Building (AMVB). If the AMVB is exceeded, the maximum allowable TGFA must be adjusted because the AMVB must prevail.

## Floor Area Increases

Section 706 allows the floor areas provided earlier in the rule to be increased only in certain specific instances and under appropriate conditions. The stated basis is the existence of public space, streets, or yards extending along and adjoining two or more sides of the building or structure. The increase is subject to the approval of the Building Official and the referenced guidelines on gross floor area and total gross floor area.

## Related Height Control

Section 707 ties maximum building height and number of storeys to occupancy, type of construction, population density, light and ventilation, road right-of-way, building bulk, parking, zoning, environmental considerations, traffic, and utility capacity. The Building Height Limit must be the value allowed under Rule VII or the duly approved local zoning ordinance, whichever is more restrictive.

## Exam Use

For LEA review, do not treat Section 706 as an automatic formula. The source-backed sequence is: determine allowable TGFA from Rule VII and Table VII.1, crosscheck against AMVB, then consider whether Section 706 conditions and Building Official approval can support an increase.
