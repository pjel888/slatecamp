# SPP Document 209: Professional Architectural Consulting Services (PACS)

## Learning Goal

Use SPP Document 209 to answer LEA questions on Professional Architectural Consulting Services: who may practice as a Professional Consulting Architect, how foreign consultants are handled, what services are included, how selection works, and how compensation is computed.

## Source-Audited Scope

SPP Document 209 covers Professional Architectural Consulting Services. It replaces the earlier consulting-services document and is tied to RA 9266, the Architect's intellectual-property protections, government procurement rules, and professional standards for consulting practice.

## Policy Basis

SPP 209 responds to the need for qualified Filipino professional consultants and to the presence of foreign consultants in Philippine projects. It cites the policy of the 1987 Constitution, Article XII, Section 14, which gives preference to qualified Filipino professionals where applicable.

## LOI 1000 Background

The document discusses Letter of Instructions No. 1000 as part of the historical background for consulting services. LEA questions may use LOI 1000 to test whether the student recognizes PACS as a professional consulting framework rather than ordinary drafting or contracting work.

## PCA and FPCA

A Professional Consulting Architect must be registered, licensed, and professionally qualified. A Filipino Professional Consulting Architect must be a Philippine Registered and Licensed Architect and a member in good standing of the IAPOA. SPP 209 also recognizes the role of certification or recognition by the concerned professional body for specialization.

## Foreign Consultants

SPP 209 places safeguards around foreign architects and foreign consultants. When a foreign consultant is engaged because no qualified Filipino Professional Consulting Architect is available, the source text requires Filipino RLA understudies in the same specialization area. Foreign participation must not bypass RA 9266 or local professional responsibility.

## PACS Service Types

The scope of PACS includes sixteen listed service types. They include consulting services connected with planning, design, management, studies, evaluation, specialized advice, and other architectural consulting work defined by the Commission, Board, and IAPOA.

## Selection of PCAs

The major selection consideration is the PCA's qualifications, including competence, capability, and integrity. For government projects, selection follows the relevant provisions of RA 9184, the Government Procurement Reform Act.

## Compensation Methods

SPP 209 lists several compensation methods for PCAs:

1. Per diem, honorarium, or hourly basis.
2. Retainer.
3. Salary cost times a multiplier, plus direct cost or reimbursable expenses.
4. Fixed fee or lump sum.
5. Percentage of total project cost.

For salary cost, the source formula is: Fee = Salary Cost x Multiplier + Reimbursable Expenses. The multiplier starts from 3.0.

## Documents, Liability, and Disputes

Where applicable, a PCA signs and seals architectural documents only when acting as Architect-of-record and assuming the related professional responsibilities. Architectural documentary outputs are protected by RA 9266. SPP 209 also requires ADR under RA 9285 before referral to court.

## LEA Traps

- Do not confuse PACS with regular design services.
- The Salary Cost multiplier starts from 3.0 in SPP 209.
- There are sixteen PACS service types in the listed scope.
- Government PCA selection follows RA 9184.
- Non-qualified persons or entities practicing PACS may be sanctioned under RA 9266 and related rules.
