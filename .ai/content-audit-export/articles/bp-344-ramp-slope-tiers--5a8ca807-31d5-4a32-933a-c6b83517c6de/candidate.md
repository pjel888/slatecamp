# BP 344 Ramp Slope Tiers

## Learning Goal

Apply BP 344 2024 Revised IRR ramp rules using the audited local source, especially minimum width, slope, landings, handrails, edge protection, and tactile warning strips.

## When A Ramp Is Required

Changes in level require a ramp unless access is served by a dropped sidewalk, curb ramp, elevator, or other mechanical device.

Accessible ramps have a minimum clear width of 1200 mm. The standard gradient is not steeper than 1:20. The source allows steeper gradients only in special cases depending on the length to be covered.

## Slope And Landing Cues

The ramp table gives a 1:20 maximum slope with a 15 m landing interval. It also identifies a 1:15 slope with a 10.50 m maximum length, 700 mm maximum rise, and 10 m landing interval.

Accessible ramps longer than 10.50 m must have intermediate landings with a minimum length of 1800 mm. A level area not less than 1800 mm is required at the upper and lower ends of any ramp.

## Handrails And Protection

Ramps must have handrails on both sides at 700 mm and 900 mm from finished floor to the top of the handrails. Handrails need 300 mm horizontal extensions at the upper and lower ends.

Raised edges on both sides of the ramp must be at least 100 mm high. Ramps must also have tactile warning strips at the head, foot, and landings; warning tactile blocks are 600 mm deep and are installed 300 mm from the head and foot and at the center of each landing.

## Practice Cue

For exam items, keep the answer inside the BP 344 ramp system: 1200 mm clear width, 1:20 standard gradient, source-limited 1:15 special case, 1800 mm landings, dual-height handrails, raised edges, and tactile warning strips.
