## Post-Construction Services: Building Administration and Operations Support
## Learning Goal
After reading this article, you should understand the key concepts tested in the LEA for this topic, including relevant code provisions, dimensional standards, and common exam applications.

Post-Construction Services extend architectural involvement beyond project completion into building operations and facility management. SPP Doc 205 establishes the framework for comprehensive post-construction service offerings. These services recognize that buildings require sustained professional attention after occupancy to maintain performance, functionality, and value. Post-construction services require multi-disciplinary professional teams coordinating across specialized domains.

### Rationale for Post-Construction Services

Building administration demands specialized professional attention distinct from design or construction phases. Occupancy creates operational demands that design documents alone cannot address. Buildings encounter maintenance challenges, system failures, and performance variations requiring expert evaluation. Professional post-construction services extend architectural responsibility into the operational life of buildings. This service category responds to owner needs for professional guidance throughout facility lifecycle.

### Building Maintenance and Preservation Services

Building maintenance services establish protocols for equipment preservation, cleaning schedules, and preventive maintenance programs. Architectural input guides material selection for long-term durability and maintainability. Service schedules address seasonal requirements, weather-related considerations, and occupancy patterns. Maintenance planning extends asset life and ensures operational efficiency. Professional maintenance oversight protects building investments and prevents premature deterioration.

### Grounds and Landscaping Supervision

Grounds supervision services oversee landscape establishment, vegetation maintenance, and hardscape preservation. These services address irrigation systems, pest management, seasonal care, and horticultural expertise. Professional supervision ensures that landscape designs mature successfully and remain healthy throughout building life. Coordinated grounds maintenance enhances building appearance, environmental performance, and site functionality.

### Equipment Maintenance and Systems Management

Equipment maintenance services address mechanical, electrical, and specialty systems requiring specialized expertise. Preventive maintenance scheduling prevents unexpected failures and extends equipment life. Performance monitoring identifies degradation trends and informs replacement decisions. Professional oversight ensures that building systems operate efficiently and safely. Equipment maintenance services protect occupant comfort and building functionality.

### Post-Construction Evaluation and Building Optimization

Post-construction evaluation assesses building performance against design intent and occupant requirements. Building performance evaluations address thermal comfort, lighting adequacy, acoustic performance, and operational efficiency. Evaluation findings inform retrofitting decisions and operational optimization opportunities. Professional assessment identifies underperforming systems and recommends improvements. These services support continuous building improvement throughout operational life.

### Business Development and Tenant Relations

Business development services support leasing operations, tenant coordination, and facility marketing. Professional coordination enhances tenant satisfaction and operational efficiency. Architectural expertise informs tenant space modifications and improvement approvals. Facilities management planning addresses changing tenant needs and operational requirements. These services optimize building revenue performance and operational effectiveness.

### Manner of Providing Post-Construction Services

Post-construction services may operate as ongoing retainer arrangements, project-based engagements, or on-call consultation. Ongoing retainer services establish sustained professional presence throughout facility operation. Project-based services address specific issues including building condition assessments or systems evaluation. On-call consultation supports facility managers addressing unforeseen challenges. Service arrangements suit varying owner preferences and facility operational models.

### Method of Compensation

Compensation methods include percentage of gross rentals, monthly or annual fees, project-based rates, or hourly consultation charges. Percentage compensation aligns with building revenue generation. Fixed monthly fees provide budget certainty and support sustained service relationships. Project-based compensation addresses specific evaluation or modification projects. Hybrid arrangements combine multiple compensation methods addressing different service categories.
