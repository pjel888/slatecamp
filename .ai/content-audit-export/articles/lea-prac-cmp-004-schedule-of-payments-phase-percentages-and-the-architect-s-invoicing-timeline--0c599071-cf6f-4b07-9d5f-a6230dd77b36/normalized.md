## Board Exam Quick Check

For schedule-of-payment questions, use the cumulative payment milestones from UAP Document 401-402 first. Do not mix them with unsourced 20/20/30/5/25 phase allocations.

## Schedule of Payments: Phase Percentages and Invoicing Timeline

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to state the source-backed cumulative payment milestones, identify the payment trigger for each milestone, and avoid unsupported phase-allocation schedules.

---

### The Controlling Schedule

UAP Document 401-402, Article 3.02 gives the payment framework for the Architect's fee. The sequence is cumulative. Upon signing of the agreement, the Owner pays a mobilization amount of at least PHP 25,000.00. Upon completion of Schematic Design Services, payment is made so total payment reaches 15.0% of the Basic Fee, less the mobilization already paid. Upon completion of Design Development Services, payment increases total payment to 35.0% of the Basic Fee. Upon completion of Contract Documents Services, payment increases total payment to 85.0% of the Basic Fee.

<ExamTip>
The safe board sequence is: mobilization minimum PHP 25,000; Schematic Design cumulative 15%; Design Development cumulative 35%; Contract Documents cumulative 85%; remaining 15% handled through construction/progress/final billing logic.
</ExamTip>

### Why Cumulative Percentages Matter

Candidates often answer as if each phase gets a separate standalone percentage. Article 3.02 is worded cumulatively. At Design Development, the question is not “what separate percentage does DD receive?” The source says the payment shall increase total payments to 35.0% of the Basic Fee. At Contract Documents, the total reaches 85.0%.

This matters in computation items. If the total Basic Fee is PHP 2,000,000 and Contract Documents are complete, the source-backed cumulative amount is 85.0% of the Basic Fee, or PHP 1,700,000, subject to the agreement's adjustment language. Do not add an unsupported 90% or 95% milestone.

### Remaining 15 Percent

Article 3.02 states that after award to the winning constructor-bidder, payments are adjusted so the total equals 85.0% of the Basic Fee computed on the lowest bona fide bid or winning bid price. The remaining 15.0% is handled through progress billing on the remaining part of the Architect's Fee based on the original SPPCC, with the balance paid upon completion based on FPCC.

Article 7.08 gives a useful cross-check in the suspension or abandonment context. It states that completion through Contract Documents equals 85.0% of the Architect's work, while the remaining 15.0% is composed of 10.0% for liability under Article 1723 of the Civil Code and 5.0% for construction phase service, including contract document forms and periodic visits during construction.

### Real-World Scenario

Suppose an architect in Davao prepares an invoice after the Owner approves Contract Documents for a small municipal building. The Owner asks why the invoice is not simply another equal phase billing. The architect should point to the cumulative Article 3.02 structure. The contract has already passed Schematic Design and Design Development; completion of Contract Documents brings total payment to 85.0% of the Basic Fee. The invoice should show the total fee base, the cumulative percentage now earned, previous payments already made, and the net amount due. If bidding later produces a winning bid different from the probable cost, Article 3.02 provides an adjustment step tied to the lowest bona fide bid or winning bid price. This is not guesswork; it is the payment sequence stated in the Owner-Architect Agreement form.

For a board computation, write the milestone as a cumulative target, not as a separate phase slice. If previous payments already reached 35.0% at Design Development, the Contract Documents billing is the amount needed to reach 85.0%, not an additional 85.0% on top of earlier payments. That distinction is the common trap.

### Unsupported Schedules to Avoid

Do not use a 20/20/30/5/25 schedule unless a separate verified source is attached. Do not say Design Development is always a standalone 20% payment. Do not say Contract Documents completion is 90% or 95% under this source. For this batch, the source-supported milestones are 15%, 35%, 85%, and the remaining 15% framework.
