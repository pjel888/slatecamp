# Francis D.K. Ching: Circulation Patterns

## Source-Audited Scope

Batch 055 limits this article to the checked local theory passages on circulation: path as perceptual thread, approach, entrance, path configuration, path-space relationships, and circulation-space form.

## Board Exam Quick Check

For circulation questions, identify which part of the movement system is being asked: approach, entrance, configuration of path, path-space relationship, or form of circulation space.

## Core Concepts

- The circulation path is described as the perceptual thread linking a building's spaces.
- The checked source lists building approach as the first phase before entering a building.
- Approach types include frontal, oblique, and spiral.
- Entering a space involves crossing a real or implied vertical plane separating here from there.
- Path configurations include linear, radial, spiral, grid, network, and composite.
- Paths may pass by spaces, pass through spaces, or terminate in a space.
- Circulation spaces may be enclosed, open on one side, or open on both sides.
- The width and height of circulation space should be proportionate to the type and amount of traffic.

## LEA Use

Use the clue words. If the question says direct straight axial route to an entrance, answer frontal approach. If it says paths extending from a central point, answer radial configuration. If it says route hierarchy, look for continuity, scale, form, and length.

## Active Recall

1. What is the circulation path? The perceptual thread linking spaces.
2. What are three approach types? Frontal, oblique, and spiral.
3. What are three path-space relationships? Pass by, pass through, and terminate in a space.
