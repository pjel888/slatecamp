# Environmental and Land-Use Planning Controls

## Board Exam Quick Check

This article is narrowed to source-checked local environmental controls: PD 1067 for water-body easements, RA 9275 for water quality and sewage/septage management, and RA 9003 for solid-waste planning. Do not answer from PD 1586 or RA 7586 unless those sources are separately provided.

## What You Need to Know for the LEA

PD 1067 Article 51 is the key site-planning rule for water-body margins. The banks of rivers and streams and the shores of seas and lakes are subject to public-use easements: 3 meters in urban areas, 20 meters in agricultural areas, and 40 meters in forest areas. The same article prohibits staying longer than necessary for public-use purposes or building structures of any kind within the zone.

RA 9275 applies to water quality management in all water bodies and primarily addresses abatement and control of pollution from land-based sources. It defines sewerage broadly as collection, transport, pumping, treatment, and disposal systems. It also requires coordination for domestic sewage collection, treatment, and disposal, and gives LGUs shared responsibility for water-quality management and improvement within their jurisdictions.

RA 9003 makes solid-waste planning a local planning issue. LGU solid-waste management plans must include diversion schedules, and the statute sets an initial target to divert at least 25 percent of all solid waste from disposal facilities through reuse, recycling, composting, and other resource recovery activities. The law also requires segregation and storage standards, materials recovery facilities in every barangay or cluster of barangays, and prohibits open dumps.

For site planning, these laws affect setbacks, water protection, utility planning, service yards, waste collection points, and open-space reservations. The exam trap is importing an unsupported environmental law into a question that only names one source. If the item asks PD 1067, answer easement distances. If it asks RA 9275, answer water quality, sewerage, septage, and LGU water-quality responsibility. If it asks RA 9003, answer segregation, diversion, MRFs, and open-dump prohibition.

## Exam Focus

- PD 1067: 3 m urban, 20 m agricultural, 40 m forest public-use easements along specified water margins.
- RA 9275: water quality management, sewerage/septage, action plans, and LGU shared responsibility.
- RA 9003: source segregation, 25 percent initial diversion target, MRFs, and open-dump prohibition.
- Site design should reserve source-required areas before placing buildings or hardscape.
