# Bahay Kubo as Climate-Responsive Architecture

The checked TOA 4 source presents the Bahay Kubo as a climate-responsive building type. Its value is not nostalgia; it is the way form, roof, openings, floor, and surrounding landscape respond to damp ground, rain, heat, and airflow.

## Source-Safe Features

- The living platform is elevated on stilts as an adaptation to damp ground.
- A high-pitched roof drains rain quickly and creates a large air space above the living area.
- Large windows support cross ventilation.
- Operable awning-type windows protect from rain while also providing shade.
- Wide roof overhangs help protect walls and openings.
- Bamboo floor slats allow air to move upward.
- Surrounding gardens contribute to the cooler setting.

## Exam Use

When a question asks why the Bahay Kubo is climate responsive, pick the answer that ties a feature to a climate task: elevation for damp ground, roof pitch for rain and heat buffer, large operable openings for ventilation, overhangs for shade and rain protection, or slatted flooring for upward air movement.

## Common Trap

Do not answer with style alone. The tested concept is performance: rain shedding, shaded openings, airflow, and adaptation to a hot-humid setting.
