# Land Use Planning and Zoning Ordinances

## Board Exam Quick Check

A zoning item is usually asking about the legal implementation of the CLUP. The safest answer starts with RA 7160 and EO 72: the CLUP is enacted through a zoning ordinance, and the Sanggunian is the legislative body that adopts or enacts it.

## What You Need to Know for the LEA

Land use planning assigns spatial policy to land and physical resources. The CLUP is the broader long-term plan. The zoning ordinance is the enforceable local law that translates the CLUP into specific controls for land use, intensity, building form, and development conditions.

RA 7160 Section 20(c) requires LGUs to prepare CLUPs enacted through zoning ordinances. The same Code gives city and municipal Sanggunians power to prescribe reasonable limits and restraints on property use, adopt a comprehensive land use plan, and enact integrated zoning ordinances in consonance with the approved CLUP. EO 72 repeats the practical rule: the city or municipal plan is submitted to the Sangguniang Panlungsod or Sangguniang Bayan for enactment into a zoning ordinance.

RPS 2008 states the planning logic clearly: the CLUP is the plan for managing local territory, and at city and municipal level it becomes a statutory plan when enacted into a zoning ordinance. RPS also distinguishes the technical component, which supplies data and analysis, from the political component, which makes policy and legislative decisions.

The HLURB CLUP Guidebook explains the substantive planning scope. A CLUP determines specific uses of land and physical or natural resources, delineates actual boundaries, integrates sectoral plans, and provides policies for settlements, production, protection, and infrastructure. Zoning must therefore be consistent with the approved CLUP, higher-level spatial plans, national standards, and the data and analysis used in the planning process.

For exam purposes, do not invent universal numeric zoning limits. Lot sizes, FAR, height limits, parking ratios, setbacks, and conditional uses are ordinance-specific unless the question provides the numbers or a separate controlling law. A source-safe zoning answer says to consult the adopted zoning ordinance and apply the ordinance's stated standards.

## Exam Focus

- CLUP: strategic spatial plan and policy framework.
- Zoning ordinance: enforceable legislative implementation tool.
- Sanggunian: adopts/enacts CLUP and zoning ordinance.
- Planning office: drafts technical content and supports the process.
- DHSUD: standards, technical assistance, review participation, and monitoring under RA 11201/IRR.
- Site regulation: permitted uses, prohibited uses, conditional uses, and dimensional standards come from the adopted ordinance.

## Practice Cue

If a proposal conflicts with the zoning ordinance, do not answer as if the designer can proceed by preference. The source-safe response is to revise the proposal, seek the procedure allowed by the ordinance, or require ordinance amendment/variance when the local law provides for it.
