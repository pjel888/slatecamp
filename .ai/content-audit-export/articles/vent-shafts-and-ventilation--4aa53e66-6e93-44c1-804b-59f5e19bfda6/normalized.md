# Vent Shafts and Ventilation

## Source Boundary

This article is limited to Rule VIII, Sections 809 through 811 of the 2004 Revised IRR of PD 1096. The local raw PDF is the source of truth; the refined markdown was used only to locate the verified provisions.

## Vent Shafts

Section 809 sizes a ventilation or vent shaft by height. The horizontal cross-sectional area must be at least 1.00 sq.m for every meter of shaft height, and the area must never be less than 1.00 sq.m. The least dimension of the vent shaft must not be less than 600 mm.

If the shaft is not open to the outer air at the top for its full area, it must be covered by a skylight with net free area, or fixed louver openings, equal to the maximum required shaft area.

Air ducts may open to a street or court through a horizontal duct or intake. That duct or intake must have an unobstructed cross-sectional area of at least 0.30 sq.m, a minimum dimension of 300 mm, and openings not less than 300 mm above the street surface or court level.

## Ventilation Skylights

Section 810 treats a ventilation skylight as a replacement for a window. The glass area must be at least the area required for the window being replaced. The movable sashes or louvers must provide aggregate net free area at least equal to the openable parts of the replaced window, unless artificial ventilation of equivalent effectiveness is provided.

## Artificial Ventilation

Section 811 requires artificial ventilation for rooms or spaces housing industrial or heating equipment when needed to prevent excessive accumulation of hot or polluted air. When artificial ventilation is required, the equipment must be designed to meet the minimum air-change requirements in Table VIII.4.

## Exam Use

For LEA review, keep the numbers separate: vent shaft area and least dimension, duct or intake area and dimension, duct opening elevation, skylight equivalence, and Table VIII.4 air-change design. Do not import foreign-code formulas unless the question separately supplies them.
