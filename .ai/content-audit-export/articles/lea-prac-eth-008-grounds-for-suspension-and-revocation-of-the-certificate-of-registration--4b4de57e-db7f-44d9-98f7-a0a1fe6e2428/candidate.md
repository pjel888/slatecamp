# Grounds for Suspension and Revocation

Section 23 of the RA 9266 IRR is the safe anchor for LEA questions about suspension or revocation of an architect's Certificate of Registration, Professional Identification Card, or Special/Temporary Permit. The Board acts upon notice and hearing. The same section also says charges must be written, sworn, and filed with the Secretary of the Board, and that the Board may also investigate motu proprio by proper resolution or order.

## Section 23 Grounds

The source gives a general opening basis for discipline: fraud or deceit in obtaining registration, gross negligence or incompetence, unprofessional or dishonorable conduct, or other specified causes. It then lists specific grounds, including:

- signing or permitting the architect's name or seal on architectural plans, designs, specifications, drawings, technical reports, valuations, estimates, or similar work not prepared by the architect or not executed under immediate supervision;
- paying money beyond regular fees to secure a Certificate of Registration;
- falsely impersonating another practitioner or practicing under an assumed, fictitious, or corporate name other than that of the registered architect;
- aiding or abetting the practice of architecture by a person not duly authorized to practice in the Philippines;
- openly soliciting projects by undertaking architectural services without a valid service agreement guaranteeing compensation, or allowing oneself to be exploited in that way; and
- violating RA 9266, its IRR, the Code of Ethical Conduct, or the Standards of Professional Practice.

## Reinstatement Anchor

Section 24 adds the reinstatement rule for revoked credentials. After two years from the date of revocation, the Board may reinstate the validity of a revoked certificate, professional ID card, or special/temporary permit upon application and for reasons the Board deems proper and sufficient. The Board may, in its discretion, exempt the applicant from taking another examination.

## LEA Study Anchor

Avoid using unverified disciplinary timelines or penalty ranges unless the exact source is in front of you. For this batch, the source-safe facts are the Section 23 grounds, the written sworn-charge requirement, the Board's motu proprio authority, and the Section 24 two-year reinstatement anchor.
