# Brutalism

## LEA Point

Brutalism is tested as more than exposed concrete. The exam cue is raw material expression, heavy sculptural massing, and an ethical/social claim that structure and materials should be legible rather than hidden behind polite finishes.

## Source Anchors

- Britannica anchors Brutalism to the mid-20th century, raw concrete, bold geometric forms, utilitarian aesthetics, postwar building programs, and decline in favor by the 1980s.
- Reyner Banham is sourceable through the 1955 New Brutalism essay and 1966 book context as a codifier of the movement debate.
- City of London and Barbican sources anchor the Barbican Estate to Chamberlin, Powell and Bon, construction from 1965 over 11 years, raised podium/highwalk urbanism, Grade II listing, and distinctive bush-hammered concrete qualities.
- Safdie Architects anchors Habitat 67 to prefabricated concrete boxes/modules and 158 residences.
- Boston.gov anchors Boston City Hall to Kallmann McKinnell and Knowles, completion in 1968, Brutalist civic identity, and a democratic-accessibility design intent.
- Kimbell Art Museum anchors Louis Kahn's 1972 building as a refined concrete-and-light comparison point, not the same as rough fortress-like Brutalism.

## Exam Pattern

If the stem says beton brut, raw concrete, massive forms, bush-hammered surfaces, postwar civic/social ambition, Barbican, Habitat 67, or Boston City Hall, answer Brutalism. If the stem says glass curtain wall and dematerialization, do not answer Brutalism; that belongs to International Style vocabulary.

## Guardrails

Do not equate every concrete building with Brutalism. Do not make the movement purely residential; it includes housing, civic, cultural, institutional, and educational work. Do not overstate Kahn's Kimbell as rough Brutalism; it is safer as refined concrete modernism with light and proportion.

## Real World Scenario

For a city-hall retrofit, a source-safe Brutalist reading would preserve the expressive concrete massing and public-plaza logic while improving access, comfort, maintenance, and wayfinding so the civic intent remains usable.
