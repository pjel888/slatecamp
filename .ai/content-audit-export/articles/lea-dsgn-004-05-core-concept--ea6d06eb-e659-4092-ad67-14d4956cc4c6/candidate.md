## Core Concept

Museums and cultural facilities are classified as _Group C (Education and Recreation)_ per **PD 1096**, along with libraries, schools, and churches. This classification is significant because museums are not commercial retail spaces (Group E) despite generating revenue through admissions.

The core challenge in museum design is balancing two competing requirements:
1. **Public Access**: General galleries must accommodate visitor flow at a density similar to retail (1 per 2.8 sqm per RA 9514 Table 3.1-2), requiring multiple exits and adequate egress widths
2. **Artifact Preservation**: Climate control (18–21°C, 45–55% relative humidity), specialized lighting (50–300 lux range, UV-free), and secure storage create distinct spatial and mechanical design demands

Museum galleries are organized by _circulation patterns_:
- **Linear**: Single path through connected galleries (common in historic house museums)
- **Radial**: Central hub with galleries extending outward (large encyclopedic museums)
- **Comb**: Main spine with galleries off a central corridor (compact urban museums)
- **Free-flow**: Visitor choice of route (modern art museums, less formal)

Gallery dimensions and aisle widths must accommodate both visitor comfort and artwork viewing:
- Primary circulation aisle: **3.0 m minimum** (allows viewing + passing traffic)
- Secondary aisle: **1.8 m minimum** (single-file with wall-hugging)
- Alcove/display area: 2.0–3.0 m (artifact-dependent)

Environmental standards are governed by the **NCCA/NHCP guidelines** and the **National Heritage Commission** (NHC) for protected artifacts. Climate control is critical: fluctuations > 2°C per day cause dimensional changes in wood, canvas, and paper. Humidity swings > 10% risk mold and salt efflorescence on ceramics and stone.

## Key Provisions

### Occupancy Classification (Group C per PD 1096 Rule VII Section 701)

Museums are Group C regardless of revenue model. **Primary occupancy** = museum (Group C). **Secondary spaces** = administrative offices (Group E, mercantile if gift shop), climate rooms (no public access, mechanical support). Fire-rated separation required between Group C and Group E per RA 9514 Section 7.2.

### Occupant Load Calculation (RA 9514 Table 3.1-2)

Museum galleries treated as exhibit galleries or retail-equivalent per RA 9514 2019 IRR Table 10.2 (verify exact occupant load factor from Table 10.3.2 or equivalent assembly/retail section in 2019 IRR)

Example (5,000 sqm museum):
- Occupant load = 5,000 ÷ 2.8 = **1,786 occupants** (use this for exit design)

### Gallery Space Program Standards

| Gallery Type | Primary Aisle (m) | Secondary Aisle (m) | Wall Display Height (m) | Lighting (lux) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| General exhibition | 3.0 min | 1.8 min | 2.4–3.0 | 50–300 |
| Paintings/works on paper | 3.0 min | 1.8 min | 2.4 | 150–300 (no direct UV) |
| Decorative arts/textiles | 3.0 min | 1.8 min | 2.4–3.0 | 50–150 (dim, UV-free) |
| Sculpture/architectural | 4.0 min | 2.0 min | 3.0+ | 100–300 |

### Environmental Standards (NCCA/NHC)

| Parameter | Target Range | Tolerance | Risk if Exceeded |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | 18–21°C | ±2°C max/day | Wood warping, adhesive failure |
| Relative Humidity | 45–55% | ±5% | Mold growth, dimensional changes |
| Light (visible) | 50–300 lux (type-dependent) | Stable output | Photochemical fading, brittleness |
| UV radiation | **0 μW/lumen** (filtered) | None—eliminate UV | Irreversible fading, paper degradation |

### Storage & Collections Management

Artifact storage rooms are **not open to public** (not counted in occupancy load):
- Temperature: 18–21°C ± 2°C
- Humidity: 45–55% ± 5%
- Racking: vertical 4–6 shelves, lateral filing systems
- Lighting: 30–50 lux, 12-hour max daily exposure
- **No public access; minimum occupant load calculation** (staff only, 1 per 25 sqm per RA 9514 office standard)

### Accessibility & Circulation (BP 344, RA 9514)

- Ramp gradient: **1:20 maximum** (per BP 344 2024 Revised IRR)
- Accessible stall (for persons with disabilities): 3.70 m × 5.00 m (BP 344 2024 Revised IRR)
- Standard parking stall: 2.4 m–2.7 m width × 5.0 m–6.0 m length (verify parking angle per PD 1096 Rule XII Section 1210)
- Door width (accessible): **900 mm minimum**
- Travel distance to exit: 60 m (sprinklered) / 45 m (unsprinklered)

## Philippine Practice Context

A typical **municipal art museum in the Philippines** (2,000 sqm gallery + 500 sqm support):
- Gallery layout: two-story atrium with comb circulation (central spine, galleries off main corridor)
- Occupant load: 2,000 sqm ÷ 2.8 = **714 visitors**
- Temperature/humidity: split-system AC with dehumidifier, 24/7 operation
- Lighting: 200 lux in paintings gallery, 100 lux in textile gallery, all UV-filtered (99.5% UV block via acrylic or museum-grade glass)
- Emergency egress: 714 persons × 7.6 mm/person = 5,427 mm; typically 2 main stairs (2.0 m each) + side exit (1.5 m) = 5.5 m adequate
- Fire separation: 2-hour wall between climate-controlled storage (Group C mechanical) and administrative offices (Group E)
- Parking: museums typically exempt or require 1 space per 50 sqm GFA for staff (municipal mandate varies)

<Example>
**Example: Occupant Load and Egress for a Provincial Museum**

A provincial museum in the Philippines has a 1,200 sqm gallery space. The architect proposes two exit stairs (each 1.8 m wide) and one side exit (1.5 m wide). Verify compliance.

- Occupant load: 1,200 ÷ 2.8 = **429 persons**
- Required exit width: 429 × 7.6 mm = **3,260 mm (3.26 m)**
- Available exit width: 1,800 + 1,800 + 1,500 = **5,100 mm (5.1 m)**
- **Result: COMPLIANT** — available width exceeds requirement. This demonstrates that museums, despite high occupant density, can meet egress requirements with well-placed stairs.
</Example>

## How It's Tested

**Occupancy Classification Pattern (Difficulty 0.7):**
"A commercial art gallery charges admission but sells no merchandise. Under PD 1096, what is the occupancy group?"
- Answer: **Group C** (museum/exhibition space) — NOT Group E, despite revenue collection. Commercial galleries are Group C per PD 1096.

**Space Program Pattern (Difficulty 0.7):**
"A museum gallery is 50 meters long and must accommodate two-way foot traffic with viewing on both sides. What is the minimum aisle width for primary circulation?"
- Answer: **3.0 m minimum** (per museum design standards) to allow 1.5 m viewing + 1.5 m passing lane

**Occupant Load & Egress Pattern (Difficulty 0.9):**
"A 3,500 sqm museum with 2 main exit stairs (each 1.5 m wide) and 1 side exit (1.2 m wide). Calculate occupant load and verify egress adequacy."
- Occupant load: 3,500 ÷ 2.8 = **1,250 persons**
- Exit width required: 1,250 × 7.6 mm/person = 9,500 mm = 9.5 m
- Available: (1.5 + 1.5 + 1.2) m = 4.2 m < 9.5 m required
- **Result: NON-COMPLIANT** — need wider stairs or additional exits

**Environmental Control Pattern (Difficulty 0.9):**
"A museum stores 5,000-year-old ceramic artifacts. What temperature and humidity maintenance schedule is required?"
- Answer: 18–21°C ± 2°C per day max; 45–55% RH ± 5%; continuous HVAC monitoring; log readings hourly per NCCA standards

<Tip>**Memory rule:** Museums = Group **C** (education), not retail (E). Occupant load = GFA ÷ 2.8 sqm/person. Gallery aisle = 3.0 m primary, 1.8 m secondary. Climate = 18–21°C, 45–55% RH, UV-free 50–300 lux. Temperature swings > 2°C/day = DAMAGE.</Tip>

**Exam Tip — ERR-DSGN-017:** Museums are Group C regardless of revenue. Even if they charge admission or have a gift shop (Group E), the primary occupancy remains Group C. The gift shop requires fire-rated separation.
