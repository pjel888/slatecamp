# Architecture and human behavior

## Source-Audited Scope

Use this topic as a human-factors and design-perception topic. The checked local sources support anthropometric, proxemic, and kinesthetic requirements; personal space; psychological and social space; territoriality; and behavior settings. Exact distance bands are not used here unless separately sourced.

## Board Exam Quick Check

When a question asks about proxemics, choose the option about spatial separation, personal space, or distance as communication. Do not confuse proxemics with anthropometrics, acoustics, or structural sizing.

## Core Concepts

- Theory of Architecture includes analysis of anthropometric, proxemic, and kinesthetic requirements of space.
- Proxemics is the study of the symbolic and communicative role of spatial separation between individuals.
- Edward Hall is identified in the checked source as the father of proxemics, and personal space is not static.
- Human factors include perception and behavior, not only dimensions.
- Social overcrowding is associated with lack of control over the environment and negative behavior.

## LEA Use

Human-behavior questions often ask which concept is being applied. Proxemics concerns interpersonal spacing and communication. Anthropometrics concerns body dimensions. Gestalt concerns visual perception and pattern grouping.
