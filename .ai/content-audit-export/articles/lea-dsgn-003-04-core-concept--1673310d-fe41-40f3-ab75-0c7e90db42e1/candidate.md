## Core Concept

Senior living facilities and low-income housing are classified as _Group A residential_ per **PD 1096**, with specific typologies defined by **BP 220** (Socialized Housing and Sustainable Community Development).

BP 220 distinguishes between two primary categories:

**Socialized Housing** targets families within the affordability level per NEDA (30% gross family income). Minimum lot areas vary by housing type per BP 220 Revised IRR Table 7:

- _Single Detached_: minimum lot area 64 sqm, minimum floor area 18 sqm per unit.
- _Duplex / Single Attached_: minimum lot area 48 sqm, minimum floor area 18 sqm per unit.
- _Row House_: minimum lot area 28 sqm, minimum floor area 18 sqm per unit.

**Economic Housing** serves families within the affordability level per NEDA (30% gross family income):

- _Single Detached_: minimum lot area 72 sqm, minimum floor area **24 sqm** per unit.
- _Duplex / Single Attached_: minimum lot area 54 sqm, minimum floor area **24 sqm** per unit.
- _Row House_: minimum lot area 36 sqm, minimum floor area **24 sqm** per unit.

These minimums ensure compliance with sanitation, ventilation, and accessibility standards while remaining affordable. Senior living facilities do not follow BP 220 minimums; use PD 1096 Group D/I standards (typically >42 sqm per NBC institutional requirements) to accommodate medical equipment and care spaces, though individual units may be classified as Group A-1 (single-family) or A-2 (multi-family) depending on ownership structure. Density calculations for these developments are regulated by **PD 957** (Subdivision Regulations), which cross-references BP 220 lot and road standards.

## Key Provisions

### BP 220 Unit Standards — Minimum Lot Area (sqm) per Table 7

| Housing Type | Socialized Lot | Economic Lot | Socialized Floor | Economic Floor |
|:----------------------- |:----------------------- |:----------------------- |:----------------------- |:--------------------------- |
| Single Detached | 64 | 72 | 18 | **24** |
| Duplex / Single Attached | 48 | 54 | 18 | **24** |
| Row House | 28 | 36 | 18 | **24** |

**Target income:** Families within affordability level per NEDA (30% gross family income).

### Road Network Standards (BP 220 Section 4)

| Classification | R.O.W. Width (m) |
|:------------- |:------------------------ |
| Major Road | 10 |
| Collector Road | 8 |
| Local Road | 6 |
| Alley | 2–3 |

### Population Density Computation

**Occupant Load Determination (RA 9514 Table 3.1-2, Residential)**

For low-income housing density planning:

- Average persons per unit: 4–5.
- Gross density (socialized SD): (10,000 sqm ÷ 64 sqm/lot) × 4 occupants ≈ **625 persons/hectare**.
- Gross density (socialized row house): (10,000 sqm ÷ 28 sqm/lot) × 4 occupants ≈ **1,429 persons/hectare**.

### Accessibility & Space Program (BP 344, PD 1096)

- Door width (accessible unit): 900 mm minimum.
- Stall (accessible parking): **3.7 m × 5.0 m** (2024 Revised IRR BP 344).
- Standard parking stall: 2.5 m × 5.0 m.
- Ramp gradient: **1:12 maximum** for ramps ≤3m rise.
- Ramp gradient (recommended): **1:20** (per BP 344 2024 Revised IRR Section 5).

## Philippine Practice Context

A typical 2-hectare socialized housing project in Metro Manila under BP 220 would contain:

- Lot size: 64 sqm (single detached socialized).
- Saleable area: 20,000 sqm ÷ 64 sqm = approximately 312 units.
- Accounting for 30% roads/amenities: 20,000 × 0.70 = 14,000 sqm buildable ÷ 64 = ~218 units.
- Unit density: ~200–220 units per net hectare.
- Common practice: mixed 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom floor plans with shared courtyards.

## How It's Tested

**Computation Pattern 1 (Difficulty 0.7):**
"A developer proposes a socialized housing project (new subdivision) on a 5-hectare site in Quezon City. Site coverage is 60%, average floor area is 24 sqm per unit. How many units can legally be developed?" 

- Answer: (50,000 sqm × 0.60) ÷ 64 sqm/lot = **468 units** (socialized SD minimum lot applies).

**Computation Pattern 2 (Difficulty 0.9):**
"For an infill socialized housing project in Manila with 150 units and a site of 6,000 sqm (excluding roads), calculate net density and verify compliance with BP 220 minimum lot requirement." 

- Required: 150 units × 28 sqm/lot = **4,200 sqm minimum** → available 6,000 sqm compliant.
- Actual lot size: 6,000 ÷ 150 = 40 sqm/unit (exceeds 28 sqm minimum).

**Application Pattern 3 (Difficulty 0.7):**
"Which occupancy group does a senior living care home for low-income seniors fall under?" 

- Answer: Low-income housing under BP 220 is classified as Group A residential per PD 1096. Senior living facilities (care homes, assisted living) are classified as **Group D** (institutions) or **Group I** per PD 1096 Section 303 if providing care services.

> **Memory rule:** Socialized SD = 64 sqm; Economic SD = 72 sqm; Row House socialized = 28 sqm; Row House economic = 36 sqm. The _housing type_ (SD vs. duplex vs. row) determines the minimum — check the type first, then the category (socialized vs. economic).

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing socialized SD (64 sqm) with economic SD (72 sqm) — always check category first.
- Using 72 sqm for socialized housing when 72 sqm is actually the ECONOMIC single detached value.
- Mixing up standard parking stall (2.5×5.0m) with accessible stall (**3.7 m × 5.0 m**).
- Confusing lot area minimums (64/48/28 socialized) with floor area minimums (18 sqm socialized vs **24 sqm economic**).
- Computing net site area without excluding roads and amenities (should deduct 20-35% for roads/amenities).
