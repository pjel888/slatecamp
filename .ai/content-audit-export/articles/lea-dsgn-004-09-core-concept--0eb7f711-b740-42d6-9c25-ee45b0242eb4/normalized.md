# Restaurants: Source-Safe Code Framing

## Source Scope

This local-first revision is limited to the checked PD 1096 RIRR, RA 9514 Revised IRR 2019, and Fire Code source-family excerpts. Prior ergonomic counter heights, kitchen-area ratios, local zoning-only parking claims, and kitchen workflow rules are not retained because they were outside the selected raw-source gate.

## LEA Anchor

Restaurant questions must separate building-code occupancy, fire-code occupancy, occupant load, egress, and parking/loading context. Do not mix these into one unsupported shortcut.

## Occupancy Classification

PD 1096 lists drinking and dining establishments with an occupant load of less than one hundred persons under Group E, Division E-2. Its parking table includes restaurants, fast-food centers, bars, and beerhouses in the checked customer-area parking row.

RA 9514 classifies restaurants and drinking establishments with fifty or more persons as assembly occupancies. Restaurants and drinking establishments with less than fifty persons are classified as mercantile occupancies. Office, storage, and service facilities incidental to the sale of merchandise and located in the same building are considered part of the mercantile occupancy classification in the checked text.

## Fire and Life Safety Checks

For assembly spaces, the checked RA 9514 text sets occupant load by assigned net area for concentrated use, less concentrated dining and drinking uses, standing or waiting space, and fixed seats. It also requires posting occupant load for assembly rooms without fixed seats. The Fire Code source family requires exits appropriate to occupancy character, occupant count, fire protection, height, and construction type.

## Exam Use

For LEA review, first determine whether the prompt is asking for PD 1096 occupancy, RA 9514 fire classification, assembly occupant load, or PD 1096 parking. Then use the matching checked source instead of importing unsupported restaurant design rules.
