## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal
Classify churches and places of worship per occupancy type, calculate assembly occupant load, apply egress standards for places of worship, and distinguish between Group C and Group H classification triggers.

Restaurants are classified as **Group E** (business/mercantile), not Group C, and require strict kitchen-to-dining ratios and FSDO/DOH regulatory compliance. Occupant load is calculated at 1.4 sqm per seat, with parking at 1 space per 30 sqm of dining area.

## Core Concept

Restaurants fall under **Group E** (business and mercantile) occupancy per **PD 1096**, distinguishing them from educational or assembly facilities. The core design challenge is balancing kitchen efficiency with patron circulation and fire safety egress. Occupant load for dining areas is calculated at **1.4 sqm per seated patron** per **RA 9514**. The kitchen-to-dining floor area ratio is **1:3** (one unit kitchen area to three units dining area), a critical trap in LEA exams.

Kitchen aisles require minimum **1.2 m width** to accommodate service and refuse handling. Food preparation counters are typically **850-900 mm height** (optimized for standing work). The dishwashing section must be physically separated from raw food preparation zones to prevent cross-contamination, per **DOH/FSDO food safety guidelines**. Parking calculations mandate **1 space per 30 sqm of dining area** (per local ordinance / NBC Rule XIX), distinguishing restaurants from hotels (1/room) and hospitals (1/4 beds).

Fire egress requirements apply as **Group E**: 45 m travel distance (unsprinklered) or 60 m (sprinklered), with exit width at **7.6 mm per occupant**. Dual exits are required at **50+ person occupancy** (RA 9514). Standard floor-to-floor height is **3.0 m** for multi-story restaurant blocks. Accessible features include **900 mm doors** (BP 344 2024 IRR) and **1:20 ramps (2024 Revised IRR)** (BP 344 §301).

**Kitchen-to-Dining Area Formula:**
Let DFA = total dining floor area (sqm)
Let KFA = kitchen floor area (sqm)
Ratio constraint: **KFA ÷ DFA ≥ 1 ÷ 3**, or **KFA = DFA ÷ 3**

Example: For 300 sqm dining, kitchen must be minimum 100 sqm.

**Parking Calculation:**
Spaces = Dining Floor Area ÷ 30 sqm/space

Example: 1500 sqm dining area ÷ 30 = 50 parking spaces minimum.

## Philippine Practice Context

Metro Manila restaurants operate under joint FSDO (Food Safety and Drug Office) and DOH (Department of Health) approval. Common compliance gaps include undersized dishwash areas, inadequate grease trap maintenance (DEP requirement), and kitchen-to-dining ratios compressed to 1:4 or 1:5 by developers seeking higher table count. Franchise concepts frequently import kitchen standards from US locations without accounting for Philippine tropical climate, ventilation loads, or local water quality. Heritage buildings converted to restaurants often trigger NHCP review. Health inspection failures commonly cite improper food prep zone boundaries, counter heights designed for non-Filipino anthropometric data, and separation failures between trash and food handling.

## How It's Tested

1. **Kitchen Ratio Trap (ERR-DSGN-031):** A 600 sqm restaurant space must allocate minimum 150 sqm to kitchen (1:3 ratio), not 120 sqm (1:5 ratio). Candidates often invert this—claiming kitchen should be "larger" than dining.
2. **Occupant Load Calculation:** A restaurant with 100 dining seats requires 140 sqm minimum (100 × 1.4 sqm/seat). Parking: If that 140 sqm dining area serves as the only dining, 5 parking spaces required (140 ÷ 30).
3. **Fire Egress Integration:** A ground-floor restaurant with 250-person occupancy and single kitchen entry/exit must have two separate dining egress routes, totaling 1900 mm exit width minimum (250 × 7.6 mm).
