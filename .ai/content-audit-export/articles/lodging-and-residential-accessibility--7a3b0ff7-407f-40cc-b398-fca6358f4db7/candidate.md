# Lodging and Residential Accessibility

## Learning Goal

Apply BP 344 2024 Revised IRR requirements for accessible guest rooms in hotels, apartels, dormitories, and other transient lodging facilities.

## Accessible Guest Room Count

BP 344 requires 2 percent of the total number of guest rooms of hotels, apartels, dormitories, and other transient lodging facilities to be accessible guest rooms.

Accessible guest rooms must include lever type door handles and dual-height peepholes, with the lower peephole installed 1000 mm from the floor.

## Reach Ranges And Room Features

Manual switches must be installed 1200 mm above the floor, measured from the center of the switch plate to the finished floor, and must be located 200 mm from the latch side of the door.

Electrical outlets must be installed not lower than 450 mm from the guest room floor to the center of the outlet. Beds must have a maximum height of 500 mm. Easy-to-open doors, preferably sliding closet doors, must be used. Adjustable or dual-height hanger racks must have the lower rack installed 1200 mm from the floor.

## Visual And Emergency Systems

Accessible guest rooms must have a visual warning system using supervised strobe lights activated and powered by the fire detection and alarm system. Strobe lights must have at least 2 candelas luminous intensity, produce no more than 3 flashes per second, have clear or white translucent lenses, and display the word FIRE.

The visual warning system must be installed in bedrooms, rooms closed off from the living area by a door except bathrooms, and the living area or hallway serving the living area. Staff must inform and orient guests with disabilities about the visual warning system. Emergency assistance buttons and vibrating pillow or bed alarms must be provided.
