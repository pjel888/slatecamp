### Table 4: Visual Fire Alarm Specifications (Sec. 17.8)

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Flash rate | **≤ 3 flashes per second** |
| Luminous intensity | **≥ 2 candelas** |
| Lens color | **White** |

> **Why white, not red?** White maximizes the contrast and visibility of the flash against room interiors. The word "FIRE" ensures the signal is not mistaken for a notification light.

### Table 6: 2024 IRR Quota Calculations — Practice Table

| Hotel Size | 2024 IRR (2%) | Govt-Owned (10%) | Old IRR |
|-----------|:-------------:|:----------------:|:-------:|
| 50 rooms | **1** | 5 | 1 |
| 100 rooms | **2** | 10 | 2 |

| 500 rooms | **10** | 50 | 6 (3+3) |
| 1,000 rooms | **20** | 100 | 11 (3+8) |

> **Key Insight:** For large hotels, the 2024 IRR's 2% rule requires MORE accessible rooms than the old tiered formula. For very small hotels (under 50 rooms), the old formula always required at least 1, while 2% of 30 = 0.6 → rounds up to **1** as well.

## REASON FOR THE LAW

### Why Lever Handles? (Sec. 17.1)
Round knobs require **grip, twist, and turn** — three distinct motor actions. Lever handles require only a **downward push**, operable with a closed fist, elbow, or prosthetic limb. RA 7277 mandates equal access, which means access even for those with limited hand dexterity.

### Why is Bed Height Capped at 500 mm? (Sec. 17.5)
The average wheelchair seat height is 480–520 mm. A bed at 500 mm allows a person to **slide laterally** from wheelchair to bed without lifting their body weight. Higher beds require a lift that many PWDs cannot perform independently — defeating the purpose of an "accessible" room.

### Why Visual Strobes AND Vibrating Alarms? (Sec. 17.8, 17.10)
Different disabilities require different leart modalities:
- **Deaf guests** may not hear audible alarms → visual strobes and vibrating alarms
- **Blind guests** cannot rely on visual cues → audible alarms remain
- **Both** may exist simultaneously → the room must cover all modalities

### Why 200 mm from the Latch Side for Switches? (Sec. 17.3)
A person in a wheelchair approaching a door from the latch side needs to **open the door AND reach the switch** without repositioning their chair. Placing the switch 200 mm from the latch side means it falls within the side reach range from the door-opening position.

### Why 10% for Government-Owned Properties?
Government facilities serve the broadest cross-section of the population, including those with limited resources who rely on public services. The higher quota reflects the **public trust obligation** of government to model accessibility, consistent with RA 7277's mandate on state responsibility.

## PROTÉGÉ EFFECT CHALLENGE

Your classmate submits a hotel design for review:

> *"The 80-room private hotel has 1 accessible room on the 3rd floor (no accessible elevator). The room has a round door knob, a peephole at 1500 mm, a light switch at 900 mm, outlets at 300 mm AFF, and a bed at 550 mm high. The fire alarm is a standard audible bell with a red strobe at 5 flashes per second. No vibrating pillow alarm is provided."*

**List every violation:**

*(Write your list before reading the answer key below)*

---

**ANSWER KEY:**

| # | Violation | Correct Standard |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Only 1 accessible room; need 2% of 80 = **1.6 → 2 rooms** | Sec. 17, 2024 IRR |
| 2 | Room on 3rd floor with no accessible elevator | Must have **at least 1 at ingress level** |
| 3 | Round door knob | Must be **lever handle** (Sec. 17.1) |
| 4 | Peephole at 1500 mm | Accessible peephole at **1000 mm** (Sec. 17.1) |
| 5 | Light switch at 900 mm | Must be **1200 mm** AFF (Sec. 17.2) |
| 6 | Outlets at 300 mm AFF | Must be **min 450 mm** AFF (Sec. 17.4) |
| 7 | Bed at 550 mm | Must be **max 500 mm** (Sec. 17.5) |
| 8 | Red strobe at 5 flashes/sec | White lens, **≤3 flashes/sec**, ≥2 candelas, "FIRE" on lens (Sec. 17.8) |
| 9 | No vibrating pillow alarm | **Required** for Deaf guests (Sec. 17.10) |

**Total violations: 9** — this room fails on every major provision.
