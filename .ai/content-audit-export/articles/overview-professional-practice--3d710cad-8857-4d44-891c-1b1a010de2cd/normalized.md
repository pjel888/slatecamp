## Learning Goal

Master the professional practice framework tested in the ALE, including: RA 9266 (The Architecture Act), SPP documents (200–208, 301), fee computation methods, construction contracts and bonds, professional ethics, CPD requirements, practice forms, and registration/liability provisions. By the end of this study map, you should understand the logical chain from legislation through services, fees, and contracts.

## Subject Coverage

Professional Practice & Jurisprudence is organized into six subtopics:

- **Architectural Services & Project Delivery** — pre-design, design, construction phase, post-construction; design-bid-build, design-build, construction management
- **Compensation, Fees & Payment Methods** — percentage-of-PCC, lump sum, time-based, per unit, cost-plus; SPP 301 standard fee scales
- **Contracts, Bonds & Construction Administration** — owner-architect and owner-contractor agreements; performance and payment bonds; change orders, submittals, certificates of payment
- **Practice Forms, CPD, Government & Emerging Requirements** — sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation; CPD units for license renewal
- **Professional Ethics & Code of Conduct** — UAP Code of Ethical Conduct, duties to client/public/profession
- **RA 9266 — Registration & Liability** — scope of practice, qualifications, Board of Architecture, grounds for suspension/revocation

## Study Strategy

### Step 1 — Master RA 9266 First

Key sections: Section 3 (definitions), Section 20 (scope of practice), Section 21 (exemptions), Sections 23–25 (licensure qualifications), Sections 30–31 (Board of Architecture), Section 37 (grounds for suspension/revocation).

### Step 2 — SPP 202 and SPP 301 in Detail

Know the five design phases and the standard percentage fee scales.

### Step 3 — Fee Computation Practice

Work through arithmetic problems: percentage of PCC, partial services, changes in scope.

### Step 4 — Ethics and Contracts

Review UAP Code of Ethical Conduct and construction contract types.

### Step 5 — CPD and Practice Forms

Sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation; CPD requirements for renewal.

## Common Exam Traps

- **Confusing fee computation methods.** Percentage-of-PCC, lump sum, time-based, per unit, and cost-plus are five distinct methods.
- **Mixing up bond types.** Performance bond (completion) vs. payment bond (subcontractors/suppliers) vs. surety bond (general category).
- **Underestimating ethics questions.** Subtle conflicts of interest, fee-splitting, referral situations — read UAP Code carefully.
- **Assuming the architect always has the same role.** Design-bid-build vs. design-build vs. construction management — different roles and authorities.
- **Confusing SPP document scopes.** SPP 201 is pre-design; SPP 202 is regular design; SPP 208 is design-build; SPP 301 is fees.
