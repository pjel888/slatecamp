## Core Concept

The Metals Lecture distinguishes soldering, brazing, and welding. Soldering joins metals using a lower-melting solder at relatively low temperatures. Because there is no alloying action between the solder and the joined metals, soldered joints have little tensile, shear, or impact strength and should not be used where a strong joint is required.

Brazing is described as a type of soldering with operating temperatures higher than soldering but lower than welding. It uses stronger, higher-melting alloys to fill joints, so the joint is stronger than ordinary soldering. The source notes brazing is generally used where shape, position, or metal composition is not adaptable to welding.

Welding joins metals so that there is an actual union of interatomic bonds. This may use close contact, heating, pressure, added molten metal, or combinations of these methods. Welding may be divided into pressure welding, where pressure and heat make the weld, and fusion welding, where heat and added metal make the weld. Fusion welding heating methods in the checked source are gas flame and electric arc.

Building Construction Illustrated treats welded connections as part of steel framing. It states that steel connections may be made with different connectors and combinations of bolts and welds, and that connection strength depends on member sizes, plates or angles, and the configuration of bolts or welds. It also lists welded or bolted members in steel trusses and metal decking secured by puddle welds or mechanical fasteners.

## How It Is Tested

Expect conceptual distinctions: soldering versus brazing versus welding, pressure versus fusion welding, gas flame versus electric arc, and where welds appear in building steel connections. If an item asks for a named welding code, qualification, inspection percentage, or symbol convention, use the item-supplied source rather than inventing it.

## Common Mistakes

Do not treat soldering, brazing, and welding as interchangeable. Do not make a weak soldered joint carry structural demand. Do not add external welding-code, certification, or inspection claims when the checked Batch 083 sources only support the general joining and connection concepts above.
