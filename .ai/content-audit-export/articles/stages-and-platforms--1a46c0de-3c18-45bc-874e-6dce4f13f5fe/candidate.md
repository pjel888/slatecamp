# Stages and Platforms

## Source Boundary

This article is limited to the stage, platform, and related projection-room provisions in Sections 1213 and 1214 of the 2004 Revised IRR of PD 1096.

## Stage Ventilation

Stage ventilators must be built of metal or another incombustible material and placed near the center above the highest part of the working stage. The total ventilator area must be at least 5 percent of the floor area inside the stage walls. The opening action must be spring or gravity operated, and glass must be protected against falling. Screens must not reduce the required ventilator area or interfere with operation.

## Stage Construction and Separation

Gridirons, fly galleries, and pinrails must be incombustible. The gridiron live load is not less than 367 kg/sq.m, and loft block wells must support 373 kg per linear meter.

Rooms accessory to the stage must be separated from each other and from the stage by not less than one-hour fire-resistive occupancy separation. The proscenium wall must be of incombustible construction with a fire-resistive rating of not less than two hours and must extend at least 1.20 m above the auditorium roof. Proscenium openings must be protected by a fire assembly with a fire-resistive rating of not less than one and one-half hours.

The stage floor must support at least 620 kg/sq.m. Trap doors must be built of wood not less than 50 mm nominal thickness.

## Platforms and Egress

An enclosed platform must have one or more ventilators that open to the outer air and have total area equal to 5 percent of the platform area. No ventilator is required when the enclosed platform floor area is not more than 45.00 sq.m.

A stage must have at least one exit at least 900 mm wide on each side. Each fly gallery must have a stair at least 750 mm wide. Each tier of dressing rooms must have at least two means of egress, each at least 750 mm wide.

## Projection Rooms

Section 1214 covers projection rooms for ribbon-type film over 22 mm and electric arc projection equipment. The projection room must be one-hour fire-resistive, at least 2.40 m high, and at least 7.00 sq.m in floor area, plus 3.50 sq.m for each additional machine. Film in the projection room must be stored in metal cabinets or accepted shipping containers.

## Exam Use

For LEA review, keep the stage numbers distinct from projection-room numbers: 5 percent ventilators, 367 kg/sq.m gridiron live load, 620 kg/sq.m stage floor load, 45.00 sq.m platform exception, 900 mm stage exits, 750 mm fly-gallery and dressing-room egress, and 2.40 m / 7.00 sq.m projection-room minimums.
