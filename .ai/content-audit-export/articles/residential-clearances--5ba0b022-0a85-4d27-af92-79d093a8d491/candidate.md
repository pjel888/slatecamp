# Residential Clearances

Residential clearances in PD 1096 2004 Revised IRR Rule VIII are tied to light, ventilation, open space, yards, courts, and building class. The LEA often tests whether the examinee can distinguish R-1, R-2, R-3, R-4, and R-5 rules instead of using one generic residential setback.

## Rule VIII Baseline

Every building must be designed, constructed, and equipped to provide adequate light and ventilation. Buildings must face a street, public alley, or duly approved private street. Existing buildings cannot be altered to reduce room sizes, window relationships, courts, or yards below the Rule VIII requirements.

Site occupancy is measured at ground level and excludes courts, yards, and light wells. If existing and proposed buildings share a lot, the existing building footprint is included in the PSO computation. If a maximum PSO conflicts with another light and ventilation requirement, the lesser footprint or ground-floor gross floor area prevails.

## Residential PSO and TOSL

Table VIII.1 gives residential PSO, impervious-surface, unpaved-surface, and Total Open Space within Lot controls. Basic R-2 is shown with 55 or 60 percent maximum PSO depending on the table condition; maximum R-2 uses 60 or 70 percent; basic R-3 uses 65 or 70 percent; maximum R-3 uses 70 or 80 percent; R-4 uses 70 or 80 percent; and R-5 uses 70 or 80 percent.

For the exam, read the entire table row. PSO is not the only number: ISA, USA, and TOSL are part of the same clearance system.

## Residential Setbacks and Abutments

Table VIII.2 gives residential setbacks. R-1 uses 4.50 m front and 2.00 m side/rear setbacks. Basic R-2 and basic R-3 use 3.00 m front and 2.00 m side/rear values in the table. Maximum R-2 and maximum R-3 show an 8.00 m total front setback at grade level, with the upper floors returning to the minimum 3.00 m setback unless the Code provides otherwise. R-4 uses 4.50 m front and 2.00 m optional side/rear conditions, while R-5 uses 6.00 m front and 3.00 m side/rear.

Rule VIII also limits abutments by residential class. R-1 lots do not allow abutments at any property line. R-2 allows abutment on only one side, not on the front or rear. R-3 and R-5 allow limited two-side or one-side-plus-rear conditions, with no front abutment. R-4 allows abutments on two sides only, with no front or rear firewall or abutment.

## Exam Use

First identify the residential class and whether the item asks for PSO/TOSL, yard/setback, court width, abutment, or ceiling height. Then apply the specific Rule VIII table and notes. When provisions conflict, use the more stringent light and ventilation restriction.
