# Architect-of-Record vs Architect-in-Charge of Construction Liability

## LEA Point

RA 9266 separates the Architect-of-Record from the Architect-in-Charge of Construction. The Architect-of-Record is the registered and licensed architect responsible for the total architectural design and for the plans, specifications, and contract documents the architect signs and seals. The Architect-in-Charge of Construction is the registered and licensed architect responsible for construction supervision.

Civil Code Article 1723 then asks why the edifice failed. If the cause is a defect in plans or specifications, analyze the professional who drew and sealed those documents. If the cause is a construction defect, inferior material, contract violation, or defective supervision, analyze the contractor and the professional who undertook supervision.

## Source Anchors

- RA 9266 IRR Section 3(2)(a): Architect-of-Record means the architect directly and professionally responsible for the total design and civilly liable for signed and sealed plans, specifications, and contract documents.
- RA 9266 IRR Section 3(2)(b): Architect-in-Charge of Construction means the architect directly and professionally responsible and liable for construction supervision.
- RA 9266 IRR Sections 20 and 32: the architect-of-record is responsible for architectural documents issued under the architect's seal or authorized signature.
- Civil Code Article 1723: collapse within fifteen years from completion is allocated by cause, including defective plans or specifications, construction defects, inferior materials, contract violations, and defective supervision.

## Exam Method

First identify the cause of failure. Design-document defects point to the Architect-of-Record. Construction defects and inferior materials point first to the contractor. If a supervising architect undertook construction supervision and defective supervision contributed to the failure, Article 1723 can make that supervising professional solidarily liable with the contractor.

Do not merge the two roles. One architect may have exposure under both tracks only when the facts show that the architect accepted both design-document responsibility and construction-supervision responsibility.

## Real World Scenario

One registered architect signs and seals the architectural plans as Architect-of-Record, while another registered architect is engaged to supervise construction. If a later collapse is tied to a design-document defect, analyze the AoR track; if the collapse is tied to construction defects, inferior materials, or defective supervision, analyze the contractor and the Architect-in-Charge of Construction track. The same person can be exposed on both tracks only if the facts show that the person held both roles.
