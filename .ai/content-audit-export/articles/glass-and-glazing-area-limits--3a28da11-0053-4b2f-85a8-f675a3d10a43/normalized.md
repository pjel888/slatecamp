# Glass and Glazing Area Limits

## Board Exam Quick Check

For PD 1096 glazing questions, first identify whether the item asks about scope, panel labeling, area limits, louvered windows, or impact exceptions. The controlling local source for this article is the 2004 Revised IRR of PD 1096, Rule XVIII Sections 1801 through 1805.

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to apply the PD 1096 glass and glazing limits for exterior glass, safety glass, louvered windows, and impact-prone glazed openings.

## Source-Grounded Core Rules

### Scope of Rule XVIII

Rule XVIII applies to exterior glass and glazing in all uses and occupancies except Groups A, B, and J occupancies that are not over three storeys in height. It also applies to interior and exterior glass and glazing in all occupancies when the glass is subject to human impact.

Each glass panel must bear the manufacturer's label showing the glass type and thickness. Special-performance glass, such as laminated, heat-strengthened, fully tempered, or insulated glass, must bear permanent identification showing its special characteristics and thickness, visible after glazing.

Fixed glass panels must be made apparent where the floor on both sides is approximately at the same level. Source-supported deterrents include horizontal bars at guardrail height, a 450 mm opaque bulkhead, distinctive glass such as etched or translucent glass, fixed flower bins, or another appropriate construction arrangement.

### Area limitation and safety glass trigger

Exterior glass and glazing must safely withstand wind pressure acting inward or outward for the applicable height zone. The individual light must not exceed the maximum allowable glass area under the wind load multiplied by the appropriate adjustment factor.

A glass panel adjacent to a wall opening must be safety glass when it is more than 600 mm wide and 180 mm or more high, unless a bulkhead of opaque material at least 450 mm high is provided.

The Rule XVIII table for large glass areas in shopping malls, commercial buildings, theaters, offices, institutional public buildings, and factories other than Groups A, B, and J gives these width and length limits:

| Glass thickness | Maximum width | Maximum length |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 mm | 1.10 m and below | 1.10 m and below |
| 10 mm | 2.25 m and below | 2.25 m and below |
| 12 mm | 3.00 m and below | 3.00 m and below |
| 15 mm | Over 3.00 m | Over 3.00 m |

### Glazing support

Glass firmly supported on all four edges must be glazed with required minimum laps and edge clearances. Edge clearance in fixed openings must be sufficient for wind and earthquake drift. If glass is not firmly supported on all four edges, the design must be submitted for Building Official approval.

Glass support is considered firm when support deflection at design load does not exceed 1/175 of the span.

### Louvered windows

Regular plate, sheet, or patterned glass in jalousies and louvered windows must not be thinner than 5.6 mm and must not be longer than 1.20 m. Exposed glass edges must be smooth.

### Impact-prone glass

Frameless glass doors, glass in doors, fixed glass panels, and similar glazed openings subject to accidental human impact must conform with the impact-load requirements. The listed exceptions are:

- bathtub and shower enclosures made from approved shatter-resistant material, including wire-reinforced glass at least 5.6 mm thick, fully tempered glass at least 4.8 mm thick, or laminated safety glass at least 6.4 mm thick;
- glass lights located at least 450 mm above the adjacent finished floor or walking surface;
- glass lights whose least dimension is not greater than 450 mm;
- glass lights 1.50 sq.m. or less in area.

## Common Exam Traps

- Do not apply the exterior glass area table to exempt Group A, B, or J occupancies not over three storeys.
- Do not forget that impact-prone interior glass can still be covered even when exterior area limits do not apply.
- Do not use 5.0 mm jalousie glass as compliant; the PD 1096 value is 5.6 mm minimum.
- Do not treat the safety glass trigger as width-only. The source uses both width and height conditions.
