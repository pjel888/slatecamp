# Parking Demand and Building Use

## Source Boundary

This article is limited to PD 1096 Rule VII parking requirements and BP 344 2024 accessible parking requirements. It does not use market parking ratios or local zoning assumptions unless a question supplies them.

## Use Controls Parking Demand

PD 1096 treats parking as an occupancy and use issue. The parking slot, parking area, and loading/unloading requirements are minimum off-street, on-site requirements for specific uses and occupancies. They must be located outside the road right-of-way.

Table VII.4 ties parking demand to the declared use or occupancy. Residential, hotel, school, commercial, industrial, assembly, and other uses are not interchangeable. In computing parking slots, a fraction of 50 percent and above is counted as one car parking slot, and at least one car parking slot is required unless the rule allows otherwise.

## Slot Sizes

The average automobile slot is computed at 2.50 m by 5.00 m for perpendicular or diagonal parking and 2.15 m by 6.00 m for parallel parking. A standard truck or bus parking/loading slot is at least 3.60 m by 12.00 m. A jeepney or shuttle parking/loading/unloading slot is at least 3.00 m by 9.00 m.

## Accessible Parking Overlay

PD 1096 expressly points accessible parking back to BP 344 and its IRR. BP 344 2024 then sets the accessible parking count, location, slot dimension, transfer space, surface, and signage rules. Accessible parking slots are located nearest to accessible main entrances and must not be placed in ramped or sloping areas.

## Exam Use

For LEA review, answer parking questions by identifying the use, applying the PD 1096 minimum off-street/on-site rule, checking the slot type and size, rounding fractions of 50 percent and above, and then applying BP 344 when accessible parking is involved.
