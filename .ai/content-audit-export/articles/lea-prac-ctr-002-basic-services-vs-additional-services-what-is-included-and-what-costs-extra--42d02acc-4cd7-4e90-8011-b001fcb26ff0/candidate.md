# Basic Services, Additional Services, and Separate Compensation

SPP 202 provides a clean exam boundary between regular design services, reimbursable expenses, additional services, and separately paid specialist or presentation services. The safe reading is not that every requested task is automatically included in the basic fee.

## Basic service compensation

For regular design services, SPP 202 lists several compensation methods, including percentage of project construction cost, multiple direct personnel expense plus other costs, professional fee plus expenses, lump sum or fixed fee, per diem or honorarium plus reimbursables, and mixed methods. The selected method should fit the project and be clear enough to avoid compensation disputes.

## Additional and separately paid services

The checked SPP 202 lines state that additional services require additional compensation. Other services recommended by the architect for owner approval are paid separately and may include a coordination fee. Scale models, three-dimensional models, and walkthrough presentations are likewise recommended for owner approval and paid separately with a coordination fee.

Owner-ordered changes after approval of drawings, specifications, or other documents also require payment for the architect's extra time, resources, drafting, and office expenses. Reimbursables, taxes, travel and living expenses for work beyond the distance threshold, and extra document sets are separate from the professional fee.

## Exam boundary

A source-safe answer should ask whether the task is regular design service, additional service, separately paid presentation or specialist work, owner-ordered change after approval, or reimbursable expense. Do not assume a service is included merely because the client requested it.
