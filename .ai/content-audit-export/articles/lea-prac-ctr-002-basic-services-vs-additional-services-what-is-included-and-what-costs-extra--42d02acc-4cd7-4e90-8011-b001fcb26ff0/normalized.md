# Basic Services vs Additional Services: What Is Included and What Costs Extra

SPP 202 and the 401-402 proforma agreement give a practical boundary for LEA questions: regular/basic services are not a blank check for every owner request. Some work belongs to the basic service phases; some work is additional, separately paid, reimbursable, or excluded from the agreement.

## Basic Services

The checked 401-402 agreement organizes basic services into schematic design, design development, contract documents, and construction phase duties. These include confirming requirements, preparing schematic studies, preparing design development drawings and outline specifications, preparing construction drawings and specifications, assisting with permits and bidding documents when instructed, making periodic visits, reviewing submissions for conformance, preparing change orders, and issuing certificates for payment and completion.

SPP 202 then ties regular design services to compensation methods such as percentage of project construction cost, multiple direct personnel expense, professional fee plus expenses, lump sum or fixed fee, per diem or honorarium plus reimbursables, and mixed methods.

## Additional and Separately Paid Services

SPP 202 states that additional services require additional compensation. Other services needed to complete the project, such as acoustic or illumination specialists, mural painters, sculptors, and other service providers, are recommended by the Architect for Owner approval, paid separately by the Owner, and subject to a coordination fee payable to the Architect.

Scale models, three-dimensional models, and walkthrough presentations are also recommended for Owner approval and paid separately with a coordination fee. Owner-ordered changes after approval of the Architect's outputs require payment for extra time, resources, drafting, or other office expenses.

## Reimbursables and Owner-Funded Inputs

The Owner pays reimbursable expenses and government taxes listed in the source. A per diem plus travel and living expenses is chargeable when the Architect or authorized representative must perform services beyond 50.0 kilometers from the established office. The Owner also pays for extra sets of contract documents and for site information, tests, specialty consulting, legal, auditing, insurance, and similar services when required.

## Agreement Exclusions

The 401-402 proforma notes that it does not cover full-time construction supervision, project or construction management, pre-design services such as space planning and research, allied architectural design services such as architectural interiors or site/physical planning, and design-build services.

## Real World Scenario

During design development, an owner asks for a marketing walkthrough, acoustic consultant, and a redesigned facade after approving the prior drawings. The source-safe response is to separate those items from ordinary phase deliverables: the walkthrough and specialist work are paid separately with owner approval, and the post-approval redesign can require additional payment for extra time and resources.

## Source Anchors

- UAP Document 401-402: lines 43-75 for basic service phase duties.
- UAP Document Standards/SPP 202: lines 414-457 for compensation methods.
- UAP Document Standards/SPP 202: lines 473-493 for additional services, separately paid services, scale/3D/walkthrough presentations, per diem/travel, extra document sets, and owner-ordered changes.
- UAP Document 401-402: lines 18-20 and 109-126 for exclusions, chargeable expenses, abandoned work, full-time supervision, and estimates.
