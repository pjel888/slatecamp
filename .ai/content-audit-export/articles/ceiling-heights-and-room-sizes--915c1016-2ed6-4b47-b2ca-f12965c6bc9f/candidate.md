# Ceiling Heights and Room Sizes

## Board Exam Quick Check

For PD 1096 room-size questions, first separate the rule being tested: ceiling height, minimum room area, air space per person, or window opening. The controlling local source for this article is the 2004 Revised IRR of PD 1096, Rule VIII Sections 805 through 808.

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to apply the PD 1096 minimums for ceiling heights, room sizes, air space, and window openings without importing unsupported foreign-code comparisons.

## Source-Grounded Core Rules

### Ceiling heights

Habitable rooms with artificial ventilation must have a ceiling height of at least 2.40 m measured from floor to ceiling. For buildings of more than one storey, the first storey must be at least 2.70 m, the second storey at least 2.40 m, and succeeding storeys must have unobstructed headroom clearance of at least 2.10 m above the finished floor.

Habitable rooms with natural ventilation must have ceiling heights of at least 2.70 m.

Mezzanine floors must have clear ceiling height of at least 1.80 m both above and below the mezzanine.

### Minimum room sizes and dimensions

Section 806 gives both minimum floor area and least horizontal dimension. Both must be satisfied.

| Room type | Minimum area | Least dimension |
|---|---:|---:|
| Room for human habitation | 6.00 sq.m. | 2.00 m |
| Kitchen | 3.00 sq.m. | 1.50 m |
| Bath and toilet | 1.20 sq.m. | 900 mm |

A narrow room can fail even if its area is large enough. A 6.00 sq.m. room that is only 1.50 m wide is not compliant as a room for human habitation because the least dimension is below 2.00 m.

### Air-space requirements

Section 807 adds per-person air-space minimums:

| Room type | Minimum air space |
|---|---:|
| School rooms | 3.00 cu.m. with 1.00 sq.m. floor area per person |
| Workshops, factories, and offices | 12.00 cu.m. per person |
| Habitable rooms | 14.00 cu.m. per person |

The room-size rule and air-space rule answer different questions. Section 806 asks whether the room is large enough as a room. Section 807 asks how much occupant air space must be available.

### Window openings

Rooms not provided with artificial ventilation must have windows with total free opening area equal to at least 10% of the room floor area, and the opening must not be less than 1.00 sq.m.

Toilet and bath rooms, laundry rooms, and similar rooms must have windows with area at least 1/20 of the floor area, and the opening must not be less than 240 sq.mm. Required windows must open directly to a court, yard, public street or alley, or open watercourse.

Required windows may open into a roofed porch if the porch abuts a court, yard, public street or alley, open watercourse, or other public open space; has a ceiling height of at least 2.70 m; and has one longer side at least 65% open and unobstructed.

## Exam Values To Memorize

| Requirement | Source-grounded value |
|---|---:|
| Artificially ventilated habitable room | 2.40 m minimum ceiling |
| Artificial ventilation, first storey of multi-storey building | 2.70 m minimum ceiling |
| Artificial ventilation, second storey | 2.40 m minimum ceiling |
| Artificial ventilation, succeeding storeys | 2.10 m unobstructed headroom |
| Naturally ventilated habitable rooms | 2.70 m minimum ceiling |
| Mezzanine clear height | 1.80 m above and below |
| Human-habitation room | 6.00 sq.m.; least dimension 2.00 m |
| Kitchen | 3.00 sq.m.; least dimension 1.50 m |
| Bath and toilet | 1.20 sq.m.; least dimension 900 mm |
| Natural-ventilation window opening | 10% of floor area; minimum 1.00 sq.m. |
| Toilet/bath/laundry window opening | 1/20 of floor area; minimum 240 sq.mm. |

## Common Exam Traps

- Do not cite Section 811 for the window-opening rule in this local source. In the checked DPWH raw markdown, window openings are under Section 808.
- Do not use area alone for room compliance. The least-dimension rule must also pass.
- Do not apply the 2.10 m value to the first or second storey. It applies to succeeding storeys in the artificial-ventilation multi-storey rule.
- Do not treat the mezzanine rule as one-sided. The 1.80 m clear height must be provided above and below.
