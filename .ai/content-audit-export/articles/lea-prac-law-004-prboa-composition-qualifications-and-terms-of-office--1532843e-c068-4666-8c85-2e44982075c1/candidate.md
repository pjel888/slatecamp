## Board Exam Quick Check

For PRBoA composition questions, answer from the appointment chain in RA 9266 IRR Sections 4 to 6: **IAPOA nominees, Commission recommendees, Presidential appointment, three Board seats**.

## PRBoA Composition, Qualifications, and Terms of Office

The Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture is the architecture profession's regulatory board under the Professional Regulation Commission. The verified source for this article is the RA 9266 IRR, Sections 4, 5, and 6.

### Composition and Appointment

Section 4 creates the Professional Regulatory Board of Architecture as a collegial body under the supervision and administrative control of the Professional Regulation Commission. The Board is composed of one chairman and two members.

The appointment path has three steps:

1. The integrated and accredited professional organization of architects submits five nominees for each Board position to the Commission.
2. The Commission chooses three recommendees from that list.
3. The President of the Philippines appoints the chairman or member from the Commission's recommendees.

This means the Board is not self-appointed by architects and is not appointed directly from an unlimited political pool. The professional organization supplies nominees, the Commission screens, and the President appoints.

### Member Qualifications

Section 5 requires each Board member, at appointment, to be a citizen and resident of the Philippines; hold a Bachelor of Science in Architecture degree from a CHED-recognized or accredited school, college, or university in the Philippines or abroad; be an architect with a valid Certificate of Registration and Professional Identification Card; and be an active practitioner of architecture for at least ten years on the appointment date.

The same section bars current faculty or review-course participants in architecture schools, colleges, universities, or review institutions, and requires former faculty or review personnel to have officially resigned and stopped teaching, advising, or review activities for at least five years before nomination. It also requires that the member has never been convicted of a crime involving moral turpitude and is not an elective officer of the IAPOA or another professional organization of architects.

### Term and Reappointment

Section 6 sets a three-year term after appointment or until successors are appointed and qualified. A vacancy is filled only for the unexpired portion of the term. Each Board member may be reappointed for one full three-year term.

For the first Board under RA 9266, the initial terms were staggered: one member as chairman for three years, one member for two years, and one member for one year. The stagger prevents all seats from expiring at once.

### Exam Takeaway

Memorize the numbers only after anchoring the structure: three Board seats; five nominees per position; three recommendees; ten years active practice; three-year term; one full reappointment; initial stagger of three, two, and one years.
