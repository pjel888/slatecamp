# Project Delivery Source Terms: Bidding, Design-Build, PM, CM, and Owner's Representative

For LEA project-delivery questions, anchor the answer on source terms used by the local UAP and CIAP documents.

Regular Design Services include a Bidding or Negotiation Phase. In that phase, the architect prepares bid documents such as forms for contract letting, documents for construction, invitation and instruction forms, proposal forms, and general or specific conditions of contract. For competitive procurement, the architect furnishes bid document sets, helps organize pre-bid conferences, responds to bidders' questions, obtains proposals, analyzes bid results, and prepares notices and construction contract documents.

CIAP Document 102 defines an Advertisement or Invitation to Bid as notice or invitation to prospective bidders that gives project and bidding information. It defines Bid Documents as documents made available to prospective bidders, including the Invitation to Bid and a copy of the contract the winning bidder must sign. It defines Contract Documents as documents attached to the agreement, generally including special provisions, general conditions, drawings, specifications, and other bid documents.

SPP 207 gives the source-safe design-build comparison. It names two types: Design-Build Services by Administration and Design-Build Services with Guaranteed Maximum Project Construction Cost.

SPP 206 and the compiled SPP describe comprehensive architectural services and project management. Comprehensive Architectural Services cover pre-design, regular design, specialized architectural services, construction services, and post-construction services. Project Management involves management activities beyond normal architectural and engineering services, with the objective of control over time, cost, and quality. The presence of a Project Manager does not relieve designers and contractors of their normal duties and responsibilities.

CIAP Document 102 also defines the Owner's Representative role. The Owner's Representative may be the Architect, Engineer, Construction Manager, or another designated person. If the owner does not designate someone in writing for a building project, the Architect performs the functions and has the authority of the Owner's Representative. The Owner's Representative also performs the Construction Manager function unless the Owner designates another Construction Manager.

Use the labels carefully. Bidding terms belong to the regular design/bid-document lane. Design-build belongs to SPP 207. Project Management and Construction Management belong to the comprehensive services/owner coordination lane. Owner's Representative authority is governed by the contract and CIAP Document 102.

## Real World Scenario

A client asks whether the architect should call the next step bidding, design-build, project management, construction management, or owner representation. Start with the source term. If the task is preparing and issuing bid documents, answering bidder questions, obtaining proposals, and preparing contract documents, it belongs to the Regular Design Services bidding or negotiation lane. If one party will carry both design and construction responsibility, use the SPP 207 design-build lane. If the owner names a representative under CIAP Document 102, that person acts for the owner under the contract; absent a written designation on a building project, the architect performs the Owner's Representative functions.
