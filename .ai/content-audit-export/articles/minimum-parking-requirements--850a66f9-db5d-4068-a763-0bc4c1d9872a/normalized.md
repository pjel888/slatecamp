## Learning Goal

After reading this article, you should be able to:

Calculate minimum off-street parking requirements by occupancy group, location (CBD vs non-CBD), and building size under PD 1096 Rule XVIII and local zoning ordinances; distinguish parking space dimensions for standard cars, compact cars, and accessible stalls; and apply the 50% reduction allowance for mixed-use developments.
---

You are designing a 10-story condominium in Quezon City. The developer insists on 80 residential units at 48 sq.m. GFA each to maximize sleas revenue. You start with two basement levels.

If you misapply the parking ratio — using the old table instead of the 2004 Revised IRR, or rounding down instead of up — you will either:

- **Under-provide** parking and fail to get a building permit, or
- **Over-provide** parking and waste millions on unnecessary basement excavation.

In the LEA, parking questions appear in nearly every sitting. The exam tests not just the ratios, but your ability to select the correct table, apply the correct bracket, and round correctly.[web:2][web:4]

## Critical Warning: Two Tables Are in Circulation

Before memorizing any ratios, understand this fundamental fact: two sets of parking standards appear in LEA question banks and reviewers.[web:2][web:4]

**Additional Parking Location Rules (Sec. 707.4, 2004 Revised IRR & BP 344)**

- All parking slots must be located **outside the Road Right-of-Way (RROW)**; the street is public domain — no private parking on it.[web:2][web:5]
- Accessible (PWD) parking must be located **closest to an accessible main entrance**; in multi-storey indoor parking, these slots must be right next to accessible elevators or as near as possible to accessible pedestrian entrances.[file:16]

## Part 2: Residential Parking Ratios

### Old Summary Table (Frequently Tested As-Is)

Source: Pre-2004 simplified parking table; confirmed in board exam question banks.[web:4]

| Occupancy Type                 | Condition                                       | Minimum Required Parking       |
| :----------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------- |
| **Low-Income Single Detached** | Individual lots not more than 100 sq.m.         | **1 pooled slot per 10 units** |
| **Multi-Family Dwelling**      | Average living floor area up to 50 sq.m.        | **1 slot per 8 units**         |
| **Multi-Family Dwelling**      | Average living floor area above 50 to 100 sq.m. | **1 slot per 4 units**         |
| **Multi-Family Dwelling**      | Average living floor area more than 100 sq.m.   | **1 slot per 1 unit**          |

### 2004 Revised IRR — Table VII.4 (Detailed Current Standard)

#### Group A, Division A-1: Single-Family and Multi-Family in Residential Subdivisions

| Lot Size and/or GFA                                     | Minimum Required Parking     |
| :------------------------------------------------------ | :--------------------------- |
| Lot: 32–72 sq.m. and/or GFA: 18–22 sq.m.                | 1 pooled slot per **6 lots** |
| Lot: 50–96 sq.m. and/or GFA: 30–42 sq.m.                | 1 pooled slot per **4 lots** |
| Lot: 100–120 sq.m. and/or GFA: 30–42 sq.m.              | 1 slot per **each lot**      |
| Lot: more than 120 sq.m. and/or GFA: more than 42 sq.m. | 1 slot per **each lot**      |

[web:2][web:4]

#### Group A, Division A-2: Multi-Family in R-5 Residential Condominiums

> **As amended by DPWH Department Order No. 059, Series of 2019** (amends Table VII.4-1, Group A 1.2, Division A-2).[web:6][web:10]

**PATTERN INTERRUPT — R-5 CONDOMINIUM PARKING TABLE (AMENDED)**

| GFA per Unit                      | Minimum Required Parking      | "Or Fraction Thereof"?                           |
| :-------------------------------- | :---------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- |
| **18.00 to 22.00 sq.m.**          | 1 pooled slot per **8 units** | Yes — round up[web:6][web:10]                    |
| **Above 22.00 up to 50.00 sq.m.** | 1 pooled slot per **6 units** | Yes — round up[web:6][web:10]                    |
| **Above 50.00 to 100.00 sq.m.**   | 1 pooled slot per **4 units** | Yes — round up[web:6][web:10]                    |
| **More than 100.00 sq.m.**        | **1 slot per unit**           | Exactly 1:1 per unit (no pooling)[web:6][web:10] |

> **"Or fraction thereof" explained:** If you have 25 units at 1 slot per 6 units, that is 25 / 6 = 4.17. Round UP to **5 slots required**. Any remainder above a whole number triggers an additional slot; never round down.[web:6][web:10]

## Part 3: Commercial, Institutional & Assembly Parking Ratios

### Side-by-Side Comparison: Old Table vs. 2004 Revised IRR Table VII.4

| Occupancy Type                                  | Old Summary Table                               | 2004 Revised IRR (Table VII.4)                                                                                          |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hotels — Highly Urbanized Cities**            | 1 slot per **10 rooms**                         | 1 slot per **3 rooms** + **2 tourist bus slots** per hotel[web:2][web:4]                                                |
| **Hotels — All Other Areas**                    | 1 slot per **10 rooms**                         | 1 slot per **7 rooms** + **2 tourist bus slots** per hotel[web:2][web:4]                                                |
| **Residential Hotels / Apartels**               | 1 slot per **5 units**                          | 1 slot per **5 units** + 1 bus slot per 60 rooms/units[web:2][web:4]                                                    |
| **Motels**                                      | 1 slot per **unit**                             | 1 slot per **unit**[web:2][web:4]                                                                                       |
| **Pension / Boarding / Lodging Houses**         | 1 slot per **20 beds**                          | 1 slot per **20 beds**[web:2][web:4]                                                                                    |
| **Theaters / Cinemas / Auditoria** (Group H)    | 1 slot per **50 sq.m.** of spectator area       | 1 car slot + 1 jeepney/shuttle slot per **50 sq.m.** spectator area; + 1 bus slot per **200 spectators**[web:2][web:12] |
| **Markets**                                     | 1 slot per **150 sq.m.** of market floor area   | Varies by market classification[web:2][web:12]                                                                          |
| **Shopping Centers**                            | 1 slot per **100 sq.m.** of shopping floor area | 1 slot per **100 sq.m.**[web:2][web:12]                                                                                 |
| **Restaurants / Fast Food / Bars**              | 1 slot per **30 sq.m.** of customer area        | 1 slot per **30 sq.m.** of customer area[web:2][web:12]                                                                 |
| **Nightclubs / Theater-Restaurants**            | 1 slot per **20 sq.m.** of customer area        | 1 slot per **20 sq.m.** of customer area[web:2][web:12]                                                                 |
| **Office Buildings**                            | 1 slot per **125 sq.m.** of GFA                 | 1 slot per **125 sq.m.** of GFA[web:2][web:12]                                                                          |
| **Elementary / Secondary / Vocational Schools** | 1 slot per **10 classrooms**                    | 1 slot per **10 classrooms**[web:2][web:12]                                                                             |
| **Colleges / Universities**                     | 1 slot per **5 classrooms**                     | 1 slot per **5 classrooms**[web:2][web:12]                                                                              |
| **Private Hospitals**                           | —                                               | 1 slot per **12 beds**[web:2][web:12]                                                                                   |
| **Public Hospitals**                            | —                                               | 1 slot per **25 beds**[web:2][web:12]                                                                                   |

**Reason for the Law:** Hotels in highly urbanized cities attract more vehicular traffic than comparable hotels in smaller towns — both from guests arriving by car and from restaurants, events, and banquet functions that draw non-guests. The 2004 IRR's urbanization-tier distinction for hotels reflects this empirical difference, while the 2-tourist-bus-slot flat requirement ensures tour operators always have a designated off-street zone regardless of hotel size.[web:2][web:12]

**Key dimensional and location rules (amended BP 344):**

- Minimum accessible parking slot size: **4.00 m width x 5.00 m length**.[file:16]
- Provide a **walkway with minimum clear width of 1.20 m** between the front ends of parked cars serving accessible slots.[file:16]
- Accessible parking slots must be located **nearest to accessible main entrances** and, in multi-storey indoor parking, next to accessible elevators or as close as possible to accessible pedestrian entrances.[file:16]
- Accessible parking slots must not be located in ramped or sloping areas.[file:16]

---

**PATTERN INTERRUPT — TRAP #6: Always Round UP**

## Quick-Fire Mnemonics

**Slot Dimensions — "2.5-5 / 2.15-6 / 3-9 / 3.6-12 / 4-5"**

- Car perpendicular: **2.5 x 5**
- Car parallel: **2.15 x 6** (wider arc needs longer space)
- Jeepney: **3 x 9**
- Bus/Truck: **3.6 x 12**
- PWD: **4 x 5** (extra width + walkway)[web:2][web:12][file:16]

**R-5 Condominium Parking — "8-6-4-1"**

- 18–22 sq.m. → 1 per 8 units
- > 22 up to 50 sq.m. → 1 per 6 units
- 50–100 sq.m. → 1 per 4 units
- Over 100 sq.m. → 1 per 1 unit
- Bigger unit = more cars = smaller denominator = more parking slots.[web:6][web:10]

**Hotel Ratios — "3-7-10"**

- Highly urbanized: 1 per **3** rooms
- All other areas: 1 per **7** rooms
- Old table: 1 per **10** rooms
- Always add **2 tourist bus slots** per hotel (2004 Revised IRR).[web:2][web:12]

**PWD Provision — "Bands then Percent"**

- 1–25 = 1 slot, 26–50 = 2, 51–75 = 3, 76–100 = 4, 101–150 = 5, 151–200 = 6, 201–300 = 7, 301–400 = 8, 401–500 = 9.[file:16]
- 501–1000 = **2%** of total slots; 1001+ = **20 + 1 per 100 or fraction thereof** over 1000.[file:16]

**The "Fraction Thereof" Rule — Always Round UP**

- Any decimal result → next whole number.
- Never round down, no matter how small the fraction.[web:6][web:10][file:16]
