# Minimum Parking Requirements

## What the LEA expects

Use PD 1096 RIRR Table VII.4 for ordinary off-street parking and loading. Use the 2024 BP 344 IRR for accessible parking slots. Treat them as two different checks: a project can satisfy ordinary parking counts and still fail the accessible parking requirement.

## PD 1096 baseline

PD 1096 requires minimum parking, loading, and unloading spaces to be off-street and on-site. Required slots must be outside the road right-of-way. The RIRR also gives standard planning sizes: a perpendicular or diagonal car slot is 2.50 m by 5.00 m; a parallel car slot is 2.15 m by 6.00 m; a truck or bus slot is 3.60 m by 12.00 m; an articulated truck slot is 3.60 m by 18.00 m; and a jeepney or shuttle slot is 3.00 m by 9.00 m.

For housing and building-use questions, read the occupancy row carefully. Examples from Table VII.4 include pooled slots for smaller residential lots or units, one slot per larger dwelling unit, and separate condominium ratios by gross floor area and market category. The table also has use-specific ratios for hotels, schools, hospitals, terminals, and transit stations. When the row says "or fraction thereof," round up.

## Accessible parking overlay

BP 344 uses a separate accessible-parking count table. It requires 1 accessible slot for 1-25 total parking spaces, 2 for 26-50, 3 for 51-75, 4 for 76-100, 5 for 101-150, 6 for 151-200, 7 for 201-300, 8 for 301-400, 9 for 401-500, 2 percent of total parking spaces for 501-1000, and 20 plus 1 for each 100 or fraction over 1000 for 1001 or more spaces.

Each accessible parking slot is at least 4000 mm by 5000 mm. Provide a 1200 mm clear walkway between the front ends of parked cars, and connect raised access walkways with a dropped sidewalk or curb ramp. Locate accessible slots nearest accessible main entrances; in multi-story parking, locate them near accessible elevators or accessible pedestrian entrances. Accessible slots must not be placed on ramped or sloping areas.

## Exam traps

Do not answer a BP 344 accessible-parking question with ordinary PD 1096 slot sizes. Do not put required parking in the road right-of-way. Do not reduce the accessible-parking count by using an ordinary parking ratio table. For 501-1000 total spaces, use 2 percent; for more than 1000, use 20 plus one additional accessible slot for each 100 or fraction over 1000.
