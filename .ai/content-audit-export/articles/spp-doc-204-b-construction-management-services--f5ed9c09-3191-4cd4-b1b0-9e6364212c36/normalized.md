# SPP Document 204-B: Construction Management Services

## Learning Goal

Use SPP Document 204-B to separate Construction Management (CM) from Construction Supervision Group (CSG) work, contractor responsibility, regular architectural services, and project-management questions.

## Source-Audited Scope

SPP Document 204-B is the RA 9266 Standards of Professional Practice document on Construction Management Services. It replaces the 1979 UAP Doc. 204-B and treats CM as a distinct professional service that may be performed by an owner staff member, an independent individual, or a construction management firm.

## Why CM Exists

Construction work often involves several contracts, specialty trades, and time-sensitive coordination. SPP 204-B frames CM as the service that helps the Owner coordinate the work, control time and cost, monitor quality, and keep the records needed for project implementation.

## Who May Serve as CM

The Architect may serve as Construction Manager when qualified. The individual CM, or the principal of a CM firm, must be a State-regulated professional. The SPP prefers an Architect with managerial capability and extensive construction experience. If the Architect serves as CM, the Architect must be a Registered and Licensed Architect with an updated professional identification card and must be a member in good standing of the IAPOA.

## Core CM Functions

SPP 204-B identifies four core CM functions:

1. Coordination and supervision of construction work.
2. Cost and time control.
3. Quality control of the work.
4. Keeping of records.

These functions include the functions of the Construction Supervision Group. The CM may hire the CSG or supervise a CSG hired directly by the Owner.

## CM Authority Limits

The CM does not take over the Contractor's responsibility for construction means, methods, techniques, sequences, procedures, or jobsite safety. The CM also must not impair the Architect's design authority or professional responsibility over the plans and specifications.

## Legal Responsibility

The CM is directly responsible to the Owner on matters involving project coordination, time, cost, quality, and records. SPP 204-B also limits CM liability for deficiencies in the plan or design when the CM did not participate in plan or design review and approval, and for construction deficiencies when the CM faithfully discharged CM functions during implementation.

## Compensation

Construction Management is not part of the Architect's regular services. If the Architect renders CM services, SPP 204-B requires separate compensation. The SPP 204-B source text confirms separate compensation but does not state a percentage fee range in the audited section.

## LEA Traps

- Do not treat CM as the same as regular design services.
- Do not make the CM responsible for contractor means, methods, or site safety.
- Do not confuse the CM with the CSG: CM includes and can supervise CSG functions, but it is the broader management service.
- For exact percentage-fee questions, verify the cited compensation document separately because SPP 204-B itself only states separate compensation.
