## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal
Classify funeral homes as dual-occupancy (Group E mercantile + Group H assembly), calculate separate occupant loads for business vs. chapel components, apply egress for mixed-use, and verify BP 344 accessibility.

Funeral homes and memorial chapels are mixed-use facilities requiring dual occupancy classification: **Group E (Business and Mercantile)** for the commercial funeral home business area (office, embalming, storage), and **Group H (Assembly)** for the chapel component depending on chapel capacity and seating configuration.

---

## Core Concept

A funeral home operates as a hybrid facility with distinct functional zones, each having separate occupancy implications.

- **Business Administrative Area**: Including the funeral director's office, reception desk, and back-office functions, this is classified as **Group E (Business and Mercantile)**.
- **Commercial Support Spaces**: The viewing room and embalming room support the Group E-classified commercial operation.
- **Chapel Component**: This is where the public assembles for funeral services and memorial rites. When the chapel has fixed seating and functions as a dedicated assembly space, it is classified as **Group H (Assembly)** Division 1. A typical funeral chapel with 200–400 seated capacity falls under **Group H Division 1**.

### Specialized Infrastructure

- **Embalming Room**: Requires 12–20 square meters with a separate HVAC system serving only that space, chemical storage, and plumbing with a floor drain and backflow prevention. It must have direct external access for receiving deceased persons.
- **Cold Storage (Morgue)**: Must be temperature-controlled (2–4°C), typically 6–12 square meters for 2–4 body capacity, with separate access and no public circulation.
- **Viewing/Visitation Room**: Occupant load is calculated per NBC Table II-2 (0.5 sqm/person for assembly), though actual design area may be increased (typically 1.1–1.5 sqm/visitor) for functional circulation.

### Safety and Egress

- **Fire Separation**: Fire separation between the Group E business office and the Group H assembly component requires a **1-hour minimum** rated wall or door assembly per NBC Rule VI.
- **Chapel Exit System**: Must be sized separately from the commercial office area. It requires 2 separate exits at a capacity of 50+ persons.
- **Travel Distance**: Maximum of 60m (sprinklered) or 45m (unsprinklered).
- **Accessibility**: Accessible routes to chapel seating must follow **BP 344** standards, including a **1:12 maximum ramp slope** per the 2024 Revised IRR and 900mm minimum clear door width.

## Key Provisions

**Functional Space Requirements:**

| Function | Minimum Area | Notes |
|:---------------------- |:----------------------------- |:--------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Embalming Room | Size per functional needs | Separate HVAC, drainage, chemical storage |
| Cold Storage | Size per operational needs | Temperature controlled (2-4°C), separate access |
| Viewing Room | Use NBC Table II-2 load factor | Includes standing circulation |
| Funeral Director Office | 9–12 sqm | Administrative, **Group E** |
| Reception/Waiting | 8–15 sqm | Public interface, **Group E** |
| Chapel (seated) | 200–400 sqm typical | **Group H Division 1** (200–400 persons) |

**Occupancy Classification Summary:**

- **Business/Commercial area**: Group E (Business and Mercantile).
- **Chapel component**: **Group H Division 1 (Assembly)**.
- **Fire separation**: Minimum 1-hour rated wall between **Group E and Group H**.

**Specialized Systems:**

- **Embalming Room HVAC**: Separate negative-pressure system to prevent odor transmission.
- **Cold Storage**: Dedicated unit with separate mechanical system.
- **Sanitary Drainage**: Indirect waste piping from the embalming room and floor drain with trap seal.

## Philippine Practice Context

Funeral homes are licensed commercial establishments regulated by local health authorities and the Bureau of Internal Revenue. A typical Filipino funeral home includes a viewing room for the _likha_ (wake) and a chapel for formal religious rites. While the viewing room accommodates multi-day informal family visits, the chapel hosts formal services. Embalming rooms must comply with **Republic Act 4167** (Funeral Services Board) regarding chemicals and cross-infection control.

## How It's Tested

**Pattern 1: Mixed Occupancy**
"A funeral home's business office area is **Group E**, while the chapel is **Group H**. Fire separation between them requires a minimum **1-hour** rated wall." 

**Pattern 2: Space Sizing**
"A funeral home expects 60 simultaneous visitors in the viewing room. Using a design factor of 1.1–1.5 sqm/person, calculate the minimum required viewing room area." 

**Pattern 3: Exit Capacity**
"A funeral home chapel seats 300 persons. Calculate the minimum combined exit width and the required number of exits (Note: 2 exits are required for 50+ persons)." 

> <Tip>**Funeral Home Anchors:** Mixed occupancy of **Group E (Business)** + **Group H Division 1 (Chapel)**. Embalming = 12–20 sqm. Viewing Room = 1.1–1.5 sqm/visitor. Chapel Exit Width = 7.6mm/person. Fire separation E–H = 1-hour minimum.</Tip>

## Common Mistakes

- **ERR-DSGN-005**: Classifying the entire funeral home as Group H instead of mixed Group E (Business) + Group H (Chapel).
- Underestimating viewing room area; it should be sized at **1.1–1.5 sqm/visitor** for circulation, not just the 0.5 sqm load factor.
- Failing to apply a 1-hour fire separation between the Group E office and the **Group H** chapel.
- Sizing the embalming room without functional justification (Note: there is **no NBC minimum** specified).
- Designing the embalming room without backflow prevention on the drain system.
- Specifying exit width for the chapel at 5mm/person instead of the assembly factor of **7.6mm/person** (stair/unit factor) per NBC standards.
