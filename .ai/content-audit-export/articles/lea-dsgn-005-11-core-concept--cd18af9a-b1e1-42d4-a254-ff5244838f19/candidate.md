## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal
Classify funeral homes as dual-occupancy (Group E mercantile + Group H assembly), calculate separate occupant loads for business vs. chapel components, apply egress for mixed-use, and verify BP 344 accessibility.

Funeral homes and memorial chapels are mixed-use facilities requiring dual occupancy classification: **Group E (Business and Mercantile)** for the commercial funeral home business area (office, embalming, storage), and **Group H (Assembly)** for the chapel component depending on chapel capacity and seating configuration.

---

## Core Concept

A funeral home operates as a hybrid facility with distinct functional zones, each having separate occupancy implications.

- **Business Administrative Area**: Including the funeral director's office, reception desk, and back-office functions, this is classified as **Group E (Business and Mercantile)**.
- **Commercial Support Spaces**: The viewing room and embalming room support the Group E-classified commercial operation.
- **Chapel Component**: This is where the public assembles for funeral services and memorial rites. When the chapel functions as a dedicated assembly space, classify it under the applicable PD 1096 assembly occupancy group and verify the exact division from the chapel capacity and layout.

### Specialized Infrastructure

- **Embalming Room**: Size and service this room from the functional program, sanitation requirements, and mechanical design. The local source packet does not establish a universal NBC minimum area, so do not memorize an unsupported number.
- **Cold Storage (Morgue)**: Treat cold storage as a specialized service space requiring temperature control, sanitary access, and separation from public circulation. Confirm capacity and temperature criteria from the project brief and applicable health or mechanical standards.
- **Viewing/Visitation Room**: Calculate code occupant load from the applicable occupancy/egress table, then increase the programmed area as needed for family circulation, furniture, and wake practices.

### Safety and Egress

- **Fire Separation**: Where occupancy groups or hazardous service rooms meet, verify the required separation from the applicable PD 1096 and RA 9514 provisions instead of applying a blanket one-hour rule.
- **Chapel Exit System**: Must be sized separately from the commercial office area. It requires 2 separate exits at a capacity of 50+ persons.
- **Travel Distance**: Maximum of 60m (sprinklered) or 45m (unsprinklered).
- **Accessibility**: Accessible routes to chapel seating must follow **BP 344** standards, including a **1:12 maximum ramp slope** per the 2024 Revised IRR and 900mm minimum clear door width.

## Key Provisions

**Functional Space Requirements:**

| Function | Minimum Area | Notes |
|:---------------------- |:----------------------------- |:--------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Embalming Room | Size per functional needs | Verify mechanical, sanitary, chemical storage, and access requirements |
| Cold Storage | Size per operational needs | Temperature-controlled service area with separate access |
| Viewing Room | Use NBC Table II-2 load factor | Includes standing circulation |
| Funeral Director Office | Program-dependent | Administrative, **Group E** |
| Reception/Waiting | Program-dependent | Public interface, **Group E** |
| Chapel (seated) | Program-dependent | Assembly classification depends on capacity and layout |

**Occupancy Classification Summary:**

- **Business/Commercial area**: Group E (Business and Mercantile).
- **Chapel component**: applicable assembly group/division under PD 1096.
- **Fire separation**: verify the required rating from the exact occupancy and hazard condition.

**Specialized Systems:**

- **Embalming Room HVAC**: Separate ventilation and exhaust strategy should be reviewed by the mechanical designer and local health authority.
- **Cold Storage**: Dedicated temperature-controlled service system.
- **Sanitary Drainage**: Indirect waste piping from the embalming room and floor drain with trap seal.

## Philippine Practice Context

Funeral homes are licensed commercial establishments regulated by local authorities in addition to building, fire, accessibility, health, and sanitation requirements. A typical Filipino funeral home includes a viewing room for the wake and a chapel for formal religious rites. While the viewing room accommodates multi-day informal family visits, the chapel hosts formal services. Treat embalming and storage spaces as specialized service areas that require project-specific health and mechanical verification.

## How It's Tested

**Pattern 1: Mixed Occupancy**
"A funeral home's business office area is **Group E**, while the chapel is treated as an assembly occupancy. Verify the exact assembly division and separation requirements from the occupancy, hazard, and egress conditions."

**Pattern 2: Space Sizing**
"A funeral home expects 60 simultaneous visitors in the viewing room. First compute occupant load from the applicable code table, then size the room for the wake layout, furniture, and circulation stated in the project brief."

**Pattern 3: Exit Capacity**
"A funeral home chapel seats 300 persons. Calculate the minimum combined exit width and the required number of exits (Note: 2 exits are required for 50+ persons)."

<Tip>
**Funeral Home Anchors:** Mixed occupancy of **Group E (Business)** plus an applicable assembly group for the chapel. Embalming, cold storage, and viewing areas must be sized from the project brief and applicable health/mechanical requirements. Chapel exit width uses the applicable egress capacity factor; separation ratings must be verified from the exact code condition.
</Tip>

## Common Mistakes

- **ERR-DSGN-005**: Classifying the entire funeral home as Group H instead of mixed Group E (Business) + Group H (Chapel).
- Treating viewing-room design guidance as a code occupant-load factor instead of checking the applicable table and project brief.
- Applying a blanket one-hour separation between business and chapel areas without checking the exact occupancy and hazard condition.
- Sizing the embalming room without functional justification (Note: there is **no NBC minimum** specified).
- Designing the embalming room without backflow prevention on the drain system.
- Specifying exit width for the chapel at 5mm/person instead of the assembly factor of **7.6mm/person** (stair/unit factor) per NBC standards.
