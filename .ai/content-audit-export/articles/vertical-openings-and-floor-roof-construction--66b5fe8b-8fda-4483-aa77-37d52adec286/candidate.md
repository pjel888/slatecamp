# Vertical Openings and Floor/Roof Construction

PD 1096 2004 Revised IRR Rule XII treats vertical openings, floors, and roofs as fire-resistance and structural-integration checks. The common exam error is answering from ordinary circulation logic instead of the construction-type and enclosure rules.

## Vertical Openings

Vertical openings must be enclosed depending on the fire-resistive requirements of the particular type of construction. Elevator and escalator enclosure walls and partitions must be not less than the fire-resistive construction required under the types of construction.

Elevator shafts extending through more than two storeys require an approved means of adequate ventilation to and through the main roof. For other vertical openings such as shafts, ducts, chutes, and similar openings, enclosing walls must conform to the construction type requirements. In occupancies other than Group A, rubbish and linen chutes must terminate in rooms separated from the rest of the building by one-hour fire-resistive occupancy separation, and chute openings must not be located in required exit corridors or stairways.

## Floor Construction

Floors must use materials and construction allowed under the fire-zone and construction-type rules. They must be framed and secured into the framework and supporting walls so that they form an integral part of the whole building. The type of floor construction must also provide means to keep beams and girders from lateral buckling.

## Roof Construction

Roof covering must be fire-retardant or ordinary depending on the fire-resistive requirements of the construction type. Roofs must be framed and tied into the building framework and supporting walls, with roof truss joints fitted, tension members tightened before loading, and diagonal or sway bracing used.

For combustible ceiling or roof construction, an attic access opening is required in the top-floor ceiling. Enclosed combustible attic spaces must be divided into horizontal areas not exceeding 250 sq m by fire-resistive partitions, unless the entire attic is protected by an approved automatic fire-extinguishing system, where the allowed area increases to 750 sq m. Draft stops are required in specified large trussed-roof conditions.

## Exam Use

Classify the item before answering: vertical opening means enclosure and fire separation; floor construction means permitted construction plus integral framing; roof construction means covering type, truss bracing, attic access, attic separation, and draft stops.
