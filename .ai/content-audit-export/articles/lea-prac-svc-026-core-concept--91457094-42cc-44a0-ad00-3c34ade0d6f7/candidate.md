# Site Development Planning (SDP)

## Source-Audited Core

SPP 203 lists Site Development Planning as a specialized allied service. The checked UAP source describes the Architect's concern as extending to the grounds and surrounding space around the building.

For small projects, landscaping may be part of the Architect's Site Development Planning service. For large-scale projects, the checked source says other registered and licensed professional specialist consultants are required.

The source-supported SDP scope includes complementing the building with its surroundings, open spaces, generic civil works, softscape and hardscape requirements, civil works and utility specifications, and assistance in bidding or negotiation with landscape, waterscape, and rock formation contractors when no specialist consultant is available.

The Architect may act in a dual capacity or as a consultant only, depending on the engagement. Compensation depends on the estimated cost of civil works plus landscaping hardscape and softscape, project complexity, and whether the Architect is also a registered and licensed professional for that specialized service. If a Landscape Architect is separately engaged by the Owner/Client, that consultant is paid by the Owner/Client and the fee is not deducted from the Architect's fee.

## LEA Practice Guardrails

Do not treat SDP as only planting design. The checked source ties SDP to the building's surroundings, open spaces, civil works, utilities, softscape, and hardscape. Also do not assume the Architect personally performs every specialized service on large projects; specialist consultants may be required.
