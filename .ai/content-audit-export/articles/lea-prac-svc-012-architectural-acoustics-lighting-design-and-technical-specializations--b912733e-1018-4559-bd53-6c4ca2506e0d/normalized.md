# Architectural Acoustics, Lighting Design, and Technical Specializations

## Source-Audited Core

SPP 203 covers specialized allied services that may enhance architecture. The checked UAP source separates those services from regular architectural design under SPP 202.

The source-supported SPP 203 list includes Acoustic Design, Architectural Lighting Layout and Design, Site Development Planning, Building Testing and Commissioning, Building Environmental Certification, and Forensic Architecture. For this article, the safest exam frame is to treat acoustics as a named SPP 203 specialized service unless a question supplies more source text.

Architectural Lighting Layout and Design has a fuller checked scope. It involves detailed planning and design of light transmission, timing, and control. The architect's scope can include drawings and specifications for illumination, fixture placement, efficiency, and energy consumption; assistance in bidding and negotiation; checking samples and fixtures; final inspection; and evaluation of amounts due.

If specialist consultants are engaged separately, the checked source says they are paid by the Owner/Client. Compensation for these specialized services follows the Architect's Guidelines.

## LEA Practice Guardrails

Do not fold all specialized allied services into ordinary SPP 202 design. For exam purposes, first identify that the service is SPP 203, then apply only the scope supported by the source. Lighting design has documented duties in the checked lines; acoustics is confirmed as an SPP 203 specialized service in the checked lines.

## Real World Scenario

A theater fit-out asks for acoustic design plus a lighting layout that coordinates illumination, fixture placement, efficiency, energy use, samples, final inspection, and payment evaluation for the lighting subcontractor. A source-safe answer classifies the work under SPP 203 specialized services, then separates what is directly documented for lighting layout and design from the narrower source support for acoustic design as a named specialized service. If the owner hires separate specialist consultants, their fees are for the owner/client's account; do not treat those consultant fees as ordinary SPP 202 regular design fees.
