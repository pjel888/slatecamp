# SPP Doc 202: Regular Design Services

## Board Exam Quick Check
SPP Document 202 is the regular architectural design services article. It explains the architect's basic design-service role, the normal service phases, key deliverables, and compensation methods. Do not import fee percentage splits unless the specific audited source line supports them.

## Learning Goal
Apply SPP Document 202 to identify the architect's regular design service phases, phase deliverables, construction-phase limits, owner responsibilities, and compensation methods.

## Source-Grounded Core Rules

### Scope and professional role
SPP Document 202 is part of the Standards of Professional Practice under the IRR of RA 9266. It applies to architects in individual or group practice. For regular design services, the architect acts as the Owner/Client/Proponent's adviser and representative. The service begins at project inception and ends when the contractor or builder turns the project over to the Owner/Client.

### Regular design service phases
The audited source lays out these regular design service phases:

| Phase | Source-grounded focus |
|---|---|
| Project Definition Phase | Consult with the Owner/Client, gather project data, clarify requirements, refine space requirements into an architectural program, and prepare an initial statement of probable construction cost. |
| Schematic Design Phase | Evaluate the program, schedule, budget, and site; consider delivery method; prepare initial line drawings; and submit a statement of probable project construction cost. |
| Design Development Phase | Develop plans, elevations, sections, outline specifications, diagrammatic systems, and an updated statement of probable project construction cost. |
| Contract Document Phase | Prepare complete drawings and technical specifications, provide seven sets for permit purposes, update the cost statement, and assist with filing documents for approvals. |
| Bidding or Negotiation Phase | Prepare bid documents, help identify prospective contractors, attend pre-bid conferences, answer questions, assist in evaluating proposals, and help prepare the abstract of bids, notice of award, and notice to proceed. |
| Construction Phase | Make decisions on claims, change orders, and guarantees; make periodic site visits; and issue certificates for payment. The source states that this is not exhaustive or continuous eight-hour supervision. Full-time supervision is a separate service when required. |

### Compensation methods in the audited source
SPP Document 202 lists several methods of compensation:

- Percentage of Project Construction Cost;
- Multiple of Direct Personnel Expense, or MDPE;
- Professional Fee Plus Expenses;
- Lump Sum or Fixed Fee;
- Per Diem, Honorarium Plus Reimbursable Expenses; and
- Mixed Methods of Compensation.

The audited source describes MDPE as a method commonly used for non-creative work and notes a multiplier range of 1.5 to 2.5 for covered costs. It also states that Lump Sum or Fixed Fee may apply to government projects.

### Owner responsibilities and separate services
The Owner/Client has responsibilities that support the architect's work. Other services outside regular design services are paid separately when required. Keep regular design services distinct from specialized allied services, full-time construction supervision, or other additional services.

## Exam Values To Memorize
- Regular design services start at project inception and end at contractor or builder turnover.
- Contract Document Phase includes seven sets of drawings for permit purposes.
- Construction Phase services include periodic site visits, not continuous eight-hour supervision.
- Full-time supervision is separate when required.
- MDPE multiplier range checked in the source: 1.5 to 2.5.
- Compensation methods include PCC, MDPE, Professional Fee Plus Expenses, Lump Sum or Fixed Fee, Per Diem/Honorarium plus reimbursables, and Mixed Methods.

## Common Trap
Do not answer SPP 202 questions with a generic five-phase design workflow if the audited source is asking for the SPP Document 202 service structure. The checked source identifies Project Definition, Schematic Design, Design Development, Contract Documents, Bidding/Negotiation, and Construction Phase services.
