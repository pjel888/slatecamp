# Wheelchair maneuvering and parking slots

Accessible parking is not only a parking count. The checked BP 344 2024 Revised IRR source treats the parking space, transfer area, signage, shortest accessible route, and nearby circulation as one usable system.

## Parking slot control dimensions

Parking slots for persons with disabilities must allow enough space for a person to transfer from a vehicle to a wheelchair. The accessible slot is located nearest to accessible main entrances. The checked dimension is a minimum width of 4000 mm and a length of 5000 mm. The added width compared with an average parking slot is intended to allow wheelchair passage.

A walkway with a minimum clear width of 1200 mm is provided between the front ends of parked cars. Where access walkways are raised, dropped sidewalks or curb ramps lead to the parking level. Accessible parking slots are never located in ramped or sloping areas.

## Location and signage

For multi-story indoor parking structures, accessible parking slots are located next to accessible elevators, or as close as possible to accessible pedestrian entrances. Where multiple accessible entrances have adjacent parking, accessible parking slots are dispersed and located closest to the accessible entrances. For parking facilities not serving a particular building, accessible parking is placed on the shortest accessible route to an accessible pedestrian entrance.

Pole-mounted parking signage is 600 mm by 600 mm and mounted at a minimum clear height of 2200 mm from the parking floor. The International Symbol of Access uses a white wheelchair figure on a blue background and faces to the right.

## Maneuvering beyond the slot

Wheelchair use continues beyond the parking stall. BP 344 corridor provisions use a 1500 mm minimum clear width, and turnabout spaces for wheelchairs are 1800 mm by 1800 mm at the required spacing. Busy or lengthy sidewalks and walkways need rest or passing spaces with a minimum clear dimension of 1800 mm.

## Exam-safe method

For parking questions, check the accessible slot dimension, transfer allowance, walkway, slope condition, elevator or entrance proximity, signage, and continuous accessible route. Do not treat the stall dimension as complete if the route from the vehicle to the entrance is blocked or sloped.
