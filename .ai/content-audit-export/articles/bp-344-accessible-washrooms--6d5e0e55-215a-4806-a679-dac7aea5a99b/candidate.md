# BP 344 Accessible Washrooms

## Learning Goal

Use BP 344 2024 Revised IRR washroom requirements for accessible toilet cubicles, stand-alone toilets, water closet placement, transfer space, accessories, and alarms.

## Required Accessible Toilet Cubicles

On each floor level or area accessible to persons with disabilities, BP 344 requires at least one accessible female toilet cubicle and one accessible male toilet cubicle for every 20 or fewer gendered toilet cubicles installed.

Accessible toilet cubicles must have a minimum area of 1800 mm by 1800 mm. Water closets must be installed 450 mm from the finished face of the side wall to the centerline of the water closet. The toilet seat height is 450 mm.

## Grab Bars and Transfer

BP 344 requires a fixed L-type grab bar on the side wall and a flip-up grab bar on the other side of the water closet. The source also requires a turning space with a minimum dimension of 1800 mm by 1800 mm outside water closet cubicles.

If a toilet facility cannot be directly accessed, a 1800 mm by 1800 mm turning space and a clear transfer space of at least 750 mm adjacent to the water closet must be provided.

## Accessories and Emergency Features

Accessible toilet cubicles include accessories such as mirrors, paper dispensers, towel racks, handrails, grab bars, bidets, diaper changing tables, reachable faucets, water closet backrest, emergency call button, and baby seat for persons with disabilities with babies.

The emergency call button must be waterproof, contrast with its background, and be located between 400 mm and 600 mm from the finished floor. When activated, it must emit an audible or visible alarm signal that is readily noticeable for summoning assistance.

## Practice Cue

Do not answer washroom questions with generic comfort-room guesses. Use the BP 344 dimensions and features: 1800 mm by 1800 mm cubicle area, 450 mm water closet centerline from side wall, 450 mm toilet seat height, grab bars, turning space, and emergency call/alarm provisions.
