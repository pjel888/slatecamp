## Board Exam Quick Check

For reciprocity questions, separate two pathways before choosing an answer: Section 27 is exam access leading to Philippine registration; Section 38 is a temporary or special permit for project-specific practice by a foreign architect who is not practicing as a Philippine-registered architect.

## Reciprocity Principles in Architectural Practice

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to:

- Explain the reciprocity proof required before a non-citizen may take the Philippine architecture licensure examination.
- Define strict and absolute equality in source-safe terms.
- Distinguish Section 27 examination access from Section 38 temporary or special permits.
- Identify the source-backed Filipino counterpart requirement for foreign project practice.

### Section 27 Reciprocity for Examination Access

RA 9266 Section 27 states that a person who is not a citizen of the Philippines at the time of applying to take the licensure examination is not allowed to take the examination unless reciprocity is proven. The foreign country must, by specific provision of law, either admit Filipino citizens to the same profession without restriction or allow them to practice after an examination on terms of strict and absolute equality with its own citizens or nationals.

The same provision includes unconditional recognition of prerequisite degrees or diplomas issued by institutions recognized by the Philippine government. This is why an exam answer based only on informal practice, professional courtesy, or general similarity of education is not enough.

### How the Proof Is Initiated

The RA 9266 IRR describes a documentary process. The foreign citizen seeking to take the exam through reciprocity initiates establishment of reciprocity by submitting a letter or document under official seal from the proper foreign official, together with an authentic or authenticated copy of the foreign law translated into English.

If the submitted letter, document, and law satisfy the Board, the foreign applicant may be allowed to file an application to take the licensure examination and submit the required supporting documents.

### Section 27 Is Not Section 38

Section 27 is about permission to take the Philippine licensure examination through reciprocity. If the person later passes the examination and obtains the required registration and Professional Identification Card, the person practices as a registered architect under RA 9266.

Section 38 is different. It covers foreign nationals who enter the Philippines to perform professional services as architects or consultants on a project. Before assuming duties, they must secure a special or temporary permit from the Board, subject to Commission approval. Section 38 also requires the foreign national to be legally qualified in the home country, to provide expertise necessary and advantageous to the Philippines, and to work with a Filipino counterpart.

### Exam Use

Use this decision rule:

- Taking the Philippine LEA: apply Section 27 and ask for reciprocity by law, strict and absolute equality, and unconditional recognition of prerequisite degrees.
- Practicing on a Philippine-soil project without Philippine registration: apply Section 38 and ask for temporary/special permit, DoLE/work authorization context, and a Filipino counterpart RLA.
- Claiming automatic practice rights: reject it unless the fact pattern shows the person has gone through the applicable RA 9266 pathway.
