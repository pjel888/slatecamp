# CPD Activity Types for Architects

## Board Exam Quick Check

For this local-first pass, use the source-backed rule first: RA 9266 IRR defines Continuing Professional Development as a sustaining and progressive learning process for maintaining, enhancing, or increasing architects' knowledge and ability. It also places CPD-program development under the integrated accredited professional organization and provider accreditation under the Board of Architecture. Treat exact activity-category schedules and credit-unit tables as final-depth material until a local raw primary CPD-law source is added.

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to:

- Explain the RA 9266 IRR basis for CPD in architectural practice.
- Distinguish source-backed CPD provider/program rules from unsourced credit-unit details.
- Classify common activity examples without claiming that RA 9266 IRR itself contains the full current CPD credit schedule.
- Apply CPD documentation thinking to renewal and audit scenarios.

## Source-Grounded Core Rule

RA 9266 IRR defines CPD as continuing learning that maintains, enhances, or increases the knowledge and continuing ability of architects. Section 28 states that all practicing architects shall maintain a CPD program to promote public interest and safeguard life, health, and property. It also states that the integrated and accredited professional organization develops the CPD program, and that other entities or organizations may become CPD providers upon accreditation by the Board.

The safest exam answer is therefore not "memorize every credit value." The safer answer is: CPD is a regulatory professional-development system, the Board and IAPOA/UAP are central to the program/provider structure, and any claimed credit category must be checked against the current PRC/Board CPD guidelines.

## Activity Types as an Exam Framework

The linked question bank uses five common CPD activity labels: structured learning, self-directed learning, work-based learning, teaching or presentation, and research or innovation. These labels are useful for classification practice, but this batch does not treat their exact credit hierarchy as source-primary from RA 9266 IRR alone.

Use the labels this way:

| Activity label | Safe classification cue | Source-safe boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structured learning | Provider-delivered seminar, lecture, course, module, conference, webinar, or workshop | RA 9266 IRR supports seminars, lectures, and continuing professional education modules delivered by accredited or registered CPD providers. |
| Self-directed learning | Independent reading, review, or study connected to architectural competence | Local source support is general CPD purpose; exact creditability needs current CPD guidelines. |
| Work-based learning | Mentoring, committee service, or challenging practice work that develops competence beyond routine delivery | Local source support is general CPD purpose; exact documentation and credit treatment need current CPD guidelines. |
| Teaching or presentation | Teaching, presenting, or conducting professional education for others | Local source support is general CPD purpose and provider/program authority; exact credit treatment needs current CPD guidelines. |
| Research or innovation | Investigation, study, testing, or professional knowledge development | Local source support is general CPD purpose; exact credit treatment needs current CPD guidelines. |

## Documentation Mindset

Even when an activity appears CPD-relevant, the architect should keep proof that connects the activity to professional development. For provider-delivered learning, that usually means provider identity, program title, date, completion evidence, and credit information from the current CPD authority. For independent or practice-based learning, it means written evidence of what was studied or done, why it improved architectural competence, and how it relates to life safety, professional service, technical standards, or public interest.

Do not invent a credit value just because an activity sounds professional. The exam may ask for a classification, but real practice requires the current CPD guidelines and provider recognition record.

## Real World Scenario

An architect is preparing a renewal file after attending a UAP seminar, reading a new accessibility guideline, mentoring a junior staff member on a public-school renovation, and presenting a short talk on tropical ventilation. The source-safe answer is not to assign exact credit units from memory. The architect should first separate provider-delivered CPD from independent or practice-based learning, keep completion and relevance evidence, and verify each item against the current Board/PRC CPD guidelines. The UAP seminar has the strongest source-supported provider link because RA 9266 IRR recognizes the integrated accredited professional organization's CPD-program role and automatic provider status.

## Common Traps

- Do not cite RA 9266 IRR as if it contains the complete current CPD activity schedule.
- Do not claim an exact credit hierarchy unless the current CPD guideline or CPD law source is in hand.
- Do not treat routine project work as CPD without showing professional-development value.
- Do not treat self-directed learning as undocumented personal reading; relevance and evidence still matter.
