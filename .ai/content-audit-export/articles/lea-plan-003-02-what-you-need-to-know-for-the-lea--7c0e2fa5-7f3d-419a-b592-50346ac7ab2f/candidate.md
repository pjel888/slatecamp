# Philippine Housing Classifications

For the LEA, classify housing by the governing standard and by the minimum criteria that the source actually states. Do not rely on stale market labels, price ceilings, or income brackets unless the exact current agency issuance is supplied in the problem.

## Core distinctions

**BP 220** governs economic and socialized housing projects. Its revised IRR says the standards are minimum design standards for economic and socialized housing projects and that the rules cover house-and-lot, house-only, or lot-only development.

**PD 957** governs subdivision and condominium projects and gives separate minimum lot and floor areas for open market and medium cost subdivision housing.

**RA 7279** frames socialized housing as housing programs for underprivileged and homeless citizens, including sites and services development, long-term financing, liberalized terms, and other benefits. In an exam item, use this as a policy definition, not as a fixed price or income threshold unless a current cited issuance is part of the item.

**RA 11201 IRR** places these categories within human settlements policy. Housing is treated as a multi-dimensional concern that includes location, tenure, cost, physical structure, livelihood access, and services.

## How to answer LEA items

First identify the law or IRR being tested. BP 220 points to economic or socialized housing; PD 957 points to open market or medium cost subdivision housing; RA 7279 points to socialized housing policy for underprivileged and homeless citizens. Then match the stated lot type, floor area, and delivery purpose to that source.

## Source-safe takeaways

- Economic housing and socialized housing are BP 220 categories with different minimum lot and floor areas.
- Open market and medium cost housing are PD 957 subdivision categories with larger minimum lot and floor areas than BP 220 housing.
- Socialized housing under RA 7279 is a policy and program category for underprivileged and homeless citizens, supported through land, services, financing, and liberalized terms.
- Current price ceilings, income brackets, and financing rules must come from current agency issuances; they are not inferred from these source excerpts.
