# Sustainable Architecture

## Source-Safe Scope

Batch 136 narrows sustainable architecture to the local tropical-architecture evidence: passive design, natural ventilation, shading, low thermal mass where appropriate, mixed-mode ventilation, and vegetation used for cooling without obstructing circulation.

The rewrite does not claim LEED, BERDE, Passive House, embodied-carbon math, or certification thresholds because those claims were not captured in the local source layer for this pass.

## How to Use the Concept

Use sustainable architecture as a design-priority test: reduce heat gain and cooling load first, then add mechanical systems only where passive or mixed-mode design cannot satisfy comfort, safety, or program needs.

## Real World Scenario

A Cebu barangay library is being designed on a hot, humid site with limited operating budget. The source-safe move is to reduce cooling demand before adding active equipment: orient openings to seasonal winds, keep windows shaded and operable, use verandas or deep balconies as shaded buffers, and place vegetation so it cools the microclimate without blocking air movement.
