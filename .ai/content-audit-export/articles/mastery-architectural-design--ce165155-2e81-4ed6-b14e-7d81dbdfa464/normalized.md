## The Internal Logic: How Subtopics Connect

Architectural Design is not a collection of 13 independent topics — it is one continuous act of thinking, broken into stages.

**Site Analysis → Design Process → Building Type Decisions.** Every building begins with its site. Topography determines drainage strategy. Solar orientation (southern arc in PH) means east-west elongation aids passive cooling. Prevailing winds (amihan/habagat) inform natural ventilation strategies.

**Design Process → Anthropometrics → Spatial Adequacy.** The design process produces spaces; anthropometrics verifies they work. Corridor widths, door clearances, counter heights, fixture clearances all flow from human body dimensions.

**Building Type → Code Application.** Every building type generates a specific regulatory context: DepEd for schools, DOH for hospitals, PD 1096 for occupancy classification.

**Code Application → Graphic Communication.** Code requirements live in drawings — fire exits shown on floor plans, accessible toilets drawn to scale to verify turning radius compliance.

## Connections to Every Other LEA Subject

### Connection to Architectural Theory

Design principles — proportion, scale, rhythm, hierarchy, unity — are the intellectual foundation of design process. Theory gives the vocabulary; Architectural Design applies it.

### Connection to Structural Conceptualization

Every design decision has structural implications: column grid selection determines floor plate geometry, shear wall placement constrains open-plan layouts, long-span systems enable column-free spaces, foundation selection affects ground-floor design.

### Connection to MEPF

Space planning for building services: mechanical rooms (3–5% of GFA), electrical rooms, plumbing cores, fire protection. The architect must allocate service spaces correctly.

### Connection to Philippine Laws and Professional Practice

PD 1096 governs occupancy classification, setbacks, and height limits. BP 344 governs accessibility. RA 9514 governs fire egress. The legal framework is inseparable from design practice.

### Connection to Site Development and Planning

Site planning bridges Architectural Design and the Planning subject. Topographic surveys, cut/fill, easements, setbacks, stormwater management — questions that present site conditions and ask for design decisions draw on both subjects.

## Integration Patterns Tested on the LEA

**Pattern 1 — Code-Constrained Design Selection:** Occupant load → exit count → exit width (RA 9514 + PD 1096).

**Pattern 2 — Accessibility Compliance Review:** Toilet layout compliance with BP 344 turning radius, grab bars, door swing.

**Pattern 3 — Site Response Design:** Building mass placement for natural ventilation given solar geometry and monsoon patterns.

**Pattern 4 — Multi-Code Building Type:** School fixture counts (PD 1096) + corridor widths + egress rules (RA 9514).

## Common Exam Traps

- **Treating design as purely aesthetic.** Every design decision must satisfy code constraints, structural feasibility, and service integration simultaneously.
- **Isolating codes from design.** BP 344, RA 9514, and PD 1096 must be applied together in every project scenario.
- **Assuming standard dimensions are universal.** Philippine-specific standards differ from international ones.
- **Neglecting service spaces.** A floor plan without mechanical rooms, electrical rooms, and riser shafts is incomplete.
