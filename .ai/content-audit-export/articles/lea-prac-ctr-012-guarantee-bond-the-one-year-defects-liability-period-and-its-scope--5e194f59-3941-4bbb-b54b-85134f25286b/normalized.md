## Guarantee Bond: Defects, Period, and Scope

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to:

- Define the guarantee bond from UAP Document 301 and CIAP Document 102.
- Identify the one-year guarantee period language in the checked sources.
- Distinguish contractual defect correction from Civil Code Article 1723 collapse liability.

## Definition and Period

UAP Document 301 defines the guarantee bond as security furnished by the contractor and surety to guarantee the quality of materials, equipment installed, and workmanship performed by the contractor.

CIAP Document 102 states that the performance and payment bonds are released when the contractor posts a guarantee bond equivalent to the amount of retention released. The CIAP guarantee bond is for one year from the date of posting and guarantees that all materials and workmanship installed under the contract are of acceptable quality.

## Defect Correction

CIAP Document 102 states that the contractor guarantees, for one year or a longer period if the specifications provide it, all materials and workmanship installed under the contract to be acceptable. If a defect develops within the guarantee period due to material or workmanship fault, the contractor must make the repairs and corrective work at the contractor's expense and commence within five days after written notice by the owner.

The same source preserves the owner's rights under the Civil Code and other laws. Civil Code Article 1723 separately addresses collapse of a building within fifteen years from completion due to defects in plans, specifications, ground, construction, inferior materials, or violation of contract terms.

## Exam Boundary

For this article, keep two rules separate: the contract guarantee bond addresses materials and workmanship for the guarantee period; Civil Code Article 1723 is a separate statutory liability rule for building collapse.
