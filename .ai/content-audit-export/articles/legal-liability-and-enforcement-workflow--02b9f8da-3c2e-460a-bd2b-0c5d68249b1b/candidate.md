# Legal Liability and Enforcement Workflow: Source-Safe Accessibility Gate

## Source Scope

This local-first revision is limited to the checked BP 344 2024 IRR, PD 1096 RIRR, RA 9514 RIRR, and Fire Code source-family evidence selected for Batch 066. Claims about RA 7277, RA 9442, RA 10754, discounts, VAT, and criminal penalty details are not repeated because those source families were not raw-audited for this batch.

## LEA Anchor

When an item asks about accessibility enforcement, first identify the source family. The checked BP 344 source supports public-use building coverage and specific accessible-route, ramp, parking, signage, door, elevator, and refuge requirements. The checked PD 1096 source supports the building-plan documentation gate for floor plans, accessibility facilities, and fire-safety documents.

## Compliance Workflow

BP 344 2024 defines public-use buildings as structures accessible to the general public, whether privately or publicly owned, with spaces available for communal use. It also defines accessible routes as continuous unobstructed paths connecting accessible elements and spaces without steps, stairways, turnstiles, revolving doors, escalators, or similar impediments.

PD 1096 requires architectural plans including floor plans drawn to scale, accessible ramps, accessible stairs, accessible lifts or elevators, accessible entrances, corridors, and walkways, plus plans and specific locations of accessibility facilities. Fire-safety documents include evacuation routes, emergency lights, signs, fire exits, and fire-resistive construction details.

## Exam Use

Do not answer a BP 344 enforcement item from memory of un-audited laws. For this batch, a source-safe answer says: check whether the project is a public-use building, confirm the accessible route and required facilities, and verify that the building-plan documents show accessibility and fire-safety details before occupancy or approval conclusions are made.
