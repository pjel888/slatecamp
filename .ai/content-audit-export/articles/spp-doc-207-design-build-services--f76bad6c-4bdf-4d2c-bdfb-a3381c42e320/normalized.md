# SPP Doc 207: Design-Build Services

## Source Scope

This local repair is limited to SPP Document 207 source material checked from the raw UAP PDF bundle and its local OCR/refined Markdown conversion. It removes fee percentages and broad liability statements that were not verified in the checked lines.

## LEA Anchor

SPP Document 207 is the standard for Design-Build Services. The checked source identifies two types:

1. Design-Build Services by Administration.
2. Design-Build Services with Guaranteed Maximum Project Construction Cost.

## Manner of Providing Services

The checked source identifies two manners of providing Design-Build Services:

1. The architect is part of or a member of the entity constructing the architect's design, works in tandem with an entity, or has authorized an entity to construct the design.
2. The architect is a State-licensed contractor implementing the architect's design or the design of others.

## Exam Use

For source-safe questions, test the document title, the two types, and the two manners of providing service. Do not use fixed percentage fees or unrelated construction statutes unless a separate verified source is attached.
