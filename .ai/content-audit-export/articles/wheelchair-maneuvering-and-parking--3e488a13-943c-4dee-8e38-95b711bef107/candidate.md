# Wheelchair Maneuvering and Parking

## Learning Goal

Apply BP 344 2024 Revised IRR accessible parking requirements for counts, location, transfer space, slot size, surface, pedestrian connection, and signage.

## Required Accessible Parking Slots

Where parking slots are required, accessible parking slots must be provided for vehicles driven by persons with disabilities or vehicles with passengers with disabilities, using the BP 344 table.

The table requires 1 accessible parking slot for 1 to 25 total slots; 2 for 26 to 50; 3 for 51 to 75; 4 for 76 to 100; 5 for 101 to 150; 6 for 151 to 200; 7 for 201 to 300; 8 for 301 to 400; 9 for 401 to 500; 2 percent of total parking spaces for 501 to 1000; and 20 plus 1 for each 100 or fraction thereof over 1000 for 1001 and over.

## Slot Size And Location

Parking slots for persons with disabilities must allow enough space for a person to transfer from a vehicle to a wheelchair. Accessible parking slots must be located nearest to accessible main entrances.

Accessible parking slots must have a minimum width of 4000 mm and a length of 5000 mm. A walkway with a minimum clear width of 1200 mm must be provided between the front ends of parked cars, and dropped sidewalks or curb ramps must lead to the parking level where access walkways are raised.

Parking slots must have a firm, level surface without aeration slabs. They must never be located in ramped or sloping areas. In multi-story indoor parking structures, accessible parking slots must be right next to accessible elevators or as close as possible to accessible pedestrian entrances.

## Signage

All accessible parking slots need pole-mounted parking signage 600 mm by 600 mm, mounted at a minimum clear height of 2200 mm from the parking floor. Pavement signs must be square, at least 1000 mm but not more than 1500 mm, and located in the center of the accessible parking space. The International Symbol of Access uses a white wheelchair symbol on a United Nations Blue background, with the figure facing right.
