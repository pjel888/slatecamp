# Philippine Housing Law Framework

## Source Scope

This local repair uses only the checked local raw PDFs and refined Markdown for PD 957, BP 220, and RA 7279. Earlier claims about RA 6552 and RA 10884 were removed because those raw sources were not audited in this batch.

## LEA Anchor

Use the housing-law framework as a classification problem. PD 957 governs open-market and medium-cost subdivision and condominium standards. BP 220 governs economic and socialized housing standards. RA 7279 supplies the socialized-housing policy frame, beneficiary criteria, and balanced-housing requirement checked in this batch.

## PD 957: Open-Market and Medium-Cost Projects

The checked PD 957 source applies the revised rules to open-market and medium-cost subdivision and condominium projects. Subdivision projects must conform with minimum design standards, local zoning or land-use controls, and relevant National Building Code provisions when the project includes housing.

For subdivisions, the checked source requires residential or otherwise appropriate location, physical suitability outside critical hazard areas, and an access road or right-of-way. A subdivision project is not to be approved without the necessary access road or right-of-way.

For open-market and medium-cost subdivision projects with an area of one hectare or more, land allocation is capped at a maximum saleable area of 70 percent of gross area and a minimum non-saleable area of 30 percent. Parks and playgrounds are mandatory for projects of one hectare or more, must be strategically located, are non-buildable, and must not be less than 100 square meters.

## BP 220: Economic and Socialized Housing

The checked BP 220 IRR applies to economic and socialized housing projects in urban and rural areas. Its minimum design standards are based on safety, health, ecological considerations, basic settlement needs, target-market affordability, and suitable location outside hazard-prone and protected areas.

BP 220 requires parks and playgrounds for projects of one hectare or more. The checked allocation table ranges from 3.5 percent to 9 percent of gross area depending on density, with a minimum park/playground area of 100 square meters.

The checked minimum lot areas are 72, 54, and 36 square meters for economic single detached, duplex or single attached, and row house units. For socialized housing, the corresponding checked minimum lot areas are 64, 48, and 28 square meters. The checked minimum floor area for a single-family dwelling is 22 square meters for economic housing and 18 square meters for socialized housing.

## RA 7279: Socialized Housing and Balanced Housing

The checked RA 7279 source defines socialized housing as programs and projects covering houses and lots or homelots for underprivileged and homeless citizens. It also identifies eligibility criteria: the beneficiary must be a Filipino citizen, must be underprivileged and homeless, must not own real property, and must not be a professional squatter or member of a squatting syndicate.

RA 7279 states that proposed subdivision projects must provide socialized housing equivalent to at least 20 percent of the total subdivision area or total subdivision project cost, at the developer's option, whenever feasible and in accordance with standards set by the housing regulator and other laws. Checked compliance modes include new settlement development, slum upgrading or renewal, joint ventures with LGUs or housing agencies, and participation in the community mortgage program.

## Exam Use

When the item asks for market-rate project standards, start with PD 957. When it asks for economic or socialized housing standards, start with BP 220. When it asks who socialized housing serves or how balanced housing is framed, start with RA 7279. Do not import Maceda Law cancellation formulas, RA 10884 percentages, or eviction-distance rules unless a separately audited source is provided.
