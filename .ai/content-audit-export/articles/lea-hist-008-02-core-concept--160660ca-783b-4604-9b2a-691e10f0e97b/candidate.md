# Art Nouveau and Art Deco

## LEA Point

Art Nouveau and Art Deco are consecutive modern decorative movements, but they answer different design problems. Art Nouveau breaks from historical revivalism through organic line, whiplash curves, and total design. Art Deco absorbs modern industry, geometry, luxury materials, and machine-age imagery into a more angular public style.

## Source Anchors

- Art Nouveau: natural forms, flowing line, whiplash curves, integrated structure and ornament, with Victor Horta and Hector Guimard as high-yield anchors.
- Art Deco: geometric abstraction, machine-age imagery, stylized luxury, and the 1925 Paris decorative arts exposition.
- The Chrysler Building is the safe Art Deco skyscraper anchor: William Van Alen, built 1928-1930, stainless-steel sunburst crown/spire, automobile-inspired ornament, and stepped massing.
- Gaudi can appear as a related organic/structural modernist cue, but do not flatten his Catalan work into a generic Art Nouveau checklist.

## Exam Pattern

If the stem says whiplash, vine-like ironwork, organic line, Horta, Guimard, or Paris Metro entrances, answer Art Nouveau. If it says zigzag, chevron, sunburst, stepped skyscraper, machine age, 1925 Paris exposition, or Chrysler Building, answer Art Deco. If the choice says William Van Alen, keep the spelling with "Alen"; "Van Lean" is a typo, not a person.

## Guardrails

Do not call Art Nouveau simply "floral decoration"; its important exam idea is a new integrated design language. Do not call Art Deco merely "Art Nouveau with sharper lines"; its source-safe identity is geometric, machine-age, and urban. Do not use unsupported exact claims about zoning unless the question is only testing setback massing as a light-and-air response.

## Real World Scenario

For a hotel lobby renovation, a source-safe Art Nouveau proposal would use continuous organic line, crafted metalwork, and integrated door/light/stair details. A source-safe Art Deco proposal would use stepped geometry, polished metal, stylized sunburst or chevron motifs, and a stronger machine-age urban image.
