# BERDE Rating System and the Architect's Role

## Learning Goal

Explain BERDE as a Philippine green building rating system, distinguish guidance from certification, and identify the project-team role of the architect without overstating a legal duty.

## What BERDE Is

The BERDE Buildings User Guide describes BERDE as a tool to assess, measure, monitor, and certify project performance above existing national and local building and environmental laws, regulations, and mandatory standards. It is the National Voluntary Green Building Rating System by the Philippine government through the Department of Energy.

BERDE was developed by the Philippine Green Building Council under the BERDE Program. BERDE development, certification, and education activities are administered by PHILGBC under that program.

## How BERDE Is Used

The guide presents BERDE as a guidance tool for building professionals in designing, constructing, and operating resource-efficient, economically viable, and socially responsible projects. It also states that all property industry stakeholders are encouraged to use BERDE.

Only projects that complete the formal assessment, rating, and certification process may claim to be BERDE Certified. The project must comply with all minimum system requirements and achieve at least the minimum rating for certification.

## Certification Stages

For new construction and fit-out projects, the guide uses Stage 1 - Design and Stage 2 - Construction. Stage 1 focuses on design and planning documentation. Stage 2 verifies construction, renovation, or implementation of the awarded Stage 1 credits, including document review and on-site assessment. Stage 1 must be completed before Stage 2.

Stage 3 - Operations focuses on assessing and verifying operational policies and procedures. The guide states that Stage 1 and Stage 2 certifications are valid for five years, while Stage 3 certification is valid for three years.

## Rating Cue

The minimum rating for BERDE certification is BERDE 1-Star. Do not describe a project as BERDE Certified just because it uses green strategies; certification requires the formal process, minimum system requirements, and the minimum rating.

## Architect's Role

The architect's source-safe role is to help the project team align design and documentation with target BERDE credits during the appropriate project stage. The guide supports architects as part of the building-professional and project-team audience. It does not, by itself, make every project legally required to seek BERDE certification.
