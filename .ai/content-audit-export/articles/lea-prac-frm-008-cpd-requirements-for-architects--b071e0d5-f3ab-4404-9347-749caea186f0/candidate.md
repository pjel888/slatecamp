## CPD Requirements for Architects

## Learning Goal

After studying this article, you should be able to:

- State the CPD requirements for architects: total CPD credit units per renewal period.
- Explain the CPD renewal cycle and how it aligns with PRC ID renewal.
- Describe the consequences of non-compliance with CPD requirements.
- Identify the CPD Council's role in accrediting CPD programs.

RA 10912, the Continuing Professional Development (CPD) Act of 2016, establishes mandatory CPD requirements for all practicing architects to maintain professional licensure. The law took effect on 16 August 2016, making CPD compliance essential for PRC Professional Identification Card (PIC) renewal starting 1 July 2017. The Board of Architecture prescribes CPD guidelines in consultation with the integrated and accredited professional organization of architects.

CPD requirements apply uniformly to all registered architects maintaining active professional practice in the Philippines. Architects retiring from practice or holding inactive licenses are not required to complete CPD. The Board determines specific credit requirements, activity types, and provider accreditation standards. These guidelines establish levels of compliance and proficiency evaluation as rating factors. Architects must understand that CPD is mandatory for professional ID card renewal and active practice authorization.

### Credit Units and Compliance Cycle

Architects must accumulate 45 credit units every three years to renew their PRC Professional Identification Card. The three-year cycle establishes the compliance period; architects track completion between renewal dates. Credit units are awarded for various professional activities, ranging from structured courses offering higher credits to self-directed learning providing modest credits. Architects must distribute their CPD across multiple activity types to ensure balanced professional development covering multiple knowledge areas.

[SLATECAMP_UI_BLOCK:ui_0003 component="ExamTip" status="preserved"]

The 45-unit requirement over three years averages 15 units annually, though no annual minimum is mandated; architects may complete requirements unevenly as long as the three-year total is achieved. Documentation requirements include course certificates, attendance records, or completion proofs from accredited providers. Architects must maintain records of all CPD activities and provide evidence upon PRC request during ID card renewal. Failure to meet the 45-unit requirement results in PIC renewal denial until requirements are satisfied.

### Distribution Across Five Professional Track Areas

CPD activities fall within five major professional track categories, with minimum credit allocations specified for each area. These tracks ensure comprehensive professional development across diverse knowledge domains. Track categories typically include technical and engineering subjects, ethics and professional practice, management and business, environmental and social responsibility, and specialized or emerging topics. Each track requires minimum credits to prevent concentration in narrow specializations.

Architects must plan their CPD portfolio to ensure compliance with distribution requirements across all five tracks. This structure prevents narrow focus on preferred subjects while neglecting essential professional competencies. A course covering multiple track areas may award credits in each applicable category, allowing flexible portfolio construction. The Board publishes guidelines specifying credit allocation for each approved activity type within each track category. Architects must verify that their selected CPD activities appropriately distribute credits across required categories.

### PRC ID Renewal Process and Timeline

PIC renewal occurs every three years, with renewal periods determined by the PRC based on registration date. Architects must submit renewal applications including proof of CPD completion before their current ID expires. The application requires evidence of all CPD activities and credit hours claimed. The Board or PRC verifies documentation and confirms CPD compliance before issuing renewal authorization. Failure to complete requirements before renewal date results in the ID becoming inactive until compliance is demonstrated.

The renewal process includes paying prescribed annual registration fees for the three-year period. Architects may renew online through the PRC system or submit applications in person at the PRC office. Fee payment and CPD documentation must be submitted together to complete renewal. Processing typically requires several weeks; architects should initiate renewal procedures well in advance of expiration dates. The renewed PIC bears a new expiration date three years in the future and confirms current registration status and continued practice authorization.

### Impact on Professional Practice Authorization

Active PIC status directly enables legal architectural practice in the Philippines. An architect without a valid, renewed PIC cannot legally practice architecture, sign and seal architectural documents, or execute professional services. Architects employed by firms or government agencies cannot practice architecture without current PIC status, regardless of employment status. This universal requirement ensures all practicing architects meet CPD standards and maintain current professional knowledge.

[SLATECAMP_UI_BLOCK:ui_0004 component="Tip" status="preserved"]

Architects with expired PIC renewal can restore active status by completing outstanding CPD requirements and submitting renewal documentation. The restoration process reactivates the PIC effective upon approval, but projects undertaken during the lapsed period may be questioned regarding professional authority. Clients may refuse to accept services from architects without valid PIC status, and government agencies cannot employ non-licensed architects for architectural work. Maintaining current PIC status through timely CPD completion is essential for uninterrupted professional practice.
