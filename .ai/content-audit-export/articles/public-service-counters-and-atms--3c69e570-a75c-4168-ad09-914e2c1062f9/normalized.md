# Public service counters and ATMs

Accessible counters and machines must be reachable, operable, visible, and communicative. The checked BP 344 2024 Revised IRR source controls ATM and kiosk clear space, counter heights, legroom, approach space, signage, audio and visual output, and hearing enhancement at service counters.

## ATMs, kiosks, and ticket machines

The checked source requires a minimum clear unobstructed space of 1800 mm by 1800 mm in front of ATMs. Kiosks, ATMs, and ticket machines are installed at a height easy to use for wheelchair users and reachable from standing and seated positions. Space under the counter is secured so wheelchair users can approach ticket machines.

Counters at kiosks, ATMs, and ticket machines are provided at a height of 750 mm. Space under the counter has a minimum depth of about 480 mm. The machines provide instructions and information through audio output with adjustable volume and visual instructions, feedback, and prompts visible from different heights and angles.

## Service and information counters

Public service counters apply universal design. Two-level counters are provided in customer service and ticketing offices and information desks, accessible in standing and seated positions. The lower level is 750 mm high, and the higher level is around 950 mm to 1100 mm. Counters have 700 mm minimum vertical legroom and 480 mm depth. The clear floor area for a forward or side approach is 1500 mm by 1500 mm, and each counter is at least 900 mm wide.

Hearing enhancement systems are provided at customer service and ticket counters, information desks, and similar counters to support effective communication.

## Exam-safe method

For LEA questions, do not answer only with counter height. Check approach space, legroom, counter depth, counter width, seated and standing reach, signage, audio/visual information, and communication support. A high counter with no lower accessible level is not a source-safe substitute for a universal design service counter.
