# Housing Production Methods Describe HOW

Housing classification answers the question: what standard or program category applies? Housing production answers a different question: how is the housing delivered, upgraded, financed, or organized?

## Source-safe production methods

**On-site development** under RA 7279 means upgrading or rehabilitating blighted or slum urban areas with a view to minimizing displacement of dwellers. It is the preferred lens when the problem asks how to improve an occupied settlement while keeping households near existing jobs, schools, services, and social networks.

**Resettlement** is the relocation of underprivileged and homeless citizens to a site with basic services and access to employment and livelihood opportunities. RA 7279 treats it as a serious intervention, not simply as moving people to any vacant land.

**Sites and services** belong to the RA 7279 socialized-housing toolbox. The source supports the idea that land, basic services, long-term financing, and liberalized terms can be combined to make a housing program viable. Do not add a fixed house type or private delivery model unless the problem supplies that source.

**Community Mortgage Program (CMP)** supports legally organized associations of underprivileged and homeless citizens in acquiring and developing land under community-based tenure and financing arrangements.

## Planning before choosing the method

The Local Shelter Planning Manual requires a local shelter plan to gather and validate data on housing need, upgrading needs, affordability, available land, infrastructure, resources, and program options. The method is chosen after those constraints are checked, not before.

## LEA application

When the question describes an occupied informal settlement, ask whether the source facts support on-site upgrading, land acquisition through a community association, or resettlement. When the question describes a city choosing programs for a shelter plan, start with housing need, affordability, resources, and CLUP or CDP alignment.

## Source-safe takeaways

- Housing production is a delivery method, not a housing classification.
- On-site development minimizes displacement by upgrading an existing settlement.
- Resettlement requires more than land; the checked source requires basic services and access to employment and livelihood.
- CMP is community-association land acquisition and development support.
- Local shelter planning should be evidence-based: need, affordability, land, infrastructure, resources, and alignment with local plans.
