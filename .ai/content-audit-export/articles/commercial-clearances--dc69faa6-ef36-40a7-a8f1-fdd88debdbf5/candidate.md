# Commercial Clearances

Commercial clearances in PD 1096 2004 Revised IRR Rule VIII are light, ventilation, site occupancy, and setback controls. The exam trap is treating a commercial lot as if one fixed setback applies to every frontage condition.

## General Rule VIII Controls

Every building must be designed, constructed, and equipped to provide adequate light and ventilation. A building must face a street, public alley, or duly approved private street. Existing buildings must not be altered to reduce room size or window-area relationships below the Rule VIII minimums, and enlargements must not reduce required courts or yards below the prescribed dimensions.

Site occupancy is measured at ground level and excludes courts, yards, and light wells. When additional construction is proposed on a lot with an existing building, the existing building footprint must be included in the Percentage of Site Occupancy computation for the Total Lot Area. If maximum PSO conflicts with another light and ventilation provision, the lesser footprint or ground-floor gross floor area prevails.

## Commercial PSO and Open Space

Table VIII.1 gives commercial controls for C-1, C-2, and C-3. C-1 lists 70 or 80 percent maximum PSO depending on the table condition; C-2 lists 75 or 85 percent; and C-3 lists 80 or 90 percent. The same table also tracks maximum impervious surface, minimum unpaved surface, and Total Open Space within Lot.

The point is not to memorize PSO alone. The table is a bundle: PSO, ISA, USA, and TOSL must be read together.

## Commercial Setbacks

Table VIII.3 sets commercial, industrial, institutional, and recreational setbacks by Road Right-of-Way width. For RROW of 30.00 m and above, the table gives 8.00 m front and 5.00 m side/rear setbacks. For RROW of 25.00 m to 29.00 m, it gives 6.00 m front and 3.00 m side/rear. For RROW of 20.00 m to 24.00 m, it gives 5.00 m front and 3.00 m side/rear. For RROW of 10.00 m to 19.00 m and below 10.00 m, it gives 5.00 m front and 2.00 m side/rear.

Highly built-up urban areas with duly established lines and grades may have different treatment only when open space, window opening, artificial ventilation if any, and firewall requirements are first fully complied with.

## Exam Use

For commercial-clearance items, identify the RROW width and the commercial class. Then apply Table VIII.1 for site occupancy/open-space controls and Table VIII.3 for front, side, and rear setbacks. If there is a conflict in light and ventilation provisions, the more stringent restriction prevails.
