# Contract Documents Under SPP 202

## Source Scope

Use SPP Document 202 for Contract Document Phase duties. Use UAP Document 401-402 for payment timing and abandoned-work compensation, because that source states the cumulative 85% milestone after Contract Documents.

## Learning Goal

For LEA questions, identify Contract Documents as the phase where approved design development becomes the detailed construction and specification package used for permits, bidding, and construction.

## Contract Document Duties

| Source duty | Exam anchor |
|---|---|
| Complete Contract Documents | Detailed designs and construction drawings set forth the work required. |
| Disciplines named | Architectural, structural, electrical, plumbing or sanitary, mechanical, electronic, and communication works are included. |
| Technical Specifications | The specifications describe type and quality of materials, finish, manner of construction, and general construction conditions. |
| Permit sets | The architect submits seven sets of construction drawings and technical specifications for building permit purposes. |
| Updated SPPCC | The architect updates the SPPCC for scope, requirement, or market-condition changes. |
| Government filing support | The architect assists the owner in filing documents for approvals from authorities having jurisdiction over the design. |

## Payment Milestone

UAP Document 401-402 says completion of Contract Documents Services increases total payments to 85% of the Basic Fee. Since Design Development reaches 35%, the Contract Documents step is the largest payment jump in the checked schedule. If the owner abandons or suspends the work after the architect has prepared the plans, specifications, and other project design documents, the source treats the completed Contract Documents Phase as equivalent to 85% of the architect's work.

## Exam Trap

Do not state that Contract Documents are only conceptual drawings. They are the detailed construction drawings and technical specifications. Also avoid saying the source gives Contract Documents only 50% of total design fees; the checked payment source states a cumulative 85% milestone after Contract Documents.
