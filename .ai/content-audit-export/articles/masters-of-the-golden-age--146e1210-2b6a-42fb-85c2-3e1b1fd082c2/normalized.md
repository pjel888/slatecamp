# Masters Of The Golden Age

## Exam Anchor

Use this cluster as a recognition map for major Filipino architects and works named in the local history sources. The checked sources place Juan Nakpil, Pablo Antonio, and Leandro Locsin in the National Artist sequence and list several works or building examples tied to them.

## National Artist Anchors

Filipino Architecture and Module - Architectural History identify Juan Nakpil as first National Artist for Architecture, Pablo Antonio as second, and Leandro Locsin as third. The same source area lists Agriculture and Finance Building, Crystal Arcade in Escolta, Quezon Institute by Juan Nakpil, Lyric Theater by Juan Nakpil, Ideal Theater by Pablo Antonio, and the Philippine Stock Exchange by Leandro Locsin.

## Related American And Commonwealth Context
