# Top 50 Traps: 2024 IRR

## High-yield BP 344 traps

The following traps focus on high-confidence rules from the 2024 BP 344 IRR. Use them to avoid common LEA accessibility mistakes.

1. Ramp access starts at 1:20 as the preferred maximum gradient; steeper cases are limited by the IRR table.
2. Accessible ramps have a minimum clear width of 1200 mm.
3. If total ramp length exceeds 10.50 m, provide an intermediate landing with at least 1800 mm length.
4. Provide level areas at the upper and lower ends of ramps, at least 1800 mm long.
5. Ramp handrails are at 700 mm and 900 mm, with 300 mm horizontal extensions.
6. Ramp raised edges are 100 mm.
7. Ramp warning tactile strips are 600 mm deep and placed 300 mm from the head, foot, and center of each landing.
8. Accessible parking counts are by the 2024 IRR table, not by ordinary parking ratios.
9. For 501-1000 total parking spaces, accessible parking is 2 percent of total spaces.
10. For 1001 or more total spaces, accessible parking is 20 plus 1 for each 100 or fraction over 1000.
11. Each accessible parking slot is at least 4000 mm by 5000 mm.
12. Provide a 1200 mm clear walkway between the front ends of parked cars.
13. Accessible parking slots must not be on ramped or sloping areas.
14. Exterior walkways are at least 1500 mm clear, with 1800 mm where two wheelchairs pass.
15. Maximum cross gradient for pathways, sidewalks, and walkways is 1:100.
16. Rest and turning spaces have a minimum clear dimension of 1800 mm and appear at maximum spacing of 12000 mm.
17. Dropped sidewalks at crossings require a base or level surface at least 1500 mm deep where the crossing or walkway is narrower than 1500 mm.
18. Dropped-sidewalk elevation difference must not exceed 19 mm.
19. A drop-off curb ramp has a 900 mm width and a gradient not steeper than 1:15.
20. All doors at accessible public entrances have at least 900 mm clear width.
21. Accessible stairs have risers not more than 150 mm and treads not less than 300 mm.
22. Warning tactile blocks at stairs and escalators are 600 mm deep and placed 300 mm before the relevant start, end, landing plate, or control point.
23. Escalator inclination is not more than 30 degrees.
24. Escalators require at least three horizontal steps at both ends.
25. Escalator or passenger-conveyance maximum speed is 30 m per minute.
26. In transport facilities, accessible elevator cars are at least 1400 mm deep by 1500 mm wide, with door opening of at least 900 mm.
27. Platform screen doors have a clear width of 900 mm.
28. An accessible route cannot include a stairway, turnstile, revolving door, escalator, or other impediment.

Primary source basis: BP 344 2024 IRR refined markdown lines 40-70, 230-305, 450-463, 539-541, 621-704, 772-792, 1062-1088, 1580-1613, and 1649.
