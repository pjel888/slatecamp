# SPP Doc 203: Specialized Architectural Services

## LEA Focus

SPP Document 203 covers specialized architectural services that complete, complement, or supplement regular design services. These services require specific expertise beyond the standard architectural plan and design services under SPP Document 202.

## Core Definition

- Specialized Architectural Services deal with specific expertise for further enhancement of the architectural interior and exterior components of a project.
- The architectural plan and design of the building remains under the Architect's Regular Design Services in SPP Document 202.
- For SPP 203, Architect refers to the Consulting Architect who may hold a Specialist Certificate issued by the Board and/or the Commission upon qualification or IAPoA accreditation.

## Enumerated Services

SPP 203 lists 23 specialized architectural service areas, including Architectural Interiors, Acoustic Design, Architectural Lighting Layout and Design, Site Development Planning, Site and Physical Planning, Comprehensive Development Planning, Historic and Cultural Heritage Conservation and Planning, Security Evaluation and Planning, Building Systems Design, Facilities Maintenance Support, Building Testing and Commissioning, Building Environmental Certification, Forensic Architecture, Building Appraisal, Structural Conceptualization, Preliminary Services, Contract Documentation and Review, Post-Design Services, Dispute Avoidance and Resolution, Architectural Research Methods, Special Building or Facility Planning and Design, Building Components, and Management of Architectural Practices.

## Selected Service Notes

- Architectural Interiors involve detailed planning and design of indoor or enclosed areas, including retrofit, renovation, rehabilitation, or expansion work, and cover architectural and utility aspects.
- Acoustic Design controls sound transmission for compatibility with the architectural design concept.
- Architectural Lighting Layout and Design controls light transmission, timing, and control for compatibility with the architectural design concept.
- Comprehensive Development Planning covers services from primary data gathering through formulation of the Master Development Plan and parallel preparation of environmental impact assessment or statement. Its components include physical, economic, socio-cultural, transport, and legal-administrative components.
- Building Environmental Certification uses a rating system that is credit-based, flexible, consensus-based, and market-driven.
- Forensic Architecture studies building well-being and may investigate deterioration, building deficiencies, faulty implementation activities, and faulty planning, design, or construction methodology.
- Post-Design Services include preparation of the Fire Safety and Life Assessment Report required by RA 9514, the 2008 Fire Code of the Philippines, and its IRR.
- Dispute Avoidance and Resolution is tied to Alternative Dispute Resolution modes under RA 9285.

## Manner and Compensation

- Several SPP 203 service sections state that the Architect may contract in two ways: dual capacity as Architect-of-record and Consulting Architect, or Consulting Architect only.
- Specialized architectural services may be classified as additional or extra services. Compensation may be through man-months, computed as 22 man-days multiplied by 8 man-hours and a factor for direct and indirect costs, or through another applicable fee mode stated in the SPP documents and Architect's Guidelines.

## Exam Traps

- Do not treat SPP 203 specialized services as automatically included in the regular SPP 202 design fee.
- Do not reduce SPP 203 to interiors only. Interiors are one of 23 listed service areas.
- Distinguish Building Environmental Certification from Forensic Architecture: one evaluates environmental performance through a rating system; the other investigates building well-being, defects, deterioration, and faulty implementation or design causes.
