# Fees for Repetitive Designs Reduced Fees and Their Justification

SPP 202 treats repetitive design first as a project-classification issue. Group 6 covers a project where the plan, design, and related contract documents are reused for repetitive construction without amending the drawings and specifications. That is the source-supported anchor for exam questions about repetitive designs in this local batch.

## What Group 6 means

Group 6 is not a general discount rule for every similar project. It applies when the same plan, design, and related contract documents are reused for repeated construction and the drawings and specifications are not amended. If new drawings, changed specifications, different site conditions, or additional design work are introduced, the question should not be answered as a clean Group 6 reuse scenario without more facts.

## Distinguish Group 6 from Group 7

Group 7 is a separate classification for housing projects that have several residential units on a single site with one set of plans, specifications, and contract documents. A project can feel repetitive in ordinary language but still be Group 7 when the source facts match a one-site housing project rather than reuse of the same documents for repetitive construction.

## Compensation method still matters

SPP 202 also lists methods of compensation for regular design services, including percentage of Project Construction Cost, multiple of direct personnel expense, professional fee plus expenses, lump sum or fixed fee, per diem or honorarium plus reimbursables, and mixed methods. The local sources checked for this batch support Group 6 classification and compensation-method selection. They do not supply a numeric reduced-fee schedule in the checked line ranges, so LEA content should avoid invented repeat-percentage calculations unless a separately audited source is added.
