# Retention Money: Standard Rates, Purpose, and Release

Retention money is an amount withheld from progress payments. CIAP Document 102 states that each request for payment is computed from completed work, less a retention of ten percent of the progress payment to the contractor. When fifty percent of the contract has been accomplished, no further retention is made on progress billings for the balance of the work.

Retention is connected to completion risk, defects, liens, and the contractor's guarantee bond. CIAP Document 102 provides that retained money is released not later than the expiration of the Period of Making Good of Known Defects, upon posting of the contractor's guarantee bond. It also connects final payment or retention release to lien protection when unpaid subcontractor or supplier claims remain.

UAP Document 301 uses similar progress-payment language for ten percent retention and stopping further retention after fifty percent accomplishment, but its release wording differs from CIAP Document 102. For exam analysis, do not mix the two instruments without naming the source. State the rule tied to the governing contract document.
