## What You Need to Know for the LEA

Use the 2024 BP 344 IRR signage rules as a wayfinding system, not as isolated trivia. The International Symbol of Access (ISA) designates accessible routes and facilities, and directional signs using ISA are installed where routes change direction toward facilities such as elevators, entrances, toilets, telephones, parking, and similar accessible destinations.

The ISA is a white wheelchair symbol on a United Nations Blue or Pantone 279 background. For accessible parking, the pole-mounted sign is 600 mm x 600 mm and has a minimum clear mounting height of 2200 mm from the parking floor.

## Core Concept

Signage under BP 344 must be readable visually and usable tactually. Signage must be simple, easy to understand, and have contrast against its surroundings. Wall and door signage with graphics, text, and Braille is mounted with the center of the sign not more than 1500 mm above the finished floor. Door-mounted signage for accessible toilets and showers is 200 mm x 200 mm.

For letters and pictograms, use the distance table in the IRR. Minimum letter height is 10 mm at 1 m, 30 mm at 3 m, 50 mm at 5 m, 100 mm at 10 m, 200 mm at 20 m, 300 mm at 30 m, and 500 mm at 50 m. If a sign projects into a walkway or route, provide at least 2200 mm vertical clearance.

Raised and Braille signage has its own checks: raised letters and numerals are 0.75 mm raised, uppercase, and accompanied by Grade 2 Braille. Raised characters are at least 16 mm high and not more than 50 mm high. Pictograms include a verbal description below the pictogram, with a pictogram border at least 150 mm high.

## How It Is Tested

Expect items that ask which signage dimension applies to a specific condition. First identify the sign type: parking pole sign, wall or door sign, toilet or shower door sign, projecting sign, letter height by viewing distance, or raised/Braille sign. Then apply only the number tied to that sign type.

## Common Mistakes

Do not use one signage number for every sign. The 600 mm x 600 mm size belongs to pole-mounted parking signage; 200 mm x 200 mm belongs to accessible toilet and shower door signage; 1500 mm is the maximum center height for wall and door signs; and 2200 mm is the clearance for signs projecting into a route.
