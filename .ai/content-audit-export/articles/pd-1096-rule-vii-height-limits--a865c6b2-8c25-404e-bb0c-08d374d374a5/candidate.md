# PD 1096 Rule VII Height Limits

## Source-Safe Scope

This repair limits the article to checked PD 1096 RIRR source evidence. It removes media blocks, derivative citations, and unsupported computed examples. The goal is to teach the sequence of height-limit reasoning without inventing values outside the checked source.

## What BHL Means

Building Height Limit is the maximum height allowed for a building or structure based on proposed use or occupancy after applying development controls and site conditions. PD 1096 generally measures BHL from the established grade line to the topmost portion of the proposed building or structure. Clearance from aviation or security authorities may still apply where relevant.

## More Restrictive Rule Controls

The BHL of a proposed building must be the height allowed under PD 1096 Rule VII or under the duly approved local zoning ordinance, whichever is more restrictive. A high value in a national table does not override a lower local zoning height control.

The checked Rule VII table records these source-safe examples:

- Residential 2 Basic: 3 storeys, 10.00 meters.
- Residential 2 Maximum: 5 storeys, 15.00 meters.
- Residential 3 Basic: 3 storeys, 10.00 meters.
- Residential 3 Maximum: 12 storeys, 36.00 meters.
- Residential 5 condominiums: 12 to 18 storeys, 36.00 to 54.00 meters.
- Commercial 1: 3 to 5 storeys, 10.00 to 15.00 meters.
- Commercial 2: 6 storeys, 18.00 meters.
- Commercial 3: 16 to 60 storeys, 48.00 to 180.00 meters.
- Institutional: 15.00 meters or the approved BHL in the major zone.
- Cultural: 30.00 meters or the approved BHL in the major zone.

## Measurement And Exceptions

PD 1096 excludes permitted projections above the roof such as signage, masts, antennas, telecom towers, beacons, and similar appurtenances from BHL. On sloping grade, the height measured from the lowest adjoining surface must not exceed the maximum height by more than 3.00 meters. Towers, spires, and steeples used neither for habitation nor storage may be limited by structural design if completely incombustible, or may extend up to 6.00 meters above the prescribed BHL if combustible.

## Bulk Coordination

Height is not the only control. Rule VII also ties building bulk to FLAR, allowable footprint, outermost building faces, total gross floor area, road right-of-way width, setbacks, and light and ventilation. The exam-safe sequence is to classify the use, find the applicable BHL, apply local zoning if stricter, then cross-check footprint, area, setbacks, and other development controls.
