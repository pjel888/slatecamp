## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal
Classify community health centers as Group D with assisted evacuation considerations, apply DOH space standards for consultation and treatment rooms, verify fire separation requirements, and apply Sentrong Sigla compliance.

Community health centers and Barangay Health Centers (BHCs) are Group D occupancies where patients may require assisted evacuation. They demand specialized space programming, fire separation, and accessibility standards distinct from acute-care hospitals.

## Core Concept

Community Health Centers and Barangay Health Centers operate as Group D occupancies under the National Building Code (NBC), classified as institutional facilities because occupants may be incapable of self-evacuation. Unlike acute-care hospitals covered under different standards, primary care facilities in the Philippines are regulated by the Department of Health (DOH) and the Sentrong Sigla program guidelines.

The core design principle centers on functional separation of clinical zones: consultation, treatment, pharmacy, and support areas must be sized and arranged to prevent congestion during medical consultation while maintaining infection control. A consultation room requires a minimum of 9 square meters (typically 3.0m × 3.0m). A treatment room requires 12 square meters. The waiting area must provide 1.5 square meters per seated occupant. Pharmacy dispensaries require a minimum of 4 square meters. Accessible toilet facilities, per **BP 344** (2024 Revised IRR), must provide minimum clear floor space of 1.50m × 1.50m per facility.

Group D occupancies require fire-resistance-rated occupancy separations per NBC Rule VII Table 703(d), minimum 1-hour, and exits must be sized per NBC Rule VIII Table 8.1 or RA 9514 Table 10 at 7.6 mm/person for doors. Travel distance to exits shall not exceed 45 meters (or 60 meters if sprinklered). At 50 persons occupant load, two separate exits are mandatory.

## Key Provisions

**Space Programming (DOH/Sentrong Sigla Standards):**

| Function | Minimum Area | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Consultation Room | 9 sqm | 3.0m × 3.0m typical |
| Treatment Room | 12 sqm | Medical equipment access |
| Waiting Area | 1.5 sqm/seat | Seated occupants |
| Pharmacy | 4 sqm | Minimum dispensary |
| Accessible Toilet | 1.50m × 1.50m clear | Per **BP 344** (2024 Revised IRR) Section 602.3 |

**Occupant Load & Fire Safety:**

- Occupancy: Group D (Institutional)
- Exit width: 7.6mm/person (**NBC Table 3**)
- Travel distance: 45m (or 60m if sprinklered)
- Minimum exits: 2 exits required at 50-person load
- Fire separation: 2-hour rated walls from other occupancy groups
- Ramp slope (accessible route): 1:20 maximum (**BP 344**, 2024 Revised IRR)
- Accessible door: 900mm minimum clear width (**BP 344**)

**Parking & Site Access:**

Standard parking stall: 2.5m wide × 5.0m long per **NBC standards**. Accessible parking: 3.6m × 5.0m per PWD stall (2024 Revised IRR), located nearest entrance.

## Philippine Practice Context

In the Philippines, community health centers serve as the primary contact point for preventive and curative services in barangays under the DOH strategic framework. Barangay Health Centers operate in Group D classification to reflect the reality that patients—particularly pregnant women, infants, and elderly—may need staff support during evacuation. The 2-hour fire separation requirement protects vulnerable occupants. DOH Memoranda and Sentrong Sigla guidelines specify space standards; architects must cross-reference current DOH facility standards during design development. The 9-square-meter consultation room is a Philippines-specific standard, not international IBC.

**Pattern 3:** _Accessible design and accessibility standards_ — "An accessible toilet serving a primary care facility must measure _____ × _____ meters per **BP 344**, and the approach ramp slope shall not exceed 1:_____."
