# Community Health Centers and Barangay Health Centers: Code Classification First

## Source Scope

This local repair is limited to PD 1096 occupancy classification, the RA 9514 2019 Revised IRR, and the local Fire Code source family. Earlier DOH Sentrong Sigla, clinic room-size, staffing, queue-area, toilet-ratio, and BP 344 statements are not carried forward because those source families were outside this Batch 064 raw-PDF audit.

## LEA Anchor

A barangay health center is not automatically a hospital, nursing home, or birth center. Identify the actual function named in the problem: outpatient clinic, health center, diagnostic clinic, lying-in clinic, hospital, nursing home, or mixed-use public service building.

## Occupancy Classification

PD 1096 includes outpatient clinics, family planning clinics, lying-in clinics, diagnostic clinics, and medical or clinical laboratories in the checked A-2 row; it also lists clinic, nursing and convalescing home, and health center in an A-1 matrix context. Larger or different medical uses may fall under institutional or C-2 matrix entries, including hospitals, medical centers, and multi-purpose clinics.

## Fire and Life Safety

RA 9514 health care occupancy applies when the facility is used for medical or other treatment or care of occupants mostly incapable of self-preservation. Where the facility is a small outpatient public service use instead, apply the actual occupancy and any mixed-occupancy or assembly triggers supplied by the problem. Fire Code review still requires exits, warning, and protective features appropriate to the occupancy and occupant count.

## Exam Use

For LEA questions, do not turn every barangay health center into a hospital. Use PD 1096 for occupancy classification, RA 9514 for health care or mixed-use fire-safety triggers, and the Fire Code source family for general egress duties.
