# Religious Buildings: Code Classification and Assembly Safety

## Source Scope

This article is repaired from the local PD 1096 RIRR occupancy tables, the RA 9514 2019 Revised IRR, and the local Fire Code source family. It does not add denominational planning rules, liturgical layout standards, or site-specific seating assumptions that were not verified in those raw PDFs.

## LEA Anchor

Religious buildings are tested through the exact use named in the question. A small chapel, a large worship hall, a multipurpose parish center, and an educational wing can fall under different source conditions. Read the table, occupant load, and fire-safety trigger before answering.

## Occupancy Classification

The checked PD 1096 matrix includes churches, mosques, temples, shrines, chapels, and similar places of worship in its public institutional and community-use context. The same source family also includes churches or similar places of worship in a separate residential-dwellings context. The safe exam move is to use the specific PD 1096 table or source condition named in the question instead of forcing one universal label.

## Fire Code Assembly Treatment

RA 9514 treats places of worship as assembly occupancies when the space is used for gathering fifty or more persons for worship or similar uses. For assembly spaces, occupant load can depend on whether the area has fixed seats, concentrated use without fixed seats, or less concentrated use. Places of assembly must provide exit lighting and signs, emergency lighting, and fire alarm systems. RA 9514 also states that sprinkler provisions for certain assembly occupancies do not apply to places of worship at the level of exit discharge with sufficient means of egress.

## Exam Use

For LEA questions, classify the religious facility from the named PD 1096 source, then check RA 9514 for occupant load, exits, alarm, sprinkler, and overcrowding requirements. Do not use unsupported seating or ritual-space assumptions as code values.
