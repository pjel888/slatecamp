# Site exterior circulation

Site exterior circulation is the accessible chain from arrival point to building entrance. The checked BP 344 source controls the accessible route, sidewalk, curb ramp, dropped sidewalk, tactile guidance, signage, headroom, and obstructions. PD 1096 is used only as a secondary check for entrance and projection conditions along sidewalks.

## Continuous accessible route

All public entrances must be accessible, including entrances that directly serve leased spaces, parking facilities, pedestrian tunnels, and elevated walkways. Entrances are accessible from arrival and departure points to the interior lobby. If an entrance is not on the same level as the site arrival grade, accessible ramps, wheelchair lifts, or similar mechanisms are provided.

Sidewalks, walkways, and pathways have a minimum width of 1500 mm, or 1800 mm where two wheelchairs must pass. They should be as level as possible and slip resistant. Running gradient should not be steeper than 1:20 where possible, with a maximum cross gradient of 1:100.

## Tactile guidance and obstacles

The checked BP 344 source requires tactile paths on the primary pedestrian route within 500 m of and inside buildings. Warning tactile blocks are used at stairs, escalators, ramps, and other hazard points. Directional tactile blocks should use simple, direct routes with minimal turns.

Obstacles, projections, and protrusions are avoided in pedestrian areas. Where protrusions are unavoidable, they must not reduce the required clear width for accessible routes. Sidewalk/walkway headroom is not less than 2200 mm. Bollards along accessible routes have at least 900 mm clear distance and must not be linked with a chain or rope.

## Entrance checks

Entrances and exits along the accessible route must prevent longer travel distances or exclusion of persons with disabilities from the standard path. Entrance vestibules require an 1800 mm by 1800 mm level area, and public doors have a minimum clear width of 900 mm.

## Exam-safe method

Read exterior circulation as a chain: parking or drop-off, sidewalk or walkway, curb ramp or dropped sidewalk, tactile route, entrance, door, corridor, and lobby. A compliant single element does not make the route accessible if another link is blocked, too steep, too narrow, or disconnected.
