# Fire pump sizing and hydraulic calculations

Fire pump questions are fire-protection documentation questions before they are arithmetic questions. The checked local source is the 2019 Revised IRR of RA 9514. It does not support generic pump shortcuts as substitutes for the Fire Safety Compliance Report, the applicable NFPA standard, and hydraulic calculations prepared for the actual project.

## Exam Anchor

When a building is required to install a wet standpipe system, automatic fire suppression system, or automatic fire detection and alarm system, the Fire Code IRR requires Fire Safety Compliance Report documentation for the FSEC stage and Fire Safety Compliance and Commissioning Report documentation for the FSIC for Occupancy stage. The FSCR is signed by the building owner, Engineer/Architect-of-Record, and BFP-accredited Fire Safety Practitioner.

## What The FSCR Must Show

For sprinkler and standpipe work, the FSCR includes the codes and standards used, the fire suppression description, the design criteria used for sprinkler or standpipe systems, water supplies, fire pumps, capacities, portable extinguishers, and hydraulic and other calculations. That is the source-safe place for pump capacity, pressure, and flow assumptions.

Automatic sprinklers, when required by the relevant occupancy division, are installed in accordance with NFPA 13 or NFPA 13R. Standpipe design, installation, and maintenance are in accordance with NFPA 14. The source's standards list also recognizes NFPA 20 for stationary pumps for fire protection and NFPA 25 for inspection, testing, and maintenance of water-based systems.

## Supervision And Maintenance

Required automatic sprinkler and standpipe systems are inspected, tested, and maintained in accordance with NFPA 25, and records are maintained and submitted with the Fire Safety Maintenance Report. For supervised sprinkler systems, monitoring includes control valves, fire pump power supplies and running conditions, water tank levels and temperatures, tank pressure, and dry-pipe air pressure. Water flow alarms are transmitted to the approved receiving facility or fire station when supervision is required.

Maintenance reporting includes records for fire pumps, sprinkler systems, portable extinguishers, standpipes, and other fire suppression systems. It also includes annual fire pump flow tests, annual main drain tests, and water-flow tests for standpipes.

## Hydrant And Water Supply Cross-Check

The checked source gives a local hydrant benchmark for housing or condominium projects: fire hydrants must be no more than 250 m apart and capable of delivering at least 3,785 L/min for one hour. It also requires reservoir or water tanks to provide a 20 percent fire reserve above Average Daily Demand Supply. Use those values only when the item is about the hydrant or water-supply provision that states this scope.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating a pump horsepower result as valid without source-supported design flow, residual pressure, water supply, and hydraulic calculations.
- Mixing sprinkler, standpipe, hydrant, and domestic water-storage requirements.
- Citing NFPA 20 without recognizing that the local Fire Code IRR is the Philippine source that requires the project documentation and refers to the NFPA family.
- Ignoring inspection, testing, and maintenance when the question asks about continuing compliance.
