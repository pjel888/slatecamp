# Choosing MDPE vs Percentage of PCC: The Decision Matrix

SPP 202 lists percentage of Project Construction Cost and multiple of direct personnel expense as two methods of compensation for regular design services. The source-safe decision is not based on an invented rule of thumb. It is based on what the source says each method is and on the instruction that each project should be examined to determine the most appropriate method.

## Percentage of Project Construction Cost

Percentage of PCC is the method expressly tied to Project Construction Cost. SPP 202 defines PCC broadly to include the structure, building systems, and other items included in the architect's and consultants' plans and specifications, while excluding professional fees and construction inspector salaries. Use this method in exam analysis only when the problem facts support compensation tied to the project construction cost.

## Multiple of Direct Personnel Expense

MDPE is a cost-based method. The source describes it as suitable only for non-creative work such as accounting, secretarial work, research, data gathering, and preparation of reports. It starts with architect, consultant, and technical staff time multiplied by rates, applies a multiplier from 1.5 to 2.5 to account for overhead and profit, and then adds reimbursable expenses to arrive at total cost.

## Other methods and mixed methods

SPP 202 also lists professional fee plus expenses, lump sum or fixed fee, per diem or honorarium plus reimbursable expenses, and mixed methods. The final source-safe step is to examine the project and choose the supported method or combination of methods rather than forcing every scenario into either percentage of PCC or MDPE.
