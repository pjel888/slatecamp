# Electrical and Mechanical Regulations

## Source Boundary

This article is limited to Rule XIII, Sections 1301 and 1302 of the 2004 Revised IRR of PD 1096. It uses the local PD 1096 raw PDF as source truth and treats the refined markdown only as a locator aid.

## Electrical Regulations

Section 1301 requires electrical systems in buildings and structures to conform to Parts 1 and 2 of the Philippine Electrical Code, as adopted under RA 7920. Plans and specifications for the electrical installations must be signed and sealed by a Professional Electrical Engineer.

For buildings more than 15.00 m high, overhead lines must maintain a horizontal clear zone of 1.80 m adjacent to the building. The zone begins at a height not over 2.45 m above the established grade.

Service-drop clearances include 2.50 m above roofs, 3.00 m above the ground or accessible platforms, 1.00 m horizontal clearance from windows, doors, porches, fire escapes, and similar locations, and 5.50 m above the crown of the street or sidewalk crossing with 3.00 m clearance at attachment.

Oil-insulated transformers with voltages over 15 kV must be installed inside transformer vaults. Transformer vaults must be accessible only to qualified personnel and must have adequate ventilation.

## Mechanical Regulations

Section 1302 requires mechanical systems to conform to the Philippine Mechanical Code adopted under RA 8495. Moving and dangerous parts must be guarded.

Elevators installed in private or public buildings for public use must be accessible to disabled persons under BP 344. Hoistways must be enclosed. Hoistway pits must provide at least 600 mm clearance. Where there are four or more elevators, not less than two hoistways are required, and not more than four elevators may be placed in one hoistway. Traction elevators require at least three hoisting ropes; drum-type elevators require at least two. The minimum rope diameter is 30 mm. Apartments and residential condominiums with five or more storeys must have at least one passenger elevator operating 24 hours a day.

Escalators must not exceed a 30-degree inclination. Width between balustrades must be from 560 mm to 1.20 m and must not exceed the step width by more than 330 mm. Rated speed must not exceed 38 m per minute.

Boiler rooms must be of reinforced concrete or masonry, must not be used for another purpose, must keep every boiler at least 1.00 m from the wall, and must have at least two separate exits. Smokestacks must withstand a 175 km/h wind load and rise at least 5.00 m above the eaves of any building within 50.00 m.

Comfort cooling effective temperature is listed as 20 to 24 degrees C, relative humidity as 50 to 60 percent, and air movement as 4.60 to 7.60 m per minute. Ducts must be non-combustible, wall or floor penetrations must be sealed with equivalent fire-resistant material, and ducts passing through firewalls must have automatic fire dampers on both sides.

## Exam Use

For LEA review, separate electrical from mechanical triggers: PEC and electrical clearances under Section 1301; PMC, elevators, escalators, boilers, refrigeration, air-conditioning, water supply, and pipe identification under Section 1302.
