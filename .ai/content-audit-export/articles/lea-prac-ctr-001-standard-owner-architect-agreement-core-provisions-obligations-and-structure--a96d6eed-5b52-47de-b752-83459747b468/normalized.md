# Standard Owner-Architect Agreement Core Provisions

The source-safe way to read this topic is to separate the Owner-Architect service agreement from the Owner-Contractor construction contract. UAP Document 401-402 is the local proforma Architect-Owner agreement source for regular/basic design services. UAP Document 301 is a construction-contract general-conditions source and should not be described as the full owner-architect service agreement.

## What the Owner-Architect Agreement Covers

The checked 401-402 proforma covers professional services by Philippine registered and licensed architects. Its notes frame the agreement around regular or basic detailed architectural and engineering design services, percentage-based fee computation, final project construction cost adjustment, exclusions such as full-time construction supervision and project/construction management, design-build exclusion, dispute resolution, and intellectual-property reminders.

Within the agreement body, the Architect's scope begins with site development planning as determined by the Owner and basic detailed architectural and engineering design for the project. The basic services then move through schematic design, design development, contract documents, and construction phase services.

## Basic Service Duties

In schematic design, the Architect consults with the Owner, confirms project requirements, prepares schematic studies leading to a recommended solution, and submits a statement of probable project construction cost. In design development, the Architect prepares drawings and outline specifications to fix and illustrate the project size and character. In contract documents, the Architect prepares construction drawings, plans, designs, specifications, building-permit sets, bidding documents when instructed, and updates to probable construction cost when scope or market conditions change.

During construction, the checked agreement gives the Architect construction-phase duties such as assisting with bidding and construction contracts, deciding claims and interpretation matters to the extent provided by the Owner-Constructor contract, reviewing submissions for conformance, preparing change orders, making periodic visits, informing the Owner of progress, and issuing certificates for payment and completion.

## Owner Duties and Payment Signals

The Owner supplies project requirements, designates representatives when necessary, examines Architect submissions promptly, issues orders to Constructors only through the Architect, furnishes or pays for site information and required tests, pays for specialty consulting services when required, pays reimbursables and taxes listed in the agreement, and gives prompt written notice of defects.

The proforma also records payment milestones: mobilization on signing, schematic design payment, design development payment, contract documents payment, adjustment after award to the winning constructor-bidder, progress billing on the construction-phase balance, and final balance based on final project construction cost.

## Boundary with UAP Document 301

UAP Document 301 defines the construction contract documents as the Agreement, General Conditions, Special Provisions, Specifications, and Drawings between Owner and Contractor. It is essential for contract administration, drawings/specifications, payment certification, bonds, disputes, and Architect status during construction. It is not the same thing as the Owner-Architect service agreement.

## Real World Scenario

A client asks whether the "standard agreement" already includes continuous site inspection, construction management, and design-build work. The source-safe answer separates them: the 401-402 proforma covers regular/basic design services and periodic construction-phase duties, while its notes exclude full-time construction supervision, project/construction management, pre-design services, allied services, and design-build from that agreement.

## Source Anchors

- UAP Document 401-402: lines 1-24 for agreement scope, fee basis, exclusions, design-build warning, ADR, and intellectual-property reminders.
- UAP Document 401-402: lines 38-75 for scope of work and basic service duties.
- UAP Document 401-402: lines 77-126 for fee/payment milestones, owner responsibilities, chargeable expenses, suspended/abandoned work, full-time construction supervision, and estimates.
- UAP Document 301: lines 472-545 for construction-contract definitions and document interpretation, used only to distinguish the construction-contract source from the owner-architect service agreement.
