# Contract Documents and the Architect in UAP Document 301

The source-safe repair for this article distinguishes two related but different topics. UAP Document 301 is a construction-contract general-conditions source. In that document, the Agreement is the contract between the owner and the contractor. Owner-architect service duties and compensation boundaries are supported separately by SPP 202.

## What UAP Document 301 defines

UAP Document 301 treats the contract documents as the Agreement, general conditions, special provisions, specifications, drawings, and related written instructions or addenda. It defines the owner as the person or entity ordering and paying for the work, and it defines the architect as the person commissioned by the owner to design or supervise the construction.

The document also states that the drawings, specifications, models, and copies are instruments of service and remain the property of the architect. The owner provides the contractor three sets free of charge, with extra copies at reproduction cost.

Contract documents are complementary. If discrepancies or omissions appear, the contractor must report them to the architect or engineer. The architect or engineer then provides supplementary details or instructions consistent with the contract documents.

## Construction-stage contract administration signals

UAP Document 301 includes construction-stage provisions such as written modifications for changes that affect cost or time, architect action on payment requests, payment certificates, substantial completion, and owner payment of certified amounts or stated reasons for amendment within the prescribed period.

## Exam boundary

Do not describe UAP Document 301 as the standard owner-architect agreement. Use it for construction contract documents and the architect's role under those documents. Use SPP 202 for owner responsibilities, additional services, reimbursables, changes after approval, and abandonment fee conditions.
