## What You Need to Know for the LEA

## Learning Goal
Distinguish NHA's role as housing producer (not financier) from SHFC (community finance) and Pag-IBIG (individual loans), describe NHA production programs (resettlement, MRBs, slum upgrading), and identify NHA's target beneficiaries.

The Philippine government houses policies and production through separate agencies: NHA builds housing; SHFC finances community programs; Pag-IBIG finances individual loans. Understanding who does what is essential for application questions.

## Core Concept

The Philippine housing system separates production from finance. The **National Housing Authority (NHA)** is the primary government housing _producer_—it builds resettlement projects, medium-rise housing for the urban poor, and slum upgrading programs. NHA does not provide loans directly to individual end-buyers but originates loans for organized community associations under CMP; it constructs completed units for allocation.

The **Social Housing Finance Corporation (SHFC)** finances community-based housing through its flagship **Community Mortgage Program (CMP)**. The CMP allows informal settler communities to collectively secure a mortgage to purchase the land they occupy, enabling in-situ upgrading—keeping communities in place rather than relocating them. This is the foundation of Philippine housing policy for the urban poor.

The distinction between NHA and SHFC is critical: NHA owns the production pipeline (government-built units), SHFC owns the financing mechanism (loans for communities buying land). Both serve low-income populations, but through entirely different delivery methods.

Neither NHA nor SHFC provides the mass of housing loans to employed individuals—that is the role of Pag-IBIG/HDMF, SSS, and GSIS (covered in LEA-PLAN-007-03).

## Philippine Context

NHA resettlement projects are highly visible in Metro Manila (Alalay sa Kaunlaran, Malasakit Village). SHFC's CMP is considered the most successful in-situ upgrading program globally—it preserves existing communities while improving housing conditions. The distinction between producer (NHA) and financer (SHFC, Pag-IBIG) is one of the most frequently tested agency roles on the LEA.

## How It's Tested

1. **Agency function identification:** "Who builds government housing?" → NHA. "Who finances community land purchases?" → SHFC/CMP.
2. **Role distinction:** "NHA provides housing loans to individual workers" (False—that is Pag-IBIG). "SHFC builds public housing" (False—that is NHA).
3. **Program matching:** "Which program allows a community to collectively mortgage land they occupy?" → CMP.
