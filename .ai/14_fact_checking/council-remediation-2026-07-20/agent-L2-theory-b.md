### Observation 1 — theo-005-01 article body mislabels Alberti treatise title (article-only, no question propagation)

`/Users/philiplewis/slatecamp/.ai/content-audit-export/articles/lea-theo-005-01-core-concept--128400dd-8642-4275-a64f-c411f6836d6d/normalized.md` line 11 says:

> "Alberti turns classical theory into Renaissance intellectual practice. His **De re aedificatoria, also known as Ten Books on Architecture** …"

The title *Ten Books on Architecture* (more precisely *De architectura libri decem*) belongs to **Vitruvius**, not Alberti. Alberti's *De re aedificatoria* is independently titled and has no canonical "Ten Books" alias (it does happen to contain ten books, but the "Ten Books" shorthand is universally associated with Vitruvius). No question in this bundle propagates the error — every question treats Alberti correctly via *De re aedificatoria* and concinnitas (see question `6bdd8eea-15a0-4ea8-bf49-815955aeb38f`, `794dd209-1150-4f48-80a4-72272d8a28e8`, and `e0f65daf-8801-4b1f-94e3-2020a3941ca7` which correctly distinguishes Alberti/De re aedificatoria from Palladio/I Quattro Libri/1570). Classified as a **content nit** outside question-edit scope.
