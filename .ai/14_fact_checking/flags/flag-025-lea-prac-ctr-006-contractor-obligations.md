### Issue 2: MEDIUM — "One-Year Guarantee Period" claim partially conflicts with Civil Code Article 1723

**Claim:** "The Contractor's Guarantee Bond enters into force for one year" and "Defects discovered after the one-year period are the Owner's responsibility unless the defect is attributable to the Contractor's negligence or intentional misconduct."

**Verdict:** NEEDS_VERIFICATION

**Evidence:**
- The article itself acknowledges the limitation: "Article 1723 of the Civil Code extends contractor liability to 10 years for total or partial demolition... or 15 years if due to defects in the ground."
- However, the phrase "attributable to the Contractor's negligence or intentional misconduct" is vague regarding the 10-year/15-year structural defect liability under Article 1723.
- The Civil Code Article 1723 imposes liability for structural defects without requiring proof of negligence — it's a form of strict liability for the contractor and architect.
- No source PDF for Civil Code Article 1723 is present in the source layer to verify this nuance.

**Impact:** MEDIUM — could mislead students about the scope of post-guarantee liability.
