### Issue 2: HIGH — Article lists only 4 grounds from UAP Code canons; actual Section 23 contains 6 grounds (a–f)

**Claim:** The article states "Section 23 of RA 9266 establishes the primary grounds" and lists them under UAP Code canons:
1. Unauthorized Signing and Sealing (Canon II Sec 2)
2. Aiding Unauthorized Practice (Canon II Sec 3)
3. Improper Solicitation (Canon IV)
4. Code and Standards Violations (Sec 23)

**Verdict:** LIKELY_INCORRECT (incomplete)

**Evidence:**
- The actual IRR Section 23 lists **six** grounds (a) through (f):
  - (a) Unauthorized signing/sealing work not prepared by architect or under immediate supervision
  - (b) Paid money except regular fees to secure registration
  - (c) Falsely impersonated a practitioner or practiced under assumed name
  - (d) Aided/abetted unauthorized practice
  - (e) Openly solicited projects without valid service agreement / allowed self to be exploited
  - (f) Violated any provision of RA 9266, IRR, Code of Ethical Conduct or Standards of Professional Practice

- The article omits grounds (b) [bribery/payment for registration] and (c) [false impersonation].
- Grounds (b) and (c) are substantive statutory grounds, not minor omissions.
- The article also incorrectly attributes grounds (a), (d), and (e) primarily to "UAP Code of Ethical Conduct" canons rather than to their statutory basis in Section 23(a), 23(d), and 23(e).

**Impact:** HIGH — students may miss two important statutory grounds for suspension/revocation.

### Issue 3: MEDIUM — "IRR Section 23 Procedural Provisions" misattributes procedural rules

**Claim:** The article groups procedural details under "IRR Section 23 Procedural Provisions" including:
- "The Board may motu proprio investigate"
- "Any person may prepare charges... sworn... filed with Secretary of the Board"
- "decision becomes final after fifteen (15) days unless appealed to the Commission"

**Verdict:** LIKELY_INCORRECT (misattributed)

**Evidence:**
- The 15-day appeal period is from IRR **Section 7(h)** (Powers and Functions of the Board), not Section 23. Section 7(h) explicitly states: "the decision of the Board shall, unless appealed to the Commission, become final and executory after fifteen (15) days from receipt of notice of judgment or decision."
- The "motu proprio" investigation authority is NOT explicitly stated in the IRR. Section 7(h) gives the Board power to "hear and decide administrative cases" but does not use the term "motu proprio." Section 23 says "upon notice and hearing."
- The "sworn complaint" and "filed with Secretary of the Board" details are not found in the available IRR text. The IRR only says "upon notice and hearing" (Section 23) and designates a Secretary (Section 8).

**Impact:** MEDIUM — procedural provisions are attributed to the wrong IRR section.
