## Flag 2: LEGAL_INACCURACY — "Certificate of Use" vs "Certificate of Occupancy"

**Impact:** HIGH
**Location:** Lines 63-74
**Article claims:** The Building Official issues a **"Certificate of Use"** (distinct from Certificate of Occupancy) for changes in use under Section 702, and that the Certificate of Occupancy is governed by Section 709.

**IRR/PD 1096 text shows:**
- **Section 309** is the Certificate of Occupancy section (not Section 709)
- IRR page 48, line 12: "A building for which a Certificate of Occupancy has been issued may further be issued other **Certificates of Occupancy** due to changes in use"
- PD 1096 original text page 364: "no change in the existing use or occupancy classification... shall be made until the Building Official has issued a **Certificate of Occupancy** therefor"
- Section 702 itself does NOT mention "Certificate of Use" at all

The IRR explicitly states that changes in use result in another **Certificate of Occupancy**, not a separate "Certificate of Use." If a "Certificate of Use" exists as a practical administrative document, it is not in the IRR text and the article's framing as a critical legal distinction may be misleading for exam purposes.
