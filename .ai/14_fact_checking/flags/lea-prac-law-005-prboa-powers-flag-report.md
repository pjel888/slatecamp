## FLAG 1 — MEDIUM — Incorrect Count of Powers (13 vs. Actual 14)

**Claim in Article (line 5):**
> "The statute lists thirteen (13) distinct powers, from (a) through (n), creating a comprehensive grant of regulatory authority."

**Verdict:** `LIKELY_INCORRECT`

**Evidence:** The RA 9266 IRR Section 7 enumerates powers from **(a) through (n)**, which is actually **14 distinct powers**, not 13. The enumeration is:

| Letter | Power |
|--------|-------|
| (a) | Prescribe and adopt IRR |
| (b) | Supervise registration, licensure and practice |
| (c) | Administer oaths |
| (d) | Issue, suspend, revoke, reinstate credentials |
| (e) | Adopt official seal |
| (f) | Monitor conditions affecting practice |
| (g) | Prescribe Code of Ethical Conduct |
| (h) | Hear and decide administrative cases |
| (i) | Prescribe CPD guidelines |
| (j) | Prepare and administer examinations |
| (k) | Approve/issue/limit/cancel special permits |
| (l) | Ensure curriculum compliance with CHED |
| (m) | Computerize licensure examinations |
| (n) | Discharge additional duties |

Counting (a) through (n) yields **14 items**, not 13 as stated in the article. Note: (a) through (n) inclusive is 14 because you count: a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h, i, j, k, l, m, n = 14.

**Impact:** `MEDIUM` — The linked question Q1 (LEA-PRAC-LAW-005-001) asks "How many specific powers...?" and the correct answer is "13 powers (from (a) through (n))" — both the article AND the linked question are wrong. The correct count is 14.

**Linked Questions Affected:** Q1 (LEA-PRAC-LAW-005-001) — the correct answer option says 13 when it should be 14.
