## Error Description

The question's correct answer specifies a penalty range of P5,000 to P100,000 for RA 9266 violations. The actual penalty range per Section 29 of the IRR is P100,000 to P5,000,000. The values are off by orders of magnitude.

Note: Interestingly, the article body actually states the CORRECT penalty range (P100K-P5M), but the linked question contradicts both the article and the law. This is a data corruption error in the question bank.

## How the Question Propagates the Error

**Question Text:** "What penalties does RA 9266 impose for violation of its prohibitions?"

**Correct Answer (option b):** "A fine of not less than P5,000 nor more than P100,000, or imprisonment of not less than 6 months nor more than 6 years, or both."

**Rationale (partially correct on section ref, wrong on values):** "Sections 29-30 specify criminal penalties: fine range and imprisonment range depending on violation severity."

## Verified Correct Answer

Section 29 of the IRR: "fine of not less than One hundred thousand pesos (P100,000.00) but not more than Five million pesos (P5,000,000.00)" plus imprisonment.

## Impact

This is the most severe error in the question bank. Students memorizing P5K-P100K will be off by two orders of magnitude. If a practicing architect relied on this information, they would significantly underestimate potential criminal exposure.

## Recommended Correction

1. Change correct answer values from P5,000-P100,000 to P100,000-P5,000,000
2. Update rationale to reflect the correct range
3. Verify that imprisonment range (6 months to 6 years) is correct against the IRR
