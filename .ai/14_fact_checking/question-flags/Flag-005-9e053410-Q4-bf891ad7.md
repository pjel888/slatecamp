## Error Description

The question propagates three errors from the article:
1. **Wrong occupancy labels:** Group G labeled as "mercantile" (actually Storage and Hazardous), Group H labeled as "high-hazard" (actually Assembly), Group A labeled as "low-cost residential" (incorrect category)
2. **Wrong document:** Uses "Certificate of Use" instead of "Certificate of Occupancy" for changes in use (Section 309.9)
3. **Wrong hazard hierarchy:** The rationale's hazard ordering is based on the wrong occupancy group labels

## How the Question Propagates the Errors

**Question Text:** "A former warehouse (Group E-2) is being converted to a grocery store (Group G, mercantile). What is the most likely outcome under Sec. 702?"

**Correct Answer (option b):** "The change may be approved as less hazardous with a Certificate of Use — grocery stores have lower fire risk and lower occupant density risk than moderate-hazard storage"

**Rationale (completely wrong classification):** "The hazard hierarchy is: High-hazard (H) > Industrial/Storage (F/E) > Educational/Institutional/Residential (C/D/B) > Low-cost Residential (A) > Mercantile (G) > Miscellaneous (J)."

## Verified Correct Classification

Per PD 1096 IRR:
- Group E = Business and Mercantile (not "Industrial/Storage")
- Group G = Storage and Hazardous (not "Mercantile")
- Group H = Assembly Other Than Group I (not "High-hazard")
- Group A includes low-density residential/community uses (not "Low-cost Residential" exclusively)
- The document for change of use is a "Certificate of Occupancy" (Section 309.9), not "Certificate of Use"

## Impact

This question teaches students entirely wrong occupancy classifications. If applied to exam questions or real practice, students will misclassify building occupancies and select incorrect code requirements.

## Recommended Correction

1. Rewrite the question to use correct occupancy groups per PD 1096 IRR
2. Replace "G, mercantile" with correct classification
3. Replace "Certificate of Use" with "Certificate of Occupancy" throughout
4. Rewrite rationale to use correct hazard hierarchy
