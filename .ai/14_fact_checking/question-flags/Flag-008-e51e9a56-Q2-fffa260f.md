## Error Description

**Important: The question is CORRECT; the article is WRONG.**

The article claims that reciprocity is required "OR substantial equivalence" as an alternative path for foreign architects. Section 27 of RA 9266 requires reciprocity proof only — no "substantial equivalence" alternative exists.

## The Contradiction

**Question's Correct Answer (option b):** "Proof that reciprocity exists between countries"

**Question's Rationale (correct):** "RA 9266 Section 27 requires proof that reciprocity exists before a non-citizen is allowed to take the Philippine licensure examination."

**The Article (wrong):** Claims that "reciprocity OR substantial equivalence" satisfies the requirement.

## Verified Correct Law

Section 27 of RA 9266 requires reciprocity proof only. There is no "substantial equivalence" statutory alternative for architecture licensure examination eligibility.

## Impact

Students using both the article and linked questions will receive contradictory information. The question teaches the correct law, but the article teaches an incorrect alternative pathway. This is particularly problematic if students trust the article over the question.

## Recommended Correction

1. No change needed to the question — it is correct
2. The article needs correction: remove "substantial equivalence" as an alternative to reciprocity
3. Consider adding a cross-reference note in the question rationale clarifying that the article needs updating
