## Why This Looks Like Lazy Design

The question asks about a legal liability distinction. The correct answer fully explains the nuance, while the distractors are short, simplistic, and obviously wrong half-sentences. Any test-taker can identify the correct answer by picking the longest option without understanding the material. This is a classic "lazy design" pattern where the content creator wrote a detailed correct answer and minimal throwaway distractors.

## Recommended Correction

Either shorten the correct answer to match distractor length or expand distractors to comparable detail (e.g., provide detailed-but-wrong explanations of the liability distinction).
