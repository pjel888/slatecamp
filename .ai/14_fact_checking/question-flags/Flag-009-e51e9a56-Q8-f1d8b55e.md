## Error Description

**Important: The question is CORRECT; the article is WRONG.**

The article claims that all foreign architects, including those from reciprocal countries, must secure a temporary/special permit under Section 38. The question correctly recognizes that reciprocity (Section 27) and temporary/special permits (Section 38) are independent pathways.

## The Contradiction

**Question Text:** "Do temporary or special permits for foreign architects require reciprocity status?"

**Correct Answer (option b — correct):** "No, temporary and special permits remain discretionary Board decisions"

**Question's Rationale (correct):** "Reciprocity establishes licensure examination eligibility; temporary and special permits remain discretionary Board decisions independent of reciprocity status."

**The Article (wrong):** Claims that all foreign architects, including those from reciprocal countries, must secure a temporary/special permit under Section 38.

## Verified Correct Law

Section 27 (reciprocity) and Section 38 (temporary/special permits) operate independently. A foreign architect from a non-reciprocal country can still obtain a Section 38 permit at the Board's discretion. Reciprocity enables examination eligibility; permits are for project-specific or specialized work.

## Impact

Students using both resources will receive contradictory signals. The question correctly distinguishes between the two pathways, but the article conflates them.

## Recommended Correction

1. No change needed to the question — it is correct
2. The article needs correction to accurately describe the relationship between Sections 27 and 38
