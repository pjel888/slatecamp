## Error Description

The article claims 13 powers enumerated from (a) through (n). The actual count is 14 distinct powers. The miscount stems from treating the enumeration as (a) through (n) = 13 items, but (a) through (n) inclusive is actually 14 items.

## How the Question Propagates the Error

**Question Text:** "How many specific powers and functions are enumerated in Section 7 of RA 9266?"

**Correct Answer (option c):** "13 powers (from (a) through (n))"

**Rationale (incorrect):** "Section 7 lists powers (a) through (n), creating a 13-part enumeration of Board authority from rulemaking through disciplinary action."

## Verified Correct Answer

Section 7 enumerates powers (a) through (n) = 14 distinct powers. The letters (a) through (n) represent 14 items: a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h, i, j, k, l, m, n.

## Impact

Students will answer "13" and lose a point on an exam question testing this specific count. The error also affects understanding of the full scope of Board authority.

## Recommended Correction

1. Change correct answer from option c (13) to a new correct option (14)
2. Update rationale to state "14-part enumeration"
3. Note: Currently, none of the four options state "14 powers" — a new option may need to be added
