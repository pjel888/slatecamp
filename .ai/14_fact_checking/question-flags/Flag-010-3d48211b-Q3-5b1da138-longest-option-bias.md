## Why This Looks Like Lazy Design

The question asks about the difference between suspension and revocation — a binary distinction. Option b (correct) fully explains BOTH concepts, while the distractors only make one-sentence claims. A well-designed question would either:
1. Keep all options similarly concise (single statement each)
2. Or ensure distractors include similarly detailed explanations

The disproportionate length makes the correct answer obvious to test-wise students who can guess without reading the question.

## Recommended Correction

Shorten the correct answer or lengthen distractors. Consider presenting the distinction more concisely, e.g., "Suspension is temporary with reinstatement possible; revocation is permanent."
