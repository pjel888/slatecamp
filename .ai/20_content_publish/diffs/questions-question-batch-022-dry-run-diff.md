Rattan, abaca fiber, or bamboo strips are wrapped tightly around joints between haligi (posts), batangan (joists), and other members. Unlike rigid metal connections, lashing allows the joint to flex under earthquake or typhoon loading — the connection can stretch and return to shape rather than snapping.
