## 🖊️ Copy Standards & Anti-Patterns

### The Voice: The "Master Architect"
We speak with the authority of someone who has built both structures and software. 

* **Rule 1: No Superlatives.** Avoid "best," "revolutionary," or "game-changing." Let the metrics speak.
* **Rule 2: Active Voice.** "SlateCamp calculates your readiness" (not "Readiness is calculated").
* **Rule 3: Name the Pain.** Start every ad with a specific student struggle (e.g., "PD 1096 Rule 7 is a nightmare to memorize.").
* **Rule 4: Zero Toxic Positivity.** No "You've got this!" Use "The data shows you are ready."

### Banned Phrases
* "Mathematical Proof" $\rightarrow$ **Calculated Certainty**
* "Guaranteed to Pass" $\rightarrow$ **Data-Backed Readiness**
* "Unlock your potential" $\rightarrow$ **Clear your blind spots**
* "We are excited to announce" $\rightarrow$ (State the feature and its value immediately)

# 🧠 EdTech Marketing Insights 2025-2026: Implementation Guide

### 1. The B2B2C Transition logic
We "warm up" target university accounts by getting individual students on the free tier first. When we pitch the Dean, we show them: *"60% of your students are already using SlateCamp. Here is the data they are seeing; don't you want to see it too?"*

### 2. The Pilot Program "Sweet Spot"
When piloting with a school or review center, we target a cohort of **15-25 participants**. This is large enough for data significance but small enough for the "high-touch" support that ensures a 100% "Success Story" rate for our first case studies.

### 3. Dark Social & Discord
The most important buying decisions for the ALE happen in private WhatsApp/Discord groups. Our "Ambassadors" are trained to provide helpful, data-driven answers in these groups, positioning SlateCamp as the expert resource in the room.

### 4. Outcomes-Based Marketing
In 2026, students and parents are data-savvy. We lead with: *"92% of users who reached a Readiness Score of 85% passed the 2025 ALE."* This is the only marketing claim that truly matters.
