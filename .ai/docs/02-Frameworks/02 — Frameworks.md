## 💰 The Multi-Tiered Pricing Framework

### Pricing Philosophy
We anchor on the **Cost of Failure**. A single ALE failure costs a student ~₱30,000 in lost time and fees. SlateCamp is positioned as the **"High-Precision Insurance"** that prevents that loss.

### 1. B2C Student & Parent Tiers (User Revenue)
| **Tier** | **Price** | **Strategic Purpose** | **Conversion Trigger** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free (The Hook)** | ₱0 | Lead generation and brand awareness. | First "Low" Readiness Score on diagnostic + 20-question daily cap. |
| **"The Camp" (Monthly)** | ₱599–899/mo | **Secondary Market:** Targeting 3rd-5th year students for school exams. | "Aha!" moment from the 3D Lab + need for unlimited mock exams. |
| **"The Insurance" (6-Mo)** | **₱3,999** | **Primary Market:** High-stakes review for ALE takers. | The "Fail-Safe" guarantee + full access to SRS/IRT engine. |
| **"Parent's Choice"** | ₱4,999 | **Tertiary Market:** Marketing to the "Economic Buyer." | Includes monthly "Guardian Progress Reports" sent to parents. |

### 2. B2B Firm & Supplier Tiers (Ecosystem Revenue)
| **Tier** | **Model** | **Value Proposition** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Talent Dashboard** | ₱15k–25k/mo | **Firms:** Access to the "Top 100" anonymous Readiness Scores for priority hiring. |
| **Specification Node** | ₱10k+ / node | **Suppliers:** Contextual ad placement within technical syllabus nodes (e.g., Lighting specs in Rule 7). |
| **Scholarship Bundle** | ₱35k+ / unit | **CSR/Marketing:** Firms buy 10 "Insurance" licenses to gift to interns, gaining brand loyalty. |

## 🚀 Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

### Phase 1: The Beachhead (Architecture / ALE)
* **Objective:** Monopolize the ALE reviewee market and prove the "Engine."
* **Tactic:** Launch the "Topnotcher SME Halo." Use recent top-10 passers as the face of the brand to establish immediate authority.
* **Success Metric:** 5,000 registered users; 15% free-to-paid conversion.

### Phase 2: The Pipeline (B2B Recruitment & Specs)
* **Objective:** Move from "Study App" to "Professional Hub."
* **Tactic:** Onboard 5-10 "Founding Firms" and "Founding Suppliers" to test the Talent Dashboard and Specification Nodes.
* **Success Metric:** ₱100k+ in B2B monthly recurring revenue (MRR).

### Phase 3: Horizontal "PRC OS" Expansion
* **Objective:** Scale the engine to Nursing, CPA, and Civil Engineering.
* **Tactic:** "The Franchise Model." Partner with the most famous reviewers in these fields, giving them a revenue share to bring their content into the SlateCamp engine.
* **Success Metric:** Launch 2 new verticals with 10k+ users each.

## 📊 Growth Loops & Acquisition Funnel

### 1. The "Topnotcher" Viral Loop
* **Step 1:** High-performing student uses SlateCamp and tops the board.
* **Step 2:** They credit their "Readiness Score" for their success on social media.
* **Step 3:** Next cycle of students joins SlateCamp to chase the same metric.

### 2. The "Supplier" Scholarship Loop
* **Step 1:** A supplier (e.g., a Paint brand) funds 50 scholarships for 4th-year students.
* **Step 2:** 50 students use SlateCamp for free, seeing that brand daily.
* **Step 3:** Upon licensure, those 50 architects are 3x more likely to specify that brand in their first projects.

## 📊 Acquisition Framework — The Funnel

### Top of Funnel (Awareness)
* **TikTok/Reels "Trojan Horse":** Visual split-screens comparing "Blurry PDF" study methods vs. "Interactive 3D Lab" mastery.
* **SEO Syllabus Wiki:** Owning long-tail search terms for Building Code and Professional Practice to capture high-intent traffic.

### Middle of Funnel (Activation)
* **The 60-Second Diagnostic:** Forced onboarding quiz that gives the student their first "Readiness Score" immediately, creating an "Information Gap" they need to fill.

### Bottom of Funnel (Conversion)
* **The "Insurance" Close:** Framing the ₱3,999 purchase as a protective hedge against the ₱30k+ cost of failing the ALE.
