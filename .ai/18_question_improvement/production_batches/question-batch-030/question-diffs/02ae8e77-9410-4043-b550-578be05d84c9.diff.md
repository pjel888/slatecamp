## Added explanation

Confuses EIA with promotional material; EIA is a technical environmental analysis required by law for certain projects. Confuses EIA with a budget item; EIA is an environmental impact study, not a cost document. Confuses EIA with design documentation; EIA is a separate regulatory analysis that informs design, not the design itself. EIA is the systematic process of identifying, predicting, and evaluating environmental effects of a proposed project before construction proceeds (DAO 2003-30). EIA is a mandatory environmental review process, not a marketing document or cost estimate.
