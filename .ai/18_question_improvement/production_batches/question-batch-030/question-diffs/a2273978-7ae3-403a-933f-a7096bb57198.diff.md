## Added explanation

Both classical and modern architecture employ both types. Random spacing is not rhythm at all—rhythm requires regularity. Both use repetition; the distinction is whether the rhythm is regular without variation (unaccented) or has emphasis/variation (accented). Ching precisely defines these: unaccented rhythm = identical units at equal intervals (calm, measured); accented rhythm = varying intervals or emphasized units (visual interest, focal points).
