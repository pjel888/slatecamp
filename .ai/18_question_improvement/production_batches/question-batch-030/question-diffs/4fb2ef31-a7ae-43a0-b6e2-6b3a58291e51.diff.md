## Added explanation

Radial balance distributes forms equally around a central point, not about an axis. Dynamic balance' is not standard terminology in Ching's framework. Asymmetrical balance distributes unequal elements while maintaining visual equilibrium. Symmetrical balance, also called axial balance, places identical or equivalent forms on opposite sides of a central axis, creating formal, static, dignified compositions common in classical and monumental architecture.
