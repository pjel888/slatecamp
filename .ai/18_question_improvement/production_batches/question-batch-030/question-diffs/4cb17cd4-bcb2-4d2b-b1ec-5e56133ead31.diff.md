While stone circles appeared in megalithic cultures, they are not part of the Egyptian tomb sequence. The Stepped Pyramid predates the true pyramid, not the reverse. This reverses the actual chronological order; pyramids evolved from mastabas, not the reverse. The chronological sequence: 'Mastaba (c. 3100–2686 BCE)... Stepped Pyramid (c. 2686–2613 BCE)...
