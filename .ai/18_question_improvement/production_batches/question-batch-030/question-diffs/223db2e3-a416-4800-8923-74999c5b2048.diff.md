## Added explanation

A pointed arch distributes weight but does not transfer lateral thrust externally. Ribbed vaults channel weight but do not solve the problem of lateral thrust alone. Tracery is ornamental stone framework in windows, not a structural system. The article defines the flying buttress as 'the revolutionary external element. An external half-arch transfers the lateral thrust of the vault from the upper nave wall to a distant outer pier, outside the building envelope.' Together with pointed arches and ribbed vaults, it forms the integrated Gothic structural system.
