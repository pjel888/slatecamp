## Added explanation

Both can use identical or equivalent elements; that doesn't distinguish them. Reversed—radial is around a point; symmetrical is about an axis. Both can be formal or informal depending on context. This is the critical distinction: symmetrical (axial) balance is about a LINE (axis), with forms mirrored across it; radial balance is about a POINT, with forms distributed equally in all directions (like spokes from a hub). Radial appears balanced from all directions; symmetrical appears balanced across an axis only. Radial appears balanced from all directions; symmetrical appears balanced across an axis only.
