## Added explanation

While stainless steel construction improves tank durability, it does not eliminate containment needs and is not the primary PEC environmental protection requirement. Fuel quality testing prevents equipment damage but does not provide environmental protection against spills and contamination. Visual inspection detects corrosion but is a reactive measure; secondary containment is a proactive engineered control that prevents contamination. PEC generator installation standards mandate secondary containment sized for 110% of the fuel tank volume. This requirement directly prevents soil and groundwater contamination by capturing any fuel leaked from tank rupture, corrosion, or overflow. The secondary containment area must be properly sloped, impermeable, and inspected annually. This is the most effective engineered control for environmental protection in fuel storage design. Secondary containment sized for 110% of fuel tank capacity is the primary PEC requirement that prevents soil and groundwater contamination from fuel storage tank failure.
