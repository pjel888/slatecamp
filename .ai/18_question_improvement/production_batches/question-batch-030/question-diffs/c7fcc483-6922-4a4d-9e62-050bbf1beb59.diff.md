## Added explanation

Site response is design strategy, not character as Ching defines it. While structure and material contribute to functional character, they don't encompass character alone. Style and ornament are part of associated character but not the complete definition. Ching identifies three sources of character: functional character (from purpose and supporting systems), associated character (from cultural/historical precedent and meaning), and personal character (from designer's interpretive choices).
