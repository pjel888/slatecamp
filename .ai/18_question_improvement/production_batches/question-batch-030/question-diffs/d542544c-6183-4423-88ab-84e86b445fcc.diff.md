## Added explanation

80% containment is insufficient to prevent environmental contamination if tank ruptures. 100% containment provides no safety margin and does not meet Philippine environmental protection standards. 150% containment significantly exceeds environmental standards and represents unnecessary cost without proportional environmental benefit. Philippine environmental protection standards and PEC generator installation guidelines require secondary containment sized for 110% of the tank volume. For a 20,000-liter tank: 20,000 liters × 1.10 = 22,000 liters minimum secondary containment. This 10% safety margin accommodates tank rupture or overflow while preventing fuel leaks from contaminating soil and groundwater. The containment area must be inspected annually and tested for structural integrity. Fuel storage secondary containment must equal 110% of tank capacity per Philippine environmental standards to prevent soil and groundwater contamination during tank failure.
