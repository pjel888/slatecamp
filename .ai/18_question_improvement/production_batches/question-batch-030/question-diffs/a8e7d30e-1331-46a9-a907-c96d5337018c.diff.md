## Added explanation

Neither distributes weight evenly; Romanesque round arches push purely outward, while Gothic pointed arches direct weight more downward. Pointed arches are not weaker; their weight distribution is more efficient, requiring less external support than round arches. The shape directly affects structural performance and weight distribution, not merely decoration. A pointed arch distributes weight more efficiently, with thrust directed downward and slightly outward along its legs rather than purely outward as a round arch does.' This efficiency 'enables higher vaults and thinner walls.'
