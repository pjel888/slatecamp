## Added explanation

Domes (like Pantheon) shed water effectively through slope; the comparison is false. Both are highly functional structural elements, not decorative. Domes use curvature and load distribution (outward/downward), not pitched water-shedding slopes like mansards. Mansards provide shelter through additional enclosed volume (historically for tax-exempt attics); domes provide shelter by spanning large circular openings with elegant curved vaults—both shelter strategies but structurally and spatially distinct.
