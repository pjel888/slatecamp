Tange was a modernist hero but not primarily identified with Critical Regionalism. Maki pursued Japanese modernism but not the explicitly critical regional framework. Isozaki pursued postmodern and deconstructivist directions rather than Critical Regionalism. Tadao Ando exemplifies Critical Regionalism through modernist abstraction—clean lines, minimal ornament, structural clarity—combined with engagement to Japanese spatial traditions, zen principles, and environmental responsiveness.
