## Added explanation

Confuses contractor obligations with architect professional services; the contractor's labor disputes are not architect professional failures. Acts of God are natural disasters beyond anyone's control and not covered by professional indemnity; insurance covers only the architect's professional errors. An owner's business decision to halt a project is not an architect professional error; professional indemnity covers only the architect's design, specification, or supervision failures. Design errors, specification mistakes, and inadequate supervision are professional failures covered by the architect's professional indemnity insurance.
