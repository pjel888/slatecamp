## Added explanation

Material type (paint, tile, wood) is covered within schedules, not as separate schedule types. Exterior/interior/basement is a location split; schedules are organized by room number, not location. Floor/wall/ceiling are finish elements listed within a single room finish schedule. Room finish schedules (walls/floors/ceilings), door schedules (type/hardware/finish), and window schedules (frame/glass/size) are the three standard schedule types in construction docs. Three standard schedules: room finishes, doors, windows. Each specifies products, sizes, and details. Memorize the structure.
