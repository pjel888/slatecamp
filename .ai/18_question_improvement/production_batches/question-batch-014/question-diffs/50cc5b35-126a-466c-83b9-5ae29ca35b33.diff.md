## Added explanation

Shared HVAC allows odor/chemical transmission to chapel; separate system required. Natural ventilation is insufficient for chemical handling and odor control. Standard building HVAC cannot control negative pressure needed for embalming room. Embalming room HVAC must be separate and maintain negative pressure (exhaust > intake) to prevent odor and chemical vapor from migrating to public areas (chapel, viewing room). (Funeral Home Design Standards, HVAC Best Practice) Embalming room requires separate negative-pressure HVAC system for odor/chemical control.
