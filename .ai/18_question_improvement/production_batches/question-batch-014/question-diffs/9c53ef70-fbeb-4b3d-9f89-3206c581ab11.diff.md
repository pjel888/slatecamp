## Added explanation

Misunderstands the insurer's role; the insurer takes an active role in claim investigation and defense strategy. Insurers do not pay claims immediately without investigation; they investigate to determine coverage and then defend at trial or settle. The architect does not control defense independently; the insurer controls the strategy and the architect must cooperate with the insurer's chosen approach. Upon claim notice, the insurer typically assigns counsel and directs the defense, with the architect's obligation to cooperate fully with investigation and defense efforts. The insurer controls the claim defense strategy and assigns counsel; the architect must fully cooperate but cannot unilaterally direct the defense.
