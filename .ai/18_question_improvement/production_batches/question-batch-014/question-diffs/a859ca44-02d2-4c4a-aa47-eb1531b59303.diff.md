## Added explanation

10-25 lbf is too low for nails; this would be appropriate for finishing nails or very small fasteners. 100-200 lbf per inch exceeds typical nail capacity. 300-500 lbf per inch is the range for lag screws, not nails. Common nails in wood provide approximately 25-50 lbf per inch of embedment, depending on wood density and nail diameter. This is relatively low compared to lag screws. Nail pull-out (25-50 lbf/in) < lag screw pull-out (300-500 lbf/in). Use lag screws for heavy loads.
