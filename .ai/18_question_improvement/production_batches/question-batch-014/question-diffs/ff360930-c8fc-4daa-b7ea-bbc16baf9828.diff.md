## Added explanation

The length comparison is reversed; finishing nails are actually shorter for the same penny weight. Both are typically steel; material type is independent of nail classification. Neither classification is tied to specific applications like roofing or flooring. Common nails (3.5 inches) have a larger diameter (0.162 inches) than finishing nails (2.5 inches, ~0.099 inches) of the same penny weight, providing greater holding power for framing but leaving larger holes unsuitable for visible trim. Nail type (common vs. finishing) determines diameter and holding power, not application exclusively. Choose based on surface finish requirements.
