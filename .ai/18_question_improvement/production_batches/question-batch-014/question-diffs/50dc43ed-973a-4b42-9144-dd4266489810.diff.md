## Added explanation

Strength is not the issue; corrosion is. A corroded carbon steel bolt eventually fails under load. Carbon steel bolts are suitable for seismic zones if they are not corroding. Material compatibility is not the issue; corrosion is. Carbon steel corrodes rapidly in salt spray, especially in coastal typhoon zones. Corrosion reduces the effective diameter and tensile strength, compromising structural reliability. Stainless steel (duplex or super-duplex) or hot-dip galvanized fasteners are required. Coastal projects = stainless steel or hot-dip galvanized fasteners. Carbon steel will corrode and fail.
