## Added explanation

Power factor correction uses tan θ calculations, not √3 in the basic voltage drop formula. Reactive power (kVAR) is calculated separately; √3 is purely a geometric voltage relationship. 60 Hz frequency is reflected in the K constant (10.8), not separately in √3. In three-phase AC circuits, the voltage between line and neutral involves the √3 relationship. The factor 1.732 accounts for the phase angle relationship in three-phase voltage drop calculations. The √3 multiplier is essential for three-phase voltage drop calculations and distinguishes 3-phase from single-phase feeder design.
