## Added explanation

5 inches provides only 2,000 lbf capacity (5 × 400). 10 inches provides 4,000 lbf capacity (10 × 400). 20 inches exceeds the requirement (20 × 400 = 8,000 lbf) and adds cost. Pull-out capacity = 400 lbf/inch × embedment depth. Solving for depth: 5,000 lbf ÷ 400 lbf/inch = 12.5 inches. Always calculate minimum embedment using the formula: Depth = Required Capacity ÷ Strength per Inch. Include a safety factor if specified in design code.
