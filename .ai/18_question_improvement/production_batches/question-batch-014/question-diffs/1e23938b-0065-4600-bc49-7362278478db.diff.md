## Added explanation

Deductibles serve risk allocation, not administrative efficiency; they require the insured to share in loss, not reduce the insurer's costs. Deductibles relate to claim cost-sharing, not coverage period; they do not extend or reduce policy duration. Deductibles are per-claim thresholds, not aggregate limits; the total coverage limit is separate from the deductible requirement. A deductible requires the architect to pay the first portion of each claim, with the insurer covering costs beyond the deductible up to policy limits. The deductible is the amount the architect pays out-of-pocket on each claim; the insurer covers the remainder up to policy limits.
