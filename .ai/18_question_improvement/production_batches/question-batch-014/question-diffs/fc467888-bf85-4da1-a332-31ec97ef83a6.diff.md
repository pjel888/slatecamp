## Added explanation

1926 is when Le Corbusier formulated the Five Points, not when International Style was formally codified. 1925 was the Paris Exposition; the MoMA exhibition was later. While Mies and Bauhaus were important, the MoMA exhibition by Hitchcock and Johnson was the formal codification. Henry Russell Hitchcock and Philip Johnson curated the 1932 MoMA exhibition that codified the International Style and gave it its name.
