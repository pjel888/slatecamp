## Added explanation

Private settlement without insurer notice breaches the policy and forfeits coverage; the insurer controls the defense strategy. Waiting for claim materialization violates notice requirements; policies require notice of circumstances indicating a likely claim, not just actual claims. Assuming a claim is minor risks coverage denial; the architect must notify the insurer of all potential claims, regardless of perceived severity. The architect must provide timely notice to the insurer when a claim arises or circumstances suggest a potential claim; failure to do so can result in coverage denial. Immediate notice to the insurer is required to preserve coverage—late notice can void the claim even if coverage would otherwise apply.
