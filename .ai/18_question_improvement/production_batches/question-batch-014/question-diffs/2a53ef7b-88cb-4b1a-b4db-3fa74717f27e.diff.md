## Added explanation

2.5 inches is the length of a 16d finishing nail, which has a smaller diameter. 3 inches is the length of a 10d common nail. 4 inches is the length of a 20d common nail. A 16d common nail is 3.5 inches long, the standard framing fastener specified in most construction documents. Memorize key penny weight sizes: 10d = 3 in, 16d = 3.5 in (common); 16d = 2.5 in (finishing). Use ASTM F1667 tables for others.
