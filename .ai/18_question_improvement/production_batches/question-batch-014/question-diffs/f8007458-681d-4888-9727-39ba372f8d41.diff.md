## Added explanation

Curtain walls have structural and conceptual significance, not purely decorative. Curtain walls don't reduce structural capacity; the frame carries all loads. While economical, their significance is philosophical and technical, not primarily economic. Curtain walls are fundamental to International Style's philosophy: they're non-structural skins that reveal the frame, express the building's essence through transparency, and communicate efficiency and modernity.
