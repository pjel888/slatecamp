## Added explanation

Wood screws have a coarse thread designed for timber, not metal. Machine screws are precision fasteners used with nuts or threaded holes, not for self-tapping applications. Lag screws are heavy-duty hex-head fasteners for timber or concrete, not sheet metal. Self-tapping screws (or sheet metal screws) have a hardened point and fine thread that cuts through metal and creates a thread path as it is driven, eliminating the need for pre-drilling or tapping. Self-tapping screws = sheet metal work; wood screws = timber; machine screws = with nuts; lag screws = heavy duty.
