## Added explanation

Unlimited coverage is unrealistic; insurers always set maximum exposure through per-claim and aggregate limits. Single-limit structures do not exist; policies distinguish between per-claim limits and aggregate annual limits to manage exposure. While some negotiation occurs, limits are not entirely flexible; policies establish fixed per-claim and aggregate amounts for the coverage period. Professional indemnity policies specify per-occurrence limits (maximum per claim) and aggregate limits (maximum total coverage per policy period). Professional indemnity policies have two limits: per-claim maximum and annual aggregate maximum—both caps on insurer exposure.
