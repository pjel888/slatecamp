## Added explanation

Breuer's achievement was industrial production of beautiful furniture, not handcraft. The Wassily Chair is highly ergonomic; comfort was central. The chair's commercial success proved Bauhaus principles were commercially viable. Inspired by bicycle frames, Breuer proved that industrial tubular steel could be mass-produced into elegant, functional furniture—exemplifying the Bauhaus principle of craft-industry integration.
