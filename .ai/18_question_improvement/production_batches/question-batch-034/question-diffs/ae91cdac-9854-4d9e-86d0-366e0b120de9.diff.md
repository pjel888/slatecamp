## Added explanation

Softscape (living elements) provides ecological function and seasonal change but is not permanent and does not establish physical boundaries in the same way. Hardscape includes permanent, built structures and surfaces such as paving, walls, water features, and furniture. These elements establish boundaries and provide durable surfaces for activities. Grading is the reshaping of land for drainage and level areas, not a permanent landscape feature itself. Xeriscaping is a design approach for water efficiency, not a landscape component.
