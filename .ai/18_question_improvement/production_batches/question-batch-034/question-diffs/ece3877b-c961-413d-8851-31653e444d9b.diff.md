## Added explanation

Painted steel loses its coating in salt spray, exposing the steel to corrosion. Standard aluminum corrodes through pitting in salt spray; galvanic corrosion occurs when aluminum contacts steel. Galvanized steel offers some protection but is inferior to stainless steel in salt spray. Salt spray rapidly corrodes painted steel and standard aluminum. Stainless steel (preferably 304 or 316 marine grade) or coated aluminum (powder-coated or anodized) resist corrosion in coastal environments. Coastal projects = stainless steel or coated aluminum hardware. Never specify standard painted or uncoated metals.
