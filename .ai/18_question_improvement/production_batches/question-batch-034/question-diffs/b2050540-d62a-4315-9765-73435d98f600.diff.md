## Added explanation

Fixed windows do not open; they provide no ventilation. Sliding windows do not shed rain effectively; water can enter around the sash during heavy rain. Casement windows open to the full width but do not automatically shed rain. Awning windows hinge at the top and swing outward at the bottom. This design naturally sheds rain while the bottom opening allows air intake, ideal for humid climates. Awning windows = hinge top, open bottom, rain shedding. Ideal for tropical and monsoon-prone regions.
