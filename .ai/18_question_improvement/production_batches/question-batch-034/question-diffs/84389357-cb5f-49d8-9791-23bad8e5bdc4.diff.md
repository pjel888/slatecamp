0.5 L, 2 L, 5 L are less common sizes. 2 L, 10 L, 50 L are non-standard in Philippines. Gallon sizes are sometimes used, but the common local standard is metric: 1 L, 4 L, 20 L. Philippine standard paint cans are 1 L (single/small), 4 L (1 gallon equivalent), and 20 L (5 gallons or bulk). These are the market standard sizes. Philippine paint cans: 1 L, 4 L, 20 L. These are the sizes to convert liter estimates into can quantities.
