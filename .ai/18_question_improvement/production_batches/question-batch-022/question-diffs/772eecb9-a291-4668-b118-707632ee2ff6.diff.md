## Added explanation

The bahay kubo evolved into the bahay na bato gradually: (1) Pre-colonial (pre-1565): Pure bahay kubo — all-organic materials, raised floor, open silong; (2) Early Spanish (16th–17th c.): Introduction of stone or brick to replace wooden posts at ground level — improving durability and fire resistance while keeping the raised floor; (3) Mature Spanish (18th–19th c.): Full bahay na bato emerges — enclosed stone ground floor (zaguan/silung), wooden upper floor with sala/cuartos/volada, capiz-shell windows (ventanillas), clay tile or GI sheet roofing. The indigenous raised-floor logic was preserved; only the materials and enclosure of the ground level changed.
