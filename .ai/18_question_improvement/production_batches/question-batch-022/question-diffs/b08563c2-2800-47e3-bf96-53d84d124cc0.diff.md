## Added explanation

CFLs achieve 60–80 lm/W and have 10,000–20,000 hour lifespan; they do not match the 80–120 lm/W efficacy range or the high-bay suitability of HID systems. Linear fluorescent (T8/T5) achieves 60–80 lm/W with 10,000–20,000 hour lifespan; while similar to CFL in efficacy, they lack HID's superior efficiency and high-bay design capability. Incandescent lamps achieve only 12–15 lm/W efficacy, have extremely short 1,000-hour lifespan, and are prohibited in the Philippines for general illumination under energy code mandates. HID lamps (metal halide, high-pressure sodium) achieve 60–150 lm/W efficacy depending on type, with lifespans of 6,000–15,000 hours. Metal halide typically ranges 60–100 lm/W; high-pressure sodium ranges 80–150 lm/W., are ideal for high-bay industrial and outdoor applications, and exhibit color shift as they age—a characteristic distinguishing feature of HID technology. HID lamps remain relevant in high-bay industrial contexts where their superior efficacy justifies color-shift maintenance requirements; understanding technology trade-offs guides appropriate fixture selection across building types.
