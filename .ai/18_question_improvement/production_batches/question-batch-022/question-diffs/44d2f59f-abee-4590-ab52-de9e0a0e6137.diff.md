## Added explanation

Mobile credentials incur cloud/app infrastructure costs, offsetting low per-card printing costs. Overall cost advantage is operational, not per-unit production. Mobile credentials operate at 2.4 GHz (BLE) or 13.56 MHz (NFC), not 125 kHz. RF frequency does not determine immunity to interception; encryption does. BLE and NFC are separate protocols; readers detect and use one or the other based on their capabilities. Readers do not automatically switch protocols. Mobile credentials (BLE/NFC via smartphone app) can be instantly revoked in real-time through the central access control server if the phone is lost, stolen, or employment terminates. Printed temporary cards remain valid for their entire validity window, creating a security window if cards are lost. Tokens are dynamically managed without physical distribution. Mobile credentials enable dynamic access revocation without physical card collection, improving security in high-turnover environments and reducing post-termination access breaches.
