## Added explanation

Every element of the bahay kubo is a direct response to the Philippine tropical monsoon climate: (1) RAISED FLOOR: Elevates living space above flood level; creates the silong (ventilated under-floor space); the batangan (joists) carry the sahig (floor slats) with gaps that allow air to flow through the floor from the silong into the living area; (2) STEEP ROOF (45–60°): The bubong of nipa/cogon/buri is steeply pitched to shed the heavy monsoon rains quickly; (3) OPEN WALLS: The dingding (woven bamboo/sawali walls) and bintana (sliding bamboo panels) can be opened entirely for maximum ventilation. The house is oriented to catch prevailing monsoon winds — long axis perpendicular to the dominant wind to maximize cross-ventilation.
