## Added explanation

This describes a feature of internal shading (roller shades, blinds) that allows occupant control but provides minimal thermal benefit—the opposite of why external shading is superior. Color rendering index relates to light quality perception, not thermal performance; external shading's thermal advantage is independent of CRI specifications. Thermal mass and humidity reflection are not relevant mechanisms for external shading thermal performance; the cooling load reduction comes from preventing solar radiation ingress, not mass or humidity management. External louvers intercept solar radiation outside the building envelope, preventing heat gain before it enters the conditioned space. This is more effective than internal shading, which allows solar radiation to penetrate into the building and convert to internal heat.
