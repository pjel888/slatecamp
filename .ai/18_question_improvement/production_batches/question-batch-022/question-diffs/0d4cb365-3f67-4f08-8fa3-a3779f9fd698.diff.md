## Added explanation

Political resistance is not what Frampton discusses. While traditional styles may engage place, Frampton's concept is about active engagement with physical-geographic realities. Structural resistance is an engineering property, not Frampton's theoretical concept. The Key Terms define 'Resistance of Place Form' as 'Frampton's concept that place possesses specific constraints and possibilities (topography, climate, available materials) that should actively inform design rather than be overcome through abstract principles.' This reframes constraints as creative resources.
