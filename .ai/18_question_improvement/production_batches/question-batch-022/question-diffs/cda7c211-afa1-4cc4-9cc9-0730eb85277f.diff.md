## Added explanation

Bundled bamboo is one of four joinery/construction techniques in bahay kubo construction: (1) Pagtatali (lashing) — rattan/abaca strips around joints; primary technique for flexibility; (2) Mortise-and-tenon — interlocking hardwood carved joints for compression strength; (3) Wedge/peg joints — bamboo or hardwood pegs through drilled holes; shear resistance with flexibility; (4) Bundled bamboo — multiple culms grouped and lashed at joints to create composite members thicker and stronger than any single culm. Bundled bamboo is particularly used for haligi (main posts) and batangan (main joists) where single culms would be insufficient in larger structures.
