## Added explanation

Pagtatali (lashing) is the defining structural technique of pre-colonial bahay kubo construction. Rattan, abaca fiber, or bamboo strips are wrapped tightly around joints between haligi (posts), batangan (joists), and other members. Unlike rigid metal connections, lashing allows the joint to flex under earthquake or typhoon loading — the connection can stretch and return to shape rather than snapping. This is why traditional bahay kubo structures have survived centuries of Philippine typhoons and earthquakes without catastrophic collapse. Metal fasteners (nails, bolts) only appeared with Spanish and later American colonial influence.
