## Added explanation

Storm surge elevation is a site/structural matter, not a window specification. Frame thickness is specified in Part 2: Products, not typically in the schedule DWP field. Installation dates are not regulatory requirements. NSCP Chapter 1 requires that windows in high-wind areas (typhoon zones) be designed for specific wind pressures. The schedule must specify Design Wind Pressure (DWP) appropriate to the location and typhoon category. Omitting this creates a gap in structural safety documentation. Philippine window schedules for coastal areas must include wind load ratings per NSCP. This is a common deficiency in architect specifications for typhoon-prone regions. The schedule connects design loads to actual products.
