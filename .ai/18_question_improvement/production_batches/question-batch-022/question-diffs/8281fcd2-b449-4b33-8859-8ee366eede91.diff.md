## Added explanation

The S-B-B-K mnemonic describes the spatial organization of the bahay kubo as you move from front to back: (1) Silong — the open space under the elevated floor, visible from the entrance; (2) Bulwagan — the main open living hall at the center; (3) Silid — the partitioned sleeping area; (4) Batalan — the back porch/service area for washing and water storage (with tapayan/water jars); (5) Kusina — the kitchen, often in a separate extension with an abuhan (clay stove). Note the doubled B: Bulwagan and Batalan, both starting with B.
