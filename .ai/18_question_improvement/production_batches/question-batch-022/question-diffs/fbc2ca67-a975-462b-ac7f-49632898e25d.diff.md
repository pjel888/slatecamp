## Added explanation

The HALIGI ng BAHAY mnemonic encodes the vertical structural path of the bahay kubo from bottom to top: Haligi (main posts driven into ground or resting on stones/tukod) carry the Batangan (horizontal floor joists/girders), which carry the Sahig (split bamboo floor slats). The Dingding (woven bamboo/nipa walls) enclose the space, and the Bubong (steeply pitched nipa/cogon/buri thatch roof) provides weather protection. The chant 'Haligi, Batangan, Sahig, Dingding, Bubong' makes this sequence memorable. Critically, the load travels downward through this path: roof → walls → floor → joists → posts → ground.
