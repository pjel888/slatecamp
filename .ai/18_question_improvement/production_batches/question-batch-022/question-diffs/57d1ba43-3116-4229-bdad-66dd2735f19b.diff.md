## Added explanation

The hagabi is a disc-shaped wooden or bamboo element fitted around the posts of the Ifugao bale (and other Cordillera dwellings) to prevent rats from climbing up the posts and into the grain stores and living space. It is both a functional pest-control device and a cultural artifact — the shape and carving of hagabi vary among Ifugao clans. The bale itself is a compact, single-room dwelling elevated on four main posts, with a pyramidal thatched roof.
