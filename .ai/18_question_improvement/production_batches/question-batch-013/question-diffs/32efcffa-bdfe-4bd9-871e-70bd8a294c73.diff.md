## Added explanation

While arches may use stones efficiently, the efficiency comes from structural properties, not labor reduction. Arches actually transfer weight to abutments (vertical supports), not eliminate them. The arch is a Roman innovation, but the motivation was structural capability, not ideological replacement of earlier systems. The arch is more efficient than the lintel because it creates compression (not tension) in its stones, and stone resists compression far better than tension.
