## Added explanation

Salt spray affects exterior concrete but is secondary; primary issue is hydrostatic pressure. Interior drainage can be enhanced with pumps, but this does not address the core issue of pressure-driven infiltration. Humidity does slow cure but is a secondary concern; waterproofing failure is primary. Negative-side waterproofing is applied after water infiltrates the concrete. In tropical climates with monsoon rainfall (200+ mm/hr), hydrostatic pressure forces water through concrete faster than interior drainage can handle. Negative-side systems are designed for non-hydrostatic damp conditions only. Positive-side (exterior) waterproofing is mandatory for tropical hydrostatic conditions. Negative-side only for non-hydrostatic interior applications.
