## Added explanation

A barrel vault is a continuous semicircular vault spanning a space, not an intersection of two vaults. A dome is a vault rotated on all axes, not an intersection of barrel vaults. Tunnel vault is another name for barrel vault, not a groin vault. The article defines: 'A groin vault (or cross vault) formed where two barrel vaults intersect at right angles, creating four arched surfaces. Groin vaults were more economical than barrel vaults because they concentrated weight at four corner piers instead of along continuous walls.'
