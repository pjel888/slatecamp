## Added explanation

Negative-side is applied on the interior after water infiltration; cannot resist hydrostatic pressure. Capillary-break is a damp-proofing technique using absorbent materials; does not provide full waterproofing. Damp-proof blocks vapor transmission but allows liquid water penetration; lighter than waterproofing. Positive-side waterproofing is applied directly on the surface exposed to water (exterior basement walls, roof decks). It resists hydrostatic pressure by blocking water before infiltration. Positive-side = exterior/source side. Negative-side = interior. Tropical hydrostatic requires positive-side.
