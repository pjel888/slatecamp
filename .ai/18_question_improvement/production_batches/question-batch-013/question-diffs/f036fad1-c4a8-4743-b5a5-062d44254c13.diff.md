## Added explanation

Hagia Sophia was converted in 1453 and lacks the extensive Iznik tilework of the Blue Mosque. The Süleymaniye (1550–1557) predates the Blue Mosque and has four half-domes. The Selimiye has four corner half-domes and eight supporting pillars and is dominated by marble rather than Iznik tiles. The Blue Mosque (Sultan Ahmed Mosque) is identified as Ottoman, 1609–1616 CE, with a central dome, cascading semi-domes, six minarets (an unusual number that was controversial at the time, as most Ottoman imperial mosques had fewer), and extensive Iznik tile decoration predominantly in blue which gives it its popular name.
