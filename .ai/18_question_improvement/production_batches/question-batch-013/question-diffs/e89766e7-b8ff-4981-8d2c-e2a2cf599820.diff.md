## Added explanation

Rigid couplings would transmit seismic forces and increase rupture risk, defeating the isolation purpose. Concrete-lined conduit is used for different applications and does not provide the necessary flexibility for seismic isolation. Lead-lined valves address radiation or chemical isolation, not seismic displacement. Per NSCP 2015 guidance cited in the article, piping systems require flex couplings at seismic isolation joints to accommodate relative motion and prevent rupture during earthquake events. Flex couplings at seismic isolation joints allow piping systems to accommodate earthquake-induced motion without rupturing.
