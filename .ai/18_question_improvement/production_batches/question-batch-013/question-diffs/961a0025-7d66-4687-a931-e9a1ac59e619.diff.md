Elevation alone may be insufficient if flood levels exceed 1 meter; check valves provide active protection regardless of elevation. Pipe diameter does not address contamination prevention; it affects flow rate only. Pipe material (copper vs. steel) does not prevent contamination from backflow or floodwater ingress. Per the article, water supply intakes in flood-prone buildings must be elevated above flood level OR equipped with check valves and automatic shutoff to prevent contamination from backflow during utility main failures.
