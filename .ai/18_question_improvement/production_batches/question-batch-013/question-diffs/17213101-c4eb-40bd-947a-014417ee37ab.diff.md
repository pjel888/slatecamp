The article identifies Ottoman domes as refined innovations, not identical systems. Ottoman domes are described as massive and structurally significant, not smaller. The article indicates Byzantine domes use quarter-domes, not that Ottoman domes avoid pendentives. Ottoman domes rest on cascading half-domes and pendentives; Byzantine domes (like Hagia Sophia's) are supported by the wall mass and quarter-domes of the building.
