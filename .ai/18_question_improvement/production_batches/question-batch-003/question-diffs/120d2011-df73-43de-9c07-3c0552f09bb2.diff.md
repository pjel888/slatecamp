## Added explanation

The Bauhaus extended beyond fine art to include industrial design, craft, and architecture. The Bauhaus was forward-looking and engaged modern materials and industrial processes, not preservation. The Bauhaus integrated art with technical production; it was not research-only. The Bauhaus 'sought a Gesamtkunstwerk (total work of art), unifying all creative disciplines' and emphasizes Gropius's pedagogical philosophy that 'the school is the servant of the workshop,' combining imaginative design with technical proficiency through hands-on making.
