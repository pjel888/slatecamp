## Added explanation

Le Corbusier's primary principle was the 'machine for living,' not form follows function. Mies van der Rohe is associated with 'Less is more' and 'God is in the details.' Wright pioneered organic architecture; he did not articulate the form-follows-function principle. Louis Sullivan published 'Form follows function' in his 1896 article 'The Tall Office Building Artistically Considered.' architectural theory 'became modernism's rallying cry after the 1930s' despite Sullivan himself using ornament in his work.
