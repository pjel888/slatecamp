## Added explanation

Steel panels are system formwork, more expensive. Aluminum/OSB is less common in Philippine practice; coco lumber dominates. Plastic formwork is rare; timber is cost-effective. Conventional formwork: timber beams (coco lumber is economical in Philippines)/joists → plywood decking → props (vertical timber or steel columns) at base. Advantages: flexible, adaptable, low capital cost, reusable ~5–10 times. Standard in Philippine low-rise construction.
