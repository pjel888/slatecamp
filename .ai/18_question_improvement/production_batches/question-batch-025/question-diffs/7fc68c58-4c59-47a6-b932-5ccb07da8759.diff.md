## Added explanation

100 µg/m³ is too restrictive and not the ASHRAE standard. 150 µg/m³ is lower than the ASHRAE threshold; paints and adhesives alone do not address total VOCs. 300 µg/m³ exceeds ASHRAE limits; standard materials do not meet IAQ requirements. ASHRAE 62.1 specifies a maximum total VOC concentration of 200 µg/m³. Low-VOC materials, adhesives, and finishes can reduce emissions by 50-70%, particularly during the commissioning phase when natural ventilation at 6-8 ACH accelerates off-gassing.
