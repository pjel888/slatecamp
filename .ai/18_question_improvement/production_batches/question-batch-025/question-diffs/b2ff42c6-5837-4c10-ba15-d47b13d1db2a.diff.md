## Added explanation

The 0.75 reduction is an older NSCP feature, not used in NSCP 2015. Wind loads are integrated into the design load combinations; they are not deferred to a separate phase. NSCP 2015 does not use a 0.5 factor on live load; the 1.0 wind factor reflects the fact that E and W already include uncertainties. NSCP 2015 specifies two distinct formulas: the combined case U = 1.2D + 1.0W + L (L = full service live load, unfactored) and the wind-governs case U = 0.9D + 1.0W. No 0.75 reduction factor is applied in NSCP 2015. Review the correct answer and associated code provision.
