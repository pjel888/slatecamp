## Added explanation

Moment connections are more expensive, not economical. Both types can be bolted or welded; fastening method is independent of connection type. Both types are used in all building classifications depending on structural demands. Moment connections are rigid and transfer full bending moments required for lateral stability (e.g., seismic frames). Shear (simple) connections allow rotation and transfer only gravity loads. Moment = rigid (seismic); Shear = simple (gravity). Seismic zones demand moment connections.
