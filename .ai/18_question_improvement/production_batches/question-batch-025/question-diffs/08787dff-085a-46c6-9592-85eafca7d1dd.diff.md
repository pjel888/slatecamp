## Added explanation

By definition, a non-bearing wall does not support the floors above. Shear walls are part of the lateral force-resisting system; a curtain wall is a non-structural component. NSCP Chapter 2 (Loads) and Chapter 6 (Wind) explicitly require seismic and wind design for non-structural components like cladding. Curtain walls must be designed to 'drift' with the building frame during an earthquake. The anchors must prevent the wall from falling off (out-of-plane forces) while allowing for horizontal movement relative to the floors. Curtain walls must accommodate inter-story drift and resist lateral pressures.
