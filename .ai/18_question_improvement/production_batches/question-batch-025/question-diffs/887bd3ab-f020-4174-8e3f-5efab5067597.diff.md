## Added explanation

That is the function of control joints, not expansion joints. Dowel bars in construction joints transfer shear; expansion joints are for movement accommodation. That is the function of isolation joints. Expansion joints allow continuous gaps that accommodate thermal expansion as temperature rises, preventing stress buildup and buckling. Expansion = thermal movement; Control = drying shrinkage; Construction = shear transfer; Isolation = complete separation.
