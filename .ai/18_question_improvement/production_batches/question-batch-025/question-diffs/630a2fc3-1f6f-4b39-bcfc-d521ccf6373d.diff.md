## Added explanation

Purchase costs and supplier information are procurement documents, not coordination schedules. These are managed separately from design coordination. Construction phasing and contractor assignments are construction administration documents prepared during project execution, not design coordination documents. Tenant improvement allowances and interior material selections are project management and design development documents, not coordination schedules. Coordination schedules focus on building systems spatial relationships. Coordination schedules document shaft dimensions, bulkhead locations, major equipment locations with access requirements, penetration locations with firestopping specifications, and access panel locations for maintenance. These provide consistent reference information across the building. Coordination schedules are reference tools for construction teams showing MEPF spatial requirements. They ensure consistency in shaft sizing, bulkhead depths, and firestopping across multiple floors and building sections. These schedules prevent installation conflicts caused by dimensional inconsistencies.
