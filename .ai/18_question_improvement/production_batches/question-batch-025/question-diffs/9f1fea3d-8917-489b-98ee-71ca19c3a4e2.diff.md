## Added explanation

Exterior walls include the entire building envelope; a parapet is specifically the portion above the roof. Parapets are typically non-bearing as they do not support floor or roof loads above them. Parapets must resist both wind and seismic lateral forces. A parapet is defined as that part of any wall entirely above the roof line. It is exposed to environmental loads (wind) on both sides and must be designed as a cantilever. Parapet = portion of wall above the roof line.
