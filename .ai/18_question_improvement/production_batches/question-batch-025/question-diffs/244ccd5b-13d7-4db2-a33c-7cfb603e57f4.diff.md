## Added explanation

ρ/g×h is dimensionally incorrect. Curing duration doesn't increase pressure; longer curing allows pressure reduction. Admixture dosage (accelerators/retarders) affects set time, not directly pressure. Concrete form pressure ≈ ρg×h (weight density × gravity × fluid height). For vertical forms, max pressure occurs at depth. Warmer concrete (~30°C) sets faster than cool concrete (~10°C), so strength develops quicker, reducing time the concrete acts as a fluid. Result: lower peak pressure on forms. Placement rate also affects pressure—rapid placement maintains higher fluid height longer.
