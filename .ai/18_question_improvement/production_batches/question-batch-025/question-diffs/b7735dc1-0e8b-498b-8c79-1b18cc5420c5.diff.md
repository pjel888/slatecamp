## Added explanation

Silicone has superior UV stability; polyurethane can yellow/degrade under prolonged UV without topcoat. Silicone is non-corrosive and superior in salt-spray; polyurethane is adequate but not optimal for coastal zones. Polyurethane requires moisture-management during application and cure; not lower cost than silicone. Polyurethane (ASTM C1619) adheres strongly to glass, metal, and painted surfaces (critical for curtain walls). Cure is rapid (24-48 hrs walkable in tropical heat/humidity), where silicone may require 7+ days. Moisture-accelerated polymerization is advantageous in tropical humidity. Polyurethane = practical choice for glass-metal bonds and rapid cure in humid tropics. Silicone = premium choice for UV/salt spray. Match sealant to substrate and cure conditions.
