## Added explanation

Two-sided bonding (top and sides) is correct; backer rod prevents three-sided. One-sided bonding would be non-functional; sealant must bond at least two sides. Multi-directional is not a relevant term for bonding surfaces. Backer rod prevents three-sided bonding (adhesion on all three surfaces: top, sides, bottom). With backer rod, sealant bonds only to top and sides, allowing proper flex. Three-sided bonding concentrates stress at the center bottom and prevents elongation. Backer rod is mandatory for all elastomeric sealant applications. Prevents stress concentration and enables proper sealant movement.
