## Added explanation

Expansion joints handle thermal movement, not settlement accommodation. Control joints manage shrinkage cracking, not differential settlement. Construction joints use dowels to transfer load; they do not allow independent movement. An isolation joint (or isolation bearing) allows independent settlement of each building without transferring loads or restricting movement. Rigid connections would crack under differential settlement. When in doubt about differential settlement or incompatible structures, specify isolation joints with bearing pads.
