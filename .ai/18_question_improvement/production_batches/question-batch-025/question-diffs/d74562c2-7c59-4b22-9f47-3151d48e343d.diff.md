## Added explanation

Equal distribution does not account for the pressure differential principle that makes cross-ventilation effective. Reversed ratios (larger on leeward) contradicts wind pressure physics and reduces ventilation effectiveness. Single-sided ventilation does not achieve cross-ventilation and limits air exchange. Cross-ventilation design typically allocates 5-7% of floor area on the windward (high pressure) side and 3-5% on the leeward (low pressure) side to leverage pressure differentials created by wind-driven ventilation. Asymmetric sizing optimizes air velocity through the space. Cross-Ventilation Design: Windward 5-7%, Leeward 3-5% of floor area
