## Added explanation

Dowel placement does not affect thermal expansion accommodation. Corrosion is a concern but not the primary structural consequence. Dowel placement is critical to structural performance. Mid-depth placement ensures dowels are fully effective at transferring shear between the two concrete pours. Surface placement leaves most of the slab thickness unbraced, reducing shear capacity and allowing relative movement. Dowel bars must be at mid-depth for full effectiveness. Surface placement = construction defect.
