## Added explanation

That describes the CLUP (Comprehensive Land Use Plan), not the zoning ordinance. The CLUP is the vision; the ordinance is the law that implements it. The Zoning Ordinance is a legislative tool adopted by the Sanggunian that translates CLUP decisions into enforceable rules specifying what uses are allowed in each zone, building height limits, setbacks, lot sizes, and other regulations. The Planning Office drafts the zoning ordinance, but the Sanggunian must formally adopt it as law. Guidelines alone are not enforceable. DHSUD reviews the ordinance for conformity with national standards, but the ordinance itself is not a construction permit—it sets the rules under which permits are issued.
