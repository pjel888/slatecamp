## Added explanation

Stupa is a hemispherical dome, not a multi-story tapering tower. Shikhara is a Hindu tower, not a Buddhist monument. Gopura is a Hindu gateway, not a Buddhist vertical monument. Pagoda is explicitly defined as a vertical Buddhist monument typically 5 or 7 stories with diminishing floor levels, domed roofs, and radiating eaves. Unlike Chinese equivalents, Japanese pagodas often house no relics—they stand as sculptural monuments.
