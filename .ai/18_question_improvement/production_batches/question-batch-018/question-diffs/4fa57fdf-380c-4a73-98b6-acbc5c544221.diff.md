Art Deco emerged in the 1920s–30s; Burnham's work predated and influenced different aesthetics. Ebenezer Howard's Garden City Movement (1898+) emphasized smaller, self-contained communities — different from Burnham's monumental civic grandeur. Burnham was the principal architect of the City Beautiful Movement (1890s–1910s), emphasizing monumental civic spaces, diagonal boulevards, parks, and classical architecture. His Plan of Manila (1905) applied City Beautiful principles to the Philippine capital. New Urbanism emerged in the 1980s, long after Burnham.
