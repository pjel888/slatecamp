## Added explanation

Typology involves transformation and reinterpretation, not exact replication. Typology is functional-structural, not decorative. Typology addresses persistent functional problems, not merely dimensional classification. The Key Terms define typology as 'System of recurring building types (courthouse, marketplace, dwelling, tower) that persist across history and cultures, providing structural solutions to persistent problems.' Rossi saw types not as historical artifacts but as 'structural solutions' within a meaningful system.
