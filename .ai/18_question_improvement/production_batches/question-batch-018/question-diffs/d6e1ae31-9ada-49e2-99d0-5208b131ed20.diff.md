## Added explanation

Intramuros has fortifications, but the Laws of the Indies mandate was about town planning principles, not exclusively military walls. The Laws of the Indies (1573) mandated a grid-plaza town plan for all Spanish colonial settlements — a central plaza (plaza mayor) with the church, cabildo (municipal hall), and governor's residence, surrounded by a regular street grid. This became the template for every Philippine colonial town. Radial boulevards are Baroque/Haussmannian — not the Laws of the Indies pattern. Linear settlements along rivers were not the mandated pattern.
