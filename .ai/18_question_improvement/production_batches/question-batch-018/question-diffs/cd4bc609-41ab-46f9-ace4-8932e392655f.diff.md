## Added explanation

Bubble diagrams are intentionally not to scale; size indicates approximate area relationship loosely, not precise dimensions. Including walls and furniture transforms a bubble diagram into a preliminary design sketch, conflating programming with design. Bubbles may be circles, squares, or irregular shapes; shape carries no architectural meaning. Bubble diagrams are abstract analytical tools. They show which spaces must be adjacent and which must be separated, but intentionally avoid prescribing form.
