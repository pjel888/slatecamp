## Added explanation

While both are urban theorists, Rowe and Koetter are specifically identified for structuralist urban analysis. While Eisenman and Tschumi engaged structural thinking, Rowe and Koetter's Collage City is the canonical structuralist urban text. Wright and Le Corbusier were designers, not primarily urban analysts in the structuralist sense. Colin Rowe and Fred Koetter's Collage City (1978) as applying 'structuralist urban analysis...examined how cities themselves operate as systems of meaningful relationships rather than collections of individual buildings.' This structuralist approach proved productive for understanding actual cities.
