## Added explanation

Jencks extended semiotic analysis but did not originate the duck/shed concept. Lévi-Strauss developed structuralism philosophically but not architectural semiotics. Rossi developed typological thinking; Venturi developed the duck/shed distinction. Robert Venturi, while not explicitly a semiotician, 'applied semiotic analysis to architecture, particularly in his development of the duck and decorated shed distinction.' This became foundational to postmodern semiotic architectural analysis.
