## Added explanation

Sant'Andrea is described as an oval church with undulating walls. The concave portico and hidden window are key features of Bernini's Sant'Andrea. Sculptural decoration is central to Bernini's dramatic Baroque approach. The coffered dome creating illusionistic height is Borromini's innovation at San Carlo, not Bernini's approach at Sant'Andrea. Bernini's Sant'Andrea features an 'oval church with undulating walls, concave portico, dramatic light pouring from a hidden window behind the altarpiece, and sculptural decoration emphasizing movement and emotion.'
