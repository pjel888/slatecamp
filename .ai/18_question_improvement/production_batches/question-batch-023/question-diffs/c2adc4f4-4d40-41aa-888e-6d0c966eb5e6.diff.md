## Added explanation

6.0 kg/m² assumes a full 40% reduction, not accounting for the correct multiplicative calculation (25% + 15% does not equal 40% when applied sequentially). 7.5 kg/m² applies only the first reduction (lean) and omits prefabrication, which is incomplete. This incorrectly adds reductions linearly (10 - 1.5) rather than applying them sequentially and multiplicatively to the baseline. Lean implementation: 10 × 0.75 = 7.5 kg/m². Prefabrication applies to this reduced baseline: 7.5 × 0.85 = 6.375 ≈ 6.4 kg/m². The two reductions are multiplicative, not additive. Total reduction is 1 - (0.75 × 0.85) = ~0.36 or 36%. When multiple waste reduction strategies are applied sequentially, reductions compound multiplicatively, not additively. Candidates must calculate each reduction against the previous baseline, not against the original.
