## Added explanation

Glass facades increase heat gain and are not used in Kéré's climate‑responsive designs. Concrete is not Kéré's primary material; he prioritizes earth and timber for local relevance and thermal performance. Steel reinforcement is not the primary function; the lattice screens serve ventilation and shading, not structural reinforcement against rain. Compressed earth blocks provide high thermal mass, absorbing heat during the day and releasing it at night. Timber lattice screens (often combined with perforated ceilings and overhanging roofs) allow cross‑ventilation and shade, promoting passive cooling—essential strategies for the hot, dry Sahel climate.
