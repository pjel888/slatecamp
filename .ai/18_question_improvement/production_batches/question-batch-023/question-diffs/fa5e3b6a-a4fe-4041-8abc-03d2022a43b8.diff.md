## Added explanation

While smartphone tools may track materials, BIM's primary waste-reduction benefit is quantification accuracy and design coordination, not logistics tracking. BIM complements design review and engineering; it does not eliminate these processes. BIM does not require or guarantee automation; it is a data integration and visualization tool used by design and planning teams. BIM integrates multidisciplinary data and enables clash detection (identify conflicts before construction) and material quantification (calculate exact volumes before ordering). BIM-driven quantity takeoff reduces error-induced waste by 5-15% compared to manual estimates. BIM reduces waste through precise quantification and coordination. Candidates must understand that BIM accuracy prevents over-ordering and design conflicts that cause rework and waste generation.
