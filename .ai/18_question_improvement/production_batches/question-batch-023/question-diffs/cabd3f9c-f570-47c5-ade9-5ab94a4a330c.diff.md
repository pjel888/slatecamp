## Added explanation

The nave and columns are structural elements, not the Baldachin. Michelangelo's dome is earlier and different in purpose and design. The front portico predates Bernini's Baroque interventions. Bernini's Baldachin (1623–1634) is a bronze canopy using 'twisted Solomonic columns, gilding, and dynamic curves' to create a 'throne of spiritual authority' above the altar.
