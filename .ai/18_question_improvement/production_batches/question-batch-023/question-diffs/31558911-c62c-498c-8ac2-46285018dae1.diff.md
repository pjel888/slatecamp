Exterior columns do not relate to the interior illusionism of perceived size. While Baroque uses dramatic light, this is not the primary device Borromini uses to create illusionistic size in San Carlo. While San Carlo has ornament, the specific illusionistic device is the coffered dome and curved walls, not ornamentation alone. San Carlo's 'coffered dome creates illusionistic height, curved walls, and geometric precision so extreme that the space feels larger than its actual dimensions.'
