## Added explanation

While 8-9% is the baseline allocation, a 25% reduction does not automatically equal 25% of all project points. Embodied carbon is one component within the materials credit. BERDE does evaluate embodied carbon and waste management as integrated factors within the materials and emissions categories, not as isolated credits. A 25% embodied carbon reduction is significant but does not guarantee BERDE 5-star certification, which requires overall scoring across all categories (energy, water, waste, indoor environment, innovation). BERDE version 5 allocates 8-9% of total points to sustainable materials credits. A 25% embodied carbon reduction may qualify for additional bonuses within this credit category, though automatic certification is not guaranteed. Embodied carbon reduction is recognized, but candidates must understand that it is one credit component within the broader green building evaluation framework.
