The interior decoration was integral to the building's function, not wasteful. While priests accessed the interior, the building's function was not personal appreciation but religious and administrative. The sculptural program was part of the original design (447–432 BCE), not a later addition. The Parthenon housed a chryselephantine (gold and ivory) statue of Athena, 40+ feet tall, now lost. The sculptural program included a continuous frieze around the naos depicting the Panathenaic procession, and pediment sculptures.
