## Added explanation

24% is far lower than the actual 76% reduction. Confusing recycled and virgin embodied carbon values is a critical error. While aluminum is lighter and lower-cost than steel, ASTM A496 specifies steel wire reinforcement; aluminum does not meet structural reinforcement standards. Recycled aluminum (2.0 kg CO2e/kg) has significantly lower embodied carbon than virgin (8.24 kg CO2e/kg). Material selection must account for this substantial difference. Reduction: (8.24-2.0)/8.24 ≈ 76%. Aluminum is not suitable for structural concrete reinforcement per ASTM standards. Its high cost and corrosion susceptibility in concrete limit use to non-structural cladding, window frames, and interior finish components. Recycled aluminum reduces embodied carbon by 76% compared to virgin but is unsuitable for structural reinforcement. Candidates must understand material-specific embodied carbon and application limitations.
