## Added explanation

Safety factors in codes are typically 1.2 to 1.6, not a 100% (2.0) margin for unspecified future uses. Unless the contractor deviated from plans, the failure is due to overloading, not material quality. Structural overloading is a human error/negligence, not an 'Act of God'. The owner is responsible for ensuring that the building is not subjected to loads greater than those for which it was designed. Doubling the load from 6.0 to 12.0 kPa is a gross violation of the design capacity. Owners must adhere to design load limits.
