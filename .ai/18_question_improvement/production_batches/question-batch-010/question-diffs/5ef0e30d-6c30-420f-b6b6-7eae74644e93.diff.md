## Added explanation

Kouroi are nude male sculptures in archaic Greek art, not figures serving as columns. Atlantes are male figures serving as supports (the male equivalent of caryatids); caryatids are female. Herms are boundary markers or posts topped with a bust of Hermes, not columns. The article defines: 'Caryatid (or Caryatids): A sculpted female figure serving as a column or support; seen in the Porch of the Maidens at the Erechtheion.'
