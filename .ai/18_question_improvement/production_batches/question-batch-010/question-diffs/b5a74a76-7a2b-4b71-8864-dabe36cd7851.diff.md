## Added explanation

ASTM C618 covers fly ash, not GGBS. Fly ash replacement rates (15-25%) are lower than GGBS because fly ash lacks cementitious properties. ASTM C33 specifies fine and coarse aggregate gradation, not cement replacement materials. ASTM C150 defines Portland cement composition; GGBS is not a Portland cement product but a supplementary cementitious material meeting its own ASTM C989 standard. GGBS is cooled byproduct slag from iron smelting meeting ASTM C989 specifications. It provides both pozzolanic (silica-based) and cementitious (calcium-based) reactions, allowing higher replacement rates of 30-50% compared to fly ash (15-25%). GGBS (ASTM C989) allows higher cement replacement (30-50%) than fly ash (ASTM C618: 15-25%) because it combines pozzolanic and self-cementing properties. Candidates must match material type to the correct ASTM standard and dosage limits.
