## Added explanation

A stoa is a portico or covered walkway with columns, not a circular temple. Cella (or naos) is the inner chamber of a temple, not a circular building type. Peristyle refers to a colonnade surrounding a building, not a specific circular building type. The Treasury of Atreus at Mycenae (c. 1350 BCE, pre-Classical but influential) featured a corbelled dome—a "tholos" or circular structure that predates Greek post-and-lintel temples. Later Greek architects revived the tholos form for certain temples and treasuries, such as the Tholos of Polyclitus at Epidaurus (c. 360 BCE).'
