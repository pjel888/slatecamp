## Added explanation

While RCA can meet ASTM C33 gradation, assuming it meets structural concrete requirements without testing is incorrect. Strength and durability must be verified. RCA's higher absorption makes it unsuitable for many fill applications if water ingress is a concern. Its use is primarily limited to controlled fill and base courses. Cost reduction alone does not justify RCA specification. Applications must match durability, strength, and absorption characteristics to intended use. RCA is acceptable for non-structural uses (fill, base course) when meeting ASTM C33 gradation. RCA exhibits higher absorption (3-8% vs 0.5-1% for virgin) and lower strength, limiting use in structural concrete without testing and strength verification. Recycled concrete aggregates are limited to non-structural applications where absorption and strength losses are acceptable. Candidates must recognize RCA limitations and avoid over-specifying reuse in structural concrete without verification testing.
