## Added explanation

Cost is a business concern, not a code requirement. Stainless steel is compatible with aluminum; they are often used together. Proper stainless hardware requires less, not more, maintenance. Coastal environments expose hardware to salt spray, which causes crevice corrosion and pitting in standard stainless steel (304/316 grades). Saltwater-grade hardware (duplex 2205 or super-duplex) resists this attack. This is critical for Philippine typhoon zones. Coastal regions require saltwater-grade hardware certification; do not assume standard stainless is sufficient.
