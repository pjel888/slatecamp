## Added explanation

10—20% is excessive and would significantly reduce strength. 0.5—1% is insufficient for freeze-thaw protection (incidental air, not intentional entrainment). 20—30% would be foam, not properly entrained air. Air entrainment typically produces 2—8% air by volume, with bubble diameter 10—1000 micrometers. This is the standard range for freeze-thaw protection. Air entrainment = 2—8% volume. Each 1% air reduces compressive strength ~3—5%. Trade-off: durability vs. strength.
