## Added explanation

Continuous hinges are typically more expensive due to their length and fabrication. Neither hinge type allows bidirectional swing without additional hardware. Continuous hinges are visible on the edge of the door; concealed hinges hide the mechanism. Continuous hinges extend the full door height, distributing stress along the entire hinge length rather than at discrete points (butt hinge locations). This is essential for heavy doors and fire-rated assemblies. Continuous hinges = load distribution; use them for heavy doors and fire-rated assemblies.
