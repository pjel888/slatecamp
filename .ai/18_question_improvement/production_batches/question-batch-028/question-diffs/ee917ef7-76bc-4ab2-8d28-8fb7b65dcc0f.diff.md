## Added explanation

Cylindrical locks are faster to install but offer lower security; typical for Grade 2-3 applications. Push-button locks are specialty hardware; not standard for commercial grades. Deadbolt-only locks lack a latch mechanism for automatic closure. Mortise locks have separate latch and deadbolt mechanisms housed in the frame, providing superior security and durability compared to cylindrical locks (all components in the knob). Grade 1 commercial can be either mortise or cylindrical if certified. Mortise lock = Grade 1 commercial; Cylindrical lock = Grade 2-3 residential or light commercial.
