## Added explanation

Additive masonry refers to stacking blocks (like Great Zimbabwe), not removing material. Dry-stone construction places stones without mortar but does not involve carving into living rock. Corbelled vaulting is a structural system using triangular arches, not relevant to Lalibela's carving technique. Lalibela employs subtractive carving—workers removed surrounding rock to reveal churches from single stone masses, creating monolithic structures.
