## Added explanation

Standard reducers (Type A) only reduce 5—10%. Accelerators don't reduce water demand; Types F/G do. Air-entraining agents (C260) are different from C494 admixtures; minimal water reduction. Types F and G are superplasticizers providing 15—30% water reduction (compared to 5—10% for standard Type A reducers). They enable self-consolidating concrete and very low water-cement ratios. Type F/G = superplasticizers = high-range water reduction (15—30%). Use for SCC, flowing concrete, high strength.
