## Added explanation

200,000 cycles is the Grade 3 residential threshold. 400,000 cycles is the minimum for Grade 2 medium-duty threshold. 800,000 cycles is a possible Grade 2 rating, but Grade 1 requires 1,000,000 cycles. Grade 1 hardware is rated for a minimum of 1,000,000 cycles, the highest BHMA performance tier for heavy commercial applications. Memorize the cycle counts: Grade 1 = 1,000,000, Grade 2 = 400,000–800,000, Grade 3 = 200,000.
