## Added explanation

The comparison is reversed; sealants are flexible. Both types are used for multiple substrates; application determines the choice. The terms are not interchangeable; they serve different functions. Adhesives bond substrates rigidly; sealants remain elastic to accommodate joint movement and prevent air/water infiltration. This is the fundamental distinction. Adhesive = permanent rigid bond. Sealant = flexible, accommodates movement.
