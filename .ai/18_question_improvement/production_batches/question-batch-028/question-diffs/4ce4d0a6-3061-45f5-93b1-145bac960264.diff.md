## Added explanation

Le Corbusier was explicitly anti-intuitive; he believed in rational, theoretical foundations. Modernism explicitly rejected historical copying. Le Corbusier integrated aesthetics with function; neither is purely separate. Le Corbusier's systematic articulation and demonstration of principles in actual buildings shows his belief that architecture can be based on teachable, communicable principles—not intuition or tradition.
