## Added explanation

Capacity is adequate in theory, but hinge type is wrong for the application. Grade 1 capacity is high, but wrong hinge type for glass. Safety factor is not the issue; application-specific hinge selection is. Although 1,500 lbf capacity exceeds the door weight, frameless glass doors have no frame to mortise butt hinges into. Pivot hinges are specifically designed for this application, with top and bottom support. Using butt hinges on glass creates a glass failure hazard. Hinge selection must match application: butt for wood doors, pivot for glass doors, regardless of load capacity.
