## Added explanation

Freeze-thaw is not a cause in tropical Philippines; efflorescence occurs without frost. Mortar joint failure is not the cause; efflorescence indicates moisture flow, not joint failure. Cracking is not the cause; efflorescence can occur in uncracked units. Efflorescence is white mineral deposits (calcium carbonate, sodium sulfate) that migrate through the unit with moisture and evaporate on the surface. Prevention requires good drainage and sealed joints to limit moisture penetration. Efflorescence is aesthetic but indicates excess moisture. It does not reduce structural capacity but signals drainage or waterproofing deficiency.
