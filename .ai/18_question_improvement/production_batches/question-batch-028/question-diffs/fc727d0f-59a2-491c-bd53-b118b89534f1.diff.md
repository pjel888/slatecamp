## Added explanation

Admixtures do affect strength indirectly through water-cement ratio modification. Water reduction increases strength (lower w/c), not decreases it. Doubling strength from 10% water reduction is unrealistic; typical gain is 5—15%. Reducing water by 10% (w/c = 0.50 → ~0.45) increases strength approximately 5—15%. The exact increase depends on cement type and curing. 30 MPa × 1.08 ≈ 32 MPa (conservative estimate). Water reduction ≈ strength increase. Lower w/c = higher strength (approximately 10 MPa increase per 0.05 reduction in w/c for normal Portland cement).
