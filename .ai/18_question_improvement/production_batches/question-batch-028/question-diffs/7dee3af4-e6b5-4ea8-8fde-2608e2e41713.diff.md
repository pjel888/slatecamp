## Added explanation

Butt hinges are for standard wooden doors; they cannot support glass doors without a substantial frame. Concealed hinges are hidden within the door but are not rated for glass door loads. Spring hinges are for automatic closure, not weight support. Pivot hinges mount at the top and bottom of the door frame, supporting the full weight of heavy or frameless glass doors through a top-mounted pivot mechanism. Butt hinges cannot support glass door frames effectively. Pivot hinges = heavy/glass doors; butt hinges = standard wood doors.
