## Added explanation

Increasing water is not the purpose of water reducers. Type A affects water, so slump is not maintained constant without adjustment. Water cannot be eliminated; admixtures reduce, not eliminate, water demand. Type A water reducers reduce water demand by 5—10% for the same slump, allowing higher strength (lower water-cement ratio) or lower cement content (cost savings). Type A = water reduction only. No effect on set time (primary advantage over Types D, E, F, G which include other effects).
