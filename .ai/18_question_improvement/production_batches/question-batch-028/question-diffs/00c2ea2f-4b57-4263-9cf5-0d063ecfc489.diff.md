## Added explanation

Renewable energy installation is desirable and can earn points, but it is not a mandatory prerequisite; many projects achieve BERDE without on-site PV. Green roofs are an innovation strategy, not a prerequisite; they are optional. Rainwater harvesting is one strategy to meet Water Efficiency prerequisite, but the prerequisite itself (potable water reduction) is mandatory; the implementation strategy varies. Water Efficiency is a mandatory BERDE prerequisite; all projects must demonstrate minimum potable water reduction targets. This reflects the Philippines' water management challenges, especially in high-precipitation regions. BERDE prerequisites are mandatory minimum performance levels; Water Efficiency, Energy Efficiency, Site Selection, Materials, and Waste Management are all required categories.
