## Added explanation

2,000 psi is C90 concrete block minimum, not clay brick. 1,500 psi is below both standards. 2,500 psi is below the 3,000 psi clay brick minimum. ASTM C62 clay brick minimum is 3,000 psi (20.7 MPa) average, compared to 2,000 psi (13.8 MPa) for C90 concrete blocks. Clay brick is stronger due to high-temperature firing. Know the strength ranking: Clay brick (3,000 psi) > Concrete block C90 (2,000 psi) > Non-loadbearing C129 (600 psi).
