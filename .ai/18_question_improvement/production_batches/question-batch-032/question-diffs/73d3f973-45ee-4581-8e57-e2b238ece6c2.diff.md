## Added explanation

0.60Fy is the allowable axial compression stress, not shear. 0.66Fy is the allowable bending stress, not shear. 0.50Fy is not a standard ASTM A36 allowable fraction. Shear stress in steel is governed by the lower allowable stress fraction 0.40Fy. For A36 with Fy = 250 MPa, Fv = 0.40 × 248 = 99.2 ≈ 99 MPa. Review the correct answer and associated code provision.
