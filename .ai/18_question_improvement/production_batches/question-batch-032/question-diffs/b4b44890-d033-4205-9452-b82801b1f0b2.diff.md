## Added explanation

A153 galvanizing is compatible with A307 and A325; the issue is A490 susceptibility. Galvanizing adds minimal material; strength reduction is negligible. Galvanizing A490 is explicitly prohibited due to H-embrittlement. A490 at 150 ksi is susceptible to hydrogen embrittlement during hot-dip galvanizing. Absorbed hydrogen causes brittle fracture under tensile load. For marine environments requiring galvanizing, specify A325 (120 ksi, galvanizing-compatible) instead, or A490 stainless (expensive but H-embrittlement safe). NSCP 2015 and RCSC explicitly prohibit galvanizing A490.
