## Added explanation

Museum preservation without use is heritage conservation without functional viability; adaptive reuse maintains active use. Facade preservation alone is not adaptive reuse; interior reconfiguration to new functions is essential. Adaptive reuse preserves the building; demolition and reconstruction is the opposite strategy. Three intersecting concerns: (1) 'Environmental sustainability—preserving embodied carbon investment rather than demolishing,' (2) 'Heritage conservation—maintaining cultural and historical significance,' (3) 'Urban economics—revitalization and reduced sprawl.' Adaptive reuse is transformative practice addressing all three.
