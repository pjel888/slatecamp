## Added explanation

Galvanizing adds minimal thickness; diameter loss is negligible. Cost drives some A490/stainless substitutions, but H-embrittlement is the primary technical concern. A490 is manufactured as standard steel; the issue is the plating process itself, not incompatibility with manufacturing. High-strength bolts (A490 at 150 ksi) are susceptible to hydrogen embrittlement during electroplating and hot-dip galvanizing processes. Hydrogen atoms are absorbed into the high-strength steel, causing brittle fracture under tensile load even when preload and design appear correct. A325 (120 ksi) is less susceptible due to lower strength, making it the pragmatic choice for galvanized applications.
