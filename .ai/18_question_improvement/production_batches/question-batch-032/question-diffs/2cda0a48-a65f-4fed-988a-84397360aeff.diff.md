## Added explanation

Partial spline breakoff is an anomaly requiring replacement, not normal variation. Over-tensioning TC bolts is prevented by the automatic spline breakoff mechanism; this is an under-tension condition. For slip-critical (moment-resisting frames), under-tension is unacceptable regardless of connection type. TC bolts are designed to break the spline at approximately 90% of UTS. Complete spline breakoff confirms proper preload. Incomplete breakoff (50% remaining) indicates the bolt did not reach target preload due to equipment malfunction, operator error, or thread contamination. For slip-critical connections (moment-resisting frames), this bolt is inadequate and must be removed and replaced. For bearing connections, under-tensioning is less critical, but code practice is to replace the bolt for consistency.
