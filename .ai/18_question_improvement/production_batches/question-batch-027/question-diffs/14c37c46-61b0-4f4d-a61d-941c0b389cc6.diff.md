## Added explanation

Some conflicts are so fundamental that disclosure and consent cannot adequately protect the client. Family relationships can sometimes be managed through disclosure; irreconcilable conflicts require recusal. Overstates the requirement; many competing interests are manageable through disclosure and consent. Disclosure and consent cannot cure irreconcilable conflicts, confidentiality breaches, or situations where fundamental professional integrity is compromised; recusal is then required. Recusal is required when conflicts are fundamentally irreconcilable, not merely when a competing interest exists.
