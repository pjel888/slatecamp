## Added explanation

Prescriptive specs mandate the specific product with no substitution option. Performance specs define outcomes without naming products; contractor selects the means. Reference standard specs defer details to published standards, not specific products. Open proprietary specifications identify a preferred product and include 'or equal' clauses permitting substitution when equality criteria are met objectively. Closed proprietary specifications name a specific product and prohibit substitutions. Open proprietary specs balance certainty (named product) with flexibility (or equal option). This is the most common specification type in Philippine practice.
