## Added explanation

Air space provides thermal and moisture benefits, but actually complicates structural load transfer. Headers in single-wythe walls are purely decorative; in multi-wythe walls, they are structural ties. Insulation has no structural bonding capacity; mechanical ties or headers are always required. A single-wythe wall is a solid, continuous mass. A cavity wall consists of two distinct leaves; without headers or ties, each leaf would buckle or sway independently, significantly reducing the total lateral resistance. [cite: 2, 7, 15] Composite action in cavity walls requires mechanical connection.
