## Added explanation

Changing the order disrupts the logical flow from framework to materials to installation. Execution cannot precede General because the quality framework is needed first. This illogical order would confuse specifications and contractor understanding. The 3-part CSI SectionFormat is standardized as Part 1 (General: quality, standards, testing, submittals), Part 2 (Products: materials and equipment specifications), and Part 3 (Execution: installation procedures and quality control). The 3-part format is the industry standard. Always organize: Part 1 (What standards apply?), Part 2 (What is being supplied?), Part 3 (How is it installed?).
