## Added explanation

Withdrawal is not always required; many conflicts can be managed through disclosure and consent. The client has autonomy to accept, decline, or impose conditions after full disclosure. Disclosure without consent is ethically insufficient; the client must affirmatively agree to proceed. Disclosure alone is insufficient; the architect must obtain written informed consent from the client, who has the right to decline proceeding or require mitigation steps. After disclosure, the architect must obtain the client's written informed consent; the client must have the right to decline or impose conditions.
