## Added explanation

CSI standards are generic; Philippine architects must add climate-specific performance criteria. Quality standards belong in Part 1, not just product selection in Part 2. Contractor selection without specified standards leads to disputes over acceptability. Philippine tropical construction requires customization of CSI specifications. High humidity promotes mold growth, intense solar radiation requires UV-stable paint, and coastal/pollution exposure requires washable, durable finishes. These are additions to CSI baseline standards, not replacements. CSI MasterFormat is a framework; Philippine architects customize it for local climate, materials availability, and regulatory requirements. Tropical durability, typhoon resistance, and humidity control are regular additions to CSI specifications.
