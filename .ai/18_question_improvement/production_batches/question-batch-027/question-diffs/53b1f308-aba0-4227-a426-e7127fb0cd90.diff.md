## Added explanation

80% coverage is not sufficient; the compound must completely seal the surface. Faster curing in uncovered areas actually creates differential drying (not beneficial); interior is still hydrating while exposed areas have dried. Patchy application is a critical defect; it voids the curing benefits. Curing compound must form a continuous, complete seal to prevent evaporation. Patchy coverage (20% uncovered) allows rapid moisture loss from uncovered areas. Differential drying creates local weak zones and internal stress concentrations, leading to random cracking and reduced durability. Curing compound application is all-or-nothing. Patchy or incomplete coverage is worse than no compound (since it may give false confidence). Always inspect coverage and reapply to achieve 100% seal.
