## Added explanation

Biomimetics and algorithmic optimization are contemporary approaches unrelated to the Bauhaus's early 20th-century educational framework. These elements were explicitly rejected by the Bauhaus, which opposed historical eclecticism, ornament, and any residual romanticism in design. Post-structuralist philosophy and computational methods did not emerge until the late 20th century and are associated with Deconstructivism and Parametricism, not the Bauhaus. The Bauhaus integrated craft ideology (from Arts and Crafts) with machine-age production methods and the belief that good design could be mass-produced democratically, forming the philosophical foundation of International Style Modernism.
