## Added explanation

Borate is insufficient; copper-based preservatives are required for UC3A. UC3A does require preservative treatment in tropical climates; NSCP 2015 mandates treatment for exterior exposure. Native hardwoods are excellent for decks but must be pressure-treated or regularly finished/sealed; borate treatment is still insufficient. AWPA UC3A specifies exterior above-ground use: roof over-hangs, uncovered porches, siding. Borate is UC1 only because it leaches rapidly in wet environments. Even UC3A (not in soil contact) receives rainwater and morning dew in tropical Philippines. Copper-based pressure-treated preservative (ACQ or CA-B) is required to prevent decay and termite attack. Match preservative to use category: UC1=borate, UC3/UC4=copper-based pressure treatment. Do not mix categories; borate is never acceptable for exterior applications.
