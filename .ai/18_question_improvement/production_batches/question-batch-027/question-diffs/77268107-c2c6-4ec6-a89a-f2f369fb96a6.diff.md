## Added explanation

Clear compound does not reflect solar heat; surface temperature rises excessively (50—60°C). Tropical rainfall is unpredictable; it cannot be relied upon for curing. High humidity (70—90%) reduces but does not prevent evaporation. High temperature accelerates hydration but also increases evaporation. Without moisture retention, curing is compromised. White-pigmented compound (Type 2) reflects solar heat, preventing excessive surface temperature rise that causes thermal cracking. It requires minimal maintenance, prevents evaporation despite high humidity, and can be applied to any surface orientation. This is the most practical and effective choice for tropical Philippines. Tropical Philippines best practice: White-pigmented curing compound (Type 2), complete coverage, applied within 30 minutes of finishing. This balances effectiveness, practicality, and cost.
