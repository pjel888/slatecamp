## Added explanation

Removing the clause reduces flexibility but doesn't solve the underlying problem of vague language in the current specification. 'Equal in all respects' is still subjective without stating the specific respects being compared. Cost limits control price, not performance conformance. Subjective phrases like 'equal or better quality' create disputes because 'quality' is undefined. Objective criteria (STC rating, fire rating per ASTM, moisture resistance per ASTM D1141) are measurable and testable. Philippine architects must avoid subjective language in 'or equal' clauses. The standard of equality must be objective and verifiable through testing or documentation.
