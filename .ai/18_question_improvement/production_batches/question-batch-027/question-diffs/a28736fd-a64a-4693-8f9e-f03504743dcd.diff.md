## Added explanation

Aggregate exposure occurs when cement binder is weak; this is a typical result. Rebar corrosion follows from high permeability and water ingress. Differential curing creates local weak zones prone to stress concentration and cracking. Inadequate curing results in HIGH permeability, not low. Incomplete hydration leaves porous concrete, allowing water infiltration. High permeability is the defining consequence of inadequate curing. Inadequate curing = weak + porous + permeable + durable. Do not confuse the consequences. Poor curing compromises durability and service life.
