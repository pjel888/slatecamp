## Added explanation

Compounds are not equivalent to moist curing; 95—100% is overstated. 50—60% is too low and suggests defective application. 40—50% is too low; compounds are more effective than this. ACI 308R data shows curing compounds (when properly applied) develop 70—80% of the strength of continuous moist curing. They are a practical alternative but not equivalent. Moist curing > Compound curing. For critical applications or maximum strength, prefer water curing (ponding, spray). Compounds are acceptable when water curing is impractical, if properly applied.
