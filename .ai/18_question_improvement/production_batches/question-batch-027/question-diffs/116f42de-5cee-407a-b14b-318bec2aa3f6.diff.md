## Added explanation

Division 06 covers Wood, Plastics, and Composites (structural wood, not window frames). Division 07 covers Thermal & Moisture Protection (waterproofing, insulation, sealants). Division 09 covers Finishes (paint, flooring, ceiling systems). Division 08 is Openings and covers all window and door systems, glazing, hardware, and weatherstripping. Division 08 (Openings) is distinct from Division 09 (Finishes). This is a frequent exam error: students incorrectly place window or door specifications in Division 09.
