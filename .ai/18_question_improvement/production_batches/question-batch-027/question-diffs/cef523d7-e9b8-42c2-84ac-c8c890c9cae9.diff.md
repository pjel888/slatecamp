## Added explanation

While it increases axial capacity, the main seismic concern is shear transfer and wythe continuity. Compressive strength increase is a benefit, but the spacing and placement targets lateral stability. Grouting header cells complements but does not necessarily eliminate the requirement for metal wall ties unless specifically engineered as a bonded wall. In Seismic Zone 4, the integration of grout and reinforcement is critical. Solidly grouting the cells where headers occur ensures that the 'bridge' between the two wythes is stiff and capable of transferring seismic shear forces. [cite: 2, 44] Grout at headers ensures wythes act together during seismic sway.
