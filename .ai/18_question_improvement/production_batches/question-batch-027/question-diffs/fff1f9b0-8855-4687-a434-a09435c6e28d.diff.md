## Added explanation

Dual representation is permissible with disclosure and consent; the issue is lack of disclosure. Withdrawal from one client does not solve the other client's undisclosed conflict. Both clients must be informed; disclosing to one while hiding from the other compounds the violation. Undisclosed dual representation violates exclusivity and candor; both clients deserve to know their interests may be divided and to consent or decline. Dual representation with opposed interests is prohibited unless both clients are fully informed and consent in writing.
