## Added explanation

Brush application does not cause checking (improper drying does); this is not the structural concern. Cost is secondary to durability; code requirements mandate adequate penetration. Annual re-application is not practical for structural lumber buried in soil; deep penetration from pressure treatment is required. Decay and insects (termites, beetles) can penetrate 1/4 inch in months, reaching unprotected interior wood. Ground contact and soil contact expose the wood to constant wetness and biological attack. Only pressure treatment (achieving deep penetration of 0.5—1.5 inches or more) provides reliable long-term protection. Brush, spray, and dip treatments are surface treatments only. For ground contact and deep structural protection, pressure treatment (AWPA U1) is required.
