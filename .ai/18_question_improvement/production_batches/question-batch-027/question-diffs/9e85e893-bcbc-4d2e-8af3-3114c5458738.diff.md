## Added explanation

This is a bed joint. This is a cavity or collar joint. Bond patterns refer to the arrangement of units (e.g., Running, Stack). Cells (or cores) are the hollow voids in CHBs. Per NSCP 2015, these are often reinforced with vertical bars and filled with grout to enhance structural capacity. [cite: 23, 44] Cell = core/void in a hollow unit.
