## Added explanation

Mortar quantity does not dictate structural superiority; the arrangement of units (bond) does. Stack bond is the weakest of all patterns and is restricted in Zone 4. While Running Bond is the most common, English and Flemish bonds are legally permissible and structurally sound. English bond consists of alternating courses of all-headers and all-stretchers, resulting in a high density of transverse ties. Flemish bond alternates headers and stretchers within the same course, providing strong but less frequent bonding than the English bond. [cite: 7, 13] English Bond = maximum transverse header frequency.
