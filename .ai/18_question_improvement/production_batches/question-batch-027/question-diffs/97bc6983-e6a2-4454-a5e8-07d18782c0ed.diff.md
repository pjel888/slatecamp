## Added explanation

Juan Nakpil (1899–1986) was the first architect to receive the National Artist Award for Architecture (1973). His most recognized work is the Quiapo Church — he designed the iconic dome and second belfry in 1930. Nakpil was an Art Deco master. His other works include the Rizal Theater, Capitol Theater, UP Administration and Library Buildings, Manila Jockey Club, Philippine Village Hotel, Rizal Shrine in Calamba, and the Boy Scouts of the Philippines HQ.
