## Added explanation

English bond provides strong mechanical interlock through alternating header/stretcher courses. Flemish bond provides interlock through alternating units in each course. Running bond is the standard interlocking pattern where head joints are offset by at least 1/4 of the unit length. Stack bond has vertical head joints that align. In high seismic zones (Seismic Zone 4), the NSCP 2015 requires stack bond walls to have continuous horizontal reinforcement to compensate for the lack of overlapping units. [cite: 42, 44] Stack bond lacks overlap and requires extra reinforcement in high seismic zones.
