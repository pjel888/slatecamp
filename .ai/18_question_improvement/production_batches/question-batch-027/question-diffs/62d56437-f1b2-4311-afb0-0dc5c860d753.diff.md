## Added explanation

Misunderstands the standard; the architect's subjective belief is irrelevant—the test is objective appearance of impropriety. Actual financial benefit is not the only trigger; even the appearance of potential benefit can create a conflict. Client inquiry is not required; the architect has a duty to recognize and disclose conflicts proactively. Conflicts are determined objectively—whether a reasonable third party would view the competing interest as compromising objectivity, not by the architect's subjective belief in their own impartiality. Conflicts of interest are determined objectively; a reasonable appearance of impropriety triggers disclosure, regardless of the architect's subjective confidence.
