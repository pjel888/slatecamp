## Added explanation

Foreign architects have specific requirements; they cannot practice independently. BPO registration does not qualify foreign architects for architectural practice. International certification alone is insufficient without local permits. Foreign architects must secure a Temporary/Special Permit, a work permit from the Department of Labor and Employment, and must collaborate with a local registered and licensed architect (SPP Doc 202). Foreign architects need special permit, DOLE work permit, and local architect.
