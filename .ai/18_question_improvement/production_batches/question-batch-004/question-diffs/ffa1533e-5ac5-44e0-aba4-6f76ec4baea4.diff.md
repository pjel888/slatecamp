## Added explanation

BPO/KPO firms face restrictions on architectural service provision. Foreign collaboration does not remove the PRC registration requirement. BPO/KPO registration does not qualify firms for architectural work. Business Process Outsourcing and Knowledge Process Outsourcing firms registered in the Philippines cannot provide architectural services for Philippine projects unless they are PRC-registered architectural firms (SPP Doc 202). BPO/KPO firms need PRC architectural registration for Philippine projects.
