## Added explanation

Uses division instead of multiplication; incorrect formula. Conflates footprint with total GFA; ignores PSO%. Unrelated metric (maintenance floor); wrong formula. AMBF = Allowable Building Footprint, the portion of the lot you can occupy. Formula: AMBF = TLA × PSO%. For R-2 (PSO=70%), a 600 sqm lot yields AMBF = 600×0.70 = 420 sqm. AMBF is always PSO% of the lot. This single-floor footprint becomes the basis for calculating TGFA when multiplied by number of floors.
