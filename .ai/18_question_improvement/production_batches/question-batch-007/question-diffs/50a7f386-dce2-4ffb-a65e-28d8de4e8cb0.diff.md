## Added explanation

A barrel vault is a continuous semicircular vault, not a structural solution for dome-on-square transitions. A groin vault is the intersection of two barrel vaults; it does not address dome placement on square bases. Flying buttresses are external support structures used in later Medieval architecture, not Byzantine dome solutions.
