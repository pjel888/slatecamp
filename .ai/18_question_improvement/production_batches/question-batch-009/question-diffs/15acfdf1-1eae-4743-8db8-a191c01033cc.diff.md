## Added explanation

Veneer is anchored to structural backup (wall or frame), not to a foundation directly. Veneer is thinner than solid stone (which is 4—12 inches). Veneer is not applied to stone substrate; it is the topmost layer. Stone veneer is thin-cut stone (1—2 inches) adhered or mechanically anchored to a backup wall (brick, concrete, or foam insulation), reducing weight and cost while maintaining appearance. Veneer = thin (1—2 in.) + adhesive or mechanical anchors + backup wall. Solid = thick (4—12 in.) + heavy + direct support. Choose veneer for cost and weight reduction; solid for maximum durability.
