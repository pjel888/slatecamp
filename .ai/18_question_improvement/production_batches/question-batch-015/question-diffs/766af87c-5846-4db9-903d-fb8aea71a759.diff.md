## Added explanation

Painter comfort is not relevant to coating performance. Faster drying does not ensure proper chemical cure; conditions matter more. Paint consumption is unaffected by application conditions. ASTM D3276 conditions optimize curing kinetics. Low temperature (<10°C) slows evaporation, trapping moisture in the film. High RH (>50%) and rain introduce moisture into the forming film, causing blistering, delamination, and adhesion loss. Poor conditions (high humidity, low temperature, rain) cause future failures. Delaying work is cheaper than repainting.
