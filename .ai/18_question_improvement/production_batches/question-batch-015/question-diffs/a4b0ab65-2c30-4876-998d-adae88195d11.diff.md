## Added explanation

0 is perfect adhesion (no loss); not the minimum, the ideal. 2 indicates 15-35% loss (ASTM D3359 classification), unacceptable per typical specifications. 5 is complete failure; rejected. ASTM D3359 defines rating 3 as 5-15% adhesion loss (moderate flaking). Rating 4 (<5% loss) is superior, but many specifications accept rating 3 as the minimum for interior walls. Ratings 0, 1, and 2 indicate higher loss and are generally rejected. Always refer to project-specific requirements. Spec minimum often rating 3 (5-15% loss). Rating 4 or better is preferred.
