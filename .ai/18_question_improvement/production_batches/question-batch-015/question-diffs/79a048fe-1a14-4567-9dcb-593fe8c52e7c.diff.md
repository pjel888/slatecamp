## Added explanation

10-30µm is too thin; primer would not adequately seal substrate. 75-150µm is the topcoat range, not primer. 200-300µm exceeds both primer and topcoat requirements. Typical primer DFT is 25-50µm per manufacturer specifications and industry practice (not specified in ASTM D3359). This seals the substrate and promotes topcoat adhesion. Primer 30-50µm; topcoat 75-150µm. Measure with wet-film thickness gauge during application.
