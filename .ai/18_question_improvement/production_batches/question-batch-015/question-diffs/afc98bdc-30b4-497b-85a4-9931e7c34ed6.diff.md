## Added explanation

Falsely assumes all ASEAN architects qualify—only those meeting specific MRA requirements and registered in the AAR are ASEAN Architects. Confuses employment with credentials—institutional affiliation is irrelevant; professional certification matters. Mistakes education for status—graduation from ASEAN schools does not confer ASEAN Architect status; PRA certification and AAR registration do. An ASEAN Architect is an architect certified by their country's Professional Regulatory Authority and registered in the ASEAN Architect Register meeting all MRA requirements. ASEAN Architect status requires PRA certification from home country AND registration in the ASEAN Architect Register—not all ASEAN architects qualify.
