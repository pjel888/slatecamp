## Added explanation

Early involvement of all consultants is not standard practice; it increases cost without proportional benefit if design direction is still being established. Bidding occurs after Design Development and Construction Documents; consultants are already involved by then. They may have detailed calculations then, but they join before—during Design Development—to inform system selection and feasibility. Consultants typically join during Design Development, once the architect has established design direction. This sequential approach saves time and cost by developing systems that support (rather than constrain) the approved concept.
