## Added explanation

Unprimed latex allows moisture absorption and mold on substrate. Wallpaper without fungicide base primer is unsuitable for high humidity. Unprimed enamel offers no substrate seal or mold protection. High-humidity bathrooms require fungicide-treated primer to prevent mold and mildew on the drywall substrate. Boysen and Davies offer fungicide-rich primers designed for tropical climates. Latex topcoat over fungicide primer provides durable, mold-resistant finish. High-humidity zones = fungicide primer + paint topcoat. Never skip primer. Boysen, Davies have proven fungicide formulations.
