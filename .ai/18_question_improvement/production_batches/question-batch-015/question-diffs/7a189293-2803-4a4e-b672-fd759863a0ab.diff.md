## Added explanation

Falsely prohibits all junior staff work; junior architects may develop drawings under the licensed architect's direct supervision. Incorrectly requires licensing of junior staff; unlicensed junior architects can work under a licensed architect's supervision. Suggests written certification is required; the statute requires direct involvement, not additional certification documentation. Direct supervision is satisfied when the licensed architect actively participates in design decisions, reviews work at each development phase, provides design direction, and conducts final verification. Supervision of junior architects or staff in developing drawings under an experienced architect's active guidance is a normal and proper part of architectural practice. The key is that the licensed architect maintains continuous involvement and personal responsibility. Active involvement in design meetings, review at each phase, and detailed final review satisfy the direct supervision requirement.
