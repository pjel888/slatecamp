## Added explanation

Hardiflex costs 20-30% more than drywall; not cheaper. Fiber cement is harder to cut than drywall; requires saws. Paint adhesion is similar; the substrate durability is the advantage. Hardiflex (fiber cement) resists water, mold, and termites, unlike gypsum. HardiFlex NexGen with MoldBlock Technology is specifically engineered for moist environments. Gypsum absorbs moisture and deteriorates, requiring replacement. High-moisture zones (kitchens, bathrooms, flood-prone areas) = Hardiflex. Gypsum will fail; Hardiflex survives.
