## Added explanation

80-120 grit leaves surface too coarse and leaves dust; fails D3276. 120-180 grit is insufficient; adhesion issues arise. 320 grit or higher is excessive for drywall; 220 is standard. Drywall manufacturers (USG, National Gypsum) recommend 220-grit minimum for paint prep per industry practice. to ensure adequate surface roughness for paint adhesion and to remove contaminants. Always 220-grit minimum. Wipe dust-free after sanding. Coarse grit = poor adhesion = future delamination.
