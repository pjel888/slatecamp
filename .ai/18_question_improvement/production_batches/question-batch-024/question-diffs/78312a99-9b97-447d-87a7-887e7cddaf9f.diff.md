## Added explanation

Both material computation and parametric design are heavily algorithmic and computational; neither is manual craft. Material computation is deeply functional (mimicking bone optimization), not ornamental. Traditional parametric design is also functionally motivated. Both approaches can address comprehensive building systems; the distinction concerns the level of optimization (material density vs. formal geometry). Material computation dissolves structure/skin/ornament binaries by varying material density locally according to stress—a strategy distinct from but complementary to parametric form-finding. Both are parameter-responsive, but material computation operates at material/structural level while parametric design often operates at spatial/formal level.
