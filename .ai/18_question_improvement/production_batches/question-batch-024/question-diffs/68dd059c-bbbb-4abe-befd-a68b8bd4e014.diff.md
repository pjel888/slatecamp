## Added explanation

Locsin pursued modernist synthesis; material specificity was secondary to formal language. Mapua was first licensed Filipino architect, not primarily known for materials advocacy. Nakpil established identity through formalism and cultural consciousness, not material advocacy. Francisco Mañosa's philosophy was 'I design Filipino, nothing else.' He used indigenous materials not decoratively but structurally, in works like the Coconut Palace and Amanpulo Resort. This approach demonstrated that cultural authenticity could achieve international acclaim.
