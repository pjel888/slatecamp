## Added explanation

90-degree hooks are for non-seismic ties; they can pop open when concrete cover is lost under seismic stress. 180-degree hooks are used for anchorage of straight bars, not typically for transverse confinement hoops. 110 degrees is not a standard code angle for hooks. Per NSCP 2015 § 402.2 (Definitions), a seismic hook must have a bend of not less than 135 degrees. This ensures confinement remains intact even if the concrete cover spalls during an earthquake. Seismic confinement requires 135-degree hooks.
