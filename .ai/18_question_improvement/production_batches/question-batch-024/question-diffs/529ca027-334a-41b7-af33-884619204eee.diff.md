## Added explanation

Pablo Antonio was an important Art Deco pioneer but not first National Artist for Architecture. Locsin (National Artist 1990) represented modernist synthesis approach. Mañosa (National Artist) came later and represented materials approach. Juan Nakpil, named first National Artist for Architecture in 1973, founded the project of establishing distinctively Filipino architectural identity. His prolific output (200 buildings) demonstrated that Filipino identity could sustain an entire career.
