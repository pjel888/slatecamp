## Added explanation

Detention is temporary; it does not eliminate runoff permanently. Detention works in conjunction with downstream infrastructure, not as a replacement. Detention releases water gradually; it is not permanent storage. Detention basins reduce peak stormwater flow by temporarily storing water and releasing it at a controlled rate over time. This decreases flooding risk downstream and provides settling time for sediment removal, improving water quality.
