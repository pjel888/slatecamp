## Added explanation

Precast involves factory fabrication and on-site assembly, not in-place construction. CIP requires active curing processes, not passive delivery. Concrete placement and curing are integral to CIP. CIP construction consists of building temporary formwork, placing reinforcement per drawings, pouring concrete (by pump or bucket), vibrating to consolidate, and curing moisture/temperature-controlled on-site. All strength gain occurs in place, enabling site customization and monolithic structural behavior.
