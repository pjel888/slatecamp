## Added explanation

Over-vibration degrades quality, not improves it. Vibration duration has major impact on quality. Over-vibration reduces bond by causing paste separation. Over-vibration causes segregation: coarse aggregate settles while paste rises, creating weak upper layers and voids below. Surface blowholes (small bubble cavities) indicate excess air removal and paste migration. Under-vibration leaves voids and poor consolidation. Proper vibration (20–30 seconds per placement zone) is optimum; operator training and equipment maintenance are critical for quality.
