## Added explanation

Adobe dwellings are more general pueblo construction; cliff dwellings specifically utilize rock alcoves. Earthen mounds are Mississippian culture structures, not Puebloan. Longhouses are characteristic of Haudenosaunee and Pacific Northwest cultures. Mesa Verde (occupation dates 600–1300 CE, Colorado) showcases cliff-dwelling pueblo architecture. Ancestral Puebloans carved alcoves into canyon walls and built pueblos within—structures like Cliff Palace nestled beneath natural overhangs.' Cliff dwellings are characterized by integration within natural rock alcoves.
