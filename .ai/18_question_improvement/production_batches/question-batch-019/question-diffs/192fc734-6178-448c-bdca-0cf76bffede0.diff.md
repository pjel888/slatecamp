## Added explanation

The techniques differ in efficiency and structural mass requirements. Corbelled vaults do require vertical support from the walls they cantilever from; this is not eliminated. Wooden lintels can support considerable weight when properly engineered and positioned. Unlike Mesoamerican corbelled vaults, the Inca used wooden lintels across doorways and windows—more efficient for narrow openings and requiring less structural mass. This technique, combined with ashlar walls, created clean interior spaces with minimal support walls.' Lintels are simpler, more direct spanning elements suitable for Inca architectural goals.
