## Added explanation

Current presence does not determine native status; some plants have been introduced and naturalized. Agricultural crops may be exotic and introduced by humans, not native. Endemic species are a subset of natives—all endemics are native, but not all natives are endemic. Native species naturally occur in a region through evolutionary processes and are adapted to local conditions. They have not been introduced by humans, distinguishing them from exotic species even if exotics are now common.
