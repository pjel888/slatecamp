## Added explanation

0.5% is sealed natural stone (still requires re-sealing). 2-5% is unsealed concrete; requires sealing. 10% is highly porous; unsuitable for counters. Engineered quartz absorbs 0.05% water or less (inherently non-porous). This is the primary advantage: no sealing required. Quartz 0.05% = non-porous, no sealing needed. Natural stone sealed = 0.5%, re-sealing required. Big difference in maintenance.
