## Added explanation

3-4 is marble hardness; too soft. 5-6 is limestone/travertine; insufficient for counters. 8-9 is diamond/sapphire; harder than quartz. Engineered quartz typically has Mohs hardness of 5-6 to 7 depending on brand/formulation (often scratched by #6 pick). Provides good but not top-tier scratch resistance. 7 (quartz mineral component). This matches natural granite and provides excellent scratch resistance. Quartz hardness 7 = excellent durability. Marble hardness 3 = soft, etches easily. Memorize the scale.
