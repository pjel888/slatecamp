## Added explanation

Speed of construction is not addressed as a distinguishing feature. Architectural drawings existed before Alberti; the distinction is about his methodological emphasis on paper-based authority, not invention of drawings. While Alberti valued mathematical knowledge, architectural theory does not establish this as the primary distinction from guild training. Alberti 'challenged the medieval guild-master model and positioned the architect as an intellectual' and that 'the architect should design on paper, with drawings and plans, before construction—establishing the architect's authority over craftspeople.' This shift from craft secrecy to rational, paper-based authority fundamentally transformed the discipline's epistemology.
