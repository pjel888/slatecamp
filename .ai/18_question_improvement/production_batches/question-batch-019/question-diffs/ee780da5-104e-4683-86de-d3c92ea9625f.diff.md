## Added explanation

Full forest doesn't establish in Phase 1; forest develops progressively through multiple phases over decades. Grassland species decline as shade increases, but this is part of natural successional transition, not ecosystem collapse. Phase 1 pioneer planting (Albizia and similar nitrogen-fixing species) rapidly creates canopy shade and enriches soil through nitrogen fixation. These conditions prepare the site for Phase 2 understory establishment and eventual Phase 3 mature forest transition. Albizia and other pioneers used in restoration are typically native or non-invasive exotics chosen for ecological restoration, not invasive species.
