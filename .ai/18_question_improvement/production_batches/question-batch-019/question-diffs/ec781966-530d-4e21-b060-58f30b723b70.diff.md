Adobe is Southwestern pueblo construction, not Haudenosaunee. Corbelled vaults are Maya structures, not longhouses. Rammed earth mounds are Mississippian structures. Walls were constructed from saplings (often elm or birch bark) lashed to the frame—a technique called wattle and bark. The bark provided weather protection while remaining somewhat flexible, accommodating seasonal wood movement.'
