## Added explanation

Corian is softer, allowing repair. Quartz scratches are permanent—correct understanding. Sealing is not the factor; hardness and repairability are. Cost does not determine repairability. Corian (Mohs hardness ~2.5-3) can be lightly sanded to remove minor scratches and re-buffed to restore finish. Quartz (hardness 7) scratches are deep; sanding would remove too much material and damage the surface. This is a trade-off: quartz is more scratch-resistant overall, but when scratched, it cannot be repaired. Corian scratches more easily but is repairable. Quartz = harder, scratch-resistant, but scratches are permanent (cosmetic concern). Corian = softer, more scratch-prone, but minor scratches are repairable. Match to household use.
