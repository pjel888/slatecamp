## Added explanation

Concinnitas is not about applied ornament but about the geometric and proportional relationships among parts. While classical proportional ratios relate to concinnitas, this option oversimplifies to a single column measurement. This describes firmitas from the Vitruvian triad, not Alberti's concinnitas. Concinnitas is explicitly defined as 'a harmonious, reasoned relationship among all parts of a whole' where removing or altering any single element would destroy the beauty—rooting beauty in mathematical proportion.
