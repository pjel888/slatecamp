## Added explanation

0-1.0 is NRC (sound absorption rating), not STC (isolation). dB is a unit; STC is not expressed in dB directly (though it relates to sound reduction in dB). 0.3-0.9 is NRC range, not STC. STC is a single-number rating 50-80 (higher = better isolation). A wall with STC 50 blocks average speech; STC 70 blocks loud music. STC = sound isolation (wall assembly property); NRC = sound absorption (interior finish property). STC 50-80; NRC 0-1.0.
