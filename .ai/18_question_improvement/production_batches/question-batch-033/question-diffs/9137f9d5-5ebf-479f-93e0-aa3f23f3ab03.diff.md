## Added explanation

PEI I is wall-only; unsuitable for any floor. PEI II is light residential use (bathrooms, bedrooms); insufficient for kitchens where daily wear is heavier. PEI IV and V are commercial-grade, unnecessary for residential but acceptable if budget allows. PEI Grade III is the standard for residential kitchens, handling normal foot traffic without visible wear within the service life. Kitchen = Grade III minimum. Bathrooms and bedrooms = Grade II acceptable. Retail and offices = Grade IV or V.
