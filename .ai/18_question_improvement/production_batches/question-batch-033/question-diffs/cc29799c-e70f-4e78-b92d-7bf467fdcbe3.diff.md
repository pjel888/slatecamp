## Added explanation

3 dB is half the increase; incorrect. 12 dB is double the increase; overstates relationship. Mass increase always improves isolation; frequency response becomes less important at higher mass. Mass law: every doubling of mass increases sound isolation by approximately 6 dB. A 100 kg/m² wall at STC 40 becomes approximately STC 46 when mass is doubled to 200 kg/m². Mass law: +6 dB per doubling of mass. For high STC ratings (70+), mass alone is insufficient; damping (decoupling, absorptive fill) is required.
