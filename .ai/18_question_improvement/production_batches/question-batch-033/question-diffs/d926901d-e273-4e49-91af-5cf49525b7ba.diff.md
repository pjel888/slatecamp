## Added explanation

m²·K/W is R-value (thermal resistance), which depends on thickness. Btu·in/(hr·ft²·°F) is imperial k-value; correct units but less commonly used in metric regions. Temperature gradient is not a unit of conductivity. k-value is thermal conductivity in W/(m·K) per ASTM C518; it describes material's ability to conduct heat, independent of thickness. k = thermal conductivity (material property, independent of thickness). R = thermal resistance (depends on thickness and k).
