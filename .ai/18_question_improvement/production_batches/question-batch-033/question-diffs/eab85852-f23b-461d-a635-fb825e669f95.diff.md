## Added explanation

Villa Emo has a classical temple front with agricultural barchesses (farm wings), not four identical facades with rotunda. Palazzo Rucellai is an urban palace, not a rural villa, and lacks the rotunda/dome structure. The Tempietto is a tiny circular temple in Rome, not a villa, and is much smaller than Villa Rotonda. Villa Rotonda (begun 1567, completed 1590s, Vicenza) exemplifies Palladio's ideal villa: centered rotunda with dome, four identical facades with pediments and colonnades creating perfect symmetry—a synthesis of rural functionality with Classical temple grandeur.
