## Added explanation

A single shell would not provide the structural redundancy or allow for the scale achieved by the double-shell system. Wooden frameworks would rot and were unavailable due to earlier wars; Brunelleschi specifically avoided this Medieval approach. Concentric masonry rings lack the sophisticated rib system Brunelleschi developed to distribute loads. Brunelleschi's double-shell dome with internal ribbing (24 meridian and 10 parallel ribs) was revolutionary—it solved the problem of vaulting 42 meters without wooden centering and became the model for Renaissance dome construction.
