Window size is one factor, but layout (partitions, flow paths) is equally critical. Both Type I and Type II can achieve high or low VE depending on layout; climate does not determine efficiency. Layout directly affects VE; design choices matter greatly. : 'Ventilation efficiency (VE) = Volume of fresh air reaching breathing zone / Total ventilation rate...
