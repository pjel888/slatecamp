## Added explanation

Semi-vitreous tiles fall in the 0.5-3% range, acceptable for light-duty areas but not ground-level installations. Non-vitreous tiles absorb 3-7%, suitable only for interior walls, not floors. Unglazed earthenware and porous materials exceed 7%, unsuitable for any interior floor application. Porcelain tiles are defined by water absorption ≤0.5% per ASTM C373, making them impervious (not non-vitreous). Semi-vitreous is 3-7%; vitreous is 0.5-3%. and suitable for wet areas. Memorize: porcelain <0.5%, semi-vitreous 0.5-3%, non-vitreous >3%. For wet areas, always specify porcelain.
