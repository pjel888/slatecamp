## Added explanation

7% is 0.07 NRC, not 0.7. 30% would be NRC 0.3. NRC directly indicates absorption percentage; can be determined. NRC 0.7 = 70% sound absorption. NRC 0 = 0% absorption (hard reflective surface); NRC 1.0 = 100% absorption (theoretical maximum). NRC 0-1.0 directly scales to absorption %: NRC 0.6 = 60%, NRC 0.8 = 80%, etc. NRC >0.7 required for effective noise control in open-plan offices.
