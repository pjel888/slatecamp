## Added explanation

Carlos Barretto (Drexel University, graduated 1908) is historically significant as the FIRST pensionado architect to complete an American architecture degree. This predates Tomas Mapua (Cornell, 1911), Juan Arellano (Penn Academy/Drexel, 1911), and Antonio Toledo (Ohio State, 1911). Barretto's career: (1) Second registered architect in the Philippines (after Mapua); (2) Pioneered the Division of Architecture at the Bureau of Public Works; (3) Organized the Philippine Institute of Architects after WWII; (4) Designed Art Deco Carnival infrastructures for the 1935 Philippine Carnival.
