## Added explanation

Responsible manufactured materials often outperform 'natural' materials environmentally and ethically. While vernacular wisdom matters, contemporary buildings may require contemporary materials used responsibly. Cost-focused selection ignores environmental and ethical consequences. Material stewardship means understanding and accounting for environmental and social consequences of material choices: energy and water in production, transportation emissions, durability (longer lifespan = fewer replacements), recycled/renewable content, worker conditions, and end-of-life recyclability.
