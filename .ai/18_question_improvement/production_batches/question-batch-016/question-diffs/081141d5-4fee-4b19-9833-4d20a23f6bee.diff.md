## Added explanation

Philippines grid is not 0% coal and not 100% renewable. Coal remains dominant fuel source despite renewable energy expansion targets. Grid is not 80%+ coal. While coal is significant, gas and renewables represent 40-50% combined, providing decarbonization pathway. Philippine Department of Energy (DOE) tracks grid composition and publishes annual energy statistics. Operational carbon estimates are available and should inform building design. 2023 Philippine grid: ~40-45% coal, ~20-25% natural gas, ~20-25% renewable (hydro, wind, solar, geothermal), ~5% other. This coal-heavy mix results in higher operational carbon per kWh consumed. Operational efficiency (HVAC, envelope, renewable on-site) becomes critical decarbonization lever in Philippines. Philippine electricity grid composition (40-45% coal) results in higher operational carbon per kWh than grid-decarbonized countries. This makes embodied carbon reduction a higher priority and increases importance of on-site renewable energy and efficiency in Philippine buildings.
