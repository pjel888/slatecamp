## Added explanation

The Red Fort is a red sandstone palace complex with courtyards, not a white marble mausoleum. The Jama Masjid is a mosque with red sandstone construction, not a marble mausoleum. Humayun's Tomb is an earlier mausoleum (1565–1572) that preceded the Taj Mahal's design. The Taj Mahal is explicitly identified as a 'White marble mausoleum, double-shell dome, four minarets, semi-precious stone inlay (parchin kari—jade, lapis lazuli, carnelian, turquoise), charbagh (four-part garden).'
