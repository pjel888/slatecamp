## Added explanation

The Commission approval requirement constrains Board rulemaking. The Board prescribes rules, though subject to Commission approval. Rules require Commission approval before taking effect. The statute states: 'The policies, resolutions, rules and regulations, issued or promulgated by the Board shall be subject to review and approval of the Commission. However, the Board's decisions, resolutions or orders rendered in administrative cases shall be subject to review only if on appeal.' This dual structure balances: in rulemaking, the Commission maintains oversight; in disciplinary matters, the Board has relative autonomy with direct appeal to the Commission.
