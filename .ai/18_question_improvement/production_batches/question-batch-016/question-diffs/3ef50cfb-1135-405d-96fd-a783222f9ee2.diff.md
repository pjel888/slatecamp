## Added explanation

Pablo Antonio's FEU (Far Eastern University) Campus is his masterwork and the largest surviving ensemble of Art Deco architecture in Manila. Built between 1938 and 1950, the campus features Antonio's signature 'clean lines, plain surfaces, and bold rectangular masses' — a radical departure from Neoclassicism. The FEU Campus received a UNESCO Asia-Pacific Heritage Awards Honorable Mention in 2005. Antonio also designed multiple Manila theaters — Ideal Theater, Life Theater (1941), Scala Theater, Lyric Theater, and Forum Theater.
