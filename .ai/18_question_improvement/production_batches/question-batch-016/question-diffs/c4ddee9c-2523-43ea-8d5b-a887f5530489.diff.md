## Added explanation

Antonio Toledo was the premier Neoclassical/Beaux-Arts architect among the pensionado generation. His Manila City Hall is the Philippine government's most iconic Neoclassical building — featuring symmetrical facades, classical columns, pediments, and a monumental central tower. This reflects the American colonial preference for Neoclassicism as the appropriate architectural language for democratic government institutions. Toledo's other major works include: National Museum (Leyte Capitol), Cebu Provincial Capitol, Bureau of Customs, and Department of Tourism Building.
