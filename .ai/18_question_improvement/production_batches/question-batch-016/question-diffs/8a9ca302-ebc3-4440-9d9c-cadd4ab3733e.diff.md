## Added explanation

Missing the 1/2 factor; would over-estimate. L/3 is the prismoidal constant, not trapezoidal. Simplified approximation; less precise. The trapezoidal formula for one interval is V = (L/2) × (A₁ + A₂), where L is distance and A₁, A₂ are cross-sectional areas. Trapezoidal: V = (L/2) × (A₁ + A₂) for a single interval.
