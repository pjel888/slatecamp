## Added explanation

The 1901 McMillan Plan (authored by Burnham, Daniel Chester French, and the McMillan Commission) reorganized Washington D.C.'s National Mall — removing Victorian-era structures, restoring the L'Enfant plan, and creating the iconic view from the Capitol Building to the Washington Monument. Burnham applied the same conceptual logic to Manila: (1) Create a grand civic axis from the Rizal Monument (south) to the planned Philippine Capitol Building (north); (2) Connect the civic center to the waterfront (Manila Bay); (3) Establish a park system and radial boulevards. The key formal analogy: Washington Monument (column/obelisk) axis → Rizal Monument (column/obelisk) axis; U.S. Capitol (government building) → planned Philippine Capitol (government building).
