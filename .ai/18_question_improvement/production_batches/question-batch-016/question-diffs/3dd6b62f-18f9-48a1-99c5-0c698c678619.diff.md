## Added explanation

Burnham's 1905 Manila plan envisioned a grand civic axis: the Rizal Monument at the south end, the Philippine Capitol Building at the north end, connected by a grand boulevard. However, the Capitol Building was never constructed due to: (1) Funding constraints — the U.S. colonial government prioritized other infrastructure; (2) Policy changes toward Commonwealth status. What WAS built: Roxas Boulevard (bayshore drive), radial avenues from Luneta, the park system, waterfront promenades. The unbuilt Capitol remains the most significant unrealized element of Burnham's vision.
