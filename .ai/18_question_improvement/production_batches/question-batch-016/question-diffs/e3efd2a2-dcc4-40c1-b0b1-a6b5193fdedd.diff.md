## Added explanation

The American colonial period (1898–1946) introduced fundamentally new building technologies: (1) REINFORCED CONCRETE (terroconcrete) — concrete with embedded steel reinforcement; (2) STEEL-FRAMED SKELETON CONSTRUCTION — steel columns and beams form a structural frame; (3) KAHN TRUSS SYSTEM — trussed bars within concrete molds for floor slabs and beams; (4) CONCRETE HOLLOW BLOCKS (CHB) — standardized modular concrete blocks that became the standard wall material.
