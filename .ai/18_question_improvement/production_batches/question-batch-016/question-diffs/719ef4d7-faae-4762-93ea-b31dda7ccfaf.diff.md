## Added explanation

Juan Arellano's Manila Metropolitan Theater is the premier example of Philippine Art Deco. The Manila civic center was only partially realized under Burnham's plan: Built: Manila City Hall (Toledo, Beaux-Arts), Metropolitan Theater (Arellano, Art Deco), Manila Post Office (Arellano, Art Deco), Jones Bridge (Arellano, Art Deco). Not Built: Philippine Capitol Building (Burnham's centerpiece).
