## Added explanation

Plate compactors are better for granular soils, not clay. Sheep's foot rollers are for cohesive soils, not asphalt. Equipment characteristics determine suitability; selection by soil type is essential. Soil compaction to required density (90–98%) uses specialized equipment. Sheep's foot rollers with protruding feet are ideal for cohesive (clay) soils: feet penetrate and knead material, achieving density. Plate compactors suit granular (sandy) soils. Smooth drum rollers provide finish work on granular materials and asphalt.
