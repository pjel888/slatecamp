## Added explanation

50 mA is a higher threshold used in dry locations, not wet locations where more stringent protection is required. 75 mA exceeds the safe protection level for wet locations and would not comply with PEC requirements. 100 mA is far too high for wet location protection and violates electrical safety standards. The Philippine Electrical Code (PEC), based on NEC/NFPA 70 standards, specifies that electrical fault current protection in wet locations must not exceed 30 mA (less than 30 mA). This lower threshold in wet locations provides enhanced protection against electrical shock hazards where moisture is present, reducing the time delay for circuit disconnection in fault conditions. Wet locations (bathrooms, outdoor) require <30 mA fault current protection per PEC; this is a critical numerical standard for electrical safety.
