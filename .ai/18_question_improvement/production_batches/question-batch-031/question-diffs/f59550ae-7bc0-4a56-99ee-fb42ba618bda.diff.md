## Added explanation

Energy absorbed up to rupture is the Modulus of Toughness, not Resilience[cite: 1]. Impact resistance is related to toughness and ductility, but the formula provided is incorrect[cite: 1]. Fy is the yield stress, which is a single point on the curve; resilience is the energy (area) accumulated up to that point[cite: 1]. Resilience is the capacity of a material to absorb energy when it is deformed elastically. It is the area under the elastic portion of the stress-strain curve, calculated as UR = Fy² / (2E)[cite: 1]. Modulus of Resilience (UR) = Fy² / (2E)[cite: 1].
