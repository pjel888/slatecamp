## Added explanation

Higher strength concrete is stiffer, meaning it deflects less, not more[cite: 1]. Deflection calculation (δ = kPL³/EI) requires the Modulus of Elasticity (E), which changes with f'c[cite: 1]. Strength (f'c) is not linearly proportional to deflection; it is related via the square root in the E calculation[cite: 1]. Deflection is inversely proportional to E. Ec(25) = 4700√25 = 23,500 MPa. Ec(40) = 4700√40 ≈ 29,725 MPa. Ratio: 29,725 / 23,500 ≈ 1.265 (rounded to 1.27). Beam A (lower E) deflects more[cite: 1]. Deflection varies with the square root of concrete compressive strength[cite: 1].
