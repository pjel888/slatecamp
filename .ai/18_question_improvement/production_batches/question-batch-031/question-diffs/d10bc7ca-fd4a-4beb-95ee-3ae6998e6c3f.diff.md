## Added explanation

The volada (literally 'thrown out' in Spanish) is a defining feature of the bahay na bato's upper floor. It is a cantilevered wooden balcony that projects outward beyond the masonry wall line of the ground floor — an engineering feat using timber brackets and balaustre (turned wooden balusters). Functionally, it shades the zaguan/silung below from sun and rain (extending the climate-responsive bahay kubo logic to the colonial hybrid), and provides outdoor living/social space for the household on the upper level.
