## Added explanation

Neovernacular deliberately preserves vernacular principles; it opposes pure modernism without context. Literal reproduction is impractical; neovernacular is interpretive. Authentic neovernacular integrates function; purely decorative application is pastiche, not neovernacular. Neovernacular is defined: 'Modern design reinterpreting traditional vernacular principles (climate responsiveness, material authenticity, cultural continuity) rather than literal reproduction.' This allows contemporary programs (high-rises, hospitals, offices) to incorporate vernacular climate intelligence without romantic historicism.
