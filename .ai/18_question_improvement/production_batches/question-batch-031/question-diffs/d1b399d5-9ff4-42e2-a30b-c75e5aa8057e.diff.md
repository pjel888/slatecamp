## Added explanation

The bahay na bato is a true hybrid: (1) AUSTRONESIAN heritage: The raised upper floor on a stone base preserves the bahay kubo's fundamental climate logic — elevating the living space above flood level and allowing air circulation under the floor. The capiz-shell window panels (ventanillas) continue the bahay kubo's tradition of open, ventilating walls. (2) SPANISH contribution: Stone and masonry construction on the ground floor, formal room names (sala, comedor, cocina), and decorative elements. The genius of arquitectura mestiza is that the Spanish colonists accepted the raised-floor logic because it worked in the tropical seismic environment, while adding stone construction for durability and status.
