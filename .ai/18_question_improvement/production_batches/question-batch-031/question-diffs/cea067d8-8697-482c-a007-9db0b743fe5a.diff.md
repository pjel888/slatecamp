## Added explanation

NRC 0.65 is acceptable for retail, not offices. Full-height walls eliminate sound transmission but do not address acoustic absorption in office spaces. Partition height affects direct sound; ceiling NRC affects reflected sound. Both matter. NRC 0.65 is insufficient for office speech privacy. Sound reflects off low-absorption tiles, traveling between offices via return air path. NRC ≥0.75 (preferably 0.80+) is needed. Cost savings here create design failure and client complaints. Match NRC to space function. Office = 0.75+. Don't compromise acoustic performance for cost.
