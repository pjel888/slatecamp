## Added explanation

6 meters is half the standard; not a typical supply length. 9 meters is not standard in Philippine practice. 15 meters is longer than standard and less common. Standard bar length in Philippine practice is 12 meters, supplied by all major manufacturers (Pag‑asa, Capitol Steel, Vulcan). Always assume 12‑meter bars when calculating quantities. This is the market standard in the Philippines.
