## Added explanation

Design drawings show intent; as-builts show reality. Cost tracking uses different documentation (progress invoices, change orders). Preliminary drawings are pre-construction; as-builts are post-construction. As-builts reflect actual field conditions (substituted materials, MEP rerouting due to conflicts, rebar placement, as-installed dimensions). Accurate as-builts are critical for operations staff (HVAC maintenance, electrical troubleshooting), future renovations (understanding existing conditions), and legal disputes (damage claims, warranty issues). Discrepancies from design must be noted.
