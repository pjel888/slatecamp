Describes designing only one building; repetitive design requires multiple versions. Multiple architects is a project management structure, not a definition of repetitive design. Refers to structural repetition (columns) within a single building, not design repetition across multiple buildings. Repetitive designs involve designing a building prototype once and then reproducing that design or variations of it for multiple units or locations, such as apartment buildings with repeated units or residential subdivisions with the same house design. Repetitive design means one prototype design is created and then reproduced (with adaptations) multiple times.
