## Added explanation

Implied axes require understanding of geometric composition, not ease of design. Hiding is not the point; organization is the point. Both classical and contemporary architecture employ implied axes. Ching's point is that an axis operates as an organizing concept whether or not it is physically marked. Many compositions are deeply organized by axes the viewer feels but doesn't see marked as walls or paths. This demonstrates that organizational logic is conceptual and perceptual, not just physical.
