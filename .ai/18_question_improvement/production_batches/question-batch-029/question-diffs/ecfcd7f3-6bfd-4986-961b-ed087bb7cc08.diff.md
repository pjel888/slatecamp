## Added explanation

Acrylic is water-based and sensitive to rain during cure; it requires 24-48 dry hours. Butyl sealants are non-hardening; they do not cure. Polysulfide sealants are moisture-tolerant but require longer cure times than polyurethane. Polyurethane sealants are moisture-curing; water triggers the cure reaction, allowing application in humid conditions or during light rain. Polyurethane = moisture-curing; ideal for tropical and humid climates.
