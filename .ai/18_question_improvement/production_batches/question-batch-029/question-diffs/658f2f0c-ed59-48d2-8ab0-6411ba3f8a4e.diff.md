## Added explanation

Compatibility is poor; adhesion will fail within weeks. Primer does not solve the incompatibility; substrate preparation (removal of old sealant) is required. No chemical bond occurs; mechanical adhesion is impossible. Silicone and acrylic are chemically incompatible. Silicone has very low surface energy and does not wet acrylic. Complete removal of old sealant to bare substrate is mandatory. When changing sealant types, remove old sealant completely. Never assume chemical compatibility.
