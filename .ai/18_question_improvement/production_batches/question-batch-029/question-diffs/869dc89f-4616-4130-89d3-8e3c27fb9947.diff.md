## Added explanation

Strength of the reinforcement steel is not the governing factor; confinement is. Column height and stability relate to slenderness, not the φ factor for a given failure mode. Code decisions are based on structural mechanics, not arbitrary preferences. The continuous helical spiral reinforcement wraps the concrete core, preventing lateral expansion and concrete spalling. When axial load is applied, the spiral confines the core, allowing the column to sustain load even after the outer concrete cracks. This provides ductility and warning before collapse.
