## Added explanation

Both are relatively brittle (not ductile) compared to flexure. Code provisions are based on structural behavior, not randomness. The calculation methods differ; the φ factor reflects similar levels of predictability and ductility. Both bearing on concrete and tied column compression are relatively brittle. Bearing failure occurs suddenly when the bearing stress exceeds the local concrete strength; there is no gradual plastic deformation to warn the structure. Tied columns lack the ductility of spirally reinforced columns. Both receive φ = 0.70 because they are less forgiving. Review the correct answer and associated code provision.
