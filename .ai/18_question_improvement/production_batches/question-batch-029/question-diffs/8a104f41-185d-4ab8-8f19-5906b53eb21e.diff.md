## Added explanation

φ = 0.75 applies to spirally reinforced (not tied) columns. φ = 0.85 applies to shear and torsion. φ = 0.90 applies to flexure and tension. Tied columns have rectangular or square ties that confine the concrete core, but less effectively than spiral reinforcement. The lower φ = 0.70 reflects the reduced ductility and brittleness of this failure mode. Review the correct answer and associated code provision.
