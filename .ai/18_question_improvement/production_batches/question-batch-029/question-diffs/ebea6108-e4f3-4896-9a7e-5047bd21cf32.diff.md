## Added explanation

Ornament is not prohibited; it must be purposeful. Aesthetic concerns are integral to architecture, not opposed to structure and function. Ornate buildings can possess venustas if coherently expressed. Venustas does not require minimalism or exclude ornament. It requires intention and integrity. A richly ornamented building possesses venustas if the ornament contributes to coherent meaning and expression. A plain box has venustas only if its plainness is meaningful, not accidental.
