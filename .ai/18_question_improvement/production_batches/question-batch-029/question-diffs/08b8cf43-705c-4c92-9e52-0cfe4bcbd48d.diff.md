## Added explanation

Francisco Mañosa's FCC framework is his philosophical test for whether a building is genuinely Filipino: (1) FILIPINO VALUES — architecture must reflect Filipino cultural values: family and community (expressed through spatial openness, transitional indoor-outdoor spaces), the tradition of ornamentation, and bayanihan; (2) CLIMATE RESPONSE — the building must respond to the tropical Philippine climate through passive strategies: overhangs, cross-ventilation, elevated floors, high ceilings; (3) CRAFT / INDIGENOUS MATERIALS — the building must use Filipino indigenous materials: bamboo (kawayan), coconut wood (coco lumber), rattan, capiz shells, volcanic rock (adobe), anahaw palm (thatch), clay tiles (teja).
