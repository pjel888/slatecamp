## Added explanation

The Third Wave of Filipino architecture (2000s–present) includes firms like BUDJI+ROYAL, WTA Architecture + Design, and Mañosa & Company (continuing under Gelo Mañosa). BUDJI+ROYAL is notable for: (1) Integrating BERDE (Philippine Green Building Rating System) and LEED (international) certification requirements with Filipino spatial traditions; (2) Using bamboo engineering — engineered bamboo as structural elements, not just decorative; (3) Earth-mass design — using the thermal mass of earth/adobe/stone to regulate interior temperature; (4) Computational tools — parametric design and digital fabrication alongside traditional craft.
