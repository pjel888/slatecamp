## Added explanation

Vernacular architecture embodies principles (often intuitively) even without explicit theoretical training. While Spanish colonizers may have been influenced by Vitruvius, the bahay na bato is indigenous synthesis, not direct application. Vernacular architecture does not sacrifice durability; stone base and proper timber joinery ensure firmitas. Bahay na bato integrates all three principles: structurally sound stone and timber (firmitas), climate-responsive spatial organization (utilitas), and proportionate graceful form (venustas). It exemplifies Vitruvian integration whether or not Vitruvian theory directly influenced its development.
