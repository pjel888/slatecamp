## Added explanation

Locsin's most philosophically significant concept — the 'floating volume' — is a Modernist reinterpretation of the bahay kubo's raised floor. The bahay kubo elevates its living space above flood level, creating a ventilated silong (open space under the floor). Locsin takes this spatial concept — a raised volume over a void — and executes it in Brutalist concrete. The Tanghalang Pambansa appears to hover above its reflecting pool. Critically, this is an ABSTRACT reinterpretation, not a material one: Locsin uses raw concrete (béton brut), not bamboo and nipa. This places him in the First Wave (Modernist/Brutalist) rather than the Second Wave (Neo-Vernacular) — Locsin expresses Filipino identity through spatial concepts, Mañosa through materials.
