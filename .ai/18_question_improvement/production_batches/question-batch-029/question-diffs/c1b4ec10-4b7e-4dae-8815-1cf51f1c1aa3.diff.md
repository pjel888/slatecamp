## Added explanation

They are conceptually related. Both are design strategies affecting spatial and functional organization. Asymmetrical compositions employ axes without symmetry. Ching distinguishes these clearly: an axis is the ORGANIZING LINE; symmetry is an ARRANGEMENT about or around that line. Many asymmetrical compositions employ axes without requiring symmetrical placement. Axes come first (conceptually); symmetry may or may not follow.
