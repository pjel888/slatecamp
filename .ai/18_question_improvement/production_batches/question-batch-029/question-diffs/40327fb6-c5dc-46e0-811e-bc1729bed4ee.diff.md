## Added explanation

Pressure-sensitive adhesives are for temporary bonding (tapes, labels). Polyurethane adhesives are more flexible; preferred for wood-to-wood applications. Silicone adhesives are flexible and not suitable for structural concrete bonding. Epoxy adhesives are two-part, high-strength products specifically designed for rigid bonding of concrete and metal. They develop full strength after 24-48 hours. Epoxy = structural bonding (concrete, metal); Polyurethane = wood-to-wood or movement-accommodating applications.
