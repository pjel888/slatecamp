## Added explanation

Anchorage zones are not purely brittle; they benefit from stress redistribution in surrounding concrete. Material strength has no bearing on the φ factor; ductility and predictability do. The match to shear φ is coincidental; each φ is assigned based on failure mode characteristics. Anchorage zones involve stress transfer that is partly ductile (concrete around the anchor deforms and redistributes stress) and partly prone to local bearing failure. The intermediate φ = 0.85 reflects this hybrid nature—less conservative than pure bearing (0.70), more conservative than pure flexure (0.90). Review the correct answer and associated code provision.
