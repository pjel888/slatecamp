## Added explanation

Secondary and tertiary axes are functional organizing devices, not decoration. The axis hierarchy doesn't change with viewing direction; it's inherent to the composition. Axes have hierarchical relationships, not equal importance. Ching establishes hierarchy: the primary axis dominates and establishes principal movement/hierarchy; secondary axes cross or perpendicular primary axes (transepts in cathedrals); tertiary axes subdivide zones further.
