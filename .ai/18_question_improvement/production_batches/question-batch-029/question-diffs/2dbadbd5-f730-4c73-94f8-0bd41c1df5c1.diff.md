## Added explanation

The EDSA Shrine (1989) is one of Mañosa's most significant ecclesiastical works. It embodies his Neo-Vernacular FCC philosophy: (1) FILIPINO VALUES — the shrine commemorates the 1986 People Power Revolution, expressing national identity and spiritual values; (2) CLIMATE RESPONSE — high ceilings, large windows, low eaves, and natural ventilation create a naturally comfortable worship space; (3) CRAFT / INDIGENOUS MATERIALS — bamboo and coconut wood structural and decorative elements, capiz shell panels. The EDSA Shrine is often compared to the Church of the Holy Sacrifice (UP Diliman, by Locsin) — both are iconic Philippine churches, but different architects and styles: Locsin's Brutalist Modernism vs Mañosa's Neo-Vernacular.
