## Added explanation

The Coconut Palace (Tahanang Pilipino), completed in 1980 in the CCP Complex, is Mañosa's most iconic Neo-Vernacular building. It is clad in coconut lumber (coco lumber) throughout — posts, beams, floors, and panels — alongside other indigenous materials. The building embodies Mañosa's FCC framework: Filipino values (ornate Philippine cultural motifs), Climate response (high ceilings, large windows, low eaves, free indoor-outdoor interchange), Craft/indigenous materials (coconut wood throughout). Critically, the Coconut Palace must not be confused with the Tanghalang Pambansa — same CCP Complex, different architects: Mañosa (Coconut Palace, Neo-Vernatural) vs Locsin (Tanghalang Pambansa, Brutalist Modernism).
