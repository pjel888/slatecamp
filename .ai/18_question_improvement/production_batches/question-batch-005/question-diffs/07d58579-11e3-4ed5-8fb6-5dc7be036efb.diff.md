## Added explanation

Bullet cameras are cylindrical and typically mounted on external walls for perimeter surveillance, not for discrete indoor use. PTZ cameras are used for dynamic coverage of critical zones but do not provide aesthetic integration or directional concealment. Panoramic fisheye cameras provide wide coverage but are not typically chosen for discrete indoor surveillance with directional ambiguity. Dome cameras are mounted flush against ceilings or walls and offer aesthetic integration with interior design. The dome housing protects optics and provides operational discretion because observers cannot easily determine the viewing direction. Dome cameras are the preferred choice for indoor environments requiring both discrete operation and aesthetic integration.
