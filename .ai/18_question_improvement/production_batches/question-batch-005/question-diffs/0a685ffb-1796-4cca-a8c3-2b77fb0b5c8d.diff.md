Gothic Revival buildings used historical ornament and stylistic details; iron was reinforcement, not the visible structural system. While the Crystal Palace pioneered iron use, it included glass infill and was an enclosed building; the Eiffel Tower is more radically structural. Victorian townhouses emphasize ornament and masonry mass, contrary to the described aesthetic.
