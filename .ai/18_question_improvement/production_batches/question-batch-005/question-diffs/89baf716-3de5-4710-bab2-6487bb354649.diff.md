## Added explanation

While true that Crystal Palace used iron, Gothic Revival also incorporated iron structurally; the difference is not material but ideology. Temporariness is not the defining contrast; many temporary structures are ornamental, many permanent ones are austere. Size and structural support don't define the contrast between these movements. The fundamental contrast is philosophical: Crystal Palace represented industrial rationalism (modular, prefabricated, material-honest), while Gothic Revival represented the opposite (ornamental, stylistic, historically derived).
