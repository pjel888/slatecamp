## Added explanation

While modular construction may have shifted labor patterns, this wasn't the primary architectural advantage. Modular buildings still require careful planning and drawings; modularity doesn't eliminate these needs. The permanence of a building doesn't determine its beauty; this is not the core advantage of modularity. Modular construction demonstrated that complex, large-scale architecture could be achieved through standardized, repetitive components. This principle became foundational to modernism and democratic design—quality buildings could be accessible, not unique luxury.
