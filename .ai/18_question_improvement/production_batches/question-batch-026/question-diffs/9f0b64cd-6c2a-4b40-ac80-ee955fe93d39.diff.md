## Added explanation

Tension in masonry is primarily handled by reinforcement, not the orientation of units. Header courses do not significantly reduce mortar consumption. Bed joints are always required in standard masonry regardless of the unit orientation. Headers are units laid perpendicular to the wall plane. Their primary job is to provide a mechanical tie between parallel wythes so the wall resists loads as a single structural unit. [cite: 7, 20] Headers = Wythe bonding/composite action.
