## Added explanation

EMT requires corrosion protection (e.g., PVC coating) for wet locations per PEC. TW is lower-rated (60°C); while acceptable, it's unnecessarily conservative for the application. THHN is single-conductor and requires separate conduit anyway; temperature rating doesn't prevent water ingress. For wet basements, THWN wire's 75°C wet rating and PVC conduit's corrosion resistance combine to provide safe, durable performance in Philippine tropical humidity. Both are rated for wet locations per PEC. Wet locations require combination of wet-rated wire insulation and corrosion-resistant conduit (PVC or IMC).
