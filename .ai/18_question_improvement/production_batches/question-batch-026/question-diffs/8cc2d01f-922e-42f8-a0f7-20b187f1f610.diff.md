## Added explanation

Molave ('Queen of Philippine Woods') is denser and harder, better suited to heavy structural applications; its hardness makes fine joinery more difficult. Yakal is very stiff and strong but less decorative; reserved for heavy-duty structural use, not fine cabinetry. Tanguile is used in paneling and general carpentry, but narra is the traditional prestige choice for frames. Narra (Pterocarpus indicus) is the traditional choice for fine joinery and cabinetry in the Philippines. Its interlocked/wavy grain, medium to coarse texture, and good natural luster allow clean mortise-and-tenon joints. It glues and finishes well, making it ideal for visible frames. Match wood species to application: narra for fine joinery, molave for heavy structural work, yakal for high-stress environments.
