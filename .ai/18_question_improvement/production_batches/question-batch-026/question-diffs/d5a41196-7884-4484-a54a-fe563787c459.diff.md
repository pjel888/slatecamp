## Added explanation

NM is restricted to dry residential locations; completely unsuitable for hospitals' critical infrastructure. AC cable lacks the fire-rating properties needed; mechanical protection ≠ fire resistance. While MC provides grounding and some protection, it lacks MI's fire-rated insulation (only 60–90°C, not 250°C). MI cable's mineral insulation (MgO) and copper/steel sheath provide unmatched fire resistance and temperature ratings (250°C dry, 150°C wet). MI cable is suitable for hospital life-safety systems requiring circuit integrity during fire per PEC. PEC recognizes MI as the premium choice for critical circuits. Life-safety and fire-rated circuits require MI cable's mineral insulation, not standard copper insulation.
