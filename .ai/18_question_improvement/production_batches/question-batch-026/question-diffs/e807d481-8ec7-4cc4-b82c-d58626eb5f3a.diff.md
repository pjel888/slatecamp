## Added explanation

The connection has adequate initial capacity; the issue is durability and long-term strength loss. Galvanized coatings, while durable, are not indefinite; salt spray and humidity degrade them over decades. 3200 lbf is below the 3500 lbf rating; the connection is structurally adequate initially. While hot-dipped galvanized fasteners (ASTM A 153 Class D) provide excellent corrosion resistance, even galvanized coatings eventually corrode in high-humidity tropical environments with salt spray. Stainless steel (316 or 316L) offers superior long-term protection and maintains bearing strength longer. NSCP 2015 and AWPA standards recommend stainless for coastal and high-humidity locations. In tropical and coastal Philippine climates, specify stainless steel fasteners for connections that must maintain strength over 50+ years. Galvanized is adequate for interior and moderate-exposure applications.
