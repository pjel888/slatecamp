## Added explanation

Mortise-and-tenon joints are strong in tension but primarily resist racking; they do not have interlocking teeth. Lap joints overlap by reducing thickness but lack the interlocking geometry of dovetails. Butt joints rely entirely on fasteners and have no geometric lock. Dovetail joints feature interlocking tails and pins with angled geometry that creates a mechanical lock, distributing shear stress over large glue lines and resisting both lateral and withdrawal forces. Recognize dovetail geometry as the only joint type with interlocking tooth features that mechanically prevent both lateral and withdrawal motion.
