## Added explanation

Option A applies the same factor regardless of building height — this is the most common error. The distinction between single-story and multi-story sprinkler bonuses reflects fire physics: in a single-story building, sprinklers control the fire in the horizontal plane without the complication of vertical smoke and flame travel through multiple floors. The 1.00-point difference between Is = 2.00 and Is = 3.00 is significant in area calculations.
