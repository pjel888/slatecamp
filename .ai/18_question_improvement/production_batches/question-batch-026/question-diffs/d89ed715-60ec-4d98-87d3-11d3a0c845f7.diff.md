## Added explanation

Two 2.5 inch shear plates (roughly 3000—4000 lbf each) provide approximately 6000—8000 lbf total capacity, exceeding 4500 lbf. Wood-to-steel connections via shear plates are a standard design method. Philippine hardwoods (narra, molave, yakal) accept shear plates well; the issue is fastener corrosion, not wood compatibility. The Philippines' high humidity and salt air near coastal regions accelerate corrosion. Unprotected fasteners rust and lose strength; NSCP 2015 and AWPA standards mandate stainless steel (preferred) or ASTM A 153 Class D hot-dipped galvanized fasteners. Environmental context (tropical climate, humidity) drives material selection; failure to specify corrosion-resistant fasteners leads to premature loss of capacity.
