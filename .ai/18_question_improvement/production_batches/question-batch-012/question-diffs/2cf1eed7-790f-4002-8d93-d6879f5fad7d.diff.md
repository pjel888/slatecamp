## Added explanation

<100 g/L is ultra-low VOC; rare and cost-prohibitive. <200 g/L is achievable but not standard; 300 is the recognized threshold. <500 g/L is for oil enamels and high-VOC products. <250 g/L per EPA federal limit for flat architectural coatings (40 CFR Part 59). Low-VOC typically <50 g/L per industry/Green Seal. This balances indoor air quality with paint performance. Interior latex = <300 g/L. Oil enamel = 300-650 g/L (high VOC). Know the difference for occupied vs. exterior applications.
