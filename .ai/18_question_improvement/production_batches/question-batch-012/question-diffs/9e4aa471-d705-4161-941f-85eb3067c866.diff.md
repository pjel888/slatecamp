## Added explanation

This is the rough-sawn/nominal size, not the actual finished size. This reduction is insufficient; 0.5 inches is the standard deduction for these sizes. Dimensions decrease during milling and drying, they do not increase. In the Philippines, standard S4S (surfaced 4 sides) lumber reduces nominal dimensions by 0.5 inches for 2-6 inch members. A 2x4 is actually 1.5 x 3.5 inches. Memorize: 1" nominal becomes 0.75"; 2"-6" nominal reduces by 0.5"; 8"+ nominal reduces by 0.75".
