## Added explanation

Sec. 1401 explicitly limits each Manila envelope to a maximum of 12 film negatives. This compartmentalizes the fuel load so that a single envelope fire cannot grow into a larger mass fire. The prohibition on expanding (accordion-style) envelopes is absolute — they can be overfilled beyond 12 films without visible indication.
