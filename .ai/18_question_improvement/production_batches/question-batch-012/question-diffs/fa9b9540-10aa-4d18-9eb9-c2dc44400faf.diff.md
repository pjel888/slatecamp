## Added explanation

The 450 kg aggregate threshold is the critical vault trigger in Sec. 1401. A facility may use multiple cabinets, each within the 110 kg individual limit, up to but not exceeding 450 kg total without requiring vault construction. At 450 kg and above, the collective fire load exceeds cabinet containment capacity, requiring vault-standard construction with 4-hour fire-resistive doors.
