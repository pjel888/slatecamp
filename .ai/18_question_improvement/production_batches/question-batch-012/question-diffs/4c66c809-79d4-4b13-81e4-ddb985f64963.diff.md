## Added explanation

The San Agustin Church in Paoay, Ilocos Norte (UNESCO World Heritage, 1993) is the quintessential Earthquake Baroque structure. Its 14 massive buttresses are aligned linearly along the church's exterior walls. Unlike decorative European buttresses, these are load-bearing seismic elements: their angled orientation converts lateral earthquake forces into compressive forces directed into the ground, preventing the thick walls from toppling. The Paoay Church also has a coral-stone bell tower detached from the main structure — another seismic isolation measure.
