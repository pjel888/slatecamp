## Added explanation

Sec. 1401 is explicit: when total film storage exceeds 450 kg, a vented storage vault is required, and vault door openings must be of 4-hour fire-resistive construction. The vault must be vented (to prevent buildup of decomposition gases that precede ignition) and vault doors must be kept closed except when in use — a propped-open 4-hour door provides zero protection. This requirement applies at any mass above 450 kg aggregate.
