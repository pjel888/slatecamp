Scale is not the defining feature of patriarchal spatial organization. Male architects can design non-patriarchal spaces; female architects can replicate patriarchal arrangements. Patriarchal space is structural-organizational, not decorative. The Key Terms define 'Patriarchal Space' as 'Spatial organization enforcing patriarchal gender roles; domestic separation, surveillance, restriction of women's mobility and autonomy.'
