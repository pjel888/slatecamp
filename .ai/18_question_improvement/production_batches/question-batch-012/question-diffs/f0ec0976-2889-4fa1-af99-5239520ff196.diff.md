## Added explanation

The Great Mosque of Damascus (705–715 CE) is hypostyle, not centrally octagonal, and completed after the Dome of the Rock. The Aqsa Mosque (708–714 CE) is hypostyle with double arcade, not centrally planned. Qasr al-Hayr al-Sharqi (~729 CE) is a desert palace, not a Jerusalem monument. The Dome of the Rock (691 CE) is explicitly identified as the first major Islamic monument with a 'central domed octagon' and demonstrates Byzantine influence.
