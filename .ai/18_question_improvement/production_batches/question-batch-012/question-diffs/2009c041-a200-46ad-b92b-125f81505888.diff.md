## Added explanation

LEED (USA, 1998) is global but reflects Northern Hemisphere bias; large windows for daylighting credit can increase solar gain in tropical climates. Passive House Standard (Germany, 1988) is heating-focused; it was not developed for tropical climates. BREEAM (UK, 1990) has regional variants but originated in temperate British climate. BERDE (Philippines, 2010) explicitly adapts sustainability criteria for tropical climate, monsoon hydrology, typhoon resilience, and vernacular architectural principles. BERDE 'recognizes that Northern Hemisphere-optimized standards...can be thermally dysfunctional in Manila's equatorial climate.'
