## Added explanation

The Laws of the Indies specified that remaining lots were distributed by lottery to settlers, with lots closer to the plaza assigned first. Elite residences (bahay na bato) were on lots closest to the plaza, expressing the social hierarchy spatially — proximity to the plaza meant proximity to political and religious power. Common residences progressively farther from the plaza housed ordinary townspeople on blocks radiating outward.
