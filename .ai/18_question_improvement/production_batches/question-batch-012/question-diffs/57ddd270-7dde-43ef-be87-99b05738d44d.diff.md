## Added explanation

80-grit is too coarse; leaves scratches. No primer allows tannins to bleed, causing yellowing/browning through stain. As-delivered wood has surface contaminants; requires sanding. Sand to 120-150 grit to open grain and remove contaminants. Apply stain-blocking primer (shellac or latex) to prevent tannins (natural wood color-bleeders) from migrating through stain, causing discoloration. Then apply stain. Proper sequence prevents color failure. Wood stain sequence: sand → primer (stain-blocker) → stain. Skipping primer = tannin bleed and color failure.
