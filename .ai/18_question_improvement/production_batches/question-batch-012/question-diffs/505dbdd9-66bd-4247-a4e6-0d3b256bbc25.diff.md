## Added explanation

30 minutes is too fast; paint is not fully cured. 1-2 hours is possible in ideal conditions but 2-4 hours is safer standard. 16-24 hours is oil-based enamel recoat time. Latex paint recoat time is 2-4 hours at standard conditions (23°C, <50% RH). This is one of latex's advantages over oil enamel. Latex = fast (2-4 hrs). Oil enamel = slow (16-24 hrs). Budget schedule accordingly. High humidity/temperature extends times.
