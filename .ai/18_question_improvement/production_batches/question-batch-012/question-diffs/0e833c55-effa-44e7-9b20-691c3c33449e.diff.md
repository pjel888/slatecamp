## Added explanation

Under the DENSE mnemonic (Dental, Establishments manufacturing, Neat original packages, Safety film, Enclosures/motion picture booths), amateur roll film in original packages below 1.40 cu.m, dental X-ray film, and safety film (identified by edge marking) are all exempt. Medical X-ray film negatives stored on open shelving at 2.50 cu.m — well above the 1.40 cu.m open shelving threshold and not in original packaging — are fully subject to Sec. 1401 requirements.
