## Added explanation

Moisture content determines if wood is S-Dry or Green, but it is not the basis for Group I-IV classification. Finish is a quality of milling, not botanical grouping. Softwoods like Pine are Group IV; Group I is reserved for high-density hardwoods. Philippine timber is grouped by the Forest Products Research and Development Institute (FPRDI) based on specific gravity. Group I (High Strength) includes Yakal, Molave, and Gijo. Group I = Hardest/Strongest (>0.70 specific gravity). Group IV = Lightest/Weakest (<0.40 specific gravity).
