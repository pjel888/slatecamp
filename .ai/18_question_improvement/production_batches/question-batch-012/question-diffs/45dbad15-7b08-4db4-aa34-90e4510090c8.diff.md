## Added explanation

Ordinance 126 of the Laws of the Indies explicitly states 'In the plaza, no lots shall be assigned to private individuals.' This provision physically enforced the plaza as a civic rather than commercial/private space. In practice, however, elite residences (bahay na bato) were assigned on lots closest to the plaza — the proximity to power principle. The church was also sometimes built directly on the plaza in Philippine practice despite the ordinance calling for a slight separation with its own forecourt.
