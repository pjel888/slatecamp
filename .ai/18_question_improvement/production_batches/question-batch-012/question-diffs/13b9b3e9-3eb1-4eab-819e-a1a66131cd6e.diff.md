## Added explanation

Oil-based primer has higher VOC; acceptable but not standard for drywall. Epoxy is for metal in corrosive environments. Intumescent is for structural fire protection. Latex or universal primer is standard for drywall. It seals the porous substrate and promotes topcoat adhesion without the VOC and recoat-time issues of oil primers. Drywall = latex/universal primer. Metal = epoxy or rust-inhibiting. Fire protection = intumescent. Match primer to substrate.
