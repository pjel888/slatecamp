De Stijl architecture uses orthogonal geometry and primary colors, not spiraling asymmetrical forms. Mendelsohn's Expressionism pursued curved, wave-like forms evocative of emotional response, not technological apparatus or revolutionary intent. Melnikov was a Constructivist architect, but his work (like Melnikov House) differed from Tatlin's; the spiral tower specifically belongs to Tatlin.
