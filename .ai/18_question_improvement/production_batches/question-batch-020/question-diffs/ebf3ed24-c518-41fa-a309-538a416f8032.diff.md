## Added explanation

Round arches do not extend beyond semicircles; the horseshoe arch's profile is distinctly different. The horseshoe arch is identified as characteristic of Visigothic pre-Islamic Spain, not primarily Byzantine. While decorative, the horseshoe arch serves structural functions in the Córdoba mosque's hypostyle system. The article defines the horseshoe arch as extending 'beyond a full semicircle, creating a keyhole profile' that 'allows visual emphasis and structural expansion without height increase' and 'becomes defining feature of Andalusian style.'
