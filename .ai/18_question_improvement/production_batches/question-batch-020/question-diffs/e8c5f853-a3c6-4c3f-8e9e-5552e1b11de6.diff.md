Incorrect calculation; may result from using 110V (non-Philippine) or incorrect formula. Results from I = 8,800 / 220 = 40 A (ignoring power factor correction). Standard entrance size above 42 A, but not the calculated current. Using single-phase formula: I = P / (V × PF) = 8,800 / (220 × 0.95) = 8,800 / 209 = 42.1 A ≈ 42 A. Philippine standard is 220V single-phase (60 Hz), NOT 110V as in some other regions. Service must be rated at least 42 A; standard entrance sizes are 50 A, 60 A, 100 A per PEC 230.79. Philippine context: Always use 220V for single-phase residential, not 110V. Power factor must be included in current calculations.
