## Added explanation

De Stijl uses orthogonal geometry and primary colors; the Einstein Tower's curves contradict De Stijl's aesthetic. Tatlin designed the Tower (a different building), not the Einstein Tower; both are early modernist but different movements. Brutalism emerged in the 1950s–70s, decades after Mendelsohn's 1920–1921 Einstein Tower. The Einstein Tower exemplifies Expressionism through its flowing, wave-like forms designed to evoke cosmic dynamism befitting its purpose as an astrophysics laboratory.
