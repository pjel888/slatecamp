## Added explanation

200 lux is too high for ancient textiles; will cause fading. Humidity range is too broad; causes dimensional instability. Lighting and temperature are both too high for long-term conservation. Textiles are 'highly sensitive' to light. NCCA standards limit them to 50 lux and strictly UV-free. Humidity must be stable (45-55%) as fluctuations cause fiber stress. Sensitive textiles: 50 lux max, 0 UV, 50% RH ±5%.
