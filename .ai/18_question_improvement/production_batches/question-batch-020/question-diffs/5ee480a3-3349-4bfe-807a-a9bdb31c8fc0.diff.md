## Added explanation

Sum of all motors without applying 125% multiplier to largest. Applies 125% to all motors; incorrect application of PEC 430.24. Calculates only largest motor × 1.25 + 1.0; misses other motor sum. PEC 430.24: Motor Demand = 1.25 × (Largest Motor) + 1.0 × (All Others). Largest = 10 kW. Others = 7.5 + 5 + 3.75 = 16.25 kW. Correct answer is d) 28.75 kW per PEC 430.24. The 125% multiplier accounts for starting inrush and ensures feeder capacity for simultaneous starting scenarios (though unlikely in practice). PEC 430.24 requires feeder sizing at 125% of largest motor PLUS 100% of all others—not 125% of total. This accounts for inrush current risk.
