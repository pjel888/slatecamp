## Added explanation

Transparent glazing achieves only 5–10% efficiency, limiting power density to ~10–20 W/m² despite dual shading function. Solar roof tiles are medium-efficiency products; total power density depends on tile size and spacing, typically lower than vertical facade systems. Thin-film amorphous modules (7–10%) are less efficient than crystalline BIPV options and unsuitable for facade applications. Facade-mounted BIPV systems deliver 100–250 W/m² on vertical surfaces, outperforming transparent glazing (5–10% efficiency yields ~10–20 W/m²) and thin-film modules (7–10% efficiency). Solar roof tiles (50–150 W/tile) are suitable for sloped roofs but less efficient per m² than facade systems. BIPV facade-mounted systems offer superior power density (100–250 W/m²) for vertical building surfaces compared to transparent glazing or thin-film alternatives, making them optimal for commercial building integration.
