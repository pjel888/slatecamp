## Added explanation

2,000 kg/m³ is close but is used only in fresh (unhardened) concrete estimates, not for cured concrete. 2,200 kg/m³ is below the standard; may confuse with lightweight concrete. 2,600 kg/m³ is for heavyweight concrete, not normal-weight concrete. ACI 211.1 and NSCP 2015 specify 2,400 kg/m³ as the unit weight for normal-weight concrete used in structural applications. Standard unit weight = 2,400 kg/m³. Use this to convert volume (m³) to weight (kg) for transportation and cost calculations.
