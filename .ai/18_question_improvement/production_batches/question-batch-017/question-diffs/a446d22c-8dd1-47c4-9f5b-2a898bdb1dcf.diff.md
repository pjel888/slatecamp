Hector Guimard designed the Paris Métro entrances, another important Art Nouveau work, but not the Tassel House. Antoni Gaudí designed the Sagrada Família, which pushed organic design but is more complex in its geometric-organic synthesis. William Van Alen designed the Chrysler Building, an Art Deco (not Art Nouveau) masterpiece. Victor Horta designed the Tassel House, where iron railings, stained glass, and wallpaper all speak the same curvilinear Art Nouveau language, demonstrating total design integration.
