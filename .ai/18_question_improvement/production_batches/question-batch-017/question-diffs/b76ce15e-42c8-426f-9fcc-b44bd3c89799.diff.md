## Added explanation

They didn't emerge in the same year; Art Nouveau peaked before Art Deco emerged. Art Deco uses sharp geometric curves and angles, not the organic S-curves of the whiplash line. The whiplash line is specific to Art Nouveau and doesn't appear in Art Deco (which uses geometric chevrons instead). This explains the common confusion: both movements rejected historicism and were early 20th-century, making them seem related. However, their design vocabularies were opposite.
