## Added explanation

Art Deco often used expensive materials (chrome, marble, exotic woods) and was not necessarily cheaper. Art Deco didn't reduce labor requirements; it required highly skilled craftsmanship. Sustainability wasn't a factor in early 20th-century design philosophy or client motivations. Art Deco's geometric forms, luxury materials, and machine-age aesthetic conveyed prosperity and forward-thinking—exactly what corporate clients wanted to communicate about their businesses and brands.
