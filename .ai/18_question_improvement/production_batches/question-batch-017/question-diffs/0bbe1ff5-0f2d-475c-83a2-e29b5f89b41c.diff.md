## Added explanation

Beams are installed after columns and bracing, not first. Fireproofing is installed after frame is complete and connections are verified. Decking cannot be installed before columns and beams support it. Correct sequence: (1) Erect columns from lowest level upward, (2) Install temporary bracing (diagonals, knee braces) to stabilize frame against wind/seismic, (3) Install floor beams and make connections (bolted or welded), (4) Install deck and concrete. Temporary bracing is critical to prevent instability and collapse during erection.
