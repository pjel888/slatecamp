## Added explanation

90 kg/m³ results from multiplying 180 × 0.50 instead of dividing. 180 kg/m³ incorrectly assumes cement content equals water content. 320 kg/m³ results from using W/C = 0.55 (for 20 MPa) instead of 0.50. Cement content = Water content ÷ W/C = 180 ÷ 0.50 = 360 kg/m³. The water-cement ratio is W/C, so Cement = Water ÷ (W/C). For higher strength (lower W/C), cement content increases.
