## Added explanation

Two MRFs add redundancy but do not reduce member sizes. Two braced systems are stiff but brittle; ductility is sacrificed. Precast elements do not enhance lateral system; this is tangential. Dual systems combine MRF (ductile, absorbs seismic energy elastically) with bracing (stiff, reduces lateral drift). MRF alone requires large members for moment capacity; bracing alone is brittle if poorly designed. Dual optimizes both seismic ductility (essential in Philippines per NSCP 2015) and stiffness, reducing member sizes and cost.
