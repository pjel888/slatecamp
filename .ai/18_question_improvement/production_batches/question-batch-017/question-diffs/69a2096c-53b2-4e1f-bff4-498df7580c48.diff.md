## Added explanation

Braced frames use axial force in diagonals, not bending. Floor decking carries gravity loads; lateral forces are in frame. MRF is not economical due to complex connections; braced frames are cheaper. MRF transfers lateral forces through bending moment at rigid beam-column connections. Advantages: architectural flexibility (open floor plans, no interior bracing). Disadvantages: require larger members for moment capacity, complex connections (welded or bolted moment plates), higher cost. Essential for ductility in seismic regions (Philippines).
