## Added explanation

Terracotta and painted plaster are not pietra dura; they are different decorative techniques. Carved sandstone blocks without mortar describe stupa or temple construction, not Mughal inlay work. Glazed ceramic tiles are characteristic of Chinese imperial architecture, not Mughal pietra dura. Pietra dura is explicitly defined in the article as an inlay technique using colored stone (marble, jade, lapis lazuli) to create floral and calligraphic patterns, signature Mughal decoration.
