## Added explanation

Direction is determined by fixture placement and angle. Color temperature of light, not saturation of finishes, affects mood. Intensity (brightness) is separate from color temperature. Color temperature fundamentally affects mood: warm light (2700K) feels intimate and relaxing; cool light (5000K) feels alert and clinical. Contemporary design uses warmer light in evenings, cooler light daytime to support circadian rhythms.
