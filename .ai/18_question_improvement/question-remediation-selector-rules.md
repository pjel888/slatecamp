# Question Remediation Selector Rules

Strict v2 excludes existing meaningful explanations, professional-practice/legal/code/dimensional prompts without explicit source support, repeated stems, duplicate/near-duplicate options, option-length giveaway risk, generic/meta distractors, missing answer keys, weak rationales, and insufficient linked-article support.

Expanded generic/meta leakage blocks phrases such as: which is not accurate, not accurate, misconception, ensuring all aspects, focusing on, formal directive, trap, outdated, different concept, ALE, exam trick, all of the above, and none of the above.
