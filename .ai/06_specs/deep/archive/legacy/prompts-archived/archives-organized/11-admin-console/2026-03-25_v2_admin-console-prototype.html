What is the minimum clear door opening width for an accessible entrance per BP 344?
A 800 mm
B 900 mm ✓
C 1,000 mm
D 1,200 mm
Why B is correct

BP 344 Section C.2.1 requires a minimum clear door opening width of 900 mm for accessible entrances. This ensures wheelchair users and persons with mobility aids can pass through comfortably.
