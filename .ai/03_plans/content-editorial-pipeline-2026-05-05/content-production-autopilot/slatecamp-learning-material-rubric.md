# SlateCamp Learning Material Rubric

Source: Claude senior educational review, adapted for SlateCamp production editing.

## Article standard

Each publish-ready article must:

1. State a specific LEA learning objective.
2. Teach one concept per section.
3. Use this pattern where useful: explanation, concrete Philippine practice example, why it matters, quick check.
4. Reduce cognitive load with short paragraphs, defined terms, and scannable structure.
5. Include a high-yield board-exam angle.
6. Include at least one common mistake or misconception correction when the topic has known traps.
7. Preserve all custom MDX and `[SLATECAMP_UI_BLOCK:...]` placeholders exactly.
8. Avoid unsupported law/code/dimension/penalty/section claims.
9. Use source support for every changed factual claim.
10. Quarantine instead of guessing when evidence conflicts or is missing.

## Voice standard

Use direct, practical Philippine architecture language. Prefer second person where it helps. Avoid em dashes and AI filler. Use concrete examples only when they do not add unsupported facts.

## Legal/code standard

Existing prose and existing rationales are not source truth. Use local PDFs, verified source packs, and official/source evidence. Do not publish changed legal/code claims without evidence.

## Component standard

Do not invent components. Do not remove placeholders. Do not hand-edit generated `candidate.md` when compose owns it.
