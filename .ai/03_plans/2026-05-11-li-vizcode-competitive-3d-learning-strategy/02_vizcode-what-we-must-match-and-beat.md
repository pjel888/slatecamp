**LI Competitive Strategy**: Cover all 5 code families with 20 reusable scene templates (see document 04). Prioritize P0 topics first. Match VizCode's breadth of topic coverage but exceed depth with 3D spatial interaction.

### 8. Visual/Diagram-Led Learning

VizCode uses static 2D diagrams and GIF previews. **LI beats this with true 3D spatial interaction.** Every diagram VizCode shows as a flat image, LI shows as an orbitable, zoomable, layer-togglable 3D scene. This is the core competitive advantage.

### 9. Support / Correction Forms

VizCode has generic contact/report forms. **LI integrates correction directly into the learning surface:** every source card has a "Report Issue" action that captures topic ID, source ref, and screenshot context. Corrections are tracked and visible.

### 10. Trust / Legal Posture

| VizCode Weakness | SlateCamp LI Strength |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| Terms page empty (0 REST chars) | Every LI scene links to verified source cards with section/table/date |
| Privacy page generic WordPress | Source verification status visible per topic |
| "Diagrams reflect our interpretation" disclaimer | LI states: "Source-verified" or "Interpretive explanation" clearly labeled |
| Manual payment verification | Integrated entitlement; instant access |

### 11. SEO / Content Marketing

VizCode has only 2 blog posts. Many pages have 0 REST-rendered text (JS-only). **LI's relationship with Wiki gives SlateCamp SEO advantage:** every LI module links to a Wiki article with searchable text content.
