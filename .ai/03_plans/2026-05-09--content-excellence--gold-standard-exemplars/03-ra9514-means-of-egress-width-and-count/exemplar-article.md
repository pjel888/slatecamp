# RA 9514 Means of Egress: Width, Count, and Illumination

## Learning objective

Given a Fire Code egress stem, decide whether the issue is exit sufficiency, minimum egress width, number of means of egress, or illumination.

## The life-safety premise

The 2019 Revised IRR of RA 9514 requires every building or structure designed for human occupancy to have exits sufficient to permit fast and safe escape in case of fire or other emergencies. The same general requirement states that exits must be appropriate in kind, number, location, and capacity for the occupancy, exposed population, fire protection, height, and construction type.

Source: RA 9514, 2019 Revised IRR, Rule 10, Division 2, General Requirements, PDF p.89, extracted from `vitruvius-connection/001_Raw-Source-Layer/001-Laws/RA-9514/RA9514-RIRR-rev-2019.pdf`.

## Width and number are separate checks

For the general means-of-egress rule, the width of any means of egress must not be less than 915 mm unless Divisions 8 through 20 provide a specific exception. The number of means of egress from any balcony, mezzanine, storey, or portion must not be less than two, except where those same occupancy divisions specifically permit otherwise.

Source: RA 9514, 2019 Revised IRR, Rule 10, Division 2, Section 10.2.5.2, paragraphs F and G, PDF p.95.

## Load threshold trap

If the occupant load for any storey or portion is more than 500 but not more than 1000, the means of egress must not be less than three. If it is in excess of 1000, the means of egress must not be less than four. Do not confuse this count rule with the 915 mm minimum-width rule.

Source: RA 9514, 2019 Revised IRR, Rule 10, Division 2, Section 10.2.5.2, paragraph G, PDF p.95.

## Illumination check

Means of egress illumination must be continuous while the occupancy condition requires the means of egress to be available. Floors of means of egress must be illuminated at all points, including corridor intersections, stair landings, and exit doors, to not less than 10.7 lux.

Source: RA 9514, 2019 Revised IRR, Rule 10, Division 2, Section 10.2.5.11, PDF p.113.
