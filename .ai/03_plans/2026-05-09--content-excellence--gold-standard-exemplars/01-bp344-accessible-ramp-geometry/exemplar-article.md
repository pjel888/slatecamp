# BP 344 Accessible Ramp Geometry

## Learning objective

Given a ramp condition in an LEA plan-reading or code-compliance question, identify the governing accessible-ramp values for width, gradient, landing, and handrail height under the 2024 Revised IRR of BP 344.

## The rule that decides the answer

An accessible ramp starts with two values: a minimum clear width of 1200 mm and a gradient not steeper than 1:20. The same 2024 BP 344 IRR excerpt allows steeper gradients only as special cases depending on the length to be covered.

Source: BP 344, 2024 Revised IRR, Appendix A, Accessible Ramps, PDF p.2, extracted from `vitruvius-connection/001_Raw-Source-Layer/001-Laws/BP-344/2024 Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 _ National Council on Disability Affairs.pdf`.

## How the table is tested

The table row for 1:15 permits a 10.50 m maximum length, a 700 mm maximum rise, and a 10 m interval for landing. The same page states that accessible ramps with a total length longer than 10.50 m must be provided with intermediate landings with a minimum length of 1800 mm.

Source: BP 344, 2024 Revised IRR, Appendix A, Accessible Ramps, Table A.1.1 and Item 10, PDF p.2.

## Handrail trap

For accessible ramps with changes in grade higher than 170 mm, handrails are required. They must be on both sides, and they are installed at 900 mm and 700 mm above ramps and stairs. Do not answer only one height when the stem asks for accessible ramp handrails.

Source: BP 344, 2024 Revised IRR, Appendix A, Handrails and Grab Bars, PDF p.7.

## Board shortcut

For a BP 344 ramp stem, answer in this order: first width, then slope, then landing trigger, then handrails. Most wrong answers mix one value from an older memory with one value from the current accessible-ramp table. The exam-safe habit is to read the condition before choosing the number.
