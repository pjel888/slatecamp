# PD 1096 Rule VII: TGFA, AMBF, and Location Controls

## Learning objective

Given a National Building Code development-control stem, separate three questions before computing: site access/location, allowable footprint, and allowable total gross floor area.

## The rule that decides the answer

Rule VII first asks whether the proposed building can legally sit on the property condition at all. Section 704 states that no building shall be constructed unless it adjoins or has direct access to a public space, yard, or street on at least one side. The same extracted paragraph says eaves over required windows must be at least 750 mm from side and rear property lines.

Source: PD 1096, 2004 Revised IRR, Rule VII, Section 704.1, PDF p.81, extracted from `vitruvius-connection/001_Raw-Source-Layer/001-Laws/PD-1096/PD 1096 RIRR 2004 - Revised IRR (DPWH Official).pdf`.

## Do not collapse TGFA into footprint

Section 705 is not a footprint shortcut. It states that the Allowable Maximum Total Gross Floor Area (TGFA) of a proposed building/structure shall only be as allowed under Rule VII. The table percentages are used against Total Lot Area (TLA) to initially determine allowable TGFA, but the answer still depends on the correct occupancy, lot type, and applicable limitations.

Source: PD 1096, 2004 Revised IRR, Rule VII, Section 705.1-2 and Table VII.1, PDF pp.83-84.

## AMBF is a separate control

The AMBF check belongs to building footprint / percentage-of-site-occupancy analysis, with firewall conditions affecting the allowable footprint under the Rule VII and Rule VIII cross-checks. The extracted Section 704 material instructs reviewers to compare the stated rules with Rule VIII Table VIII.1, Tables VIII.2 and VIII.3 on setbacks/yards/courts, and the applicable Rule VII provisions. If the stem asks footprint, do not answer with a TGFA value.

Source: PD 1096, 2004 Revised IRR, Rule VII, Section 704.4, PDF pp.81-83.

## Board shortcut

Read the stem label before calculating: `TGFA` asks total floor area; `AMBF` asks the maximum building footprint; `PSO` asks site occupancy; `setback/yard/court` asks open space geometry. Many wrong answers are correct-looking numbers applied to the wrong control.
