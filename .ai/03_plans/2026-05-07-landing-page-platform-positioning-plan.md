## 1. Goal

Revise the landing-page plan before implementation so SlateCamp is not too tightly coupled to the board exam. Licensure Examination for Architects readiness remains the highest-intent wedge, but the umbrella brand should support architecture students, reviewees, educators, apprentices, and practicing architects.

Target positioning:

> **SlateCamp is a source-backed architecture learning platform for school, boards, and practice.**

Brand-cue rule:

> **Use “Philippine” as a scope/trust/SEO qualifier, not as the first prestige cue in the hero.**

The product should feel premium and global-caliber first, then prove that it understands Philippine codes, Licensure Examination for Architects readiness, architecture school, and local practice realities.

## 2. Non-goals

- Do not position SlateCamp as an official PRC/PRBoA/UAP source.
- Do not imply guaranteed passing or exam prediction.
- Do not attack or replace review centers.
- Do not claim university partnerships before signed agreements.
- Do not offer project-specific professional/legal advice through calculators or code pages.

## 3. Landing-page offer architecture

**Gated-free acquisition rule:** Many free resources may intentionally live behind signup/dashboard access. This is not a blocker. The landing page should show enough public preview to prove value, then route users into a free account for full Wiki/resources, quiz diagnostics, saved tools, and 3D Lab access. Copy must be honest (`free after signup`, `create a free account`, `preview`) and privacy/consent compliant because the strategy supports onboarding, analytics, ads/remarketing audiences, and product learning.

The first two scrolls must make the offer clear through four pillars:

1. **Knowledge resources** — source-backed articles, code explainers, visual guides, and field-guide pages.
2. **Quizzes** — diagnostics, drills, simulations, readiness score, and mistake repair.
3. **3D Lab** — interactive spatial/code understanding, led by a setbacks/buildable-envelope preview where feasible.
4. **Free tools** — calculators and utilities for school, review, and practice.

## 4. Narrative sequence

1. Premium umbrella hero: architecture learning platform / study companion, not only board-exam app and not a government-like “Philippine resource center” first impression.
2. Audience bridge: students, reviewees, educators, apprentices, architects.
3. Four pillars: Knowledge Resources, Quizzes, 3D Lab, Free Tools.
4. Licensure Examination for Architects pathway: “Taking the boards? Get a free readiness score.”
5. Resource proof: code atlas / wiki / source-backed explanations.
6. Quiz proof: question → explanation → source/3D/Wiki → readiness update → next topic.
7. 3D proof: show actual interactive spatial/code examples; prioritize an NBCP/PD 1096 setbacks/buildable-envelope demo with zoning/RROW/lot controls.
8. Free tools proof: paint, tile/flooring, drywall, CHB, lot/buildability, occupancy/egress calculators.
9. Review-center stance: review centers teach; SlateCamp helps measure, organize, and reinforce.
10. University stance: educator-friendly resources and diagnostics that support instruction.
11. Legal trust: educational resource, source-backed, verify with current law/professionals.

## 5. Copy direction

Primary umbrella line:

> Your architecture study companion — source-backed resources, quizzes, 3D labs, and free tools for school, boards, and practice.

Local trust line:

> Built around Philippine codes, Licensure Examination for Architects readiness, and the way architecture students actually study.

Licensure Examination for Architects line:

> Taking the boards? SlateCamp turns review into a readiness map so you know what stuck and what to fix next.

University line:

> Built to support architecture schools, review groups, and self-study — without replacing your instructor or review center.

Professional-practice line:

> Keep common references and calculators close, with source-backed explanations and clear educational boundaries.

## 6. Acceptance criteria before implementation

- Visitor can name the four product pillars in under 10 seconds.
- Hero feels premium/global-caliber first while using Philippine specificity as proof, scope, SEO, and trust.
- Landing copy is not board-exam-only; Licensure Examination for Architects readiness appears as a major pathway.
- Page contains a respectful review-center companion stance.
- Page contains a university/educator-compatible stance.
- Free tools are visible, not buried.
- Legal/official-source boundaries are clear in plan and ready for UI/footer/disclaimers.
- Claims about sources, 3D, question coverage, pass rates, and partnerships are either verified or softened.
