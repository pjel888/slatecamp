Preparing for a licensure exam in the Philippines can feel overwhelming, but choosing the right review center can make all the difference. Based on performance statistics, passing rates, and student feedback, here are the top review centers across various professional fields this 2025.

* * *

### 1. **Carl Balita Review Center (CBRC)**

**Best for**: Licensure Exam for Teachers (LET), Nursing, Criminology

CBRC has become a household name in the review industry with over 125 branches nationwide. Known for its results-driven approach, CBRC consistently produces board topnotchers. It combines traditional and online learning, making it accessible to both urban and provincial students.

**Highlights:**

*   LET: Over 85% national passing rate among CBRC reviewees
*   Strong online presence and mobile app for self-paced learners
*   High student satisfaction with comprehensive materials and coaching

* * *

### 2. **CEVAS Philippines**

**Best for**: Education licensure exams (LET, Principal’s Test)

CEVAS is recognized for its academic excellence, supported by awards from the Asia Pacific Excellence Awards and the Consumers Choice Awards. With a robust network of educators and personalized review programs, CEVAS delivers solid pass rates.

**Highlights:**

*   LET: Around 80% success rate in recent years
*   Modular coaching with a focus on weak areas
*   Affordable pricing with flexible payment terms

* * *

### 3. **Merge Review Center**

**Best for**: Teacher Licensure (LET), Education Board Exams

Since 2003, Merge has been quietly building a strong reputation, particularly in Luzon and Visayas. It offers review programs tailored for different academic backgrounds and has helped thousands of aspiring teachers pass the board.

**Highlights:**

*   High provincial passing rates
*   Intensive in-person and hybrid sessions
*   Community-style support groups

* * *

### 4. **Southville International School and Colleges (SISC)**

**Best for**: Nursing Licensure Exam (NLE)

SISC isn’t just a review center—it’s an institution with a consistent record. Their nursing graduates have achieved a 100% passing rate for eight consecutive years, including multiple topnotchers.

**Highlights:**

*   NLE: 100% passing rate from 2015 to 2023
*   Small review batches for personalized mentoring
*   Emphasis on clinical and situational exam training

* * *

### 5. **Alcorcon Engineering Review Center**

**Best for**: Mechanical Engineering Board Exams

With a 94.40% passing rate in the 2022 board exam, Alcorcon has become a top pick for engineering aspirants. It blends intensive lectures with practical problem-solving and updated materials.

**Highlights:**

*   Top-performing school in mechanical engineering
*   Optional night classes and weekend sessions
*   Online quizzes and mock board exams

* * *

### 6. **MEGAREVIEW & Tutorial Center**

**Best for**: Civil Engineering Board Exams

MEGAREVIEW has built its brand on consistently high results, averaging over 80% passing rates and producing topnotchers year after year.

**Highlights:**

*   Civil Engineering: Multiple topnotchers annually
*   Regular intensive group studies and refresher courses
*   Updated problem banks aligned with PRC standards

* * *

### 7. **Competence Management Consultancy Inc. (CompMan)**

**Best for**: Maritime Licensure Exams

Founded in 2013, CompMan quickly earned a reputation for excellence in maritime exam preparation. It boasts a perfect 5-star review rating and is known for its updated curriculum and professional instructors.

**Highlights:**

*   High national passing rate for deck and engine exams
*   5.0 average star rating from over 500 reviews
*   Emphasis on simulation-based training

* * *

### Your Next Step

Choosing the right review center is a crucial step toward passing your licensure exam. Whether you’re aiming to become a teacher, nurse, engineer, or mariner, the centers listed above have the proven track record to support your journey. Consider your location, preferred learning style, and budget—and invest in a review center that sets you up for success.
