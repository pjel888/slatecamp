## Abstract

As global trading has been present in the Philippines, the rampant consumption of the Filipinos on foreign brands has been excessive. Thus, the Consumerism of Filipinos on Foreign Brands is a qualitative study that elaborates on the concept that Filipinos tend to patronize internationally recognized products over local products as this follows the concept of Dependency Theory. Colonial mentality, taken into a deeper sense, can create an impact towards an individual consumption behavior to dependency state (David, 1980), as global trading has taken over a huge leap within the Philippines, it created a boost on exposure of different cultures. Influenced also by media (Armecin, 2017), internationally produced products have caught the attention of Filipinos to their consumption. And as Japson et al., (2017) state, "People around the globe have shifted their spending behaviors from the functional to materialistic and aesthetic purposes."

## FAQ's

![Image 23: sparkles](http://a.academia-assets.com/images/icons/sparkle.svg)
AI generated

What impacts consumer preference for foreign brands in the Philippines?add

The study identifies colonial mentality, advertising influence, and perceived product quality as key factors influencing Filipinos' preference for foreign brands over local products. A Nielsen survey indicated that 73% of Filipinos recognize advertising as a crucial influence on their brand choices.

How significant is the role of social media in brand awareness among Filipinos?add

Respondents highlight social media as a primary source of awareness regarding foreign brands, indicating its strong influence on consumer behavior. This reflects a broader trend where digital platforms shape consumer preferences in emerging markets.

What are the economic implications of Filipinos' dependency on foreign brands?add

The respondents noted that continued patronage of foreign brands could jeopardize local industries, potentially harming the Philippines' GDP and reducing domestic production. This dependency could also diminish national pride in locally made products.

What are common reasons for choosing foreign brands over local options?add

Respondents cited better quality, trendy designs, and lower prices as reasons for preferring foreign brands. Some remarked that products from developed countries are seen as more sustainable and superior in quality.

How did the study assess the preferences of Filipino consumers?add

Qualitative interviews were conducted with four Filipino respondents from diverse backgrounds to gather insights on their consumption behaviors. The narrative analysis focused on shared experiences that highlighted preferences for foreign products.
