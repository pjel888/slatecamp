# SlateCamp landing/brand positioning research brief — 2026-05-07

Working directory: `/Users/philiplewis/slatecamp`

## Mission
Produce evidence-backed strategic research for repositioning SlateCamp's landing page and brand so it can compete and win in the Philippine LEA prep niche.

## User direction to honor
- Current copy over-emphasizes SRS, quizzing methods, active recall, IRT, EMA, SM-2, etc.
- Target users care more about assurance, comfort, knowledge, reliability, and knowing what to do next.
- Ethical fear marketing is allowed: name the real fear of failing the LEA, but convert fear into control and a plan. Do not shame users.
- SlateCamp should not position itself as a primary review center replacement. It should be a supplementary buddy/companion beside review centers like JPT/CDEP: the user's LEA Pokédex.
- Closest competitor is Vizcode. Things Vizcode provides, SlateCamp should provide for free where strategically useful, but in 3D and interactive form.
