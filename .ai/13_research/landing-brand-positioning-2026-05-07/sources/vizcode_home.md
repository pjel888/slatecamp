## Frequently Asked Questions

 What is VIZCODE?

VIZCODE is a **visual learning platform** for architectural codes in the Philippines. We simplify complex standards through **diagrams, illustrated guides, and interactive learning tools**—making code review more intuitive, especially for those preparing for the ALE.

 What is VIZCODE’s scope?

**VIZCODE covers visually illustrated interpretations of key architectural codes and standards in the Philippines—particularly those relevant to the Architecture Licensure Exam (ALE) and professional practice.** Our content focuses on provisions that can be effectively translated into visual diagrams to make code review clearer, faster, and more engaging.

These include:

*   PD 1096 – National Building Code of the Philippines
*   RA 9514 – Fire Code of the Philippines
*   BP 344 – Accessibility Law
*   BP 220 – Economic and Socialized Housing
*   PD 957 – Subdivision and Condominium Law
*   Philippine Green Building Code

 Is this only for ALE takers?

Not at all. While VIZCODE is highly valuable for Architecture Licensure Exam (ALE) review, it’s also built for professionals in practice who want a faster, more visual way to navigate architectural codes. Licensed architects and firms can access VIZCODE through our Enterprise plans, as outlined on our website.
