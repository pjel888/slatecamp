What is VIZCODE?

VIZCODE is a **visual learning platform** for architectural codes in the Philippines. We simplify complex standards through **diagrams, illustrated guides, and interactive learning tools**—making code review more intuitive, especially for those preparing for the ALE.

How do I sign up for VIZCODE?

Simply go to our website: [www.vizcodeph.com](http://www.vizcodeph.com/), then on **Sign Up** at the top right corner. Fill in your details, choose a plan that works for you (Single or Group), and you’re in! Once payment is verified, you’ll receive access to your VIZCODE account via email.

Are there any discounts?

Yes! We offer two types of discounts:

*   **Group Plan Discount** – If you’re signing up with your friends, classmates, or organization, you can avail of our [**Group Plan**](http://vizcodeph.com/group-plans), which gives each member access at a reduced rate. Everyone still gets their own individual account.
*   **Affiliated Partner Discount** – If you’re part of a student organization or group that has an official partnership with VIZCODE, you may be eligible for an **exclusive partner discount**. To claim it, simply reach out to your organization’s president or designated officer.

What is VIZCODE’s scope?

**VIZCODE covers visually illustrated interpretations of key architectural codes and standards in the Philippines—particularly those relevant to the Architecture Licensure Exam (ALE) and professional practice.** Our content focuses on provisions that can be effectively translated into visual diagrams to make code review clearer, faster, and more engaging.

These include:

*   PD 1096 – National Building Code of the Philippines
*   RA 9514 – Fire Code of the Philippines
*   BP 344 – Accessibility Law
*   BP 220 – Economic and Socialized Housing
*   PD 957 – Subdivision and Condominium Law
*   Philippine Green Building Code

Your account is for **personal use** and can be accessed on **one device at a time**. While you can log in from different devices, please note that logging in on a new device will automatically log out the previous one.

Licensed architects and firms can access VIZCODE through our Enterprise plans, as outlined on our website.

What is Isolation Mode?

This mode allows users to concentrate on a single topic or code set at a time (e.g., Accessibility Law, Building Code, Fire Code). It’s an effective tool for focused study, especially when strengthening a specific area of weakness or targeting a particular topic in preparation for the board exams.

What is Comparison Mode?

Comparative Mode presents multiple questions across different topics or standards side by side. This format enables learners to analyze similarities and differences between key provisions, enhancing comprehension and retention of interrelated concepts across various codes.

![Image 2: 📍](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/17.0.2/svg/1f4cd.svg) Recommended for synthesizing knowledge across subjects and for higher-level comparisons.

What is Game Mode?

Game Mode introduces a competitive, time-bound learning experience that simulates a quiz or mock exam environment. It helps users sharpen their recall under pressure while earning points to track performance. This approach is ideal for individuals who benefit from gamified learning or are preparing for time-limited assessments.
