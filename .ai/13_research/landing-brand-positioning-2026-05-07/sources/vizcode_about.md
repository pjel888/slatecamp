# An Enhanced Learning Platform

We envision a future where architects in the Philippines have seamless access to a comprehensive visual representation of architecture standards.

[Watch How It Works](http://vizcodeph.com/about-us/#)

The Problem

## Studying architectural standards efficiently is difficult

Future architects and designers have to learn and understand thousands of regulatory requirements that oftentimes overlap with each other. With no platform where these concepts are consolidated visually, there is much confusion, errors, and inefficiencies in both academic and professional settings.

![Image 5](https://vizcodeph.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/VizCode-GIFs_Problem_720x510px.gif)

![Image 6](https://vizcodeph.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/VizCode-GIFs_Solution_720x510px.gif)

The Solution

## We bridge the gap between national building codes and efficient visual learning

VIZCODE is a comprehensive, user-friendly, visually oriented tool that integrates all pertinent codes and standards, simplifying the learning process and improving the quality, safety, and efficiency of our architects.
