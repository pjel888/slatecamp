# Archive Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-04-16

## What qualifies for archive

Archive a document or folder when it is:

- deprecated but still historically important
- superseded by a newer canonical document
- implemented and no longer an active working surface
- experimental, abandoned, or retained for recovery/reference value
- legacy material from an older operating model that should remain retrievable

## What archive is not

- Archive is **not deletion**.
- Archive is **not permission to erase context**.
- Archive is **not a silent rewrite of project history**.

## Archive rules

1. Preserve recoverability.
2. Keep enough metadata or folder structure so humans can understand provenance.
3. Prefer adding archive notes or indexes before moving items.
4. Do not archive active source-of-truth materials without documenting the replacement.
5. If duplicates exist, archive only after a human can identify the canonical source safely.
