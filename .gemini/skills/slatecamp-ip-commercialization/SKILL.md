## Revenue Stream 1: White-Label Engine Licensing

**What it is:** Other organizations (review centers, universities, professional associations) pay to run SlateCamp's mastery engine under their own brand.

**The pitch to a review center:**
> "You have the students, the brand, and the classroom. We have the mastery engine, the scoring algorithm, and the taxonomy. Give us ₱500/student per exam cycle. We run the digital layer — branded as yours. You take the credit when they pass."

**Pricing model:**
- Per-student license: ₱400–₱800/student/exam cycle
- Annual platform fee: ₱50,000–₱200,000/year (for larger institutions)
- Revenue share: 15–25% of any digital revenue the partner generates using the platform

**Who to target:**
- Top 10 LEA review centers in Metro Manila (immediate — they already have student lists)
- Architecture department chairs at UST, PLM, Mapua, FEU (semester license)
- UAP student chapter programs (subsidized institutional deal)
- PRC-affiliated review programs (government partnership play — longer cycle but strategic)

**Technical requirement:** A white-label toggle in the admin dashboard. Logo + color scheme swap. No new code needed beyond a brand theming layer.

**Revenue potential (Year 2):** 5 review centers × 100 students × ₱600 = ₱300,000/exam cycle × 2 cycles = ₱600,000/year from white-label alone.

## Revenue Stream 2: Readiness Score API (Algorithm-as-a-Service)

**What it is:** Other EdTech companies or institutions pay to use SlateCamp's Readiness Score algorithm via API. They send in quiz performance data; SlateCamp returns a calibrated readiness score.

**The commercial case:**
- Building a reliable, IRT-calibrated readiness scoring system from scratch takes 12–18 months and significant data science investment
- SlateCamp has already built it
- Other exam prep platforms (for nursing, civil engineering, CPA) can license the algorithm and apply it to their own question banks

**Pricing model:**
- Monthly API fee: ₱5,000–₱20,000/month depending on volume
- Per-score call: ₱2–₱5/score generated (for low-volume users)
- Enterprise API license: ₱100,000–₱500,000/year (for large platforms)

**Target licensees:**
- Philippine exam prep platforms for other PRC-regulated professions
- Corporate training companies needing skills readiness measurement
- TESDA or CHED-affiliated digital assessment programs (government sales)

**Technical requirement:** An API wrapper around the existing scoring engine. Authentication layer. Rate limiting. Documentation. ~4–6 weeks of engineering.

**IP protection:** Register the Readiness Score methodology and algorithm as a trade secret. Trademark "Readiness Score" as a brand term. License agreements must include non-compete clauses for the architecture/LEA market.

## Revenue Stream 4: Outcome-Based / Performance-Tied Pricing

**What it is:** SlateCamp charges a premium tier where a portion of the fee is refunded (or not charged) if the student does not pass the LEA.

**The model:**
```
SlateCamp Guarantee Pro:
- Price: ₱4,999 (vs. ₱1,999 standard Exam Pass)
- Promise: If you use SlateCamp for 60+ days, complete 80%+ of the Camp Trail,
  and still fail the LEA — you get ₱2,000 back.
- Terms: Requires verified exam attempt + usage data meeting activity threshold
```

**Why this works:**
1. It is a premium price point (₱4,999) — 2.5x the standard Exam Pass
2. The refund condition requires heavy platform usage (60 days, 80% trail) — these users are the most likely to pass anyway
3. The psychological effect on conversion is massive: "They're betting on you passing."
4. The actual refund rate, for users who meet the activity threshold, will be very low (SlateCamp's design is to make them pass)

**Commercial math:**
- 100 Guarantee Pro users × ₱4,999 = ₱499,900
- Refund rate estimate: 5–10% (based on activity threshold filtering)
- Net refunds: 8 × ₱2,000 = ₱16,000
- Net revenue: ₱483,900 — vs ₱199,900 at standard pricing for the same 100 users
- Uplift: +142% revenue from the same cohort

**The "win-win" framing:** This is the literal commercial embodiment of Philip's insight. When the user wins (passes), SlateCamp keeps the full fee. When SlateCamp wins (user stays highly engaged), the user almost always passes. The guarantee is not a risk — it's a confidence signal.

## Revenue Stream 5: Platform Expansion — Same Codebase, New Professions

**What it is:** Deploy the SlateCamp engine for other Philippine PRC-regulated board exams — same platform, new taxonomy, new content.

**The commercial logic:** SlateCamp's entire technical infrastructure (Next.js, Supabase, adaptive testing engine, Readiness Score, Heatmap, Camp Trail) is profession-agnostic. Expanding to a new profession is a content and taxonomy problem, not a code problem.

**Expansion priority sequence:**
```
1. Architecture (LEA) — current beachhead ✓
2. Nursing (NLE) — largest PH board exam by volume (~100,000+ candidates/year)
3. Civil Engineering (CE Board) — structural overlap with architecture content
4. CPA (CLE/CPA Board) — high-income profession, high willingness to pay
5. Medicine (PLE) — highest stakes, highest ARPU potential, requires deepest content investment
6. Law (Bar Exam) — different structure but massive prestige market
```

**Revenue math for Nursing alone:**
- NLE candidates: ~100,000+/year × 2 cycles
- Capture 2% as paying users: 2,000 × ₱399/month × 4-month average = ₱3.2M/year
- Same codebase. New content investment: 3–6 months of SME content production.

**Build vs. license decision:**
- Self-operate new professions: Higher margin, more control, slower
- License the platform to a profession-specific partner (e.g., a nursing review center that already has the content): Revenue share model, faster to market, lower risk

## Revenue Stream 6: 3D Lab as Standalone Curriculum Product

**What it is:** SlateCamp's 3D Lab WebGL modules are licensed to architecture schools as supplemental digital curriculum tools — separate from the exam prep platform.

**The value proposition to schools:**
> "Your students can now walk through a parametric building code model in 3D on their laptop. No physical models. No expensive software. Browser-based, mobile-accessible, code-cited. License for one semester for ₱5,000 per class."

**Pricing:**
- Per-class semester license: ₱3,000–₱8,000
- School-wide annual license: ₱30,000–₱80,000
- International architecture programs (SEA region): $500–$2,000 USD/semester

**Why this is a separate product:** Architecture schools have education budgets separate from students' personal spending. The school pays for curriculum tools; the student pays for exam prep. Two separate purchase decisions, two separate budget lines.

**Content moat:** The 3D Lab modules are LEA-calibrated, PD 1096 / RA 9514 / NSCP accurate, and built by licensed Filipino architects. This combination does not exist anywhere else. International architecture programs in SEA with Filipino faculty or Filipino students are natural buyers.
