# Early Christian and Byzantine Architecture Refined Source Map

## Stable Source Anchors

- Early Christian: basilica plan, nave, aisles, apse, narthex, processional movement toward altar.
- Byzantine: centralized/domed space, pendentives, Hagia Sophia, San Vitale, SS Sergius and Bacchus, mosaics, gold/light, sacred interior atmosphere.
- Structural contrast: linear basilica versus centralized pendentive-dome system.

## LEA Content Boundary

- Ask for basilica versus Byzantine plan logic, pendentive recognition, Hagia Sophia identity, and Ravenna/San Vitale mosaic interiors.
- Avoid exact dimension tests unless the local source map captures the exact figure.
