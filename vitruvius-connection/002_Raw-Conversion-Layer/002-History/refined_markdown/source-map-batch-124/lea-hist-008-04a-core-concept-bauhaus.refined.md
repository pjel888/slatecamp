## Claim Map

- Bauhaus is anchored to 1919-1933, with Walter Gropius founding/directing from 1919, Hannes Meyer succeeding him in 1928, Mies van der Rohe becoming director in 1930, and Nazi pressure forcing closure in 1933.
- The Bauhaus moved from Weimar to Dessau in 1925; the Bauhaus Building opened in 1926 and translated school ideas into steel, concrete, glass, workshops, bridge, and functional wings.
- Bauhaus pedagogy is anchored as art/craft/technology/industry integration, workshop learning, preliminary-course structure, and cross-media production rather than a single style to copy.
- Bauhaus-Archiv anchors Moholy-Nagy in the preliminary course and materials/space work, Klee and Kandinsky in form/color/fine-art teaching, and Schlemmer in theater.
- MoMA anchors Bauhaus as a school founded in 1919 and shut down by Nazis in 1933, with broad output across industrial design, furniture, architecture, graphics, photography, textiles, ceramics, theater, painting, and sculpture.
