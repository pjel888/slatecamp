# Refined Source Conversion: International Style Modernism in America

## Exam claim set

1. The MoMA 1932 exhibition is the safest source anchor for International Style canonization.
2. International Style recognition should use volume, regularity, industrial materials, and little applied ornament.
3. Mies is the high-yield American corporate-modernism anchor.
4. Saarinen, Kahn, and Pei are modernist but not interchangeable with Miesian International Style.
5. Postmodern questions should emphasize critique, historical reference, symbolism, and communication.

## Question edit policy

Linked questions should separate glass-grid International Style cues from expressive structural, monumental, geometric, and postmodern cues.
