# Refined Source Conversion: Maori Architecture of Aotearoa

## Exam claim set

1. Whare, wharenui, marae, whakapapa, and pa are the primary exam terms.
2. Wharenui should be interpreted as ancestral and communal architecture.
3. Carving, weaving, and tukutuku communicate whakapapa, narrative, status, and identity.
4. Colonization and the 1840 Treaty changed land, governance, material, and institutional conditions; later design reasserts Maori identity.
5. Contemporary Maori architecture depends on mana whenua, place, community process, and cultural values, not motif copying.

## Question edit policy

Linked questions should remove unsupported colonization details and emphasize cultural meaning plus contemporary bicultural process.
