# Megalithic Architecture Official Source Map - Refined Source Map

## Stable Source Anchors
- UNESCO Stonehenge/Avebury anchors the late Neolithic and Bronze Age monument landscape, including standing stones, lintelled stone settings, and ritual/funerary/astronomical interpretation boundaries.
- UNESCO Megalithic Temples of Malta anchors Ggantija and related Maltese temple complexes as prehistoric megalithic temple architecture, not passage graves or menhir alignments.
- UNESCO Bru na Boinne anchors Newgrange/Knowth/Dowth as passage-tomb and chambered mound monuments with ritual/funerary significance.
- Historic Environment Scotland anchors Calanais/Callanish as a cruciform standing-stone setting on Lewis and cautions against overclaiming single-purpose interpretations.

## LEA Content Boundary
- Use for monument type, period, and cautious site-function claims; avoid unsupported construction-machine, exact-alignment, or universal-purpose claims.
- Treat site purpose and technical reconstruction cautiously unless directly anchored to one of the listed official pages or local conversion sources.
