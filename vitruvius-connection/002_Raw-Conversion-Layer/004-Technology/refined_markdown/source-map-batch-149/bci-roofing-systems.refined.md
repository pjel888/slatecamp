BCI supports roof-material selection by slope and water-shedding behavior. Flat roofs rely on continuous membranes and drainage; low-slope roofs rely on roll or membrane systems; steeper roofs may use shingles, tiles, or sheet materials. The roof also participates in moisture, thermal, air, fire, gravity-load, wind-uplift, and drainage performance.
