## Safe Exam Claims

1. A standard hook can be a 180-deg bend with an extension of at least 4 bar diameters and not less than 65 mm.
2. A standard hook can be a 90-deg bend with an extension of at least 12 bar diameters.
3. For stirrup and tie anchorage, 16 mm and smaller bars may use a 90-deg bend with an extension of at least 6 bar diameters.
4. For stirrup and tie anchorage, 19 mm to 25 mm bars using a 90-deg bend need an extension of at least 12 bar diameters.
5. For 25 mm and smaller stirrup or tie anchorage, a 135-deg bend needs an extension of at least 6 bar diameters.
6. Closed ties and continuously wound ties use a 135-deg bend plus an extension of at least 6 bar diameters, but not less than 75 mm.
7. Minimum inside bend diameters for standard hooks other than small stirrups and ties follow the table: 10 mm through 25 mm bars use 6db, bars larger than 25 mm through 40 mm use 8db, and bars larger than 40 mm through 57 mm use 10db.
8. For stirrup and tie hooks, inside bend diameter is not less than 4 bar diameters for 16 mm and smaller bars; larger bars follow the table.
9. A cross tie has a hook not less than 135 deg with at least a six-diameter extension, but not less than 75 mm, at one end and a hook not less than 90 deg with at least a six-diameter extension at the other end.
10. A hoop is a closed tie or continuously wound tie; closed tie elements use 135-deg hooks with six-diameter extensions at each end, but not less than 75 mm.
11. A later frame-member tie rule in the same source requires hooks not less than 135 deg with extensions not less than six tie-bar diameters or 60 mm.
