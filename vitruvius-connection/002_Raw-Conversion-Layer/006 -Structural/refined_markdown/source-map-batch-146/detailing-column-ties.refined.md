## Safe Exam Claims

1. Column lateral reinforcement includes spirals and ties for compression members.
2. Spirals for cast-in-place columns are not less than 10 mm diameter, have clear spacing between 25 mm and 75 mm, and are anchored by 1.5 extra turns at each end.
3. Tie reinforcement encloses all bars; ties are at least 10 mm for longitudinal bars 32 mm and smaller, and at least 12 mm for 36 mm to 57 mm and bundled longitudinal bars.
4. Vertical spacing of ties does not exceed 16 longitudinal bar diameters, 48 tie diameters, or the least dimension of the compression member.
5. Ties are arranged so every corner and alternate longitudinal bar has lateral support by a tie corner with included angle not more than 135 deg.
6. No vertical bar is farther than 150 mm clear on each side along the tie from a laterally supported bar.
7. Compression reinforcement in beams is enclosed by ties or stirrups satisfying the column tie size and spacing limits where compression reinforcement is required.
8. Special transverse reinforcement can use single or overlapping hoops or cross ties of the same bar size and spacing, and cross ties engage peripheral longitudinal bars.
9. Special transverse reinforcement is not spaced more than one-quarter of the minimum member dimension nor 100 mm, and cross tie or overlapping hoop leg spacing is not more than 350 mm on center perpendicular to the member axis.
10. The special transverse reinforcement length from a joint face is not less than the member depth at the joint face, one-sixth of clear span, and 450 mm.
11. Where special transverse reinforcement is not provided throughout the full column length, the remaining length contains spiral or hoop reinforcement at not more than the smaller of 6 times the longitudinal column bar diameter or 150 mm.
12. A later frame-member tie rule in the same source requires hooks not less than 135 deg with extensions not less than six tie-bar diameters or 60 mm.
