## Safe Exam Claims

1. Structural connections can be categorized geometrically as point bolted connections, line welded connections, and plane glued connections.
2. Connections usually use transitional elements such as steel angles, tees, or plates.
3. Pin or hinge joints allow rotation but resist translation, while rigid or fixed joints restrain rotation and translation and provide force and moment resistance.
4. Steel column-beam connections can be made moment-resistant by bolting or welding the beam flanges to the column.
5. Braced frames can use simply connected pin joints, making their fabrication and erection more economical than moment-resisting connections.
6. Eccentrically braced frames introduce bending and shear into the frame, lowering stiffness but increasing ductility.
