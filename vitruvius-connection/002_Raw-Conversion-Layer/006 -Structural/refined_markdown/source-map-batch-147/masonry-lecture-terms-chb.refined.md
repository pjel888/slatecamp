## Safe Exam Claims

1. Masonry is built-up construction or a combination of clay, concrete, or stone set in mortar, or plain concrete.
2. A bed is the horizontal surface on which stones or bricks lie in courses, and a course is a continuous layer of masonry units.
3. A wythe or tier is each continuous vertical section of wall one masonry unit thick.
4. Bond is the connection formed by lapping masonry units to prevent vertical joints from falling over each other.
5. A stretcher is laid lengthwise of a wall; a header extends over the thickness of a wall; a heading course is all headers.
6. Common bond consists of five stretcher courses and then a header course.
7. English bond consists of alternate courses of stretchers and headers.
8. Flemish bond consists of alternate headers and stretchers in each course.
9. Concrete hollow block is a hollow masonry unit with two or three cells or cores.
10. The lecture lists common CHB sizes as 100 mm, 150 mm, and 200 mm thick by 200 mm high by 400 mm long.
11. The lecture states CHB should be laid on a full bed of mortar with horizontal and vertical joints 10 mm thick.
12. The lecture states reinforcement intersections should be tied, reinforced cells filled with cement mortar, lintel or beam block course every twelfth course, and column stiffener every 4.80 m length.
