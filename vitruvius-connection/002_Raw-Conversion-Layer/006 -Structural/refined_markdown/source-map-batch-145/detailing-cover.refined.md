## Safe Exam Claims

1. Reinforcement must be accurately placed and adequately supported before concrete is placed, and cover tolerances are controlled.
2. For cast-in-place concrete cast against and permanently exposed to earth, the source states 75 mm minimum cover.
3. For cast-in-place concrete exposed to earth or weather, the source states 50 mm cover for 19 mm through 57 mm bars and 40 mm cover for 16 mm and smaller bars.
4. For cast-in-place surfaces not exposed to weather or ground, the source states 20 mm cover for slabs and walls with 36 mm and smaller bars.
5. For cast-in-place beams and columns not exposed to weather or ground, the source states 40 mm cover for primary reinforcement, ties, stirrups, and spirals.
6. For plant-control precast wall panels exposed to earth or weather, the source states 40 mm cover for 40 mm through 57 mm bars and 20 mm cover for 36 mm and smaller bars.
7. For plant-control precast slabs and walls not exposed to weather or ground, the source states 30 mm cover for 40 mm through 57 mm bars and 16 mm cover for 36 mm and smaller bars.
8. The source treats plant-control precast beams and columns as a separate cover category; this batch does not collapse that category into the cast-in-place beam and column value.
9. If cover values conflict, the source says the larger value governs. Fire protection or corrosive exposure can also require greater cover.
