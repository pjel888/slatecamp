## Safe Exam Claims

1. Flat plates can achieve greater spans or reduced slab thicknesses with post-tensioning, and post-tensioning improves control of deflection and cracking.
2. Ribbed concrete construction can extend to longer spans when post-tensioning is used.
3. Precast concrete slabs are one-way spanning units that may be supported by site-cast concrete, precast concrete, masonry bearing walls, or structural frames.
4. Precast units may be manufactured with normal-density or structural lightweight concrete and prestressed for greater structural efficiency, resulting in less depth, reduced weight, and longer spans.
5. A reinforced concrete topping can bond with precast units to form a composite structural unit.
6. For diaphragm behavior, steel reinforcement should tie precast slab units together at supports and end bearings.
7. Concrete members may be prestressed by pretensioning at a factory site or by posttensioning at the construction site.
8. Cast-in-place post-tensioned concrete systems inherently provide moment resistance where columns, beams, and slabs meet.
9. Precast walls can provide high-quality concrete finishes and may be prestressed.
