# Refined Source Conversion: Space and Form in Architecture

## Exam claim set

1. Form is the visible/mass property of architecture; space is the bounded or organized void that users inhabit.
2. Shape, size, color, texture, position, orientation, and visual inertia are form-reading cues.
3. Articulation makes parts clear in relation to each other and can be expressed through material, color, texture, pattern, corners, openings, and light.
4. A good exam answer treats form and space as interdependent rather than ranking one as automatically superior.
5. Scale analysis must distinguish visual scale from human scale.
