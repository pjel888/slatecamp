- Universal Design means products and environments usable by all people, to the greatest extent possible, without adaptation or specialized design.
- The seven principles are Equitable Use, Flexibility in Use, Simple and Intuitive Use, Perceptible Information, Tolerance for Error, Low Physical Effort, and Size and Space for Approach and Use.
