Safe claims captured:
- A foundation supports and anchors the superstructure and transmits loads safely into the earth.
- Foundation selection responds to soil, rock, and water below the building.
- Soil bearing capacity can limit building size or require deep foundations.
- Shallow or spread foundations are used when stable soil of adequate bearing capacity occurs relatively near the ground surface.
- Mat foundations distribute concentrated loads where individual spread footings would be inefficient or differential settlement is a concern.
- Pile or caisson foundations are used when building loads exceed the bearing capacity of supporting soil.
