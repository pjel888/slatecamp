Safe claims captured:
- Roof and exterior wall assemblies shelter interior spaces and control moisture, heat, and air flow.
- The roof system is the primary sheltering element for interior spaces.
- Roof form and slope must be compatible with the roofing type used to shed water to drains, gutters, and downspouts.
- Flat roofs require continuous membrane roofing, a recommended minimum slope of 1/4 inch per foot (1:50), and drainage to interior drains; secondary overflow drains or scuppers are needed where water could be trapped.
- Low-slope roofs require roll or continuous membrane roofing; medium- and high-slope roofs may use shingles, tiles, or sheet materials.
- Roof systems carry gravity loads, rain/snow loads where applicable, equipment loads, and wind uplift, and may need fire resistance and thermal/moisture control.
- Thermal insulation and vapor-control placement affect roof assembly performance; protected membrane systems put insulation above the membrane.
