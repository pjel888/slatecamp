## Captured Claim Anchors

- Le Corbusier formulated the Five Points of new architecture in 1927; Villa Savoye applies the five points.
- Villa Savoye and its lodge are anchored to Poissy, France, 1928-1931; construction continued to delivery in summer 1931.
- The Five Points are pilotis, roof garden/roof terrace, free plan, free facade, and ribbon/banded windows, made possible by reinforced concrete frame logic.
- Modulor, Unite, Chandigarh, and Ronchamp are guarded as later or different anchors, not proof that every Le Corbusier building is a Five Points checklist.
