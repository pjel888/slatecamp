## Captured Claim Anchors

- Barcelona Pavilion is anchored as Mies and Lilly Reich, 1929 German Pavilion, glass/steel/marble/travertine/onyx, disassembled after the exposition and reconstructed in 1986.
- Farnsworth House is anchored through NPS as designed by Mies in 1945 and constructed in 1950-1951 on the Fox River floodplain, with glass-box clear-span logic.
- Seagram Building is anchored as Mies/Philip Johnson, 1956-1958/1958, glass and bronze, modular curtain wall, plaza relationship, and proportional detail.
- Seagram bronze I-beam vocabulary is used as expressive curtain-wall rhythm, not as a claim that the exterior mullions carry the structural load.
