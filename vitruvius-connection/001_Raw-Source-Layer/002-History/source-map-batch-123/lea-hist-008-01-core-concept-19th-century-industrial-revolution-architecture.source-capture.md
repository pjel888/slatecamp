## Captured Claim Anchors

- The Crystal Palace is treated as an iron-and-glass, prefabricated exhibition hall designed by Joseph Paxton for the 1851 Great Exhibition.
- The Eiffel Tower is treated as an 1887-1889 open-lattice wrought-iron structure and 1889 exposition gateway, not as masonry monumentality.
- The Home Insurance Building/Jenney anchor is limited to early internal iron-and-steel skeleton-frame high-rise development; do not erase its masonry sidewall limits.
- Sullivan and Arts and Crafts are used as exam vocabulary for function-led design and material/craft reform, not as identical movements.
