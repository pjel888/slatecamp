## Captured Claim Anchors

- Bauhaus is anchored as founded by Walter Gropius in 1919 and active until 1933, with Weimar/Dessau/Berlin phases.
- The Bauhaus moved to Dessau in 1925; the Gropius-designed Bauhaus Building opened in 1926 and used steel, concrete, and glass.
- Fagus Factory is anchored through UNESCO as Gropius work beginning around 1910 and a major modern functionalist/curtain-wall precedent.
- Gropius House is anchored as a 1938 Lincoln, Massachusetts house that combines New England elements with Bauhaus/modern materials and pedagogy.
