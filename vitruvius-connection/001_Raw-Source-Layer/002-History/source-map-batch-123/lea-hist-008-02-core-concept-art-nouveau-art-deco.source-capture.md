## Captured Claim Anchors

- Art Nouveau is mapped to whiplash curves, natural forms, integrated ornament, Horta, Guimard, and early modern break from historicist design.
- Art Deco is mapped to geometric, machine-age, luxury/function vocabulary, the 1925 Paris decorative arts exposition, and Chrysler Building/William Van Alen.
- The Chrysler Building is treated as a 1928-1930 Art Deco skyscraper by William Van Alen with sunburst stainless-steel crown/spire and geometric decoration.
- Gaudi/Catalan Modernisme is used as an organic/geometric bridge only; it is not forced into a simple Paris Art Nouveau formula.
