## Use Limits

- Do not claim oldest, continuously used, exact arrival century, or exact mosque completion facts unless the local source map or original question carries them.
- Do not treat Philippine Islamic architecture as imported Middle Eastern form; source-safe framing is Islamic spatial vocabulary adapted to local materials, climate, and ethnolinguistic traditions.
