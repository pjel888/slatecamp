## Captured Claim Anchors

- UNESCO anchors the Imperial Palaces listing to the Ming and Qing imperial palace complex in Beijing and Shenyang; the Beijing palace is the Forbidden City and functions as the major official monument anchor.
- UNESCO anchors the Temple of Heaven as an imperial ritual complex for sacrifices to Heaven and harvest prayers, with axial/cosmological planning cues.
- Local Chinese Architecture and HOA Module 3 locators carry the exam vocabulary for dougong, siheyuan, raised platforms, Temple of Heaven recognition, Forbidden City recognition, and the Yingzao Fashi/manualization layer.
- Fletcher is used as a historical comparative source only for broad typology and vocabulary, not as a sole source for modern conservation facts.
