## Source-safe corrections

- Keep International Style precise; do not use it as a synonym for all American modernism.
- Keep postmodernism as a critique of austerity and abstraction, not decoration alone.
- Keep Saarinen/Kahn/Pei as distinct modernist lanes where the question clue supports them.
