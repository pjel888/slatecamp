## Captured claim map

- UNESCO anchors Wright's organic architecture to open planning, blurred interior/exterior limits, and innovative modern materials.
- Robie House/Prairie sources support low horizontal domestic composition, open spatial flow, overhangs, and landscape relation.
- Fallingwater sources support house/waterfall integration and the reinforced-concrete cantilever as the exam-safe structural cue.
- Usonian and Guggenheim anchors are retained as local exam guardrails: economical domestic design and circulation-as-form.

## Source-safe corrections

- Avoid ranking Fallingwater with unsupported "most tested" language.
- Avoid treating ornament as forbidden; the source-safe point is integrated pattern/material/design.
- Avoid equating Wright with strict International Style glass-box modernism.
