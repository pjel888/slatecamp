## Captured claim map

- Budj Bim anchors engineered Indigenous cultural landscape, water channels, weirs, dams, stone features, and long-term Gunditjmara management.
- Aboriginal shelter sources support regional names and climate/resource adaptation rather than a single universal typology.
- Hyde Park Barracks anchors Francis Greenway, convict/public institutional architecture, and colonial administration.
- Old Government House anchors imported authority adapted to Australian climate through verandahs and shaded/ventilated edges.

## Source-safe corrections

- Replace temporary-only claims with a spectrum of mobile, semi-permanent, and engineered place-responsive forms.
- Avoid pilotis wording for Old Government House; use verandahs and climate adaptation.
- Keep colonial architecture tied to governance, labor, material, and climate.
