## Captured Claim Anchors
- Schumacher's Parametricism source anchors the movement as a theoretical/design-methodology claim rather than a universally accepted historical period label.
- ZHA identifies the Heydar Aliyev Centre as 2007-2012, designed by Zaha Hadid and Patrik Schumacher with Saffet Kaya Bekiroglu, and describes continuous surface/plaza-building relationships and advanced computing coordination.
- ZHA identifies the Serpentine North/Sackler Gallery conversion and tensile extension, 2009-2013, in Kensington Gardens.
- buildingSMART identifies IFC as a standardized digital description of the built environment including identity, attributes, and relationships.
- NIBS NBIMS defines BIM as a digital representation of physical and functional facility characteristics and a shared knowledge resource.
- Autodesk Revit source anchors parametric change coordination in BIM workflows without equating BIM with avant-garde Parametricism.
