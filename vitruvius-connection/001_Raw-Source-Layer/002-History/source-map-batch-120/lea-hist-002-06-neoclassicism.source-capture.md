## Captured Claim Anchors

- AOC source-maps the U.S. Capitol as 19th-century neoclassical architecture whose ancient Greek and Roman design sources evoke founding ideals.
- Monticello source-maps Jefferson designing and redesigning his house over decades and identifies it as a UNESCO World Heritage presidential home.
- Berlin.de source-maps Brandenburg Gate as a classicist building, built 1788-1791, designed by Langhans, and inspired by the Propylaea of the Athenian Acropolis.
- HOA Module 2 P3 anchors the exam vocabulary for Neoclassical restraint, archaeological seriousness, Monticello, civic symbolism, and the Baroque/Rococo contrast.

## Use Limits

- Do not overstate democracy as a universal motive for every Neoclassical building; use it where the question is civic or American.
- Treat publication and excavation references as movement catalysts, not as the only causes of Neoclassicism.
