## Captured Claim Anchors

- Vatican News source-maps Bernini as designer of the Baldacchino, begun in 1624 and completed in the 1630s, using twisted columns and a monumental scale for St. Peter.
- UNESCO Versailles source-maps the palace and park as a major royal complex tied to Louis XIV and formal court power.
- HOA Module 2 P2 and Fletcher anchor Baroque spatial movement, Bernini versus Borromini, Jesuit visibility, Versailles political theater, and Rococo lightness as a later reaction.

## Use Limits

- Do not treat every dramatic 17th-century building as Counter-Reformation church space; Versailles is a political-court example.
- Do not cite unsupported exact dates for minor churches unless carried by the question source map.
