- Hitchcock/Johnson principles are kept exam-safe as volume over mass, regularity, and avoidance of applied decoration; do not overstate them as absolute functionalism in every case.
- International Style criticism is framed as risk of placeless universalism and climate/culture mismatch, not as an unsupported single-cause political claim.
