## Captured Claim Anchors

- Aalto is anchored as a Finnish modern architect and designer whose mature work is user-friendly, humane, and responsive to material, place, and nature rather than a purely severe geometric formula.
- Paimio Sanatorium is anchored to the 1929-1933 competition/design/construction frame, tuberculosis-care program, patient-oriented details, sanatorium furniture, and bent plywood Paimio chair lineage.
- Villa Mairea is anchored to 1938-1939, Aino and Alvar Aalto, wood/stone/rendered/tactile materials, free-form entry canopy, open garden relationship, and intimate modern domestic planning.
- Armchair 42 / Small Paimio is anchored to Aalto in 1932, Paimio Sanatorium, form-bent birch lamella, and form-pressed plywood.
- Finlandia Hall is anchored as Aalto-designed, completed in 1971, with white Carrara marble, black granite contrast, civic-cultural symbolism, and landscape/setting integration in Helsinki.
