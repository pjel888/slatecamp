NPS Castillo supports Spanish Florida coquina and bastion fortification. Mount Vernon supports Palladian estate composition and the enslaved-labor/dependency context. Architect of the Capitol supports neoclassical public symbolism for the Capitol and Supreme Court.

## Claim decisions

- Keep New England central chimney and steep roof regional, not universal.

- Keep Spanish Florida separate from English colonial regional examples.
