## Local source notes

UNESCO Brasilia supports the Costa/Niemeyer pairing, the 1956-1960 creation of the capital, and the city-scale modernist vocabulary of axes, monumental civic spaces, superblocks, and official buildings. UNESCO Luis Barragan House supports regional adaptation of modernism, traditional Mexican and vernacular integration, gardens, water, color, light, and sensory space. Pritzker sources support Niemeyer's sculptural/curvilinear modernism and Barragan's pure planes, texture, color, water, and nature.
