## Local source notes

CAC Home Insurance supports Jenney, 1885, Chicago, early metal frame skeleton, and non-load-bearing exterior sheathing. CAC Monadnock supports the transition between load-bearing construction and skeleton framing. CAC Reliance supports terra-cotta/glass facade and elevator-era office use. CAC Burnham & Root and Encyclopedia of Chicago support the Chicago tall-building context and Masonic Temple as an approximate 20-plus-story guardrail. Britannica Wainwright supports Sullivan's office-building/skyscraper context.

## Claim decisions

- Home Insurance language is softened from "frame carried all loads" to "metal frame carried much of the load and reduced reliance on load-bearing masonry."
- Sullivan is retained as form-follows-function, but ornament rejection is corrected.
- Masonic Temple story-count language remains approximate and tied to source anchors rather than treated as a precision measurement.
